# Emmy · Lance's voice and taste profile · draft 1

What this is: how Lance writes and what he likes, drawn only from his own work. Every pattern has
real lines of his behind it, with the source named. It is a draft until Lance has read it.
Corrections go into Emmy's file as dated rules, and this profile is updated to match.

Sources studied 2026-10-15, read only:
- **Longhand** (Drive): his fiction and essays, 2012 to 2020. 42 of his pieces read.
- **Production** (Drive): pitches, talks, art direction, TULA, the Mechanics of Being book
  proposal, 2016 to 2026. 36 documents and 19 images.
- **Art & Design** (Drive): the Adam Smith Trading Co. brand papers, 2006 to 2007, and the tools
  he collected. No artwork of his is in this folder.
- **In His Own Words** (this project): his dictation, published newsletters and the manuscript,
  2021 to 2026.

Newest wins. When two periods disagree, the 2026 voice is the reference.

---

## 1. Which voice for which job

| Job | Register | Say it like |
|---|---|---|
| Book, newsletter, product, anything public today | Plain, spoken, first person, short. Mechanical, not mystical. | "The work is not adding. It is removing." |
| Pitch, proposal, talk | Blunt diagnosis first, credentials as plain counts, owns his part. | "I am here today because the games industry is broken. And it's no secret." |
| Art direction | Operational. Forces in tension, one image, room to move. | "Should show our concept in 1 image" |
| Internal notes, working docs | Loose, deadpan, sometimes profane. | "THIS SECTION NEEDS ALL THE HELP IT CAN GET" |
| Fiction (2014 to 2020) | A friend telling a story at a bar. Profane, wisecracking. | "*Get this,*" / "*Anyhoo*" |

The fiction register never goes into brand, product or coaching copy.

## 2. How he writes

**1. He names what is broken before he offers the fix.**
- "We're living in system collapse. It's propelled by greed, corruption, and narcissism" (book proposal, 2026)
- "Covid wrecked office culture and trust in leadership. I'm changing that." (TULA LinkedIn script, 2023)

**2. Short declaratives, often in threes, with repetition as the beat.**
- "Studios have become corporatized. Team identity has become corporatized. Values have become corporatized." (TED talk script, 2023)
- "One framework. One destination." (newsletter 014)

**3. "Not this. That." He clears the wrong idea, then lands the right one in a fragment.**
- "The purpose of the spiritual journey, mechanically stated, is to bring stability to the mind. Not peace. Not bliss. Stability." (newsletter 016)
- "Not a metaphor for it. The actual mechanism." (manuscript, opening)
- "This is a discovery. It is not a finished system." (newsletter 014)

**4. Inner life explained as engineering. On purpose: it takes the mysticism out.**
- "The nervous system is like copper wiring to electronics. It's the send-receive." (book proposal)
- "The OS is the operating system. The nervous system is the hardware." (dictation, Mar 27)
- "You press X, Mario jumps." (2024 deck)
- "Mechanics take the mysticism and magic out of it." (book proposal)

**5. Agency, not rescue. Removing, not adding.**
- "No one is coming to save you." (dictation and manuscript, many times)
- "You're releasing what you are not to reclaim what you are." (dictation)

**6. He does not persuade. He hands you the test.**
- "I do not ask you to believe my read of it. I ask you to run the mechanics and watch your own body answer." (manuscript, Ch01)
- "You want to prove me wrong? Show me that the practice is wrong." (book proposal)

**7. Credentials as counts, never adjectives. And he owns his part.**
- "I've been working in video games for the last 30 years leading teams as small as five and as large as hundreds." (LinkedIn script)
- "I take responsibility for what doesn't work in games; here is how I was the Problem." (TED talk script)

**8. Origin in the body, told plainly.** His fiction opens on a body waking up in trouble; his
non-fiction opens on his own.
- "I was nine years old, home alone, and I couldn't breathe. It's not a figure of speech." (book proposal)
- "William began to stir but found it impossible to motivate his unresponsive body." (Longhand, 2014)

**9. Emotion arrives sideways: one plain sentence or one gesture, then move on.**
- "Making light of a shitty situation was how I coped when I was in trouble." (Downlands)
- "A single tear falls from Alfred's face" (Shroud of the Bat)

**10. Humor: self-deprecating, deadpan, mock-heroic, with a scoreboard.**
- "First step: write something for myself to prove I could do it. I did it, and it sucked." (Downlands foreword)
- "Hangover 1, William 0. Hangover wins." (The Sanctuary)
- "Zen Garden This Shit (For Mental Health)" (Quantum XPR mood deck, internal)

**11. Pop culture as instant imagery, mostly 1970s and 1980s film, TV and comics.**
- "*Next stop, Willoughby*." (Downlands)
- "rooted in 80s anime and 90s graphic comics" (Anonymous art deck)

**12. He coins and counts systems.**
- "108 body addresses, 54 a side, plus four field nodes, 112 in all" (book proposal)

**13. Punctuation.** He types a double hyphen "--" for a cut-off line or a beat before a reveal,
and a spaced hyphen " - " in essays. Real em dashes in his files are Word autocorrect, not
choice. In product copy the rule is no em dashes; "--" or a new sentence instead.
- "A man with a purpose. A man with a mission. A man--" (Nyquil)

**14. His edit is a cut.** From 2015 on, every revision removes: italics, asides, parentheses.
- 2014: "And when he smiled big--*I mean, REALLY BIG--lips stretched ear to ear BIG*--"
- Later: "And when he smiled his lips stretched forever, crossing ear to ear." (the two Ethan drafts)

## 3. What he values, as it shows in the work

- **Truth over comfort.** "The truth hurts, but it also makes you better" (Downlands foreword).
  "Tell the truth. Those who can hear or want to hear will find it." (manuscript, Ch02)
- **Craft humility and the drive to improve.** "there is no excuse for not aiming to improve." (Open Apology)
- **Credit to the team; makers get paid.** "Our designers are our stars, earning royalties" (Adam Smith plan, 2006)
- **Distrust of concentrated money and power.** "The promise that technology would be the great uniter was a lie." (Anonymous notes, 2015)
- **Technology with a heart.** "Wants to use tech to help people connect, in a real way to themselves and others." (Johnny Mars bible, 2020, probably co-written)
- **Identity and the overlooked kid.** "If you don't know who you truly are, you'll never know who you can truly become." (story worksheet, 2020)
- **Kindness to animals and small creatures,** often the moral center of a story (ScratchPost, Ethan, Letters from an Uncivil War).
- **Theme first.** He names what a story is about before he plots it. "Story about power." (Life After Earth, 2012)

A thread through all of it: a mind trapped in, cut off from, or hijacked out of its body. It runs
through his fiction from 2014 to 2020 and becomes The Mechanics of Being.

## 4. His visual taste

Seen in his drawings, diagrams, decks and art-direction briefs. **His oil paintings have not been
seen yet** (the Drive reader cannot open video or Photoshop files), so treat painting taste as unknown.

- **Light on dark.** Glowing filaments, waves and auras on near-black. (Awareness, Tula Banner, Hidden Mental Tech)
- **Warm against cool carries meaning.** Orange, red and gold for charge and stress; teal and deep blue for calm and awareness. (Stress, Awareness, A fragmented Mind)
- **The hand comes first.** Ink and marker, confident contour, hatching, caps annotations. "The drawing came first and the framework came after it." (book proposal) AI images are for ideation; the canon is hand-made: "There are forty-nine of them, and I drew every one."
- **A symbol system of simple geometry.** Circle, ring, dot, triangle, segmented wheel. His written rule: "Simple geometry: circles, triangles, squares" and "No sacred geometry or organic forms" (TULA brand deck).
- **The eye** recurs as an emblem (Black Mage, The Watchers Over Perseverance).
- **One image shows the concept,** built as forces pulling against each other: "3 forces pulling against each other" (ROAM Beauty Corner).
- **Stylized over photoreal; suggestion over reveal.** "Some expressions of the creature do not need to be explicit, Jaws" (ROAM). Nature reclaiming structure.
- **Do not over-lock a style.** "Intention is not to go to 100% and paint ourselves into a corner. We want wiggle room to innovate" (ROAM direction).
- **A premium base with one accent; mystery over volume.** "occasionally adorned with Swarovski crystals" and an "underground/mysterious feeling" (Adam Smith, 2006, tentative).
- Range: quiet heraldry (the crown banner) to loud mysticism (the fire eye). Both are his.

## 5. Do not imitate

- **The AI-drafted TULA marketing copy, 2023 to 2024.** Taglines like "Unleash Your Inner Ninja",
  "Stat Alert" blogs, generic LinkedIn topics, a leftover chatbot sign-off. It is the opposite of his terse register.
- **The 2022 coaching voice** that capitalized abstract nouns ("become Aware of Your Authentic Nature"). He has moved past it.
- **Typos and speed slips** in drafts. Copy the voice, not the typos.
- **The fiction's crude register** in anything public or product-facing.
- **Unsourced numbers.** Old decks contain projections ("approximately $500,000 saved annually").
  Under his rule, every number shown is real; flag these, never reuse them.
