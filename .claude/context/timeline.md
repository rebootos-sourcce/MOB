## What the timeline shows

**Terminology moved from de- to re-.** 2022 is *Deprogramming the Mind*; 2025
is *The Little Book of Reprogramming*. The negative framing became positive.

**Self-worth flipped sides.** Renounced as ego fuel in Feb 2022; built as the
goal by Jul 2025. This is the single largest unreconciled change in the corpus.

**The private became the published.** The Feb 2022 vow list (*"Honor my word.
Mind my intentions. Speak truth."*) is recognisably the CQ trait table of
Jul 2025 (Honor, Intention, Truth).

**Nov 2023 is the hinge.** Everything before it is notebook fragments;
everything after it is structured product. The dictated thread is where the
scattered observations became a system with a name.

**The eight-chakra doctrine predates the seven-chakra products by two years**
and was never formally revised — the products simply shipped with seven.
