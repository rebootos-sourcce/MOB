## Contested terms — resolve before writing new material

**How many chakras?**
- Nov 2023 doctrine: **eight**, with Will (thyroid) distinct from Throat, plus 108 mid-sized
- Little Books and children's book: **seven**, Will collapsed into Throat

**Is it four features or four tools?**
- Doctrine, features of awareness: attention, **intention, perception, presence**
- `Modes of Vital Energy.jpg`, tools to move energy: attention, **intention, intuition, imagination**

**Self-worth: fuel or cure?**
- Feb 2022: ego fuel, to be renounced
- Jul 2025: therapeutic goal, three decks devoted to it

**De- or re-programming?**
- 2022: *Deprogramming the Mind*
- 2025: *The Little Book of Reprogramming*
- Both appear; "reprogramming" is current.

**Sacral polarity**
- Little Book spec table: Shame / **Pleasure**
- Back matter of all six books: Shame into **Joy**
- Wonder pair 1 is Shame/Pleasure; pair 9 is Apathy/Joy
