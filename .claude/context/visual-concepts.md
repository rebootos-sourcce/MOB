## Body UI — the somatic macro system

From `Edited/Body UI.jpg`. Not present in any document.

> *THE BODY COMES WITH A SOMATIC UI SYSTEM THAT FUNCTIONS LIKE MACROS THAT RUN
> WHEN ENVISIONED.*

The claim: imagination envisions an image; sensations (vibration, emotion,
"modes of being") are *linked* to that image; envisioning the image replays
whatever was linked to it. Icons can be chained to other UI elements.

> *The color of the icon envisioned determines initial intensity.*

Two worked macros are written out:

**Golden Sun** — envision 20 seconds, 5 breaths. Linked actions:
1. Words: *I am my lightest-light, I am my brightest-bright*
2. Vibrate solar plexus chakra
3. Spin / radiate gold energy

**Clear Sight** — linked action:
1. Words: *Open Third Eye* (noted as stimulating the corpus callosum)

Also labelled on the page: "somatic block anchors."

This is the most product-shaped idea in the corpus — a user interface metaphor
for somatic practice, with named, reusable, composable macros.

## The blocked-chakra cascade

From `Book Session 6_15_2023/Will-Power and Intention.jpg`.

Each block does not merely impair its own function — it **disables the one
above it**. This is the mechanism behind the "Tula law" asserted in SoulCraft
(*"If the root is unstable, all coherence above collapses. No anchor, no
ascension."*), and it is written out here in full:

| Blocked | Diminishes | Which makes impossible |
|---|---|---|
| Root | safety / scarcity / security | being creative |
| Sacral | pleasure, creativity, desires | expressing desires |
| Solar | personal power — self-value, self-respect, self-confidence, self-worth | loving self & others |
| Heart | love of self & others | expressing the self |
| Throat | willpower; "no voice to contribute or make change" | perceiving perspectives |
| Third Eye | shift in perspective; "duality appears real" | opening to reality |
| Crown | access to reality | spiritual growth |

Supporting statements on the same page:

> *Sub-conscious + unconscious limiting beliefs create energetic blocks in the
> body at the site of the chakras.*

> *A STORY creates energetic resistances that limit the self… blocking action.
> Remove the resistances, action becomes clear. More resistances, more
> willpower is required.*

> *Identifying with willpower is required to move through the resistances.*

That last line is the definition of will in this system: willpower is not a
virtue, it is the tax you pay in proportion to unremoved resistance.

## Charge of Words — the three-step release protocol

From `Book Session 6_15_2023/Charge of Words.jpg`. The most concrete
procedure in the corpus.

1. **Identify influence** — the object & experience, the story label, the
   sensation; thinking / believing / feeling
2. **Defuse intensity** — "by letting go of modifiers, connecting to the
   energy of the experience"
3. **Inject the truth** — labelled *(Reprogram)*

The page also draws the **intensity ladder** — the doctrine's abstract
"intensity curve" made concrete as an ordered vocabulary:

> bothered → annoyed → deflated → disappointed → sad → ashamed → scared →
> guilty → defeated → heavy → suicidal

This is the missing operational piece. The doctrine says *"stress is simply an
intensity curve with limiting beliefs attached to points on the curve… a little
annoyed is less intense than paralyzed with fear."* Here are the points.

## Modes / Aspects of Vital Energy

From `Edited/Modes of Vital Energy.png`. Four tools "to move energy, change
modes, and change dimensional sandboxes":

**Attention · Intuition · Intention · Imagination**

**Note the conflict.** The doctrine names four *features of awareness* —
attention, intention, **perception, presence**. This page names four *tools* —
attention, intention, **intuition, imagination**. Two overlap, two differ. It
is not clear whether these are meant to be the same quartet.

Techniques described on the page:
- **X-ray** — pinpoint pain; "acts like a beacon when combined with attention,
  intuition & the heartbeat; has an area of effect"
- Finding blocked chakras; a marginal note reads *"attention & intuition seem
  interchangeable, research says they are not"*
- *"Use attention, intuition, knowledge of anatomy, imagination to release
  energy stalled in tight muscles — will your way to a limber body"*

## The field notes — a third voice register

Two images are not teaching material. They are **experimental logs**, and they
read in a completely different register from everything else in the corpus:
measured, hedged, quantified, and seeking corroboration.

`Edited/Energetic Poles.jpg`:

> *When energies become sensitive, wave palm over the crown of the head about
> 6" — a cone of energy can be experienced. Appears to be 6" in diameter, core
> is strongest 2" in diameter. When the chakras become unblocked, the same
> energy can be felt at the throat, heart, navel, taint. It appears to run
> through the skull, through the body, out between the legs, and projects
> forward. As much as I can tell, it goes beyond my reach.*
> ***CONFIRMED — Keely & Kerey experience it too.***

`Edited/Energy Healing.png`:

> *Right palm, gross energy. Left palm, finer subtle energies… Intense energy
> from palms to upper forearm pulsates to a rolling rhythm — no pain. Can
> intuitively amplify or reduce intensity through intention. Single lines of
> energy… they meet hubs — maybe a small nerve plexus? Pursed fingers to thumb:
> beam up to 70 feet (so far). Palm open: fan, less intense, runs two-way.*

Note the habits: numbers with units, a hedge (*"as much as I can tell"*,
*"so far"*), a self-correction (*"research says they are not"*), and
independent confirmation by name. Nothing in the published books shows this
register. If the bot is ever asked to write research or process notes rather
than teaching copy, **this** is the voice to match, not the affirmation voice.
