# Style patterns observed in the corpus

Descriptive, not prescriptive. Every pattern is backed by something countable or
quotable in `corpus/`. Counts come from the extracted texts.

## The corpus contains four distinct registers

They are not variations of one style — they differ in sentence length, person,
and punctuation. Identifying which register a piece belongs to is the first
question for any editorial task.

### 1. Teaching / affirmation — the Little Books, the card decks

The most formalised, and the only register with measurable rules.

- Card-deck fragments run **4–16 words, mean 8.6, median 9**. Never a complete
  sentence — always the completion of a fixed stem.
- The stems are fixed: `I am letting go of believing, perceiving, thinking,
  behaving, acting, and feeling that I am…` and `I am embracing the truth that I am…`
- Booklet fetters run as a single unbroken comma-chain with no full stop until
  the end.
- **Opening words are polarised.** Across the 2,400 Subject-format fragments,
  limiting ones open
  with *too / unable / undeserving / afraid / incapable / unworthy / stuck /
  ashamed / destined / scared*; empowered ones with *deserving / confident /
  trusting / worthy / free / capable / secure / proud / empowered / brave / open*.
- Each set closes on an inversion of its own opening:
  `fundamentally incapable of feeling safe, calm, or grounded` ↔
  `fundamentally safe, calm, and grounded in who I am`
- **Impact cues are body-part + verb, and the truth inverts the fetter literally.**
  > `Jaw tight, brow furrows, breath shallow, heart restless.`
  > `Jaw softens, brow smooths, breath deepens, chest opens.`

  **Count is four in five of the six books — but Will uses three.** Courage,
  Wonder, Power, Love and Insight run four cues throughout. Will runs three
  from pair 2 to pair 16 (`rigid shoulders, tight diaphragm, cold neck.`),
  with only its first pair at four. The spec permits 3–5, so Will is compliant
  with the spec and inconsistent with its five siblings. Match four unless
  deliberately writing in Will's register.

### 2. Narrative — the children's book

Short declaratives, fragments for weight, simile drawn almost exclusively from
body and landscape:
> *Her breath trembled like candlelight.*
> *Houses leaned into each other like old men too tired to stand.*
> *the sound of a rope fraying, fiber by fiber*

The Old Man does not lecture. He asks a question, states an observation, or
gives a two-word command — `Watch. Learn.` / `Enough.` / `Again.` He never runs
past about four sentences before a child interrupts.

Chapters close on a repeated formula: three dreams (boy, girl, Old Man), then an
aphorism attributed to the river. The aphorisms invert or chiasm rather than
summarise — *The heart that forgives cannot be conquered.* ·
*To speak is to breathe. To silence is to die. Choose breath.* ·
*Meaning is not given. It is remembered.*

### 3. Direct address / protocol — the cleanse, the practices

Second person, imperative, checkmarked lists. Hard requirements reframed as
desirable with an em-dash:
> *This isn't punishment — it's purification.*
> *If it's fake, processed, engineered, addictive, or requires justification —
> it's probably out.*

Instructions numbered and verb-led. Practices structured on the four-step loop
**Discover → Play → Flow → Embody**.

### 4. Field notes — the research images

Quantified, hedged, seeking corroboration. This register appears in no published
document; it exists only in the photographed notebook pages.

Its habits: numbers with units; hedges (*"so far"*, *"as much as I can tell"*);
open questions (*"maybe a small nerve plexus?"*); explicit self-correction
(*"attention & intuition seem interchangeable, research says they are not"*);
and naming who independently confirmed an observation.

## Patterns holding across all four registers

**Emotion is located in the body and rendered as a physical verb.** Chest,
stomach, throat, jaw, shoulders, breath — never abstract. *It twists. Her chest
loosened. His fists unclenched.*

**The organising metaphor is computing, not mysticism.** Awareness described as
an operating system, beliefs as pattern programming, the body as having a UI,
identity as a keypress. The corpus's most distinctive habit, consistent from
2022 to 2026.

**Mechanism precedes remedy.** A pattern is named and explained before any
practice is offered. *Shame is a thief. It steals before you even try.*

**Concepts are defined against their opposite.** fear/courage, shame/pleasure,
wrath/compassion, silence/expression, leak/hold.

**"Leak" is the standing metaphor for incoherence** — not sin, not failure.
*Falsehood is a leak. Blame and excuses are leaks.*

## Source-integrity notes

- `Tula Text Test.txt` is 85% ChatGPT output. The author's own words are
  extracted to `corpus/tula-way-source-prompts.txt`. Conclusions drawn from the
  full transcript would describe the wrong writer.
- `Fiction & Non Fiction Nobel Prize Study.pdf` is reference research, not
  authored material.
- Two images in `Illustrations/Edited/` are private journal entries and personal
  vows. They are context for how the published frameworks developed; they are
  not product material.
