## The three pillars

Stated verbatim by Lance:

1. **Mind** — "how we are pattern programming our behavior and our experience,
   and by unraveling concepts and limiting beliefs regain control over our mind"
2. **Body** — "as we regulate the nervous system we create peace in the mind,
   creating equanimity in the body… as the body enters deeper states of peace
   through the removal of psycho energy it frees the flow of vital energy"
3. **Energy** — "access to the creative power of opening channels and deeper
   levels of awareness"

The pillars are sequential, not parallel: Mind unlocks Body, Body unlocks Energy.

## Core definitions (verbatim)

**Limiting belief**
> *psychoelectric energy in the form of a bias that blinds us to deeper more
> profound experiences*

**Stress**
> *simply an intensity curve with these limiting beliefs attached to points on
> the curve that describe its intensity. A little annoyed is less intense than
> paralyzed with fears*

**Letting go**
> *really letting go of the ignorance of a behavior, or ignorance of the way we
> have previously defined that stress… emptying the body of all stories is
> simply regulating the nervous system from previous labels of stress*

**Chakras**
> *chakras are simply split nerves, much like a hair split in, but these form a
> triangle in the body. The spinning wheel sensation of chakras is simply the
> energy flowing around the three points of a plexus*

This is the origin of the chakra-to-nerve-plexus mapping that appears in
`SoulCraft Development` (Root = Coccygeal plexus, FEAR = Lumbar Plexus, etc.).
It is a deliberately physiological reading, not a metaphysical one.

**Integrity**
> *absolute truth in every moment. Honesty in every situation. Love regardless
> of the challenge. It is keeping your word to yourself and to others… Our
> soul's primary ingredient for a successful experience in this lifetime is
> integrity.*

Lance's own aside, kept because it is the voice: *"Now if you're like me,
that's a tall order."*

## Awareness

> *The awareness is our ageless, timeless aspect of our experience. It is our
> operating system, our detector of the subtle shifts in energy inside and
> outside of our system.*

**Four fundamental features: attention, intention, perception, presence.**
Two of these — Intention and Presence — became CQ traits in SoulCraft.

**The ball-of-light model.** Awareness has "a core intensity and a fall off."
The falloff is the lower conscious state, in constant flux from *vrittis* —
sensory input waveforms carrying an emotion. This is how bias and memory form.

**Two modalities:**
- *Superconscious* — "the aspect of mind unimpeded by resistance"
- *Lower conscious / duality* — "a binary process of the mind that only
  perceives good or bad, triggered by our reward or stress system"

**The labeling mechanism** (the mechanism the whole practice targets):
> *if the sensation was previously experienced like anxiety, then we believe
> we're anxious. If a sensation arises and it has never been labeled we let it
> pass or ignore it until we label it.*

## The not-self ladder

A stepwise negation ending in identity with the field. We are not:
atoms (they decay and replace) → mind (ever-changing, contents modifiable) →
feelings (sensations interpreted through meaning) → prana (the binding
life-force) → a collection of experiences → awareness/soul itself.

> *removing all of these leads to a simple conclusion that our subjective self
> is the unified field creating the experience of reality*

## Perception is a rendering, not a window

- Senses act "like lidar"; electrical impulses "in essence freeze matter"
- Everything unseen is potential energy
- Nuclei are spaced apart — "we are more empty space than physical"
- **Objects have no intrinsic color.** What we see is "the frequency of the
  atom's vibration producing the illusion of the color by way of the eyes'
  receptors. This is the illusion."
- Reality is "in essence fictional because there is nothing physical since
  everything has a decay rate"

## The 0–10 experience scale

> *0 is no experience or never experienced or no knowledge, a 10 is mastery…
> a person with a 10 in a subject appears to speak over the heads of people
> with a one.*

This is the direct ancestor of the CQ 1–10 self-assessment scale.

**Why communication fails:** "we speak in headlines or stories… Because we
speak in headlines or short stories we never fully describe an experience…
We presume and assume to connect concepts and story in communication."

## Stress → disease mechanism

Blocked chakra → reduced energy flow to connected organs → reduced current to
the cells on those nerves → "the cells require a certain voltage to operate
normally, but stress, specifically a psycho energetic stress, inhibits this
flow" → cellular errors → disease.

Stress is identified with Jung's shadow and Freud's inner child:
> *a negative limiting belief is a sensation or bias describing stress*

## Ego formation

## CONFLICT: how many chakras?

Lance states in this source:

> *there are eight primary chakras that run from the spine to the crown and an
> additional 108 mid sized chakras within the system*

He then enumerates them, and **Will (thyroid) is separate from Throat**:

> *The thyroid is the home to your will… When both the will and the solar are
> open they form willpower… The throat is home to expression of self.*

**The Little Book series contradicts this.** It ships seven books and collapses
the two: `Will – Throat Chakra (Silence into Expression)`. The children's book
also uses seven arcs, with Throat covering voice/truth.

So the doctrine says eight; both finished products say seven. This is unresolved
and worth deciding before the Crown/Source book is written.

## Energy / channels (third pillar)

Channels = Vedic *nadis* = fascia. "Both describe channels that move liquid and
energy through the body just below the surface of the skin originating at the
belly button."

> *There are mundane expected channels like our emotions — you can look at
> anger, or sadness, or being upset as a channel — it's a vibration that's been
> labeled, that's the channel.*

Channeling is defined non-mystically as becoming aware of a channel's
properties. "Everyone has a special skill that they know is unique to them;
they've learned to tap into their unique channel."

Prana, chi and kundalini are declared interchangeable terms.
