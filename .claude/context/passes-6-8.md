## Pass 7 — the vector files

**Negative result, recorded so nobody repeats it.** The `.ai` files carry no
extractable text.

Two files tested, from different years and different Illustrator versions:

| File | Authored | Version | Text operators |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heart Brain Toggle.ai | 12 Oct 2022 | 26.0 | `Tj` 0 · `Tx` 0 · `TJ` 0 |
| Data Visualization.ai | 25 Sep 2023 | 27.8 | `Tj` 0 · `Tx` 0 · `TJ` 0 |

Both are EPS-format PostScript containing only vector paths and procedure
definitions. This matches the SVGs, which have zero `<text>` elements. Three
independent confirmations: **the vector artwork is outlined or was never typed.
Labels live only in the photographs.**

Two things did come out of it:

**The art is built on placed stock assets.** Both files embed a purchased EPS
(`1-04.eps`, `wave4.eps`), consistent with the `AdobeStock_*.ai` files sitting
in the same folder.

**Drive timestamps are not authoring dates.** `Heart Brain Toggle.ai` carries an
embedded creation date of **12 Oct 2022** but a Drive modified time of
**19 Dec 2023** — a fourteen-month gap. The timeline in `timeline.md` was built
from Drive metadata and is therefore a record of *when files were touched*, not
when work was done. Treat its dates as upper bounds.

### The raw vocabulary is blunter than the published vocabulary

`Limiting Belies.jpg` (the JPEG twin of the one `.psd`) is a scatter of
self-directed labels:

> POOR · Fat · Dumb · EVIL · Slow · Guilt · SPINELESS · CHEATER · CRAZY ·
> GRIEF · DARK · FUCKER · ASSHOLE

The published decks render this same material as *unworthy*, *inadequate*,
*not enough*. The source is profane and specific; the product is clinical and
general. That gap is an editorial decision worth making consciously — the raw
register has a force the sanitised one does not, and at least some of it may
belong in the finished work.

## Pass 8 — contradiction sweep

### The four-cue rule is not universal. I was wrong about this.

I stated in `style.md` and `body-ui-spec.md` that impact cues run *"exactly
four, no exceptions"* and called it *"the one rule the corpus never breaks."*

**Will breaks it.** From pair 2 through pair 16 it uses **three** cues:

> `rigid shoulders, tight diaphragm, cold neck.`
> `fluttering breath, tight abdomen, cool hands.`
> `dry tongue, shallow chest, tight brow.`

Only its first pair runs four. The other five books — Courage, Wonder, Power,
Love, Insight — run four throughout.

The spec permits 3–5, so **Will is compliant with the spec and inconsistent
with its five siblings.** Both files are now corrected.

Why the error happened: I read all six books in one sitting, formed the
four-cue pattern early from Courage and Wonder, and then read Will's threes
without registering them as a break — the shape was right, so the count went
unchecked. A pattern confirmed early is the one you stop counting.
