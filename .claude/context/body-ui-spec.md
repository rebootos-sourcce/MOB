# Body UI — design spec

A design for the Body UI concept as an audio track. The macro definitions are
medium-agnostic; the track structure is one delivery of them.

Everything under **Source** is transcribed from `Illustrations/Edited/Body UI.jpg`.
Everything under **Design** is proposed and marked as such.

---

## Source

> *THE BODY COMES WITH A SOMATIC UI SYSTEM THAT FUNCTIONS LIKE MACROS THAT RUN
> WHEN ENVISIONED.*

- Imagination envisions an image — an **icon**
- Sensations (vibration, emotion, "modes of being") are **linked** to that icon
- Envisioning the icon **replays** whatever was linked to it
- *"The color of the icon envisioned determines initial intensity"*
- Icons *"can be linked to other UI"*
- Also on the page: **somatic block anchors**

Two macros are already written out:

**Golden Sun** — envision 20 seconds, 5 breaths. Linked actions:
1. Words: *I am my lightest-light, I am my brightest-bright*
2. Vibrate solar plexus chakra
3. Spin / radiate gold energy

**Clear Sight** — linked action:
1. Words: *Open Third Eye* (noted as stimulating the corpus callosum)

## Icon specification

A Body UI icon is a five-field object. This is the unit of the whole system.

```
ICON
  name          Golden Sun
  chakra        Solar Plexus
  colour        gold            → intensity
  envision      20s
  breaths       5
  words         "I am my lightest-light, I am my brightest-bright"
  actions       [vibrate solar plexus, spin/radiate gold]
  release       the fetter this icon answers
  impact        four cues, body-part + verb
  links         [other icon names]
```

**`impact` is the exit condition, not decoration.** The run is complete when the
four cues are true in the body — the same logic as the closing directives the
Little Books specify and never shipped:

> *Turn the page only when the body softens and the breath flows again.*

## Colour → intensity

Source says colour sets initial intensity. **Design:** bind it to the existing
book palette, so an icon's colour already names its chakra *and* its dose.

| Colour | Chakra | Book | Intensity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Red | Root | Courage | highest — survival, use sparingly |
| Orange | Sacral | Wonder | high |
| Gold / Yellow | Solar Plexus | Power | medium-high |
| Green | Heart | Love | medium |
| Cyan | Throat | Will | medium-low |
| Indigo | Third Eye | Insight | low |
| Violet | Crown | *(unwritten)* | lowest — diffuse |

Intensity governs the audio: amplitude of the carrier, depth of the pulse, and
how insistent the voice is. Red is loud, slow, close. Violet is quiet, wide, far.

## Track structure

Three minutes. The timings for envision and breath count come from the source;
the rest is proposed.

| Time | Phase | What happens | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | **Discover** | The block is named aloud. One sentence. | Silence, then a single struck tone |
| 0:20–0:40 | **envision** | *20 seconds* — the icon is held in the mind. No instruction. | Carrier tone alone, swelling to the colour's intensity |
| 0:40–1:30 | **Play** | *5 breaths.* Emotion is located in the body. | Breath pacing at ~10s/cycle; carrier pulses with it |
| 1:30–2:30 | **Flow** | Linked actions fire in order, ~20s each | Words spoken on the exhale; action cues between |
| 2:30–3:00 | **Embody** | The four impact cues are spoken, one per phrase | Carrier decays; last cue lands in silence |

**Design notes**

- **Breath rate ~10s/cycle** puts five breaths at fifty seconds and sits near
  the coherence rate. It also makes the phase length fall out of the source's own
  "5 breaths" rather than being chosen arbitrarily.
- **Words land on the exhale.** Every release instruction in the corpus is an
  exhale instruction.
- **The four impact cues are the outro.** Nothing follows them. The track ends
  where the body confirms.
- **Carrier tone per chakra** — pitch should be set from whatever bowl set you
  actually use. I'm not inventing frequencies; the doctrine says bowls resonate
  at chakra-associated frequencies, so the real bowls decide. Parameter, not fact.
- **Binaural offset** — your SoulCraft Resources list already has "Binural
  Audio" as a slot. Proposal: a lower offset under Discover/Play where something
  is being released, a higher one under Embody where something is being set.
  Exact bands to be decided by testing, not asserted here.

## Worked example — Golden Sun

Solar Plexus. Gold. Answers the Power fetters: weakness, insecurity,
powerlessness, inferiority.

```
0:00  "Name the place you made yourself small today."
0:20  [ 20s — gold disc, held. no words. ]
0:40  [ 5 breaths. 10s each. ]
      "Where does it sit? Stomach. Chest. Jaw."
1:30  "I am my lightest-light."                    ← on exhale
1:50  "I am my brightest-bright."                  ← on exhale
2:10  [ vibrate solar plexus — hum, chest-led ]
2:30  "Chest lifts."
2:40  "Breath deepens."
2:50  "Core warms."
3:00  "Eyes steady."
      [ silence ]
```

The four closing cues are lifted from the Power booklet's own impact lines, so
the track and the book teach the same body.

## Worked example — Clear Sight

Third Eye. Indigo. Lowest intensity, longest silences.

```
0:00  "Name what you are certain about."
0:20  [ 20s — a clear indigo point. ]
0:40  [ 5 breaths. ]
1:30  "Open Third Eye."                            ← on exhale
1:50  [ silence — this icon has one action, not three ]
2:30  "Brow smooths."
2:40  "Eyes soften."
2:50  "Breath evens."
3:00  "Mind quiets."
```

Note the asymmetry: Golden Sun fires three actions, Clear Sight fires one.
**Icons are not required to be the same length.** Higher chakras should carry
fewer instructions and more silence — that is the gradient the whole system
argues for.

## Chaining

Source: *"Can be linked to other UI."*

**Design:** a chain runs bottom-up and stops at the first icon whose impact cues
don't land. This is the blocked-chakra cascade made into control flow —

> *If the root is unstable, all coherence above collapses. No anchor, no ascension.*

```
Steady Ground → Golden Sun → Warm Field
   ✓               ✓            ✗  stop here. run Warm Field alone tomorrow.
```

A chain that doesn't complete isn't a failure — it's a diagnosis. It tells you
which chakra is actually blocked, which is the thing the CQ assessment is trying
to find by self-report.

## What this needs from you

1. **Crown affirmation.** Blocks the seventh icon. Same blocker as the seventh book.
2. **Bowl frequencies.** Which set do you actually use? The carrier pitches come
   from real instruments, not from a table.
3. **Binaural offsets.** Needs testing rather than asserting.
4. **Somatic block anchors.** The phrase is on the source page with no
   definition. My reading is that an anchor is an icon bound to a *specific*
   stored block rather than a general state — a user-created icon rather than a
   library one. Unconfirmed.
5. **Does an icon need a visual?** The source says *envisioned*, which suggests
   the user builds the image. A supplied illustration might help onboarding and
   hurt ownership. Open question.
