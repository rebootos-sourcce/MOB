## 2. The rule that decides what may leave the product

The guard has two halves and `GUARD.md` says so. `refuse.js` reads the
**words**. `match.js` reads **who a line is served to**: it matches a line to
the charge and seat the person's own reading is heaviest at, and it refuses
every hook to `BUYERS.md` level 1, serving the engine's own direction for the
Collapsed band instead.

**Open social strips the second half off.** A post has no field reading behind
it, so it cannot be matched, and it has no band, so it cannot be refused to
anybody. Every line posted cold is served to everybody, including the 35 of
1000 the band gate exists to protect (106 on the 20 September arithmetic) and the 180 of 10,000 `PANEL-10k.md`
names S17, high load, never to be sold to by policy.

**And the word gate does not separate the lines.** Measured 26 September:
every line in `hooks.js`, all 27 of them including the doors, the roles, the
clear entry and the door out, plus 25 more from the funnel pages, `BRAND.md`,
`RESEARCH-icp.md` and `PANEL-10k.md`, run through `refuse.js` and through
`.claude/skills/atuned-voice/check.py --line`. **None refused, none with a hard
voice failure.** Every candidate is clean on its words. What decides is the
thing `refuse.js` names in its own `JUDGEMENT` list as a person's job: *a line
aimed at a charge the person is not carrying*.

**So the test for a line going out cold is one sentence.** It must be a line
that would be right read by anybody, including somebody at the floor: it names
no charge the reader has not been read for, it asks for no confrontation, and
it promises nothing the free result page does not show.

### Why the art travels and the copy does not

Every row in `hooks.js` carries its copy and its art on the same row, keyed to
one charge and one seat. The copy is second person and names the charge's
sensation: *Shame does not feel like shame. It feels like being reliable.* Read
by somebody carrying that key, it is recognition. Read by anybody else, it is a
claim about them the instrument never made.

The art names nothing. H01's figure is *the pelvic floor from below, one nerve
branch lit, the rest of the body drawn as a thin ring and left unlit*. H05's is
*the lumbar plexus alone, drawn as a nerve fan, seen from behind*. A reader who
carries nothing at that seat sees an anatomical plate, and `AESTHETIC.md`
section 9 already argues that *the more an anatomical figure looks like a plate
out of a reference text, the harder it works*. That is content that is the
product, and it can go anywhere.

## 4. What the funnel actually does today, read off the source

A distribution plan has to plug into something real. This is what is real.

**The pages.** `funnel/index.html`, the landing. `funnel/about.html`.
`funnel/buy.html`. `funnel/quiz.html`, the test. Built single file copies sit
in `funnel/dist/` as `atuned-funnel.html`, `atuned-quiz.html`,
`atuned-about.html` and `atuned-buy.html`.

**Every page says, in its own footer, that it makes no request of any kind.**
That sentence is the constraint on everything below. A tracking pixel, an
embedded video, a newsletter form script or a social widget on any of these
pages makes the footer false. `AESTHETIC.md` section 3 says the same thing of
marketing surfaces generally.

**The test.** A hundred questions, one per screen, five answers from never to
always, scored against the 21 laws of integrity and nine charges. Resumable in
the browser's own storage. Nothing leaves.

**The reading it lands on**, in order:

- the coherence figure, set at 68 to 86 pixels, with the band word beside it
- the band's definition, what it does to a day, and its direction out
- the five laws holding least
- the seven seat ring, the same drawing that fills in the header as a person
  answers, and a bar per seat with its mean load
- the heaviest single address, how many addresses are carrying, and how many
  are above the line where it starts to cost
- up to four saboteur cards
- the scope note: it read self report, and a clinician reads a person
- the door: save the record as `atuned-record.json`, copy it, or change an
  answer
- his close

**What it captures: nothing.** No email, no account, no server. What a person
carries away is a JSON profile the app's own loader accepts.

**There is no share control.** Searched: no share, no image export, no link.

**The referral exists as one sentence.** `funnel/buy.html`: *invite somebody
and you get 50 patterns when they join, up to four invitations a month*, which
is the fifty `DECISIONS.md` rules on. It cannot run. Checkout is not open, there
are no accounts, and `DECISIONS.md` holds open whether the community, and so
the invite, lives on Discord, on Beehiiv, or on both.

**And the funnel is not on a public address yet.** `DECISIONS.md`: Cloudflare
Pages deploys from the branch with one DNS record, and *his exact domain string
is still needed*. Nothing in this file can be linked to until that lands.

### Why a screenshot of the reading is not the share

The segment most likely to post a result, S7, *shares it instantly if the

largest type on the page, which is the coherence figure and the band word.

- **The band word.** `TIERDEF` still names the four low bands Incoherent,

- **The figure.** A number posted publicly is a rank against everybody else who

  `RESEARCH-ladder.md` records Instagram's own result: hiding a score after it

## 5. The share card. This is the loop.

**A fourth control in the door on the result page, generated in the browser
from the quiz's own ring.** The ring is `RING.svg` drawn off the same `LOADS`
array the bars print, so the card cannot disagree with the reading. Drawn to a
canvas and saved as an image, or handed to the phone's own share sheet. No
upload, no server, no request, and the footer stays true.

**It carries:**

- the seven seat ring, in the seat inks, muted as ruled
- **Carrying most: Sacral**, which is the string the quiz header already
  prints (`rlab`), reused word for word so there is one wording for one fact
- optionally, the figure from the `hooks.js` art column for that seat, which is
  the part of the row that travels
- the mark, Atüned, with Source OS under it in `--au`, the real gold
- the address a follower types, one path reserved for the card, section 7

**It never carries:** the coherence figure, the band word, a saboteur name, an
address name, a law value. The address names are the engine's and `hooks.js`
H03 states the rule for them: *the name of the address is available to them
and is not put in front of them as a label*. A card is a label in front of
everybody they know.

**The caption is pre-filled, editable, and in the first person, because the
person is the one speaking.** `COPY-questions.md` question 16: the only first
person in the product is the line the person speaks. Run through both gates, 26
September, and it passes:

    Seven seats. Mine carries most at the sacral. Where does yours sit?

**It is not offered at the two lowest bands.** `COPY-questions.md` question 3,
the default: at the two lowest readings the surface shows the reading, one line
saying the instrument stops here, and nothing else, *no offer, no tier, no
streak, no next step*. A prompt to broadcast is a next step. `GUARD.md` refuses
every hook to level 1 on the same reasoning. Not offered on an unread profile
either.

**No reward for posting it.** The fifty patterns land when somebody joins, and
only once accounts exist. A reward for the post itself turns every card into an
advertisement its reader cannot see was paid for, which is the same defect the
`testimonial` rule refuses from the other side.

**Why this closes the loop and the hooks do not.** A follower sees a ring with a
shape and a seat, and knows that loads have a shape and sit somewhere, and does
not know where theirs does. `MAP.md` mechanism 3: curiosity peaks when a person
holds a little knowledge, and a card is exactly a little. They type the address,
take the test, and get their own ring. The line on the card was produced by the
sharer's own field, so it is the one piece of outward material that is
correctly addressed by construction, and it shows no verdict.
`RESEARCH-icp.md`: five of the seven original arrivals named a person rather
than an ad. `PANEL-10k.md`: 4,970 of 10,000 enter through somebody else's
screenshot. **The card is the channel. Everything in section 6 feeds it.**

## 7. What comes back, and what it changes

A feedback loop is only a loop if something comes back and changes the
material. Here is what can come back without breaking the promise every page

|---|---|---|
| Arrivals per channel | One path per channel on the landing, and one for the card. Counted from the host's own server side request counts. No script on the page | Which door line and which address gets the next production slot. Compare paths against each other, never against a target typed into this file |

## 8. The arithmetic, and why paid waits

**What is not there.** No checkout: `funnel/buy.html` says so in one line. No
accounts. Three of four prices unruled: 12, 29 and 59 are the recommendation,
99 is his. No click data, no conversion data, no churn data. Every panel figure
in this repository is simulated.

**So this is what would have to be true, not a forecast.** Lifetime value is
the monthly price over the monthly churn, with a margin near one because the
product is a file. Cost per paying person is the cost of a landing visit over
the rate at which a visit becomes a payer. For a three to one ratio at tier
one's recommended 12:

| If monthly churn were | Lifetime value | Cost per payer must be under | At 1 per visit, visit to paid must be at least | At 2 per visit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 percent | 240 | 80 | 1.3 percent | 2.5 percent |
| 10 percent | 120 | 40 | 2.5 percent | 5 percent |
| 15 percent | 80 | 27 | 3.8 percent | 7.5 percent |

**The churn and the cost per visit are placeholders**, chosen to show the shape,
and neither is a measurement. What the table says is that cold paid traffic has
to convert at a few percent into a subscription, with a hundred questions
between the click and the first price. `RESEARCH-icp.md` simulated 310 of 1000
ad viewers starting the test and 186 finishing, before any ask at all.

**That number is bad, and it should be said.** Paid social does not pencil on
this funnel until four things are true: checkout exists, the low band words are
mechanical so a paid visitor is not handed a moral word by 84 percent odds, the
cost line under the score exists so the ad's lead line is paid on the page it
lands on, and an organic visit to paid rate exists to compare a paid one
against. Until then the card and the newsletter are the acquisition, and they
cost production time rather than money.

   at the floor reads says two opposite things. Keep it everywhere except the

   of readers lose your line. Keep it on the landing only: every result page
