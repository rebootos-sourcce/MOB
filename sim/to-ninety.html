Chasing ninety

The ninety day run graded 38.91. The instruction was to keep simulating until it reaches ninety. Seventeen changes were modelled, each one alone and then cumulatively, to the same stopping rule the baseline used. It reached 78.66, and this page is mostly about why that is not ninety.

78.66of 100, B

From 38.91 with every traced change applied. +39.75 points over 16 steps. The formula’s own maximum is 96, so ninety was arithmetically available and it was not reached: the distance left is 11.3 points and the page names what each of them costs.

The rule, and it is the whole exercise. The grade only moves if the product would move. Nothing on this page gains a point by lowering a friction cost, raising an opening rate, reweighting a criterion or grading a different thing. Every step is one named product change, with the file it lands in and roughly what it costs to build, and the parameter it turns is the consequence of that change rather than the change itself. Where a consequence could be measured it was: the fold counts come off a Chromium probe of the shipped build, the sniffer’s read rate comes off the shipped parseStory with the patch applied in memory, and the release arithmetic is driven through the shipped engine. One step breaks the rule on purpose, and it is marked, separated and reported twice.

Where it flattens, and what is left

The curve flattens at C8b, the step called “The top collapses to two”, which reaches 78.49. Every one of the seven traced steps after it adds less than a point, and all of them together add +0.17.

And the curve peaks before it ends. The highest traced step is C2b at 80.18, and the one step after it take -1.52 off again: C2a -1.52. Those are the release changes. They cost points on the scoreboard and they are the changes the product most needs, which is the same sentence as the ceiling section below and is the reason the build order at the end of this page is not the ranking at the top of it.

The shipped arithmetic moves the reading +0.54 across a quarter of daily work. The three changes together move it +1.11, and the band after ninety days is “Corrupt” either way.

And that is the finding of this pass, because it is not the answer the backlog expected. The three fixes named as the cure for the flat loop were the removal compounding, the install reaching the far pole, and a cleared address staying cleared. All three are modelled here and all three are applied together, on a person who does everything right for ninety consecutive days with no model in the way. The reading moves +1.11 instead of +0.54. Addresses holding the opposite stay at 4.0. The band does not change. +1.11 over a quarter of perfect daily practice is still standing still, so the three changes are necessary and are nowhere near sufficient, and the ceiling is deeper than the fix the backlog names.

Where it is deeper is visible in the same table. Patterns of ground fall from 444 to 80 as the release actually removes charge, so the fixes work: there is less load. The reading barely notices, which means the reading is not mostly built out of the load a release can reach. The height of the line is the intake, and the intake is answered once, before day one. Until a ruling exists on whether somatic work moves the stated coherence at all, no arithmetic inside the release will move it, and this is the owner’s call rather than a design decision: the quantity is shared with the book.

And the grade barely notices, which is the most important sentence on this page. C2a, C2b and C2c moved the total by -0.12, -0.06 and +0.00 applied alone. Not because the change is small but because almost nobody in the cohort reaches a release at all: 121.9 releases across 1000 arrivals in ninety days. An arithmetic fix that only pays off after somebody stays cannot show up in a grade whose retention has already collapsed, and a grade that cannot see it is not a reason to leave it broken. It is the reason the release goes first in the build order and last in the scoreboard.

What the remaining distance costs

Point by point, and which of them are arithmetic rather than interface.

  • Core loop, 6.2 points. Arithmetic. Full marks need everybody to close story, release and a kept practice, and to close it twelve times. At 121.9 releases a thousand arrivals in the baseline, this is the retention problem stated as a product number.
  • ICP alignment, 4.4 points. Arithmetic, and it is retention wearing a different name. The term is built out of each figure’s own day seven, day thirty, loop share and read share, so it cannot rise until those do. Buying it costs whatever the loop costs.
  • Behavioral flow, 2.9 points. Interface, and it has a floor the interface cannot cross. The chrome alone is 16 controls above the fold at 1600 before any content, against a target of twelve. Folding the rails and collapsing the top and the bar is most of what can be done, and the residue is the stage: Knowledge carries 56 controls in its own centre column.
  • Retention, 2.0 points. Arithmetic, and the scale is the cap. Ten needs 35 per cent of arrivals active in the seven days ending at day thirty. The category median the scale is anchored on is 3.3 per cent. No interface reaches 35, and a product that did would not need a grade.
  • Monetization, 1.5 points. Half interface, half cohort. The price and the enforcement are days of work. The rest of the term is the share of people who reach the end of the gift, and in the baseline that is 0 of a thousand, so it cannot be bought with a screen.
  • First touch, 0.2 points. Interface, and it is the cheapest gap on the table.
  • Emotional, 0.1 points. Half interface, half language. The read share moves with the lexicon and the measurement is real. The other half of the term is the inferred share, and stating more addresses is not the same as guessing less often.

Adding it up, and the arithmetic is the argument. Ninety is 11.3 points away. The formula has 17.3 points of headroom left in total, so the distance exists. But 12.6 of that 17.3 sits in three criteria that are all one quantity, whether anybody stays: ICP alignment, Core loop and Retention. Everything else on the table put together is worth 4.7. So ninety cannot be reached without moving retention, and 11.3 of the 12.6 retention shaped points would have to be bought.

Retention is the one that will not be bought. Ten of ten needs 35 per cent of arrivals active in the seven days ending at day thirty, against a category median of 3.3 per cent that the scale is anchored on. Every change on this page together took day thirty from 3.86% to 20.30%. Reaching the top of that scale is not a product this grade would be measuring, and the two criteria with no headroom at all, Visual at 8.0 and Referral at 8.0, are already at their formula maximum and cannot contribute another point.

The build order this implies, which is not the ranking

The ranking above is by what moved the grade. The order below is by what moves the product, and they are different lists on purpose.

  1. C2a. The removal compounds inside the run. The release takes 21 per cent of the weight once, whatever the length of the run, so a twelve line run and a three line run remove the same amount. Each line of the run takes from what is left instead. Grade alone -0.12. Lands in atuned_src/ui/release.js, and the contract with SOURCE. Days in the code and a ruling before it. This is arithmetic the book shares, so it is the owner’s call and not a design decision.
  2. C2b. The install reaches the far pole. The release installs 62 per cent of what it took, so the address is left holding less of the fetter and not the opposite state. The install is placed at the pole instead. Grade alone -0.06. Lands in atuned_src/ui/release.js. Hours in the code, and the same ruling as C2a.
  3. C2c. A cleared address stays cleared. A story may push an address that was cleared straight back over the line, so the same charge is released repeatedly and nothing accumulates. The level a release left an address at becomes a floor. Grade alone +0.00. Lands in atuned_src/engine/compute.js and the charge model. A week. It is a new piece of state on the profile, so it is schema, and schema is the cross compatibility contract.
  4. C3. The release reads carrying rather than loaded. An address enters the reading at four of ten. Below that a person carrying load at a hundred addresses is told nothing is carrying and the release has nothing to offer. The engine already returns carrying for exactly this reason and the action surfaces still read loaded. Grade alone +5.07. Lands in atuned_src/ui/release.js, atuned_src/ui/summary.js. Hours.   5. C1. The narrow door. A one line box on the surface a person already landed on, committing on enter, opening the same engine call the Story page opens. How this was run

The harness is the baseline’s harness with knobs added, and every knob is off by default. Whether the defaults still reproduce the baseline is checked rather than claimed: this pass settled at 38.91 over 19 runs and the earlier report settled at 38.91 over 19 runs, which is the same total and the same run count, so the knobs added nothing when they were all off. A knob may remove a friction only by removing what causes it, and may change arithmetic only where the product’s arithmetic would change. The one knob that is a bare return rate is C6n, it is 12 per cent of otherwise skipped days, nothing in this repository measures it, and the curve is drawn so the reader can cut it off.
