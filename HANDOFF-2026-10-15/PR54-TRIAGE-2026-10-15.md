  a build that passes `build.py --check` byte for byte, canon drift 0, canon

**A merge time consequence the owner must know:** PR 54 changes wording
inside five canon tables, so the regenerated `0001_canon.sql` differs from
the one production's D1 was seeded with. Wrangler will not re-run an
applied migration, so after a merge the live canon rows would differ from
the build's until they are re-seeded on purpose. Not a reason to merge or
not; a step that belongs in the merge plan.
