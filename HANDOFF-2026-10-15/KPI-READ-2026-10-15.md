## What the numbers say, plainly

- **The first rung is not people.** 6,867 opens and 14 choices of ground
  means the opens are mostly machines: crawlers, previews, and automated
  checks that open the page. The server makes a session on every first
  open, before anyone has done anything. That rung cannot be used as a
  people count until the app records which door opened it (the "quiz door"
  field on the KPI gap list). That is the next KPI build.
- **The human rungs are single digits**, and some of those are the team's
  own walks through the product while testing. There is no marketing yet,
  so this is expected. The ladder works: a person who chose a ground had a
  roughly 40 percent chance of finishing the tutorial and releasing (6 of
  14), and 4 of 6 came back to say what changed.
- **Paid and cancel read zero** because the four Stripe tiers do not exist
  yet and checkout is closed by a switch. Those two rungs turn on the day
  the tiers exist.
- **Day 1, 7 and 30 return are not counted, on purpose.** The privacy page
  promises nothing about how a person uses the app is reported. The alpha
  retention number is the second month's renewal, which Stripe reports.
