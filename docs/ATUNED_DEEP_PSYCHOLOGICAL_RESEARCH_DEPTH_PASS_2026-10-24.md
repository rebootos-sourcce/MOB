## Core cross-record findings

- **Clinical terminology:** project records that historically say “Bipolar,” “BPD,” “OCPD,” “Histrionic,” “Anxiety Disorders,” “Depression,” or “ADHD” preserve those source notes but are not equivalent to diagnoses. Research on PID-5 traits supports dimensional, overlapping constructs, not one-to-one diagnosis from a custom label.
- **Dark-trait constructs:** narcissism, Machiavellianism, psychopathy, and the Dark Factor have overlapping but non-identical literatures. A shared factor does not prove all traits are interchangeable or present in one person.
- **Schema and emotion regulation:** self-sacrifice, abandonment, defectiveness/shame, unrelenting standards, avoidance, rumination, and flexibility are researched constructs. The project’s compound labels remain provisional.
- **Archetype theory:** scholarly literature explicitly debates universal/innate versus emergent/cultural accounts. MIRROR and BLUEPRINT figures are preserved as project-authored symbols; no tradition-wide meaning is asserted.
- **Dante/CIRCLES:** scholarly sources establish the Inferno’s literary structure and interpretive context. The project’s psychological patterns are modern adaptations, not Dante’s diagnostic taxonomy.
- **CASCADE:** state metaphors are kept state-based. Avoid applying dehumanizing labels as fixed identities.
