# THE CASCADE GRAMMAR
**Extracted from Lance's nine ANXIETY dictations · 2026-09-03**

Not inferred from theory. Measured from what he actually said, nine times.

---

## THE SPINE

Every cascade runs the same six movements. Some pairs merge two, none skips.

**1 · THE NAMING.** One to four short clauses naming the charge flat.
> *that I'm afraid* · *that I'm panicking* · *that I need to escape* · *that I have no control over myself*

**2 · THE BASE RUNGS, carrying the charge as their object.** Tense, stressed, bothered, irritated, annoyed. Never standing alone. Always attached.
> *tense, stressed, worried, bothered that I can't control my actions*

**3 · THE CLIMB.** Frustrated, angry, pissed off, terrified, petrified, frozen, paralyzed. Rises with the pair, does not always reach the top.

**4 · THE SHAME GATE.** Embarrassed, humiliated, ashamed, guilty. **Always in that order. Always four.** This is the most consistent thing in the corpus.

**5 · THE COLLAPSE.** Defeated, deflated, depleted, sad, depressed, sorrowful, remorseful.

**6 · THE CLOSE.** `I'm letting all that go.`

---

## THE FIVE ATTACHMENT FORMS

A rung never floats. It attaches by one of five, and Lance mixes them inside a single cascade.

| Form | Example | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| `with [charge]` | tense **with** anxiety | 9 uses, heaviest in ANXIETY |
| `that I'm [charge]` | worried **that I'm** anxious | the recursive layer |
| `that I was [charge]` | ashamed **that I was** anxious | the shame gate, past-facing |
| `from [charge]ing` | depleted **from** worrying so much | the collapse |
| `that I can't [X]` | frustrated **that I can't** control my actions | capacity charges |

**The recursive layer is `that I'm [charge]` attached to a rung.** Not a separate movement. It is how the rungs are hung.

---

## THE SHAME OBJECT IS DIFFERENT IN EVERY PAIR

The single most important finding. **Shame never attaches to the feared event.** It attaches to a different thing in each of the nine.

| Pair | Shame attaches to |
|---|---|
| Anxiety | having been anxious |
| Anticipation | the time lost waiting |
| Fear | being afraid |
| Vigilance | always needing to be in control |
| Worry | worrying for nothing |
| Panic | the attack itself |
| Compulsion | ruminating and repeating |
| Control | control being taken, and needing it |
| Escape | not standing tall |

**Finding the shame object is the hardest part of writing a pair and it cannot be generated.** It is the one place a composed cascade always sounds wrong.

---

## THE INSTALL

Three openings, all his:

- **`I know that I am [state].`** Steadiness, Ease, Surrender, Facing. The default.
- **`I'm not [charge], because...`** Calm, Courage. Negation first.
- **`Because...`** Presence, Trust. Runs straight on from the charge.

Then **repeated `I know`**, each clause negating a specific charge clause.

Then **`because`** and two to four capacities. Concrete, plural, present tense. *I sleep through the night. My jaw unclenches. My mind signs off.*

Two of the nine end on fragments and they are the strongest closes in the book.
> *Steady. Calm. At peace.* · *Standing bold. Standing tall. And moving through.*

| Install words | 45 to 92 | 74 |
| Clauses per charge | 12 to 26 | 18 |

**Install is roughly 60 percent of charge.** Every composed volume had them at 100 percent, which was wrong.

**Nine channels, not six.** Believing, perceiving, thinking, behaving, acting, feeling, speaking, saying, voicing.

**Ceiling 132.** The page cannot hold more.

---

## WHAT LANCE NEVER DOES

- No apprehensive, no fearful, no panic-stricken. He says scared, afraid, terrified, petrified, frozen, paralyzed.
- No named apex moment. No *in the hour before it lands*. He states the state.
- No `sick of`. That was mine.
- No arbitrary numerals.
- No floating rung. Every one attaches.
- No identical opening twice. All nine differ.

---

## THE REWRITE RULE FOR THE OTHER NINE VOLUMES

Everything above is applicable except the shame object, which must come from the corpus or from Lance.

**Derivable:** the six movements, the attachment forms, the ladder vocabulary, the install shape, the length ratio, the close.

**Not derivable:** the shame object, and the capacities after `because`.

For the nine composed volumes, the shame object gets pulled from the Let Go corpus where an entry names it. Where the corpus is silent, it is flagged rather than invented.
