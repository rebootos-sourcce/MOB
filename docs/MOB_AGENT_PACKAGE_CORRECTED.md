# III · SAMPLE CHAPTER

**Chapter One: The Four Levels of Awareness**

The familiar iceberg model says what is above the water is known and what is below is hidden. That model has two zones. The accurate model has four.

**Above the water, Known Knowns.** Everything visible to the conscious mind. What a person says they believe, what they know about themselves, their stated values. The tip of behavioral presentation. What people report when asked.

**At the very tip, beyond the clouds, Unknown Knowns.** The higher self. The access point to Source. It cannot be conceptualized into a known known. It has to be experienced. The fog is not ignorance, it is the absence of the lived encounter. Once experienced it crosses over and does not cross back. This is why it cannot be taught as information. It has to be reached through the practice.

**Just below the surface, Known Unknowns.** What Jung called the shadow. The limiting beliefs that inhibit the rise. A person may sense these exist and have some language for one or two. They cannot see them clearly because they are looking through them. **The lens is the thing you cannot see, because you are looking through it and not at it.** This is where the recognition work lives.

**In the depths, Unknown Unknowns.** Superego projections. Generational patterns installed before the person was born, running in a nervous system they inherited. Cultural and national projections. These shape behavior with no signature the person can identify. The behavior just seems like personality. Clearing at this depth requires sustained practice. The system reveals them as the upper layers clear. They cannot be reached by intent alone.

The ego is what we think we are. It attaches dualistic choices to the masks worn through the day. It is never authentic nature, which is being without the mask. **The masks are not the self. They are what the system reached for when survival required a face that was not the real face.**

# IV · WHAT I REMOVED

**The thousand-iteration wargame with the percentage bands.** There is no model behind it. The bands were written to look like output. If an agent asks how the pitch was tested, the honest answer is that it was not, and that is fine, because nobody asks.

**Nothing on biophotons.** I cut this and Lance corrected me. Ultraweak photon emission from biological tissue is measured science, documented since the 1920s and detected with photomultiplier tubes. A skeptic can look it up. What loses them is framing it as *the aura is a measurable biophoton field*, which reads as a metaphysical claim wearing a physics word. Stated as a fact it is an asset, and it is restored in the proposal.

**"Instant acquisition" language.** Nothing in a submission should tell an agent what they are about to feel.
