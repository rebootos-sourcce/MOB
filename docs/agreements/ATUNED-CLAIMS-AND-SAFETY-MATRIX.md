# ATÜNED Claims and Safety Matrix

**Status:** Draft, not approved for marketing or legal publication.  
**Purpose:** Match public product claims to evidence and prevent language that exceeds the current product.

| Claim area | Permitted wording baseline | Do not claim without additional evidence | Evidence and approval required |
|---|---|---|---|
| What ATÜNED does | “A self-report instrument that reflects back patterns in the words and answers you provide.” | That it reads the user's body, mind or energy as an objective fact | Current product spec, tested sample output, counsel/content review |
| Mirror / interpretation | “An interpretation you can accept, correct or reject.” | “The system knows the truth about you” or an unchallengeable diagnosis | Exact user correction path, data sources, fail/error behavior |
| Release | “A guided interaction intended to help you examine or release a pattern.” | A guaranteed release, a guaranteed relief response or a measured neurological effect | Evidence for what completion means; controlled outcome substantiation if efficacy is claimed |
| Body maps and sensations | “A user-reported sensation or an illustrative map associated with the user's input.” | Clinical measurement, diagnostic body scan, identification of disease/injury/deficiency | Exact measurement proof and regulatory/legal assessment |
| Reframe | “A user-defined alternative framing or intention.” | That positive language treats a condition or guarantees results | Content review; appropriate research before efficacy claim |
| Verification | “Your report of what you noticed after the interaction.” | Objective or independently verified change where only self-report exists | Method, time window, validation and source provenance |
| Practice history | “A log of practices you recorded.” | Durable behavior change, habit formation or therapeutic outcome from a practice count alone | Longitudinal, prespecified evidence and confounder/missingness analysis |
| Results-as-a-service | Use as internal positioning only until substantiation, scope and pricing claims are approved | Guaranteed psychological results, clinical efficacy, permanent change or measurable treatment response | Product owner + counsel review; substantiation dossier |
| “Releases X patterns” | A transparent count only when item identity, unique completed executions and meter authority are proven | Equating a button click/line display/repeated sentence with a unique pattern released | Catalog with stable pattern_id, immutable content hash, authoritative event/meter, duplicate and replay tests |
| Starter gift | Describe a 100-item gift as unavailable until approved, frozen, hash-verified set exists | “Your 100 patterns are ready” based only on a numeric grant or 150 candidate rows | Owner-approved exact 100 stable IDs, set/catalog version/hash, exact-head integration run |
| Privacy / local-only story | Claim only a precise path verified for that field and flow | Broad “your story never leaves your device” while sync path unresolved | Network trace, serializer/schema proof, storage inspection, notice alignment |
| Security / deletion | State exact completed action and known scope | “Permanently deleted,” “encrypted everywhere,” “never shared,” “fully private” or 90-day restore unless verified | Data map, vendor register, deletion receipts, backup config/expiry and tests |
| Practitioner / cohort access | Omit until exact feature and authorization rules are proven | Any implied default access, provider access or employer visibility | Field-level access matrix, opt-in, revocation, audit and cross-user negative tests |
| Clinical / crisis boundaries | State that product is not medical care and not a substitute for licensed care, subject to counsel wording | Promising crisis intervention or suggesting product replaces care | Counsel review, safety content review, contact/resource verification |

## Safety operations

- A user may reject or correct the Mirror without penalty.
- Avoid diagnosis, coercion, shame, or certainty about the cause of a user's distress.
- Distinguish the user's input from model interpretation and from any independently validated evidence.
- For crisis copy, verify every resource, geography and current contact before release; do not suggest the product monitors or responds to crisis content if no such function exists.
- Do not collect real sensitive stories for implementation testing until privacy notice, any required consent, vendor terms and incident response are ready.
- If the product or marketing team wants to add an efficacy claim, open a claim record with exact proposed text, target audience/jurisdiction, evidence, limitations, reviewer and approval date.
