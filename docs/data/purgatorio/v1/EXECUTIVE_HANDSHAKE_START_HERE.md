## FOUR RESEARCH LAYERS, FOR EACH OF THE 23 RECORDS

1. **Source and context:** terrace images / virtues in Dante; source URL and original literary boundaries.
2. **Behavioral movement:** observable action and the change in approach, avoidance, attachment, concealment, hold/release, etc.
3. **Belief and consequence:** user-recognizable protective belief, triggering condition, real-world loop, counter-belief and practice.
4. **Discriminants and oscillation:** alternative explanations, safe counterexamples, evidence that capacity is replacing distortion in lived situations.

These are **four analytical research layers** in the new records. They are not a claim of four independently controlled experiments or a separate clinical assessment. The owner's decision is that complete behavioral profiles and template alignment are enough to proceed with product design. Do not impose an external-certification gate.

## ARCHITECTURE: USE THE EXISTING COMPASS

Existing `CQ` → `medianRange(CQ)` → Purgatorio waist **only as spatial orientation**. Active terrace patterns are derived from **recorded observation and self-reported experience, not numerical CQ**. Purgatorio steps are not arbitrarily distributed into seven slices of 40–60; all seven can be live together, and their intensity varies independently. `readPurgatorio(registry, r.CQ, observedEvents)` returns `{waist,region,terraces,observedEvents,ignoredEvents}`; each terrace returns pole identifiers, last observed side, counts and actual swings. Oscillation is a directional reading of behavior, not a redundant score.

Follow the existing ATUNED chain when actual engine links are hooked up:

`Signal / Experience → Node/Nerve → Fetter → Saboteur → Complex → Hyper/Super-Complex → Archetypal Expression → Purgatorio vice/virtue oscillation → Practices / Observable actions → Next Compass reading`.

**Crucial:** Treat this as a *traversal / visualization of existing data*, not a new causal ladder from a vice into a person's nerves. Links to Inferno are behavioral similarity and reuse source IDs. Actual Nerve, Fetter, Saboteur and Super-Complex record IDs must be taken from the active canonical engine records during wiring and not manufactured.
