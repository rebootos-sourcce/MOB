# purgatorio:P01-C — Humility

**Record kind:** purgatorio_capacity | **Template:** TPL-PURGATORIO-SUPERCOMPLEX-COMPAT-V1 | **State:** OWNER_APPROVED_BEHAVIORAL_STANDUP

## Behavior profile

Humility — constructive pole
Behavior: Acknowledges what is true, credits others, asks for correction, admits limits, and holds equal regard without shrinking.
Movement: RECEIVE correction → RELEASE status defense → CONNECT as equals → EXPRESS responsibility.
Belief/reframe: My worth is not dependent on rank; I can acknowledge others and still stand firmly.
Trigger: Correction, another person receiving credit, losing face, public uncertainty, or comparison to an expert.
Real-world example: When a colleague points out an error, the person pauses, checks the fact, owns the error, and thanks them rather than attacking their standing.
Practice: Name a correction accurately, credit another person by name, and make one repair without explaining away the impact.
Differential: Confidence, leadership, disagreement, and healthy pride in real achievement are not this pattern; humiliation or self-erasure is not humility.
Success: In an actual status threat, receives feedback, credits another, corrects course, and can repeat this without rank restoration.

# purgatorio:P02-C — Charity / Kindness

**Record kind:** purgatorio_capacity | **Template:** TPL-PURGATORIO-SUPERCOMPLEX-COMPAT-V1 | **State:** OWNER_APPROVED_BEHAVIORAL_STANDUP

## Behavior profile

Charity / Kindness — constructive pole
Behavior: Recognizes another person’s good, offers genuine goodwill or help, and acts toward one’s own values without rivalry.
Movement: RECEIVE another’s good → EXPAND possibility → CONNECT → EXPRESS kindness.
Belief/reframe: Another person’s success does not cancel my possibility; I can contribute without losing myself.
Trigger: A peer succeeds, receives attention, is loved, or gains a resource I wanted.
Real-world example: A peer receives an opportunity. Instead of discrediting them, the person congratulates them and makes a constructive plan for their own work.
Practice: State one real thing you value in the other person, offer non-transactional support when appropriate, then take one step toward your own goal.
Differential: Recognizing injustice, seeking fairness, or wanting a similar opportunity is not envy; generosity does not require tolerating exploitation.
Success: Can witness a peer’s success without punishment, support their success where appropriate, and keep a clear personal path.
