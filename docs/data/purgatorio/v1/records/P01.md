# purgatorio:P01 — Terrace 1: Pride to Humility

**Record kind:** purgatorio_terrace | **Template:** TPL-PURGATORIO-SUPERCOMPLEX-COMPAT-V1 | **State:** OWNER_APPROVED_BEHAVIORAL_STANDUP

## Behavior profile

TERRACE 1: Pride ↔ Humility
DANTE: Purgatorio X–XII. The proud bend beneath stone, while carved examples of humility reverse their former upward self-display. Dante begins the seven-terrace purification here.
LIMITING BELIEF: I must be above others to be worthy, safe, or seen.
REFRAME: My worth is not dependent on rank; I can acknowledge others and still stand firmly.
TRIGGER: Correction, another person receiving credit, losing face, public uncertainty, or comparison to an expert.
VICE MOVEMENT: EXPAND self-image → CONCEAL fallibility → REPEL correction → HOLD position.
CONSTRUCTIVE MOVEMENT: RECEIVE correction → RELEASE status defense → CONNECT as equals → EXPRESS responsibility.
OBSERVABLE VICE: Claims special standing, redirects credit, dismisses criticism, hides mistakes, or insists on being right to preserve rank.
OBSERVABLE CAPACITY: Acknowledges what is true, credits others, asks for correction, admits limits, and holds equal regard without shrinking.
OSCILLATION: Status threat → defensive self-elevation → short relief → damaged mutual trust → more vigilance about rank.
INTEGRATION: Name a correction accurately, credit another person by name, and make one repair without explaining away the impact.
EVIDENCE OF CHANGE: In an actual status threat, receives feedback, credits another, corrects course, and can repeat this without rank restoration.
ALTERNATIVES: Confidence, leadership, disagreement, and healthy pride in real achievement are not this pattern; humiliation or self-erasure is not humility.
ENGINE: read within existing 40–60 Compass median; do not turn terrace into CQ arithmetic or a diagnosis.
