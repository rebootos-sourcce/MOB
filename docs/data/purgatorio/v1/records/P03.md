## Behavior profile

TERRACE 3: Wrath ↔ Meekness / Forbearance
DANTE: Purgatorio XV–XVII. The angry walk through blinding smoke and receive visions of measured responses, forgiveness, and peacemaking.
LIMITING BELIEF: If I do not attack or punish, I lose power and will be harmed.
REFRAME: I can protect a boundary and respond forcefully without retaliating.
TRIGGER: Perceived disrespect, threat, betrayal, obstruction, unfair treatment, or loss of control.
VICE MOVEMENT: CONTRACT threat focus → INITIATE attack → PENETRATE boundary → HOLD grievance.
CONSTRUCTIVE MOVEMENT: HOLD a pause → RECEIVE facts → EXPRESS firm boundary → RELEASE retaliation.
OBSERVABLE VICE: Escalates voice or force, assigns hostile intent before checking, attacks character, seeks revenge, or goes into resentful silence.
OBSERVABLE CAPACITY: Names the harm and boundary directly, maintains restraint, listens for reality, and chooses proportionate action.
OSCILLATION: Threat interpretation → surge or freeze → retaliatory move → short sense of control → worsened threat field.
INTEGRATION: Pause one breath before replying; describe what happened without motive claims; state a concrete boundary and next action.
EVIDENCE OF CHANGE: During real provocation, sets limits without humiliation, violence, or prolonged retaliation and repairs harm when needed.
ALTERNATIVES: Anger is not inherently wrong; self-protection, protest, accountability, and firm refusal can be coherent. Meekness is not passivity.
ENGINE: read within existing 40–60 Compass median; do not turn terrace into CQ arithmetic or a diagnosis.
