## Behavior profile

Meekness / Forbearance — constructive pole
Behavior: Names the harm and boundary directly, maintains restraint, listens for reality, and chooses proportionate action.
Movement: HOLD a pause → RECEIVE facts → EXPRESS firm boundary → RELEASE retaliation.
Belief/reframe: I can protect a boundary and respond forcefully without retaliating.
Trigger: Perceived disrespect, threat, betrayal, obstruction, unfair treatment, or loss of control.
Real-world example: After being interrupted, the person states that they want to finish, asks for time, and follows through without insults or threatening conduct.
Practice: Pause one breath before replying; describe what happened without motive claims; state a concrete boundary and next action.
Differential: Anger is not inherently wrong; self-protection, protest, accountability, and firm refusal can be coherent. Meekness is not passivity.
Success: During real provocation, sets limits without humiliation, violence, or prolonged retaliation and repairs harm when needed.
