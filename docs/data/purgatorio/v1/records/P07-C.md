## Behavior profile

Chastity / Respectful Intimacy — constructive pole
Behavior: Feels desire without coercion, communicates intentions, checks consent, respects refusal, and integrates sexuality with care.
Movement: RECEIVE desire → DIFFERENTIATE self from other → CONNECT consensually → HOLD respect.
Belief/reframe: Desire can be felt without ownership; intimacy requires mutuality, consent, and honest regard.
Trigger: Attraction, rejection, boredom, fear of intimacy, insecurity, novelty, or relationship conflict.
Real-world example: A person wants closeness, describes their intentions clearly, hears a boundary, and maintains respect without pressure.
Practice: State what you want and what you can offer honestly; invite a real yes or no; respect the answer without punishment.
Differential: Consensual sexuality, attraction, sexual orientation, high libido, nontraditional relationships, and abstinence choices are not themselves moral deficits. Chastity here means integrity of desire, not a prescribed sexual norm.
Success: Responds to attraction or rejection with consent, candor, reciprocal care, and preserved autonomy.
