# purgatorio:P01-V — Pride

## Behavior profile

Pride — limiting-belief expression
Behavior: Claims special standing, redirects credit, dismisses criticism, hides mistakes, or insists on being right to preserve rank.
Movement: EXPAND self-image → CONCEAL fallibility → REPEL correction → HOLD position.
Belief/reframe: I must be above others to be worthy, safe, or seen.
Trigger: Correction, another person receiving credit, losing face, public uncertainty, or comparison to an expert.
Real-world example: When a colleague points out an error, the person pauses, checks the fact, owns the error, and thanks them rather than attacking their standing.
Practice: Name a correction accurately, credit another person by name, and make one repair without explaining away the impact.
Differential: Confidence, leadership, disagreement, and healthy pride in real achievement are not this pattern; humiliation or self-erasure is not humility.
Success: In an actual status threat, receives feedback, credits another, corrects course, and can repeat this without rank restoration.

### Pass 3: limiting belief and real-world consequence

Belief: I must be above others to be worthy, safe, or seen. Trigger: Correction, another person receiving credit, losing face, public uncertainty, or comparison to an expert. Cost: Trust and collaboration narrow; people withhold honest feedback; repair becomes costly.

Sources: https://www.abct.org/get-help/what-is-cognitive-behavioral-therapy/, https://www.abct.org/get-help/what-to-expect-from-cognitive-behavioral-therapies/

### Pass 4: polar discriminants and journey integration
