# purgatorio:P04-V — Sloth / Acedia

**Record kind:** purgatorio_vice | **Template:** TPL-PURGATORIO-SUPERCOMPLEX-COMPAT-V1 | **State:** OWNER_APPROVED_BEHAVIORAL_STANDUP

## Behavior profile

Sloth / Acedia — limiting-belief expression
Behavior: Repeatedly delays chosen action, escapes into distraction, passively agrees while not starting, or abandons meaningful commitments.
Movement: AVOID difficulty → WITHDRAW initiative → HOLD imagined failure → PRESERVE stuckness.
Belief/reframe: My effort will not matter, or beginning will expose that I cannot succeed.
Trigger: Overwhelm, fear of failure, uncertain payoff, monotony, fatigue, shame, or too-large commitments.
Real-world example: A person avoiding an important message writes two honest sentences and sends it, rather than rehearsing catastrophe for days.
Practice: Choose one meaningful task smaller than five minutes; begin, record completion, then stop or continue by choice.
Differential: Rest, disability, depression, sleep loss, burnout, survival stress, and strategic waiting must not be called sloth.
Success: Repeatedly begins chosen, appropriately sized action under uncertainty and uses recovery without self-attack.

## Four depth passes

### Pass 1: literary and source image

Virgil locates sloth at the center of the mountain as insufficient love of a good; the penitents run with urgent energy and cite examples of zeal. The vice/virtue names form a source frame, not a psychological diagnosis.

Sources: https://danteworlds.laits.utexas.edu/purgatory/06sloth.html, https://www.ismreview.yale.edu/climbing-the-mountain-of-the-beatitudes

### Pass 2: movement and observable behavior

AVOID difficulty → WITHDRAW initiative → HOLD imagined failure → PRESERVE stuckness. Observable discriminant: Repeatedly delays chosen action, escapes into distraction, passively agrees while not starting, or abandons meaningful commitments.

Sources: https://www.abct.org/get-help/what-is-cognitive-behavioral-therapy/, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK470241/

### Pass 3: limiting belief and real-world consequence

Belief: My effort will not matter, or beginning will expose that I cannot succeed. Trigger: Overwhelm, fear of failure, uncertain payoff, monotony, fatigue, shame, or too-large commitments. Cost: Agency and trust in self decline, commitments accumulate, and avoidance makes the task feel heavier.

Sources: https://www.abct.org/get-help/what-is-cognitive-behavioral-therapy/, https://www.abct.org/get-help/what-to-expect-from-cognitive-behavioral-therapies/

### Pass 4: polar discriminants and journey integration

Vice/virtue discriminant: Rest, disability, depression, sleep loss, burnout, survival stress, and strategic waiting must not be called sloth. Cycle: Demand → hopeless forecast → avoidance → immediate relief → rising burden → renewed avoidance.

Sources: https://danteworlds.laits.utexas.edu/purgatory/06sloth.html, https://danteworlds.laits.utexas.edu/purgatory/06sloth.html
