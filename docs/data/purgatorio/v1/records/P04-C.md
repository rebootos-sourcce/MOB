## Behavior profile

Zeal / Diligence — constructive pole
Behavior: Begins a viable action, stays with a manageable commitment, requests necessary support, and returns after interruption.
Movement: RECEIVE capacity limits → INITIATE small action → PRESERVE continuity → EXPAND agency.
Belief/reframe: A small chosen action matters even before I know the final outcome.
Trigger: Overwhelm, fear of failure, uncertain payoff, monotony, fatigue, shame, or too-large commitments.
Real-world example: A person avoiding an important message writes two honest sentences and sends it, rather than rehearsing catastrophe for days.
Practice: Choose one meaningful task smaller than five minutes; begin, record completion, then stop or continue by choice.
Differential: Rest, disability, depression, sleep loss, burnout, survival stress, and strategic waiting must not be called sloth.
Success: Repeatedly begins chosen, appropriately sized action under uncertainty and uses recovery without self-attack.

## Four depth passes

### Pass 1: literary and source image

Virgil locates sloth at the center of the mountain as insufficient love of a good; the penitents run with urgent energy and cite examples of zeal. The vice/virtue names form a source frame, not a psychological diagnosis.
