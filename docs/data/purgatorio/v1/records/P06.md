## Behavior profile

TERRACE 6: Gluttony ↔ Temperance / Sufficiency
DANTE: Purgatorio XXII–XXIV. The sixth terrace places desired fruit and water out of reach; exemplary stories emphasize temperance and right relation to appetite.LIMITING BELIEF: More consumption will finally fill the discomfort or emptiness I feel.
REFRAME: I can notice the actual need and choose sufficiency without denying ordinary pleasure.
TRIGGER: Stress, emptiness, boredom, reward cues, loneliness, uncertainty, or fatigue.
VICE MOVEMENT: SEEK relief → APPROACH substitute → HOLD repetition → AVOID underlying feeling.
CONSTRUCTIVE MOVEMENT: RECEIVE signal → DIFFERENTIATE need from substitute → HOLD enough → RELEASE compulsive repetition.
OBSERVABLE VICE: Repeatedly consumes or seeks stimulation past a chosen useful limit while the underlying need remains unaddressed.
OBSERVABLE CAPACITY: Names the need, enjoys pleasure consciously, recognizes enough, and stops or changes course when appropriate.
OSCILLATION: Discomfort → substitute intake → short relief → unmet need persists → repeat.
INTEGRATION: Before a habitual reach, ask “What do I actually need?”; choose nourishment, rest, connection, pleasure, or a pause deliberately.
EVIDENCE OF CHANGE: Experiences discomfort or appetite, accurately distinguishes needs, and chooses sufficient behavior without harsh deprivation.
ALTERNATIVES: Hunger, enjoyment, celebration, medical appetite changes, food insecurity, or healthy self-soothing are not automatically gluttony.
ENGINE: read within existing 40–60 Compass median; do not turn terrace into CQ arithmetic or a diagnosis.
