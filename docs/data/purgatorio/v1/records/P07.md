## Behavior profile

TERRACE 7: Lust ↔ Chastity / Respectful Intimacy
DANTE: Purgatorio XXV–XXVII. Dante depicts the seventh terrace as purifying desire by fire, with examples of chastity. Historical theology and the poem’s judgments about sexuality are not imported as contemporary behavioral judgments.
LIMITING BELIEF: Being desired, possessing someone, or obtaining pleasure is what makes me worthy or complete.
REFRAME: Desire can be felt without ownership; intimacy requires mutuality, consent, and honest regard.
TRIGGER: Attraction, rejection, boredom, fear of intimacy, insecurity, novelty, or relationship conflict.
VICE MOVEMENT: ATTRACT validation → PENETRATE boundaries → CONCEAL need → AVOID genuine intimacy.
CONSTRUCTIVE MOVEMENT: RECEIVE desire → DIFFERENTIATE self from other → CONNECT consensually → HOLD respect.
OBSERVABLE VICE: Uses seduction to control, obscures intentions, pressures boundaries, treats people as sources of validation, or pursues repeated novelty despite harm.
OBSERVABLE CAPACITY: Feels desire without coercion, communicates intentions, checks consent, respects refusal, and integrates sexuality with care.
OSCILLATION: Worth threat or craving → pursuit of validation through another → short relief → loneliness or conflict → renewed pursuit.
INTEGRATION: State what you want and what you can offer honestly; invite a real yes or no; respect the answer without punishment.
EVIDENCE OF CHANGE: Responds to attraction or rejection with consent, candor, reciprocal care, and preserved autonomy.
ALTERNATIVES: Consensual sexuality, attraction, sexual orientation, high libido, nontraditional relationships, and abstinence choices are not themselves moral deficits. Chastity here means integrity of desire, not a prescribed sexual norm.
ENGINE: read within existing 40–60 Compass median; do not turn terrace into CQ arithmetic or a diagnosis.
