## Behavior profile

TERRACE 4: Sloth / Acedia ↔ Zeal / Diligence
DANTE: Purgatorio XVII–XIX. Virgil locates sloth at the center of the mountain as insufficient love of a good; the penitents run with urgent energy and cite examples of zeal.
LIMITING BELIEF: My effort will not matter, or beginning will expose that I cannot succeed.
REFRAME: A small chosen action matters even before I know the final outcome.
TRIGGER: Overwhelm, fear of failure, uncertain payoff, monotony, fatigue, shame, or too-large commitments.
VICE MOVEMENT: AVOID difficulty → WITHDRAW initiative → HOLD imagined failure → PRESERVE stuckness.
CONSTRUCTIVE MOVEMENT: RECEIVE capacity limits → INITIATE small action → PRESERVE continuity → EXPAND agency.
OBSERVABLE VICE: Repeatedly delays chosen action, escapes into distraction, passively agrees while not starting, or abandons meaningful commitments.
OBSERVABLE CAPACITY: Begins a viable action, stays with a manageable commitment, requests necessary support, and returns after interruption.
OSCILLATION: Demand → hopeless forecast → avoidance → immediate relief → rising burden → renewed avoidance.
INTEGRATION: Choose one meaningful task smaller than five minutes; begin, record completion, then stop or continue by choice.
EVIDENCE OF CHANGE: Repeatedly begins chosen, appropriately sized action under uncertainty and uses recovery without self-attack.
ALTERNATIVES: Rest, disability, depression, sleep loss, burnout, survival stress, and strategic waiting must not be called sloth.
ENGINE: read within existing 40–60 Compass median; do not turn terrace into CQ arithmetic or a diagnosis.
