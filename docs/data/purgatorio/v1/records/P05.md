## Behavior profile

TERRACE 5: Avarice / Prodigality ↔ Generosity / Stewardship
DANTE: Purgatorio XIX–XXII. Dante places avarice AND wasteful prodigality on the same terrace; the penitents lie face down, and examples praise generosity and honorable use of goods.
LIMITING BELIEF: I am safe and valuable only if I keep more than enough—or spend to prove that I matter.
REFRAME: I can create safety through realistic stewardship while sharing from choice and enoughness.
TRIGGER: Fear of loss, comparison, insecurity, sudden money, status pressure, or opportunity to control through resources.
VICE MOVEMENT: ATTACH to resource → HOLD or DISRUPT its flow → CONTRACT safety to possession → REPEL reciprocity.
CONSTRUCTIVE MOVEMENT: PRESERVE essentials → RECEIVE enough → RELEASE unnecessary control → CONNECT through fair exchange.
OBSERVABLE VICE: Hoarding beyond needs or spending to secure approval; leverages possession for control; sacrifices trust to acquisition.
OBSERVABLE CAPACITY: Meets responsibilities, budgets realistically, shares without coercion, and uses resources for actual values and relationships.
OSCILLATION: Loss cue → possession-as-safety belief → hoard or prove worth through spending → short relief → deeper insecurity.
INTEGRATION: Identify one resource choice: fulfill an obligation, release one unnecessary grasp, or practice bounded generosity without debt or resentment.
EVIDENCE OF CHANGE: Makes sustainable resource decisions under scarcity cues, honors obligations, and gives freely only within real capacity.
ALTERNATIVES: Poverty, prudent saving, disability-related needs, ambition, and financial planning are not greed; impulsive overspending is not generosity.
ENGINE: read within existing 40–60 Compass median; do not turn terrace into CQ arithmetic or a diagnosis.
