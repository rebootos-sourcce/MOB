# purgatorio:P02-V — Envy

## Behavior profile

Envy — limiting-belief expression
Behavior: Keeps score, discounts achievements, gossips, withdraws goodwill, or gains relief when a rival fails.
Movement: SEEK comparison → CONTRACT possibility → REPEL another’s good → WITHDRAW warmth.
Belief/reframe: Someone else receiving goodness reduces what is possible for me.
Trigger: A peer succeeds, receives attention, is loved, or gains a resource I wanted.
Real-world example: A peer receives an opportunity. Instead of discrediting them, the person congratulates them and makes a constructive plan for their own work.
Practice: State one real thing you value in the other person, offer non-transactional support when appropriate, then take one step toward your own goal.
Differential: Recognizing injustice, seeking fairness, or wanting a similar opportunity is not envy; generosity does not require tolerating exploitation.
Success: Can witness a peer’s success without punishment, support their success where appropriate, and keep a clear personal path.

### Pass 3: limiting belief and real-world consequence

Belief: Someone else receiving goodness reduces what is possible for me. Trigger: A peer succeeds, receives attention, is loved, or gains a resource I wanted. Cost: Relationships become competitive; attention leaves personal direction and attaches to monitoring others.

Sources: https://www.abct.org/get-help/what-is-cognitive-behavioral-therapy/, https://www.abct.org/get-help/what-to-expect-from-cognitive-behavioral-therapies/

### Pass 4: polar discriminants and journey integration
