## Behavior profile

TERRACE 2: Envy ↔ Charity / Kindness
DANTE: Purgatorio XIII–XV. Penitents are seated together with their eyes sewn shut; voices recount acts of selfless love, and Sapia recognizes rejoicing in others’ misfortune.
LIMITING BELIEF: Someone else receiving goodness reduces what is possible for me.
REFRAME: Another person’s success does not cancel my possibility; I can contribute without losing myself.
TRIGGER: A peer succeeds, receives attention, is loved, or gains a resource I wanted.
VICE MOVEMENT: SEEK comparison → CONTRACT possibility → REPEL another’s good → WITHDRAW warmth.
CONSTRUCTIVE MOVEMENT: RECEIVE another’s good → EXPAND possibility → CONNECT → EXPRESS kindness.
OBSERVABLE VICE: Keeps score, discounts achievements, gossips, withdraws goodwill, or gains relief when a rival fails.
OBSERVABLE CAPACITY: Recognizes another person’s good, offers genuine goodwill or help, and acts toward one’s own values without rivalry.
OSCILLATION: Comparison cue → scarcity belief → devaluation or withdrawal → temporary control → increased comparison.
INTEGRATION: State one real thing you value in the other person, offer non-transactional support when appropriate, then take one step toward your own goal.
EVIDENCE OF CHANGE: Can witness a peer’s success without punishment, support their success where appropriate, and keep a clear personal path.
ALTERNATIVES: Recognizing injustice, seeking fairness, or wanting a similar opportunity is not envy; generosity does not require tolerating exploitation.
ENGINE: read within existing 40–60 Compass median; do not turn terrace into CQ arithmetic or a diagnosis.
