# ATUNED Compass — Four Figures, Nine Depth Checks Each

**Date:** 2026-10-25  
**Research:** 3 rounds × 3 independent analytic checks × 4 figures = **36 substantive checks**. These are textual examinations, **not** 36 independent historical experiments.  
**Scope:** research overlay only. Existing ATUNED canonical engine, named mirror assignments, CQ/DQ, and live runtime are **not modified**.  
**Principle:** default behavior is what the figure *does*. Do not confuse trigger, technique, resulting condition, and observer inference.

## Three rounds per figure

### Charon — Gatekeeping

**Inferno:** `inferno:F01` · **Existing Compass axis:** `TR` · **Primary:** Gatekeeping · **Secondary:** Arbitrary Exclusion.  
**Decision:** SUSPEND_SOURCE_EQUIVALENCE.  
**Cut as default:** Threshold Paralysis.  
**Evidence calibration:** HIGH for gatekeeper role; LOW for negative moralization.

#### Round 1 — SOURCE TRIANGULATION

**Check 1. Primary Dante action.** Inferno III: Charon ferries the dead across Acheron and initially refuses the living Dante. His action is granting or denying passage, not his own inability to cross.

Sources: [external text](https://danteworlds.laits.utexas.edu/gate.html).

**Check 2. Earlier classical action.** Virgil, Aeneid VI: burial rites and the golden bough determine access; Charon also ferries those who qualify. Selective passage is a function of an established threshold.

Sources: [external text](https://www.theoi.com/Text/VirgilAeneid6.html).

**Check 3. Internal Inferno consistency.** Archived inferno:F01 uses THRESHOLD_GATEKEEPING and notes separate/protect/challenge as unapproved verb candidates; the figure is not assigned as a pathological condition.

Sources: `COMPASS.zip/01_INFERNO/INFERNO_NORMALIZED_REGISTRY_V1.json#inferno:F01`.

#### Round 2 — BEHAVIOR EXTRACTION AND POLE ALIGNMENT

**Check 4. Repeated default movement.** Across two primary traditions, repeated acts are selection, denial, and transport at a boundary. Core source behavior is GATEKEEPING; a shadow variant is unjust EXCLUSION.

Sources: [external text](https://danteworlds.laits.utexas.edu/gate.html); [external text](https://www.theoi.com/Text/VirgilAeneid6.html).

**Check 5. Motive vs behavior.** The sources establish a role and conditions for passage; fear of vulnerability, perfectionism or endless preparation is not established as Charon's motive.

Sources: [external text](https://www.theoi.com/Text/VirgilAeneid6.html); `COMPASS.zip/01_INFERNO/INFERNO_NORMALIZED_REGISTRY_V1.json#inferno:F01`.

**Check 6. Engine polarity stress test.** ATUNED currently pairs Charon with Rumi's TR/Trust and gives him 'Threshold Paralysis'. A refusal to admit another is not the same action as failing to take one's own next step. The TR link is an existing design pair, not demonstrated behavioral equivalence.

Sources: `atuned_src/engine/data/compass.js`; `docs/data/compass/teachers/v1/TEACHER_OPPOSITES_REGISTRY_V1.json#compass-inverse:Charon`.

#### Round 3 — ADVERSARIAL GAP TEST AND CUT RULE

**Check 7. Good-faith counterexample.** Charon transports the properly qualified dead and responds to credentials. Neutral boundary protection cannot be automatically coded as decoherence; the harmful human pattern would require arbitrary, discriminatory or coercive exclusion.

Sources: [external text](https://www.theoi.com/Text/VirgilAeneid6.html); [external text](https://danteworlds.laits.utexas.edu/gate.html).

**Check 8. Alternative falsification.** 'Threshold Paralysis' would require Charon repeatedly to postpone his own crossing because of doubt; the narrative instead has him operating the ferry. Evidence for the claimed equivalence does not appear.

Sources: [external text](https://danteworlds.laits.utexas.edu/gate.html); [external text](https://www.theoi.com/Text/VirgilAeneid6.html).

**Check 9. Cut decision.** KEEP Charon as a literary Inferno figure with core GATEKEEPING. CUT 'Threshold Paralysis' as a source-backed default and SUSPEND any semantic equivalence to a human trust deficit; retain historical identity and the existing engine pole separately until a revised explicit polarity is approved.

Sources: `COMPASS.zip/01_INFERNO/INFERNO_NORMALIZED_REGISTRY_V1.json#inferno:F01`; `atuned_src/engine/data/compass.js`.

### The-Furies — Vengeance

**Inferno:** `inferno:F06` · **Existing Compass axis:** `RE` · **Primary:** Vengeance · **Secondary:** Intimidation.  
**Decision:** SUSPEND_SOURCE_EQUIVALENCE.  
**Cut as default:** Ideological Rigidity.  
**Evidence calibration:** HIGH for pursuit/retribution and Dante threat; LOW for doctrine rigidity.

#### Round 1 — SOURCE TRIANGULATION

**Check 1. Greek primary pattern.** Aeschylus, Eumenides: the Erinyes relentlessly pursue Orestes to exact payment for blood crimes. Their function is avenging/retribution, not an automatic refusal to consider evidence.

Sources: [external text](https://www.theoi.com/Text/AeschylusEumenides.html).

**Check 2. Dante scene.** Inferno IX: the Furies threaten the travelers and summon Medusa to halt them. INTIMIDATION and threatened immobilization are directly observable in this scene.

Sources: [external text](https://ahc.leeds.ac.uk/discover-dante/doc/inferno/page/4).

**Check 3. Internal Inferno consistency.** Archived inferno:F06 identifies INTIMIDATION, and warns that they do not establish an equation between heterodox belief and pathological rigidity.

Sources: `COMPASS.zip/01_INFERNO/INFERNO_NORMALIZED_REGISTRY_V1.json#inferno:F06`.

#### Round 2 — BEHAVIOR EXTRACTION AND POLE ALIGNMENT

**Check 4. Across-tradition default.** Greek source role VENGEANCE/RETRIBUTION; Dante's local tactic is INTIMIDATION. VENGEANCE is the more general default, while coercive intimidation is a mode of expression.

Sources: [external text](https://www.theoi.com/Khthonios/Erinyes.html); [external text](https://www.theoi.com/Text/AeschylusEumenides.html); [external text](https://ahc.leeds.ac.uk/discover-dante/doc/inferno/page/4).

**Check 5. Trigger and expression.** The Furies respond to perceived violations of blood and kinship obligations. Their obsessive pursuit and threats may bypass due process. This is different from holding a doctrinal belief.

Sources: [external text](https://www.theoi.com/Text/AeschylusEumenides.html); [external text](https://beta.perseus.tufts.edu/urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0085.tlg007.perseus-eng2:299-395/).

**Check 6. Engine polarity stress test.** The existing RE Revelation mirror names Jesus and the Furies and calls the Furies 'ideological rigidity'. Vengeance might invert forgiveness/mercy, but does not logically invert revelation without a further owner-defined intermediary mechanism. This semantic mapping remains unproved.

Sources: `atuned_src/engine/data/compass.js`; `docs/data/compass/teachers/v1/TEACHER_OPPOSITES_REGISTRY_V1.json#compass-inverse:The-Furies`.

#### Round 3 — ADVERSARIAL GAP TEST AND CUT RULE

**Check 7. Evidence opposing static rigidity.** In Aeschylus' resolution the Furies ultimately accept an arrangement by Athena and become Eumenides, making 'incapable of updating a belief' a particularly poor global claim.

Sources: [external text](https://www.theoi.com/Text/AeschylusEumenides.html).

**Check 8. Scholarly disagreement.** The Princeton Dante commentary states that proposed allegories for the Furies and Medusa are diverse, with no consensus. 'Heresy' or 'hardened will' should not be promoted as the Furies' uncontroversial essence.

Sources: [external text](https://dante.princeton.edu/cgi-bin/dante/DispCommentByTitOrId.pl?INP_ID=240419&SMALL=0).

**Check 9. Cut decision.** KEEP Furies under VENGEANCE as the cross-tradition default and INTIMIDATION as Dante's tactic. CUT 'Ideological Rigidity' as a source-backed default and SUSPEND the semantic equivalence to Revelation's inverse, retaining the pre-existing symbolic engine mirror for separate owner review.

Sources: [external text](https://www.theoi.com/Text/AeschylusEumenides.html); `COMPASS.zip/01_INFERNO/INFERNO_NORMALIZED_REGISTRY_V1.json#inferno:F06`.

### Lucifer — Separation

**Inferno:** `inferno:F11` · **Existing Compass axis:** `IL` · **Primary:** Rebellion / Treachery context · **Secondary:** Self-Perpetuating Stasis.  
**Decision:** RETAIN_AS_ATUNED_MODEL_NOT_LITERARY_EQUIVALENCE.  
**Cut as default:** False Light.  
**Evidence calibration:** HIGH for narrative rebellion/stasis; MODERATE for model abstraction separation.

#### Round 1 — SOURCE TRIANGULATION

**Check 1. Primary poem evidence.** Inferno XXXIV: Lucifer is immobilized in Cocytus; flapping wings reinforce the frozen lake; Judas, Brutus and Cassius are chewed. Stasis is an exhibited condition, not a complete account of an originating action.

Sources: [external text](https://dante.princeton.edu/cgi-bin/dante/DispSummaryByTitOrId.pl?INP_TITLE=S+Inf+XXXIV).

**Check 2. Narrative origin.** In the Dante commentary and later Christian tradition Lucifer's rebellion against God precedes his fall. At the center of Hell he is separated from the highest love and unfree. The core narrative context is treachery and revolt.

Sources: [external text](https://danteworlds.laits.utexas.edu/circle9.html).

**Check 3. Internal Inferno consistency.** Archived inferno:F11 explicitly distinguishes TREACHERY as the circle's category, STASIS as condition, and Lucifer as figure. They must not be collapsed into a behavioral diagnosis.

Sources: `COMPASS.zip/01_INFERNO/INFERNO_NORMALIZED_REGISTRY_V1.json#inferno:F11`.

#### Round 2 — BEHAVIOR EXTRACTION AND POLE ALIGNMENT

**Check 4. Movement vs end-state.** ATUNED's pre-existing engine text explicitly says Lucifer's pride cuts a person off from Source. That defines SEPARATION as the model's movement; Dante's frozen condition illustrates its end-state. Model claim and direct literary observation are distinct.

Sources: `atuned_src/engine/data/compass.js`; [external text](https://danteworlds.laits.utexas.edu/circle9.html).

**Check 5. Nearest source behavioral term.** Rebellion/self-exaltation and severed relationship support self-SEPARATION; betrayal is Dante's location category, not an observed modern person trait. 'Stasis' is the feedback consequence, not the person's default choice.

Sources: [external text](https://danteworlds.laits.utexas.edu/circle9.html); [external text](https://ahc.leeds.ac.uk/discover-dante/doc/inferno/page/6).

**Check 6. Engine polarity stress test.** Jesus/Love vs Lucifer/SEPARATION is a coherent conceptual inversion within the owner's model of connection to Source. 'False Light' describes an optional technique for obtaining recognition; applause seeking is absent from Inferno XXXIV itself.

Sources: `atuned_src/engine/data/compass.js`; [external text](https://dante.princeton.edu/cgi-bin/dante/DispSummaryByTitOrId.pl?INP_TITLE=S+Inf+XXXIV).

#### Round 3 — ADVERSARIAL GAP TEST AND CUT RULE

**Check 7. Historical text boundary.** The 'Lucifer' morning-star vocabulary in Isaiah XIV originally addresses an arrogant king, and later angelic/Satanic readings are an interpretive tradition. Avoid pretending one stable historical figure story across all traditions.

Sources: [external text](https://new.bible.org/seriespage/isaiah-14).

**Check 8. Adversarial false-positive check.** Being unable to act, being depressed, traumatized or physically immobilized is NOT evidence of separation from Source, betrayal, or a Lucifer behavioral pattern; the moralized stasis inference is not allowed.

Sources: `COMPASS.zip/01_INFERNO/INFERNO_NORMALIZED_REGISTRY_V1.json#inferno:F11`.

**Check 9. Cut decision.** KEEP conceptual core SEPARATION, with narrative trigger PRIDE/SELF-EXALTATION and Dante outcome STASIS; preserve the established Jesus/Love polarity as an explicit ATUNED construct. CUT 'False Light' as the primary/default textual behavior (may remain a distinct optional expression, not an asserted historical act).

Sources: `atuned_src/engine/data/compass.js`; [external text](https://danteworlds.laits.utexas.edu/circle9.html).

### Asmodeus — Obstruction

**Inferno:** `inferno:X03` · **Existing Compass axis:** `DE` · **Primary:** Obstruction · **Secondary:** Possessive Obstruction (version dependent).  
**Decision:** RETAIN_CONDITIONAL_MODEL_MAPPING.  
**Cut as default:** Compulsive Craving.  
**Evidence calibration:** HIGH for violent obstruction; CONDITIONAL for jealousy or possessiveness.

#### Round 1 — SOURCE TRIANGULATION

**Check 1. Primary act.** Tobit 3:8: Sarah's seven husbands are killed before their marriages can be consummated. Repeated relationship-blocking harm is explicit; 'generic desire' alone does not describe this signature.

Sources: [external text](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/tobit/3).

**Check 2. Source motive variation.** In Tobit 6:15 some textual witnesses say the demon loves Sarah; other Greek textual witnesses omit this. A motive of possessive love cannot be assumed as invariant across versions.

Sources: [external text](https://www.bible.com/bible/37/TOB.6.CEB); [external text](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/tobit/6).

**Check 3. Source resolution.** Tobit 8:3 describes the demon's expulsion and confinement while Sarah and Tobiah's marriage proceeds, confirming the narrative obstruction's removal.

Sources: [external text](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/tobit/8).

#### Round 2 — BEHAVIOR EXTRACTION AND POLE ALIGNMENT

**Check 4. Default movement.** The lowest-inference shared source behavior is OBSTRUCTION, specifically repeated violent obstruction of intimacy/marriage. 'Possessive Obstruction' is a plausible compact two-word interpretation in versions that include the demon's attachment.

Sources: [external text](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/tobit/3); [external text](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/tobit/6).

**Check 5. Tradition separation.** Encyclopaedia Iranica documents Aēšma as wrath/destructive force, with desire and lust related but distinct associations; later demonologies are not proof that craving is Asmodeus' direct behavioral default in Tobit.

Sources: [external text](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/aesma-wrath/).

**Check 6. Engine polarity stress test.** ATUNED pairs Asmodeus against Ramakrishna's devotion/surrender of desire under DE. Possessive obstruction is a useful *hypothetical* grasping-vs-surrender contrast, but is not independently established as one-to-one by Tobit.

Sources: `atuned_src/engine/data/compass.js`; [external text](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/tobit/3); [external text](https://www.bible.com/bible/37/TOB.6.CEB).

#### Round 3 — ADVERSARIAL GAP TEST AND CUT RULE

**Check 7. Discriminating act from emotional intensity.** Healthy desire or consensual pleasure is not the adverse behavior. The differentiator is destructive interference with others' safety and agency, not the fact of wanting.

Sources: [external text](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/tobit/3); [external text](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/tobit/6).

**Check 8. Alternative motive falsification.** Jealousy, coercive possessiveness, rage and lust are interpretive alternatives, not equally evidenced motives. The narrative act persists even if one removes all motives: partners are harmed and union blocked.

Sources: [external text](https://www.bible.com/bible/37/TOB.6.CEB); [external text](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/aesma-wrath/).

**Check 9. Cut decision.** KEEP source core OBSTRUCTION (more precisely RELATIONAL OBSTRUCTION). Retain 'Possessive Obstruction' only as a version-dependent behavioral reading and DE pole mapping as an ATUNED model candidate. CUT 'Compulsive Craving' as the asserted primary story behavior; a later lust-related tradition may remain a clearly different secondary association.

Sources: [external text](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/tobit/3); [external text](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/aesma-wrath/).

## Gap tests and limits

1. **Source-name equality is not behavioral equivalence.** Keep figure identity joins for traceability; suspend unsupported behavior-to-axis interpretation rather than erasing records.
2. **Neutral story roles are not moral faults.** Charon's boundary enforcement requires additional evidence of unjust exclusion before becoming a negative pattern.
3. **Local tactics ≠ universal defaults.** The Furies' threat in Inferno IX is not proof that the Greek Erinyes are primarily doctrine defenders; Dante allegorical interpretations are disputed.
4. **Conditions ≠ choices.** Lucifer's immobilization must not be projected onto depressed, disabled or traumatized people. Separation here is the owner's concept of self-isolating pride, not clinical inference.
5. **Multiple textual recensions require separate variants.** Tobit 6:15's 'loves her' differs between witnesses, so possession/lust cannot be presented as uniformly established historical motive.
6. **Do not silently edit source Canon or rewrite the original owners' Compass polarities.** For Charon and Furies, revise or reject the semantic join during a subsequent runtime contract review; the historical figure record remains.
