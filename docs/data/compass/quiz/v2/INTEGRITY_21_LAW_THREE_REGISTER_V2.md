# ATUNED — Unified 21-Law Diagnostic · 63 Numeric Questions

**Status:** V2 draft for owner comprehension review. No live intake code or CQ arithmetic changed. Core 10 in funnel = 30 questions; remaining 11 in app = 33 questions. All three registers use 0–10; the third is reverse-facing, never directly averaged with aligned integrity ratings.

**Every prompt names an occasion and a visible response.** Only rate times when the situation actually happened, and use Not applicable instead of zero when it did not. 0 Never; 5 about half the opportunities; 10 always. A proposed protective motive is a hypothesis and can be rejected.

## 1. Truth (Throat) — Core 10

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When someone asked you for an honest answer about something important, how often did you tell the truth?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When telling the truth could disappoint someone, how often did you still give an honest answer when it was safe?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When you worried someone would be upset by the truth, how often did you leave out an important fact to keep them happy?

## 2. Transparency (Throat) — Core 10

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When your choices affected another person, how often did you tell them the information they needed?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When you made a mistake that affected someone else, how often did you tell them even if it was embarrassing?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When you worried a mistake would make someone think less of you, how often did you hide that mistake from the person it affected?

## 3. Justice (Throat)

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When deciding what was fair, how often did you use the same rules for yourself and others?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When being fair cost you something, how often did you still act fairly?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When doing what was fair might cost you an advantage, how often did you bend a rule to keep that advantage?

## 4. Unity (Crown) — Core 10

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When you disagreed with someone, how often did you still treat them with respect?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When people you wanted to fit in with mocked someone, how often did you choose not to join in?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When you wanted a group's approval, how often did you join in putting down someone the group disliked?

## 5. Awareness (Crown)

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When you felt a strong reaction, how often did you notice it before acting?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When you were rushed or upset, how often did you check what drove your reaction?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When a strong feeling rose suddenly, how often did you react to it before checking what you were actually feeling?

## 6. Nature (Crown)

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When something was beyond your control, how often did you adjust what you could do?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When a process needed time, how often did you let it follow a realistic pace?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When you could not control an outcome and felt worried, how often did you pressure someone else to change it instead of choosing your own next step?

## 7. Presence (3rd Eye) — Core 10

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When someone was talking to you, how often did you pay attention to what they were saying?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When worries distracted you during a conversation, how often did you bring your attention back to the person speaking?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When a conversation brought up an uncomfortable feeling, how often did you turn to your phone or another distraction to avoid that feeling?

## 8. Humility (3rd Eye)

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When shown a real mistake, how often did you consider it fairly?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When admitting an error could embarrass you, how often did you acknowledge it?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When someone pointed out a mistake and you feared looking foolish, how often did you argue before checking whether they were right?

## 9. Equanimity (3rd Eye)

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When plans changed suddenly, how often did you pause before responding?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When frustrated, how often did you avoid passing it on to someone else?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When you felt frustrated by an unexpected problem, how often did you take the frustration out on someone nearby to feel some relief?

## 10. Compassion (Heart) — Core 10

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When someone told you they were struggling, how often did you listen before responding?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When you were tired and someone needed support, how often did you respond with care while respecting your own limits?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When someone's distress made you uncomfortable, how often did you change the subject to avoid hearing more about their pain?

## 11. Forgiveness (Heart)

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When someone hurt you, how often did you choose not to retaliate?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When an old hurt returned, how often did you keep it from controlling how you treated others?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When you remembered an old hurt during a new disagreement, how often did you bring it up to punish the person rather than address the current issue?

## 12. Generosity (Heart)

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When you had resources to spare, how often did you help without expecting repayment?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When no one would notice, how often did you still choose to help when you could?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When you helped someone and wanted to be appreciated, how often did you remind them of your help to get recognition?

## 13. Aesthetic Beauty (Heart)

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When making or caring for something, how often did you give it the care it needed?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When rushed, how often did you still make a thoughtful choice about quality?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When you worried your work would not be good enough, how often did you leave it unfinished rather than let someone see an imperfect result?

## 14. Courage (Solar) — Core 10

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When something important needed to be said, how often did you speak up when it was reasonably safe?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When speaking up might bring disapproval, how often did you still say what mattered when it was safe?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When you feared someone would reject you for speaking up, how often did you stay silent even though it was safe to speak?

## 15. Duty (Solar) — Core 10

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When you freely made a realistic promise, how often did you follow through?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When something unexpected prevented you from keeping a promise, how often did you tell the affected person before the deadline?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When you feared disappointing someone, how often did you agree to a task you already knew you could not reasonably finish?

## 16. Responsibility (Solar)

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When your actions caused a problem, how often did you acknowledge your part?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When other people also made mistakes, how often did you still name your part?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When your choices contributed to a problem and you feared blame, how often did you point to someone else's mistake instead of naming your part?

## 17. Accountability (Solar) — Core 10

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When your mistake affected someone else, how often did you acknowledge what you did?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When admitting a mistake could damage your reputation, how often did you still own your part?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When you feared being blamed for a mistake, how often did you make excuses instead of acknowledging your part?

## 18. Temperance (Sacral) — Core 10

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When you noticed you had enough of something you wanted, how often did you stop?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When you were stressed, how often did you stop at enough instead of continuing just for relief?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When you felt upset, how often did you keep doing a comforting activity after you wanted to stop so you would not have to face the feeling?

## 19. Detachment (Sacral)

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When outcomes were uncertain, how often did you do your part without trying to control everything?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When others chose something you disliked, how often did you respect their choice while keeping your boundaries?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When you felt anxious about an outcome you could not control, how often did you pressure another person to make the choice you wanted?

## 20. Non-Harm (Root) — Core 10

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When you were upset with someone, how often did you avoid saying or doing something meant to hurt them?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When setting a boundary with someone who upset you, how often did you do it without trying to punish them?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When you felt disrespected, how often did you say something cutting because you wanted the other person to feel hurt too?

## 21. Patience (Root)

**BEHAVIOR — 0 to 10:** When you had to wait, how often did you stay respectful?

**PRESSURE — 0 to 10:** When someone's pace slowed you down, how often did you avoid taking frustration out on them?

**MECHANISM — 0 to 10:** When you worried about running out of time, how often did you interrupt someone who was still speaking?
