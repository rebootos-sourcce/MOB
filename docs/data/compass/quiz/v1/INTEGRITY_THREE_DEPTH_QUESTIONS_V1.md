# ATUNED — 21 Integrity Laws, Three-Depth Quiz

**Status:** Proposed content, not connected to the live funnel, CQ formula, or journal. **Source:** `atuned_src/engine/data/canon.js#SI`. Total: **21 laws × 3 = 63 prompts**.

The first question asks **what did you do?** The second asks **what happens when doing it is difficult or costly?** The third asks **what outcome were you avoiding or protecting against?** The deepest step is a user-reported interpretation, not a finding that AI assigns.

**Shared numeric scale:** For the past 30 days, considering situations where acting was reasonably possible: **0 = Never · 5 = About half the eligible times · 10 = Always**. Use Not applicable or Prefer not to answer rather than a false zero. The 4–6 band is ATUNED's conceptual oscillation range, not a verified population average.

## Integration rules

- Q1 holds the user's **unmodified self-reported baseline** for that law. Q2 separately shows a pressure score and the difference; do not silently average into canonical CQ.
- Q3 is an **optional proposed mechanism**, confirmed, edited or rejected by the user; do not treat a selected concern as proven root cause. More than one option may apply.
- When Q1 and Q2 disagree, Source AI can ask for a concrete event rather than assuming deception. A journal may later suggest a dated, evidenced revision subject to user approval.
- Do not assume a low score from lack of opportunity, unsafe circumstances, or constraints outside the user's control.
- Keep the existing `funnel/questions.js` 100-item bank and `atuned_src/engine/intake.js` 63-item left/right/neutral intake unchanged until an approved versioned migration.
- Canonical CQ Model 2 versus proposed Model 3 is unresolved; this content release does not implement a calculator or change the engine.
