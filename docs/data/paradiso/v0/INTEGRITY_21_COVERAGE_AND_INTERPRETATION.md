## Conflict and integrity safeguards

- Law-name match is not sufficient for a verified behavioral edge. Each relationship includes an explicit action criterion in the corresponding Paradiso record and the 44-row CSV.
- Truth is not ideological certainty. Transparency does not mandate exposure of protected private information.
- Justice requires procedural fairness and outcome awareness; an institutional title does not imply moral rightness.
- Humility is not shame, forced deference, or poor boundaries.
- Compassion, Unity and Forgiveness cannot be invoked to erase a person's legitimate grievance or allow further harm.
- Courage is not coercion or violence. Non-Harm does not require inaction in an emergency.
- Detachment is not coldness; Temperance is not deprivation; Patience is not indefinite passivity.
- Aesthetic Beauty is present as an existing exact law; it is not a measured vibration or a claim that one visual style is universally coherent.
- Nature in this framework is a canonical moral-integrity label; literature about medieval cosmology does not establish laws of physical reality.
- The 21-law inventory is only the active `SI` source used in this branch; do not silently merge incompatible older 10-, 13- or 47-law lists.
- All 11 named Compass masters remain existing coordinates, not Paradiso sphere identities. The twelfth master is currently unnamed in source code.
