## Behavioral profile
Bearing a real cost to uphold a justified principle while using the least harmful effective means.

**Movement:** PROTECT → INITIATE chosen action → HOLD a boundary → RELEASE needless aggression.

**Trigger:** A principle is costly to defend, or force seems easier than patient courage.

**Observed example:** A colleague challenges retaliation against a vulnerable coworker and accepts personal risk without threatening anyone.

**Practice:** Identify a difficult protective act, its real risks, and the least harmful way to take it.

**Distorted imitation:** Reckless warfare glorified as bravery, martyrdom sought for identity, threats justified by righteousness.

**Evidence to record:** Names a vulnerable person who needs protection, speaks a hard truth with a plan, avoids unnecessary injury, answers for foreseeable consequences.

**Disconfirmation:** Enjoys harming an adversary, escalates under criticism or calls collateral damage courageous by definition.

**False-positive guard:** Self-defense and protecting others are situational; evaluate necessity, proportionality, available nonviolent actions.
