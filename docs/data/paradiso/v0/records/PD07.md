## Behavioral profile
Remaining available to experience without needing constant output, noise, explanation or performance.

**Movement:** WITHDRAW from stimulation → RECEIVE present experience → HOLD attention → RELEASE compulsive doing.

**Trigger:** Silence or nonproductivity provokes discomfort and urges constant stimulation.

**Observed example:** A person spends a deliberate quiet interval noticing what is present, then returns to a task with clearer priorities.

**Practice:** Choose five minutes of quiet; afterward record what was actually observed, not what was hoped for.

**Distorted imitation:** Withdrawal disguises fear of action; serenity denies another's injury; retreat becomes a badge of superiority.

**Evidence to record:** Tracks what was actually experienced, regulates response under provocation, returns to an agreed task or protective action.

**Disconfirmation:** Uses 'being above it' to abandon responsibility or refuses timely intervention.

**False-positive guard:** Quiet time, illness, disability and deliberate rest must not be pathologized as sloth.
