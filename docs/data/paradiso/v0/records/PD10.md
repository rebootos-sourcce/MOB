# paradiso:PD10: Empyrean

## Behavioral profile
Experiencing connection without making a person, role or exalted state the property of the ego.

**Movement:** RELEASE identification → CONNECT without ownership → RECEIVE difference → EXPRESS care without transaction.
**Trigger:** A moment of closeness, recognition or accomplishment tempts appropriation or spiritual superiority.
**Example:** After a meaningful collective success, someone shares credit, remains open to correction, and serves the next need without claiming special rank.
**Practice:** After meaningful connection, name what you can enact for someone else without claiming the experience makes you superior.
**Distorted imitation:** Spiritual superiority masquerades as unity, emotional numbness as detachment, bypassing accountability as forgiveness.
**Observable change:** Shares credit, respects autonomy, repairs a harm where possible and does not claim ownership of people or extraordinary states.
**Disconfirming behavior:** Uses 'we are all one' to deny harm, refuses apology because separation is an illusion, demands devotion to their awakening.
**False positive:** The Empyrean is Dante's transcendent theology, not a literal human nervous-system state or a CQ threshold.
