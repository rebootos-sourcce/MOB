# paradiso:PD01: Moon

## Observed behavior
Staying true to a chosen commitment while acknowledging limits, coercion, and what was beyond one's control.

RECEIVE reality → DIFFERENTIATE chosen from imposed duty → PRESERVE honest commitment → RELEASE false self-blame.

## Universal laws of moral integrity
- Duty [Solar]: chosen responsibility is acted upon (PRIMARY)
- Responsibility [Solar]: real commitments are carried (PRIMARY)
- Truth [Throat]: the actual limits are reported (SECONDARY)
- Patience [Root]: time is allowed for a realistic response (SECONDARY)

## Four research depth passes
### Pass 1 - SOURCE_STUDY_DANTE
Piccarda's incomplete vow is caused by violent removal from the convent; Beatrice differentiates types of will, debates responsibility under coercion, and warns against foolish vows. A vow has moral gravity, but blind performance of a harmful promise is not virtue.

### Pass 2 - MOVEMENT_DECOMPOSITION
Fidelity means choosing obligations honestly and accounting for feasible conduct, not claiming one must die to meet externally imposed demands.

### Pass 3 - INTEGRITY_LAWS_21_CROSSWALK
Existing 21-law SI source exact names and bands. In this source-space: Duty (Solar): chosen responsibility is acted upon; Responsibility (Solar): real commitments are carried; Truth (Throat): the actual limits are reported; Patience (Root): time is allowed for a realistic response. These are behavioral alignment hypotheses, not new independent laws or CQ inputs.

### Pass 4 - ADVERSARIAL_AND_PSYCHOLOGY_CHECK
Distorted imitation: Duty without consent becomes coercion; responsibility misassigned to a victim becomes self-blame; patience becomes indefinite toleration. Disconfirming behavior: Repeated promises without follow-through or coerced agreement presented as voluntary devotion. More than a self-rating is needed to claim behavior change.


## Behavioral test
A promise is renegotiated promptly when circumstances change; no false fulfillment is claimed; affected people receive clear notice; threats are documented.

**Contradiction:** Repeated promises without follow-through or coerced agreement presented as voluntary devotion.
**Misidentification:** Cultural or family pressure can mimic chosen duty. Distinguish choice, safety, contract, disability and consent.
