## Behavioral profile
Holding realistic trust, future-directed possibility and generous regard together without losing factual judgment.

**Movement:** PRESERVE warranted trust → SEEK viable future → CONNECT compassionately → EXPRESS follow-through.

**Trigger:** Doubt, a discouraging setback or another person's need tests professed values.

**Observed example:** A team names uncertainty, commits to a realistic path, and supports an affected member without concealing risks.

**Practice:** Write one warranted trust, one evidence-based hope and one tangible act of care; test each in action.

**Distorted imitation:** Blind belief made into trust; unrealistic assurance made into hope; enabling harmful behavior called love.

**Evidence to record:** Admits uncertainty, sets viable commitments, helps at personal cost within capacity, confronts harm without stripping anyone's dignity.

**Disconfirmation:** Ignores evidence, makes unfounded promises or demands unconditional access in the name of love.

**False-positive guard:** Religious faith is the poem's source concept; the ATUNED behavior does not require adherence to that belief.
