## Behavioral profile
Doing work that benefits others without making reputation the condition of effort.

**Movement:** SEEK contribution → EXPRESS useful work → RELEASE audience-dependence → CONNECT results to shared benefit.

**Trigger:** A useful act will be unseen, or a peer receives public credit.

**Observed example:** A team member documents another person's contribution and completes an unglamorous repair without demanding acknowledgment.

**Practice:** Choose one valuable action no one will notice; record its real result and whether visibility was needed.

**Distorted imitation:** Public modesty performed to win fame, hidden coercion in 'service,' or treating legitimate payment and fair credit as vice.

**Evidence to record:** Provides a useful service, credits contributors, makes the work auditable and accepts appropriate acknowledgement without chasing an audience.

**Disconfirmation:** Changes priorities solely to gain acclaim; demands visibility before helping; withholds credit from the actual doer.

**False-positive guard:** Working professionally for salary, acclaim, promotion or visibility is not automatically selfish; evaluate concrete motive and harm.
