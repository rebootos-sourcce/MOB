## Behavioral profile
Treating desire as energy for consensual, reciprocal connection without turning a person into possession or proof of worth.

**Movement:** ATTRACT → CONNECT with consent → RECEIVE the other's perspective → RELEASE ownership.

**Trigger:** Longing, rejection, infatuation or an opportunity to coerce affection.

**Observed example:** Someone voices attraction honestly, hears a refusal, respects it, and stays humane and self-respecting.

**Practice:** State a desire without demand, ask what the other person chooses, and honor the answer.

**Distorted imitation:** Attachment called love; self-erasure called generosity; withholding warmth called detachment; biological essentialism misread as Nature.

**Evidence to record:** Explicit consent checked, limits respected, welcome given without debt, differences accepted, a refusal followed without retaliation.

**Disconfirmation:** Persists after refusal, extracts intimacy for proof of worth, assigns rigid identity from someone's supposed 'nature'.

**False-positive guard:** Loving intensely, consenting sexuality and disappointment are not by themselves decoherent behavior.
