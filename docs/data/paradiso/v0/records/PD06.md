## Behavioral profile
Applying transparent standards with humility about what is unknown, especially when holding power over others.

**Movement:** RECEIVE facts → DIFFERENTIATE interests → ORDER fair procedure → EXPRESS accountable decision.

**Trigger:** An institution must decide under uncertainty or personal allegiance conflicts with fair process.

**Observed example:** A manager discloses a conflict, hears the affected people, applies known criteria, and explains a reversible decision.

**Practice:** Audit one decision for conflicts, excluded voices, evidence and a fair appeal route.

**Distorted imitation:** A ruler assumes justice because of their own authority, hides criteria, evades review or labels opponents unworthy.

**Evidence to record:** Applies declared standards, records rationale, discloses conflicts, hears impacted parties, permits correction or appeal.

**Disconfirmation:** Exempts allies, suppresses dissent, changes the standards after choosing the beneficiary.

**False-positive guard:** A procedural step alone does not guarantee a just outcome; consequences and unequal access still matter.
