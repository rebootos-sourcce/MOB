## Behavioral profile
Seeking truth through evidence, humility and the capacity to recognize insight in those who disagree.

**Movement:** SEEK understanding → RECEIVE contradiction → DIFFERENTIATE evidence from certainty → CONNECT perspectives.

**Trigger:** A credible critic contradicts a belief or a rival produces a better explanation.

**Observed example:** A researcher updates a cherished hypothesis after testing it and openly credits a different school of thought.

**Practice:** Name a belief, the strongest counterevidence, and the specific observation that would change your view.

**Distorted imitation:** Intellectual superiority dressed as truth, endless doubt dressed as humility, data cherry-picked under the word discernment.

**Evidence to record:** Names an actual error, revises a position with evidence, acknowledges an opponent's genuine contribution, publishes corrections.

**Disconfirmation:** Refuses to answer counterevidence, withholds disconfirming data or claims certainty by authority alone.

**False-positive guard:** Expertise and firm conclusions are consistent with humility when evidence and revision conditions are explicit.
