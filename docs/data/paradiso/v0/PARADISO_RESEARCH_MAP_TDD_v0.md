## Core design ruling: Paradise as expression, not promotion of human worth
Dante has nine moving heavenly spheres plus the motionless Empyrean. The blessed belong together in the Empyrean and appear in lower spheres to make differences intelligible; these are not ten levels of moral or human worth. In ATUNED these become ten *lenses for constructive behavior,* capable of being expressed simultaneously. **No one is assigned a sphere by CQ.** Existing CQ describes Compass orientation, not proof of a virtue, a divine state, or the person's identity. Existing masters are coordinates on the coherent cone, **not** the top of the upward spiral. Source remains the underlying condition, not an unlockable destination.

### Record semantics
Every `paradiso:PD01` through `paradiso:PD10` record has 21 exact Super-Complex-compatible descriptive keys, four **distinct analytical passes**, source URLs, a trigger→movement→action→effect chain, discriminants, practical behaviors and explicit provisional crosswalk status. Every `oppositeId` stays `null` until an existing inverse record has been compared by behavioral mechanism and approved; candidates do not create runtime graph edges.

### What Dante says vs what ATUNED proposes
- **Dante literary layer:** the poem's setting, characters, themes, theological significance. Retain original Christian-medieval context; never present Dante's spheres as proven science.
- **ATUNED interpretive layer:** real-world behaviors of fidelity, service, love, wisdom, courage, justice, presence, trust/hope/love, coordination and unity. These are application hypotheses requiring evidence in context.
- **User evidence layer:** person's actual observations; no diagnosis, no automatic assignment, no invented conduct.
- **Framework graph layer:** established axis IDs and approved links, with unknown relationships left unresolved.

## Specific source boundaries
- Moon: Piccarda was coerced. Neither voluntary consent nor moral failure can be assumed from a broken vow.
- Venus: no condemnation of consensual adult sexuality, orientation, or ordinary desire.
- Mars: the medieval Crusader setting is not justification for violence; courage may protect without aggression.
- Saturn: contemplation is not avoidance; rest and limitations are not sloth.
- Fixed Stars: faith/hope/charity are source-laden theological concepts; product analogies cannot require religious belief.
- Primum Mobile: the nine angelic orders are distinct literary records if later built. They do not correspond automatically to existing ATUNED nerves, chakras or psychological archetypes.
- Empyrean: neither an 'enlightenment score' nor a claim that a user is divine. Dante's ending is a literary-theological revelation and love is the final organizing image.
