### B04 · DAN-ENVY-001 · Envy
**Source:** *Purgatorio* terrace II. **Family:** ENVY. **Status:** RESEARCH_COMPLETE / UNPROMOTED.
- **P1:** Upward comparison plus felt deprivation may yield resentment; rumination or rival loss can reinforce it; fair comparison and self-directed goals interrupt it.
- **P2:** Recurrent attempts to diminish another's good *because* they have it. Ask whether objection concerns their advantage or documented injustice.
- **P3:** B their gain reduces mine; P peers as rivals; T compares; F resentment; H tracks rank; A undermines; S belittles; E self-defines by deficit; R competes destructively.
- **P4:** Whistleblowing, equity concerns, and criticism of corruption can resemble envy; do not infer motive without evidence. **Confidence:** 0.70.
