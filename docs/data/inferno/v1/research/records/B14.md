### B14 · DAN-INCONT-001 · Incontinence / disordered appetite
**Source:** Scholarly umbrella for *Inferno* Circles II–V. **Family:** IMPULSE_OVERRIDES_CHOICE. **Status:** RESEARCH_COMPLETE / UNPROMOTED.
- **P1:** Strong immediate appetites or aversive emotions may override longer-term commitments; immediate relief reinforces; supported self-regulation interrupts.
- **P2:** Repeated choice-inconsistent action with consequential costs. Ask whether expectations, capacity, and options were reasonably clear.
- **P3:** B urges must be obeyed; P delay as intolerable; T short-term rationalizes; F urgency; H yields; A abandons plan; S excuses; E identifies with impulse; R promises become unreliable.
- **P4:** This is a higher-order family spanning lust, consumption, avarice, wrath, not an extra duplicate individual primitive. Neurodivergence, crisis, deprivation, and disability require context. **Confidence:** 0.67.
