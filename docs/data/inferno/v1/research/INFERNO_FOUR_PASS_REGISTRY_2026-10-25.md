# ATUNED Inferno Behavioral Research Registry

**Working research release:** 2026-10-25, R1  
**Scope:** 15 inherited descent research rows, 9 Inferno level records, 11 named figure records, and 3 cross-tradition name records.  
**Authority:** Research candidate only. No production, scoring, graph promotion, clinical classification, or person-level identity assignment.  
**Basis:** The 2026-10-20 ATUNED archetype package, `SOURCE_TDD_AI_HANDOFF_MASTER_REBUILT_V1.1_DEEP_PASS.md`, and the 2026-10-22 Fetter Complex TDD. The four-pass mandate comes from the 2026-10-25 conversation.  

## Execution rules

1. **One record, four passes, every time.** Pass 1 tests the possible mechanism (trigger, maintaining loop, interruption). Pass 2 defines what an observer could verify, including a discriminant question. Pass 3 tests nine expression channels independently. Pass 4 seeks alternatives, counterexamples, duplicated concepts, and reasons to downgrade the label.
2. **Source and inference never merge.** Record narrative role, source location, and source tradition separately from the ATUNED interpretation. A literary depiction supports a literary fact, not an empirical causal mechanism.
3. **Behavior first.** A figure can express multiple candidate behaviors; several figures can share the same behavior. A character never automatically creates a new primitive. The TDD's canonical verb vocabulary (`INITIATE`, `ASSERT`, `OBSERVE`, `EXPRESS`, `CREATE`, `PROTECT`, `CONNECT`, `SEPARATE`, `ANALYZE`, `STRUCTURE`, `ADAPT`, `EXPAND`, `CONTRACT`, `PERSIST`, `TRANSFORM`, `SEEK`, `LEAD`, `SERVE`, `CHALLENGE`, `WITHDRAW`, `ACCELERATE`, `STABILIZE`) remains distinct from descriptive behavior domains such as fraud or consumption.
4. **Retain original IDs.** Do not rewrite the earlier 15 rows. Normalize through an additive alias/crosswalk ledger. Duplicate root categories may share a normalized behavioral family while keeping their separate literary provenance.
5. **No false precision.** Confidence here is *research confidence* in a proposed mapping, not population prevalence, statistical validation, predictive accuracy, a user's psychological score, or a promotion permission. A high-confidence source role may coexist with a low-confidence psychological interpretation.
6. **Non-harm is mandatory.** Never infer a diagnosis, addiction, personality disorder, suicidality, moral worth, identity, or intent from a symbol. Preserve protective conduct, disability, grief, exhaustion, skepticism, privacy, and consensual desire as counterexamples when relevant.
7. **Cultural boundaries are first class.** Keep the Ugaritic Baal/Hadad tradition separate from later demonological Bael; keep the Book of Tobit's Asmodeus separate from later lust attributions; leave `Malak` without a forced identification. A deity in one religion is not automatically a demon or behavior pathology in another.
8. **Recursion and stopping rule.** Research → distinguish → crosswalk → collision test → repair → repeat until every in-scope row has four passes, a source boundary, a competing explanation, a conservative status, and a connected ID or explicit unresolved edge. Do not promote unresolved rows merely to reach 100% apparent completion.
9. **Graph:** `source figure → proposed behavioral family → observed story evidence → user confirmation/correction → possible pattern state`. Separate symbolic resemblance edges, family aliases, functional capacities, and owner-approved Canon edges. Missing user evidence stays UNKNOWN.
10. **Release gate:** Check source locations, row counts, ID uniqueness, graph references, duplicate families, contradictions, all four fields, and any scope exclusions. Report unresolved items explicitly. Do not update GitHub or runtime without a separate request.

## Reading the passes

`P1` = candidate mechanism, not established etiology. `P2` = observable signature and discriminator. `P3` = expressions with `B` belief, `P` perception, `T` thought, `F` feeling, `H` habitual behavior, `A` action, `S` speech, `E` self-expression, `R` relationships. These are **hypotheses to test**, not a claim all nine occur together. `P4` = competing explanation and falsification / collision test. The status `RESEARCH_COMPLETE / UNPROMOTED` means the desk-research record is filled; it does **not** mean scientific validation or Canon promotion.

**Source:** *Inferno* VI, Circle III (corrects an erroneous `Inferno III` reference in the older CSV). Cerberus attacks the gluttons; the consumer psychology is ATUNED's interpretation. **Family:** CONSUMPTION. **Status:** RESEARCH_COMPLETE / UNPROMOTED.

### L01 · Circle I / Limbo · ATUNED non-engagement / disbelief hypothesis
**Literary:** *Inferno* IV, virtuous non-Christian/classical figures without Christian baptism; Dante does **not** say their condition is behavioral resistance. - **P3:** B nothing new can matter; P novelty as danger; T pre-emptively dismisses; F discomfort; H disengages; A avoids testing; S says no without examination; E distances from growth; R stalls mutual inquiry.
- **P4:** Legitimate skepticism, consent, disagreement, discrimination, cultural difference, or simply insufficient evidence falsify automatic resistance. This is an ATUNED allegory, not Dante's moral claim. **Status:** RESEARCH_COMPLETE / UNPROMOTED; **interpretive confidence:** 0.48.

### F01 · Charon · threshold enforcement
**Literary:** *Inferno* III, ferryman across Acheron, confronts the living Dante and polices admission before Circle I. **Candidate:** GATEKEEPING/THRESHOLD_RESISTANCE. **Relations:** L01; canonical verb candidates `SEPARATE`, `PROTECT`, `CHALLENGE` (not Canon edges).

- **P2:** Sets or enforces terms for crossing a boundary. Ask whether criteria are fair, consistent, consent-based, and necessary, versus obstructive or arbitrary.
- **P3:** B boundaries keep order; P newcomer as disruption; T assesses eligibility; F vigilance; H checks entry; A permits/denies crossing; S issues commands; E performs authority; R controls access.
- **P4:** Transporting/guarding a threshold is a functional story role, not evidence of malicious resistance. Fair safety gates and necessary refusal are strong counterexamples. **Interpretive confidence:** 0.58. **Status:** RESEARCH_COMPLETE / UNPROMOTED.

### F02 · Minos · judgment/classification
**Literary:** *Inferno* V, hears confessions and assigns souls to their circle by winding his tail. **Candidate:** RIGID_CLASSIFICATION (only if behavior proves harmful). **Relations:** L02; possible `OBSERVE`, `ANALYZE`, `STRUCTURE`.
- **P1:** Need for certainty, procedural order, or control could drive categorization; agreement or authority reinforces it; calibration and revisable decisions interrupt rigidity.
- **P2:** Repeatedly assigns a fixed category to another person. Ask whether findings are evidence-based, appealable, and revised when contradictions appear.
- **P3:** B everyone has a fixed place; P difference as class; T sorts; F certainty; H labels; A allocates; S pronounces verdict; E acts as judge; R establishes hierarchy.
- **P4:** Classification may be fair and necessary. Minos is not a clinical `Judger` simply because he judges; a harmful primitive requires evidence of distortion, humiliation, or irreversible labeling. **Confidence:** 0.52. **Status:** RESEARCH_COMPLETE / UNPROMOTED.

### F03 · Cerberus · insatiability/consumption
**Literary:** *Inferno* VI, three-headed hound attacks and torments gluttons. **Candidate:** CONSUMPTION with INSATIABILITY signature. **Relations:** L03, B01/B08; possible `SEEK`, `PERSIST`, `CONTRACT`.

### F04 · Plutus · possession/resource guarding
**Literary:** *Inferno* VII, guardian of the avaricious and prodigal; blocks passage with opaque speech. **Candidate:** POSSESSIVE_GUARDING. **Relations:** L04/B07; possible `PROTECT`, `CONTRACT`, `PERSIST`.

2. **Behavioral pass:** Actions are rechecked against source descriptions and discriminant questions. *Result:* corrected Cerberus `Inferno III` to `Inferno VI`, separated Charon's Canto III from Circle I, separated wrath and violence, and separated Minos's judgment from harmful condemnation.
3. **Expression pass:** Every row explicitly tests B/P/T/F/H/A/S/E/R expression channels. *Result:* prose expressions are hypothesis prompts; a channel without source access stays `unknown` (especially cultural deities' private feeling and speech).
4. **Depth/contradiction pass:** Each row has a competing explanation, duplicate warning, and/or counterexample. *Result:* no promotion of threshold enforcement, judgment, intimidation, domination, Harpies' self-harm, Lucifer's stasis, or `Malak` identity to a canonical harmful primitive.

These are four *analytical desk-research passes* with source comparisons, **not** four independent prospective experiments, clinical validations, expert panels, or a claim that each row has four independent primary publications.
