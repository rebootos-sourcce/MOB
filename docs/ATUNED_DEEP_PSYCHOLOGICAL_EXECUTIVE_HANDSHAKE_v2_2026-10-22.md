## Strategy and execution

1. **Source extraction:** primary source/version, terminology, definition summary, section/anchor, hash, direct claim versus interpretation.
2. **Independent challenge:** independent corroboration where available, counter-evidence, version drift, contradictions, limitations. Re-reading the same source is not independent corroboration.
3. **Cross-record synthesis:** parent/child/sibling consistency, taxonomy implications, path impact, runtime contract, and test needs. Keep proposed, approved, and implemented-and-tested separate.
