# 8 · THE FOUR CONSTRAINTS A PUBLISHING PLAN MUST SOLVE

**1. Print economics.** Volumes are 32 to 34 pages. Amazon KDP prints spine text only above 79 pages, so singles cannot carry a spine. Three-volume clusters clear it comfortably. Digital has no such limit.

**2. ISBN.** Author has ruled digital-first, no ISBN purchase. Direct PDF sales require none. Print and retail do. A 100-block runs $575 and costs less than the 20 required at minimum.

**3. Audience split.** Modelling shows practitioners convert on twelve of seventeen products while the largest consumer segment converts on almost nothing except a free diagnostic. **This suggests a licensing or certification business rather than a direct-to-consumer publishing business,** and that decision precedes any launch sequence.

**4. Sequencing.** The three highest-value products are all unbuilt: the Communications Guide, the Purpose Workbook, and the Road Map. The eleven built volumes model lower than any of them.
