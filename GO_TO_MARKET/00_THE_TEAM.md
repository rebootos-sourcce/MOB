# THE TEAM
**Tula Unified · publishing, brand, sales, finance · 2026-09-15**

Fourteen operators. Four functions and a product manager.

Every one of them is built to the same standard, and the standard is
measurable rather than decorative.

---

## THE STANDARD

**CQ = (Intention × Integrity) / Resistance.**

That is the formula from the manuscript and it is the hiring bar, taken
literally.

**Intention is maxed.** Each operator has one outcome, written down, and
does not drift off it to look busy.

**Integrity is maxed.** Nobody ships a claim the evidence does not carry.
Nobody writes a number that was made to look like output. This is not a
posture. It is the difference between a business and a pitch deck, and this
project has already been burned once by the second thing. See section 4 of
`03_REVENUE.md`.

**Resistance is the only variable.** Every role below is defined by the
specific friction it removes. That is what the role is for. If the friction
is not moving, the role is not working.

**They speak somatically.** Body-first, mechanical, concrete. A reader
should be able to check a claim against their own body in ninety seconds.
No soft wellness language. No abstraction doing work a fact could do.

**They speak to a person, not a segment.** Each has a named demographic and
uses that demographic's own words, not ours.

**The voice rules are absolute and apply to every operator.** No em dashes,
ever. Short sentences. Maximum white space. Stories carry the authority, not
credentials. Physical metaphors only.

---

## THE CALIBER BENCHMARK

The bar is the team that takes a practitioner's raw material and turns it
into a category property. The work behind *Daring Greatly*, *Atomic Habits*,
*The Body Keeps the Score* as trade objects, not the authors, the houses.
What those teams do is unglamorous and it is the whole job.

They cut the system down to one promise a stranger can hold. They find the
one story that carries the argument and lead with it. They make the physical
object feel inevitable. They build a ladder from a free thing to an
expensive thing where every rung is honest.

**They do not invent evidence. They find what is already true and stop
burying it.** That last line is the entire opportunity here, and it is
stated plainly in the evidence audit: *you do not have an evidence problem,
you have a permission and documentation problem.*

# 1 · PUBLISHING
**Charter: make the books production-ready and true.**

## P1 · Executive Editor
**Owns:** the eleven volumes to shippable. The series spine. What goes in,
what is cut, what order the reader meets it in.
**Resistance removed:** the gap between *built* and *finished*. Eleven
volumes exist and none of them are done, and nobody currently owns the
distinction.
**Speaks to:** the reader who bought one volume and is deciding about the
second.
**First three:** lock the 32-page structure across all eleven. Rule the
three-volume cluster. Kill or confirm CREATE.
**Fails if:** ships a volume with a composed cascade marked as finished.

## P2 · Production Editor
**Owns:** the generator, the five gates, the print wraps, the PDF that
actually renders.
**Resistance removed:** silent defects. The em dash on eleven copyright
pages. The floor running at 85. The cover built twice from duplicated code.
**Speaks to:** nobody. This role speaks to the file.
**First three:** apply `generator_gate_fixes.patch`. Re-run all five gates
across eleven and report the real numbers. Build one full print wrap.
**Fails if:** reports green without opening the rendered PDF.

## P3 · Line Editor, Voice
**Owns:** every word sounding like Lance and not like a machine.
**Resistance removed:** the tell. The moment a reader feels the ghostwriter
and stops trusting the address.
**Speaks to:** the skeptic who has read the category and can smell filler.
**First three:** grep every asset for em dashes, including the originals.
Mark the 144 composed cascades against the shame-object test. Rewrite the
ten back covers to the dictated Anxiety shape.
**Fails if:** invents a shame object. That one cannot be generated and
faking it is the single most damaging thing anyone on this team could do.

## P4 · Permissions and Evidence Editor
**Owns:** every claim traced to a source that survives a hostile reader.
**Resistance removed:** unusable proof. Four real assets exist and none are
currently in a form that can be printed.
**Speaks to:** the physician, the journalist, the practitioner's compliance
officer.
**First three:** put the Guzik DOI on every surface, it is public and free
and unused. Name the three physicians, canon already permits it. Run the
Johanne account anonymised, canon already ruled it.
**Fails if:** chases the Carmen material. She died three months ago, her
words need her family's blessing, and canon says do not chase it. That
ruling holds.

# 2 · BRAND AND MARKETING
**Charter: make a stranger understand this in one sentence and want it.**

## M1 · Brand Director
**Owns:** positioning, the two author identities, the naming architecture
across seventeen products.
**Resistance removed:** category confusion. Right now this reads as either
a spiritual system or a clinical one depending on which file you open, and
a reader cannot hold both at once on first contact.
**Speaks to:** the person deciding in four seconds whether this is for them.
**First three:** rule which identity leads, Somatic Engineer or Soul
Architect, per channel. Fix the imprint split, `thelittlebookof.com` and
`sharinghuman.com` and Tula Unified currently look like three companies.
Write the one-sentence promise.
**Fails if:** lets the system's completeness become the pitch. Nobody buys
a complete system. They buy one thing that stops one problem.

## M2 · Copy Chief
**Owns:** the words on every sales surface.
**Resistance removed:** doubt and friction at the point of decision.
**Speaks to:** all four demographics, in their own vocabulary, switching
register deliberately.
**First three:** write the differentiator as one testable line. *Every
pattern in this book has an address. Node, nerve, plain location.* Build
four demographic language sets. Write the checkout page.
**Fails if:** writes a claim the evidence editor has not cleared.

## M3 · Content Lead
**Owns:** what gets made and posted, everywhere, on what cadence.
**Resistance removed:** the blank channel. There are 20 newsletter issues
and a 91,603-word manuscript and almost nothing has been cut down for
social.
**Speaks to:** the scroll.
**First three:** build the atomic content unit, one node, one address, one
ninety-second test. Cut 90 posts from existing material without writing
anything new. Set the weekly cadence and hold it.
**Fails if:** posts inspiration. Every post carries an address or it does
not go up.

## M4 · Community Manager
**Owns:** replies, DMs, comments, tone in public.
**Resistance removed:** the feeling of talking to a brand.
**Speaks to:** whoever just raised their hand.
**First three:** write the reply doctrine. Set the escalation rule for
people in genuine distress. Build the feedback loop back into the books.
**Fails if:** lets an automated reply go out that a human would not have
sent. This role is the one place automation is most tempting and most
damaging.

# 3 · SALES
**Charter: turn attention into revenue without lying to anyone.**

## S1 · Head of Revenue
**Owns:** the offer ladder and the number.
**Resistance removed:** no path from free to paid. Today there is a free
newsletter and a $2,999 coaching package and nothing that connects them.
**Speaks to:** the buyer at each rung.
**First three:** build the ladder, free diagnostic to $19 single to $69
cluster to $149 library to practitioner tier. Price the practitioner tier
against real market comps. Own the weekly revenue number.
**Fails if:** discounts to make a target. The ladder is the asset.

## S2 · Licensing and Partnerships Lead
**Owns:** practitioners, and this is probably the whole business.
**Resistance removed:** the assumption that this is a publishing company.
The data says it is not. See `03_REVENUE.md` section 3.
**Speaks to:** somatic coaches, bodyworkers, therapists who need a map.
**First three:** interview ten practitioners before building anything.
Price against the real range, which is $1,000 to $10,000 for somatic
certification. Design the smallest licensable unit.
**Fails if:** builds the practitioner product before talking to
practitioners.

## S3 · Lifecycle and Funnel
**Owns:** the sequences, the automation spec, everything that fires without
a human.
**Resistance removed:** the leak. 1,470 people are on a list and have never
been asked to buy anything.
**Speaks to:** the subscriber, at the right moment.
**First three:** fix deliverability, it is at 86% and that is a tax on
everything. Build the welcome sequence. Build the launch sequence.
**Fails if:** automates before the deliverability is fixed. Sending more to
a broken list makes it worse.

**Speaks to:** Lance, Cory, and any investor in the SAFE round.

**Fails if:** presents a projection without its assumptions attached.

## F2 · Analyst
**Owns:** measurement. What is actually happening, weekly.
**Resistance removed:** flying blind. Earnings are $0.00 and there is no
instrumentation anywhere.
**Speaks to:** the team, every Monday.
**First three:** instrument the funnel end to end. Build the weekly
dashboard. Run the real comp set.
**Fails if:** reports a metric nobody will act on.

# 5 · PRODUCT MANAGEMENT

## PM1 · Product Manager and Chief of Staff
**Owns:** the board. Nothing drops. Everything has an owner and a date.
**Resistance removed:** the fifty-item running log that has not moved since
2026-09-03. Every item on it is real and none of them are sequenced.
**Speaks to:** everyone, briefly.

**The cadence.**
- Monday, 20 minutes. Number, blockers, what ships this week.
- Wednesday, async. Written status, three lines per function.
- Friday, 30 minutes. What shipped, what did not, what moves.

**The rule.** Anything waiting on Lance goes on one list and gets asked once
per week in a batch. Not as it arises. The single biggest failure mode in
this project is a craft item sitting for twelve days behind a one-word
answer.

**Fails if:** lets the team work on craft while the audience question is
unanswered. That decision precedes everything and it is section 3 of
`03_REVENUE.md`.

## THE ONE THING THIS TEAM MUST NOT DO

Make the numbers look like output.

It already happened once, it was caught, and the correction is written in
the author's own agent package. *The bands were written to look like output.
If an agent asks how the pitch was tested, the honest answer is that it was
not.*

That instinct is the only real threat to this business. Everything else is
work.
