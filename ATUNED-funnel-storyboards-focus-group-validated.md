# ATUNED Funnel Storyboards
## Focus Group Validated Flow

ENCOUNTER → CURIOSITY → STORY → NOTICE → TEST → FIELD → SYMBOL → ART → PATTERN → CHANGE → VERIFY → MIRROR → CURIOSITY 2 → ENTER ATUNED

The goal is to let the person experience ATUNED before asking them to believe in it.

---

# STORYBOARD 01
## ENCOUNTER

### Purpose
Create curiosity before explanation.

### Visual
Full screen living human figure. A subtle field moves through the body. Almost no interface.

### Copy
**Something is running underneath the way you live.**

Small supporting line:

**ATUNED helps you see it.**

CTA:

**See what's running**

### Interaction
The figure responds subtly to presence.

### User thought
“What is this?”

### Transition
The field opens into the first question.

---

# STORYBOARD 02
## CURIOSITY

### Purpose
Give the person an immediate way into their own experience.

### Visual
Six symbolic forms:

Knot  
Flame  
Closed Door  
Mirror  
Open Field  
Voice

Seventh option:

**I'll tell you**

### Copy
**What's been following you lately?**

### Interaction
The selected symbol becomes active while the others quiet down.

### Example
User selects **Closed Door**.

System:

**Tell me about it.**

### User thought
“Okay. This is about me.”

---

# STORYBOARD 03
## STORY

### Purpose
Get the person's actual language into the system.

### Visual
Large open writing field with a living field around it.

### Copy
**Tell me something that happened.**

Optional example:

“My boss asked me to present the work and I immediately wanted to avoid it.”

### Interaction
As the person speaks or types, meaningful words begin to register visually.

Example:

**avoid**

The word becomes more visible. A subtle thread connects it to the body.

### Rule
Do not immediately call the word a pattern. It is only a signal.

### User thought
“Wait. It picked that up.”

---

# STORYBOARD 04
## NOTICE

### Purpose
Show the first piece of intelligence.

### Visual
Three distinct levels:

**YOU SAID**

The person's actual words.

**ATUNED NOTICED**

A highlighted signal.

**LET'S TEST**

A possible interpretation.

### Example

YOU SAID:

“I wanted to have the conversation, but I kept finding reasons not to.”

ATUNED NOTICED:

**Avoidance**

LET'S TEST:

**Are you avoiding the conversation, or avoiding what you expect will happen if you have it?**

### Rule
Keep fact, observation, and hypothesis visibly separate.

---

# STORYBOARD 05
## TEST

### Purpose
Demonstrate that the system can be corrected.

### Visual
The current hypothesis appears as a living object, not as a verdict.

### Copy
**Is that what you meant?**

Options:

**That's it**

**Not quite**

**Show me another possibility**

### Interaction
If the person rejects the hypothesis, the system changes it.

Example:

User:

**Not quite.**

System:

**Okay. Let's look closer.**

New hypothesis:

**You may be avoiding the reaction you expect from him.**

### Rule
The system never defends its first interpretation.

### User thought
“It changed because I corrected it.”

---

# STORYBOARD 06
## FIELD

### Purpose
Connect story, language, body, and experience.

### Visual
The human figure returns. A phrase from the story connects to the body.

### Copy
**When you think about it, where do you feel it?**

Choices:

Chest  
Throat  
Gut  
Head  
Somewhere else

### Rule
The person identifies the sensation. ATUNED does not invent it.

### Visual response
The selected region becomes active and the surrounding field responds.

### User thought
“So this is what it means by seeing where it runs.”

---

# STORYBOARD 07
## SYMBOL

### Purpose
Use symbolic language to investigate behavior without reducing the person to a label.

### Visual
An archetypal symbol appears.

Example:

**WARRIOR**

The symbol begins at approximately 50% saturation.

The archetype is always present. Its expression changes.

### Copy
**When something challenges you, which feels more like what happens?**

Two visual expressions:

**Move toward it**

**Pull away**

### Follow-up
**When you pull away, what are you protecting?**

Safety  
Control  
Belonging  
Peace  
Identity  
Something else

### Rule
Do not automatically label behavior as “overexpression.” Describe what happened and let the person clarify.

---

# STORYBOARD 08
## ART QUESTION

### Purpose
Gather another signal without making the interaction feel like a questionnaire.

### Visual
Two or more abstract moving forms.

One contracts.  
One opens.  
One gathers.  
One disperses.

### Copy
**Which feels more like you right now?**

### Interaction
The person selects one. The system records the choice as evidence.

### Optional follow-up
**What made you choose it?**

### Rule
Art questions must affect the investigation. They cannot become decorative personality tests.

---

# STORYBOARD 09
## PATTERN

### Purpose
Show the relationship between events rather than simply naming a trait.

### Visual

**EVENT**

Your boss asked you to present.

↓

**REACTION**

You felt tension.

↓

**RESPONSE**

You wanted to avoid it.

↓

**PROTECTION**

You expected conflict.

↓

**PATTERN**

Avoiding confrontation when you expect emotional cost.

### Copy
**Something is here.**

Then:

**Does this happen elsewhere?**

Yes  
Sometimes  
No  
I'm not sure

### User thought
“Oh. That's not just one incident.”

---

# STORYBOARD 10
## CHANGE

### Purpose
Let the person experience the system working with the pattern.

### Visual
The pattern becomes a living object.

It contracts. The tether tightens. The field gathers. Release occurs. Space opens. The system becomes quiet.

### Copy
**Want to see what happens if we work with it?**

Options:

**Try it**

**Not now**

### Release sequence
Preparation → Observer activation → Pattern presentation → Channel activation → Release → Somatic observation → Pattern decision → Reframe → Integration → Cooldown

### Rule
Do not make the animation imply that the pattern is definitely gone. Show a state transition. Verification comes next.

---

# STORYBOARD 11
## VERIFY

### Purpose
Let the person determine what actually changed.

### Visual
Before and after appear side by side. Not a score. A comparison.

### Copy
**What changed?**

Options:

**I feel different**

**I see it differently**

**Something moved**

**Nothing changed**

**I'm not sure**

### Example

Before:

**I kept avoiding the conversation.**

Now:

**I can imagine having it without the same reaction.**

System records:

**Internal change observed**

If behavior has not changed:

**Behavioral change not yet verified**

### Rule
Never convert a positive feeling into proof of behavioral or causal change.

---

# STORYBOARD 12
## MIRROR

### Purpose
Reflect the person's emerging path.

### Visual
Story, language, body, pattern, question, release, and verification gather into one living composition.

### Copy

**Something is becoming clear.**

You noticed yourself avoiding the conversation.

You stayed with the reaction instead of moving away from it.

You found where it landed in your body.

You tested the first interpretation.

You changed the way you saw it.

Then:

**That is the beginning of seeing how you operate.**

### Rule
Mirror is interpretation over evidence. It is not a new source of truth.

### User thought
“It remembers what I just did.”

# THE THREE TRUST STATES

Every intelligent observation should visibly distinguish:

### YOU SAID
Direct evidence from the person.

### ATUNED NOTICED
Something the system extracted or connected.

### LET'S TEST
A hypothesis that still needs confirmation.

This should become a foundational visual language throughout ATUNED.

# CORE EXPERIENCE
