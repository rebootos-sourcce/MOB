The charge
Rigid
I'm letting go of believing, perceiving, thinking, behaving, acting, and feeling that I am unwilling to bend, not listening but, unwilling to see, unwilling to open up, uneasy, tense, hesitant, silenced, unheard, dismissed, misunderstood, swallowed, choked, suppressed, invisible, disconnected from my voice, unable to say the true sentence, unable to disagree out loud, afraid of being heard, afraid of my own words.

Expression
I know that I am clear, honest, direct, heard, unguarded, true, steady, plain, unhurried, open in the throat, saying the true sentence first, able to disagree out loud, done rehearsing the sentence, letting the jaw go, speaking at the speed of thought, true in what I say, true in what I withhold, true in the room, true when it costs me.
open throat, loose jaw, full breath, easy neck
