printed cover wins.

## THE STRAP CHANGED

The generator carried:

> Every pattern has an address in the body. This is the map.

The printed covers carry:

> **A key to unlocking tension stored in the body and restoring natural
> presence**

Corrected in `build_volume.py`. The older line still reads well as a
marketing line and is kept in the messaging doc. It is not the cover strap.

## Colour

| | Field | Title | Series line and strap | Mark |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| COURAGE, Root | deep red | white | cream | gold |
| WONDER, Sacral | vermilion orange | white | cream | gold |

**Title is always white. Everything else is cream. The mark is always gold.**
The field carries the band. That is the whole colour system and it needs no
per-volume decision beyond the field.

## Two inconsistencies between the two covers

**1. The border.** WONDER sits inside a cream margin. COURAGE bleeds to the
edge. One of them is wrong, or the border is a later revision that COURAGE
has not had. Needs a ruling.

**2. The seal.** WONDER carries a small four-point star low right. COURAGE
has nothing there. That position is where `build_volume.py` places the crown
seal, so WONDER matches the generator and COURAGE does not.

## Marks are per volume, not decorative

| Volume | Band | Mark |
|---|---|---|
| COURAGE | Root | `triangle` |
| WONDER | Sacral | `eye` |

Confirms the mark library maps to volumes. The remaining seven marks should
be assigned as the other volumes are confirmed.

## Note on which series this is

These covers carry **The Somatic Book of REPROGRAMMING** over titles from the
**original nine Little Books** line, COURAGE for Root and WONDER for Sacral.

That suggests the two series are converging rather than sitting side by side.
Flagged rather than assumed. It changes what the eleven volumes are called
and needs confirming.
