# Copy questions

Thirty questions for the owner, before any copy is drafted. Nothing here is a
draft. Every question states why it matters, what it unblocks, what happens if
it is not answered, and the default that ships if he says nothing.

Where the research already implies the answer, it says so and asks him to
confirm or overrule. Those are faster to clear and they are marked
**confirm or overrule**.

Read in order. The order is by consequence, not by size.

---

paragraph and no output line can be written without being rewritten later.
Everything else in this file can be written against its default.

## Group A. The question that sets every other answer

### 1. What does a person feel in the second the reading lands

Name the state. Not the information, the state. Recognised. Caught out.
Steadied. Sobered. Braced. Relieved. Named at last.

**Why it matters.** Every register decision below is downstream of this. The
same arithmetic can be written to land as a verdict, as a diagnosis, as a
mirror or as a mechanic's estimate, and the words are different in all four.

**Unblocks.** All of it. Headers, the output line, the band entries, the result
page, the ad.

**If unanswered.** The writing drifts surface by surface. That is the drift
`terms.py` is already reporting: six concepts written five ways each, because
nobody ruled the feeling and each string was written to its own idea of it.

**Default.** Recognition, not revelation. The person feels described rather
than judged, and the first thing they feel is that something they already knew
in their body has been given a location. Mechanic's estimate, not verdict.

## Group B. The band names and the floor

### 2. Do the four low band names change. Incoherent, Corrupt, Severe, Collapsed

**Confirm or overrule.** Four panels reached this independently.
`PANEL-10k.md` prices it as the single largest commercial item in the product
and the cheapest to build. In its simulated scenario B, 1,645 of the 1,948
people who reach a score are handed one of these four words.

**Why it matters.** Corrupt and Severe are moral adjectives delivered by
software with no standing to judge. Two of the six reference profiles are
handed Severe. One reads Incoherent at a mid reading with nothing held.

**Unblocks.** `TIERDEF`, and every other label class, because `toward` on all
fifteen classes points at one of these words. Tranche one of the label model
cannot be written until the names are ruled.

**If unanswered.** The names stand and the entries carry them. That works and
it costs the sentence "it is not a statement about your character", which is
doing work a different word would not need.

**Default.** The four become degrees of one mechanical concept the engine
already measures, load: `Under load`, `High load`, `Heavy load`,
`Fully loaded`. Thresholds do not move.

### 3. Where does a person go from the two lowest readings

**Why it matters.** At the two lowest readings the instrument should not be the
thing a person is using tonight. The label model reserves one field for this
and it is the only field in the product with no content.

**Unblocks.** The floor copy, and Phase 1 of the quality plan, which does not
close without it.

**If unanswered.** Nothing can be invented. A guessed destination is worse than
none.

**Default.** At the two lowest readings the surface shows the reading, one line
saying the instrument stops here, and nothing else. No offer, no tier, no
streak, no next step, no subtitle. Silence is shippable. A guess is not.

### 4. May a label the product puts on a person ever be a moral word

**Why it matters.** The band names are seven labels. The saboteurs are
thirty three, and the roster includes Manipulator, Aggressor, Nihilist, Cynic
and Control Freak. If the ruling is that the product never puts a moral word on
a person, it lands on the saboteur roster too, which is a far bigger rewrite
than four words.

**Unblocks.** Whether tranche one is 33 labels or 66.

**If unanswered.** The bands are fixed and the saboteurs keep their names,
which leaves the posture inconsistent at exactly the place a hostile reader
looks.

**Default.** A label describing a pattern may be blunt. A label describing the
whole person may not. Saboteur names stand, because a saboteur is a pattern
with an address. Band names change, because a band is put on the person.

### 5. Is Mastery a destination or a ceiling

**Why it matters.** The engine puts Mastery arithmetically out of reach for
anybody carrying load. The reference profile that reaches it carries nothing
and has every law at ten. A top band nobody can reach, displayed as the top of
a ladder, is a promise the arithmetic refuses.

**Unblocks.** The top entry, and whether the ladder is shown as a ladder at all.

**If unanswered.** It stays on screen as a destination and the most numerate
segment does the arithmetic and publishes it.

**Default.** Mastery is described as a limit and not a target. The entry says
what it would take, plainly, and the direction on the band below points at the
band below it and not at Mastery.

### 6. At the floor, is the register instruction, or nothing at all

**Why it matters.** Every band entry currently ends with a direction. At the
bottom, a next step reads as a cheerful product talking over somebody who
cannot use it. But an entry with no direction is a verdict, which is the thing
the entries exist to prevent.

**Unblocks.** The two lowest entries, and the shape of the `toward` field.

**If unanswered.** The drafts already in the tree ship, which say "off the
floor, and nothing more ambitious than that" and "weight off, and not alone".
Those are defensible and they are still instructions.

**Default.** One physical instruction, smallest possible, no programme, no
count, no encouragement: one seat, one address, one line. Nothing that implies
a week.

### 7. Is the zero state a reading or a refusal

**Why it matters.** With nothing answered the app currently prints a band name,
a shut law and three charges at 3.0, and three rows above it prints
`Held: nothing`. It diagnoses a stranger and contradicts itself on the same
panel.

**Unblocks.** The first screen a person ever sees, which is the highest traffic
copy in the product.

**If unanswered.** The instrument keeps opening with a fabricated verdict.

**Default.** A refusal, in the instrument's own voice: nothing is measured, the
wheel is empty, and one line saying what to do first. The interval already
knows it knows nothing. The copy says the same thing in words.

## Group C. Names and nouns

### 8. What is the product called

**Why it matters.** Four names are live: the wordmark reads Atüned, the browser
tab reads `SOURCE · your field`, the repository is MOB, the brief says
Atüned / SOURCE. A person who bookmarks it gets one name and a person looking
at the screen gets another.

**Unblocks.** The landing page, the tab, the wordmark, the ad, the share, the
practitioner page. No acquisition copy can be written without it.

**If unanswered.** The landing page cannot ship.

**Default.** Atüned is the product. SOURCE is the engine inside it and is named
only where the engine is the subject. The tab matches the wordmark. MOB stays
the repository and the book, and never appears in front of a person.

### 9. What is a person called

**Why it matters.** Six words are in use for one concept: you, profile, persona,
your field, whose field, client. `terms.py` reports you ten times, profile five,
your field once. It is the subject of the first sentence on every surface.

**Unblocks.** Every string. This is the cheapest high value ruling in the file.

**If unanswered.** The drift continues and the product reads as several products.

**Default.** Addressed, they are **you**. Written about, they are **the person**.
The saved record is a **profile** and nothing else is. Never persona, never
user, never member. **Client** exists only on practitioner surfaces, in the
practitioner's own voice.

### 10. What is the release called

**Why it matters.** The act has four names on screen: a release, release and
reframe, letting go, and a run. The unit the tiers are priced in is a pattern,
and nothing in the interface says what a pattern is.

**Unblocks.** The release surface, the games surface, the meter, and all
pricing copy.

**If unanswered.** The priced unit stays undefined in the product that charges
for it.

**Default.** The act is a **release**. The thing spoken is a **line**. One line
over one address through one channel is one **pattern**, which is the priced
unit and is defined on screen the first time it appears. A session is a **run**.
"Release and reframe" comes out as a second name for the first thing.

### 11. What is a paid plan called

**Why it matters.** Tier currently means four things: the coherence band, the
paid plan, the depth of a practice, and a layer of compounding. Band means two
more: the seven seats and the accuracy interval.

**Unblocks.** Every word of pricing and upgrade copy.

**If unanswered.** Pricing copy cannot be written without ambiguity in the noun.

**Default.** A paid plan is a **plan**. Tier belongs to coherence only. Band, in
front of a person, means the accuracy interval only. Seats are **seats**.
Practice depths are **depths**. Compounding layers are **layers**.

### 12. What are the nine called

**Why it matters.** They are called child fetters in the left rail, poled axes
in the same heading, Fetters on the stack tab, charges in the engine, and
"the nine" in body copy. It is the core noun of the instrument.

**Unblocks.** The axis entries, which are 27 strings in tranche one, and the
whole left rail.

**If unanswered.** The most used noun in the product stays the most drifted.

**Default.** An **axis** in the interface, nine of them, each with a held state
and a coherent opposite. **Fetter** is kept for the book and the knowledge base,
where the lineage matters. **Charge** is the quantity on an axis, never the axis
itself.

### 13. Do CQ, DQ and SQ stay as letters in front of a person

**Why it matters.** Three letters carry the whole reading. The letters are
precise and they are also the product asking a person to learn an alphabet
before the first screen means anything. Coherence, shadow weight and segment
depth are already written beside them in tooltips.

**Unblocks.** The key strip, the result page, the summary hero, the record rows.

**If unanswered.** The letters stay and the words stay in hover, which a phone
cannot reach.

**Default.** The word leads and the letter follows once, in brackets, on first
use per surface: coherence (CQ). The letters stay in the record and the export,
where density is the point.

### 14. Is the whole panel a reading, a diagnostic, or a field

**Why it matters.** The product calls itself a diagnostic in the code comments,
an instrument in the brief, a field in the tab title, and a reading in the right
rail. A person needs one word for the thing they are looking at.

**Unblocks.** Section headers across all eight tabs and the help sheet.

**If unanswered.** Four words for the object stay in rotation.

**Default.** The object is **the field**. What the instrument produces from it
is **a reading**. The whole thing is **an instrument**. Diagnostic never appears
in front of a person, because of question 23.

## Group D. Voice and stance

### 15. Does the copy address a person as you

**Why it matters.** It mostly does, and inconsistently. Some surfaces speak to
the person, some describe a profile in the third person, and the reference cases
force the third person because the profile on screen may not be the reader.

**Unblocks.** Person and tense for all 714 strings.

**If unanswered.** Every writer guesses per string, which is how the current
drift happened.

**Default.** Yes. Second person, present tense, everywhere a person is looking
at their own field. Third person only where the profile on screen is explicitly
somebody else, and in that case the surface says whose it is first.

### 16. May the instrument ever say I, or we

**Why it matters.** The release script opens "Welcome to release and reframe.
We will be here for a few minutes." That is a companion speaking. Nothing else
in the product has a first person. Meanwhile the release lines themselves are
first person because the person speaks them: "I am letting go of ..." Two
different I's, and only one of them is legitimate.

**Unblocks.** The release script, the ritual surface, the result page, and the
privacy copy, which currently says we never sell anybody's data.

**If unanswered.** The instrument has two voices and a person cannot tell which
one is talking.

**Default.** The instrument never says I. The only first person in the product
is the line the person speaks. **We** is permitted in exactly one place, the
promise about data, because a promise needs somebody making it. The release
script loses its we and becomes instruction.

### 17. Is the instrument allowed to tell a person what to do

**Why it matters.** `toward` on every label class is an instruction. The
interrupt on all 33 saboteurs is an imperative. That is the most useful copy in
the product and it is also the product taking a position of authority over
somebody's week.

**Unblocks.** The interrupt lines, the direction field, the ritual surface.

**If unanswered.** The imperatives ship as drafted, which is probably right and
unruled.

**Default.** Yes, for a physical action at a named address, in the imperative,
with no timeframe attached. No, for anything about a person's life, work or
relationships. The instrument instructs the body and never the biography.

### 18. How far does the register move between surfaces

**Why it matters.** The same voice has to carry an ad, a result page, an
interrupt line read at two in the morning, and a knowledge base entry. One
register for all four is either too cold for the ad or too warm for the floor.

**Unblocks.** Whether the copy team writes one voice or a named set.

**If unanswered.** The ad and the floor get written in the same register and one
of them is wrong.

**Default.** Three registers, one voice. **Ad**, shortest, present tense,
mechanical, second person. **Instrument**, neutral, states what it measured and
what it does not know. **Floor**, shortest of all, no numbers, no offers, no
count. Nothing in any of the three reaches for wonder, because the mechanism is
the wonder.

### 19. Does the product's own copy ever swear

**Why it matters.** The owner's own voice does, with timing. The in app copy
never does. If the ad is allowed to and the product is not, that is a rule, not
an accident.

**Unblocks.** Ad and social copy.

**If unanswered.** Nothing swears anywhere, which loses a register the owner
actually has.

**Default.** No profanity inside the instrument. Permitted in the long form and
in the owner's own byline, where it is a person speaking and not a machine.

### 20. Is the intake framing sentence the product's opening claim

The sentence in the tree: "Your energetics were fixed at the moment you were cut
from your mother."

**Why it matters.** It is the strongest sentence in the app and it sits over
seven identifying fields, one of which the product cannot use, because the place
lookup resolves nine exact strings. A strong claim over a broken field is the
worst ratio in the product.

**Unblocks.** The intake, the privacy posture, and whether birth data is asked
for at all before the lookup is fixed.

**If unanswered.** The product keeps asking for the most identifying data it
will ever hold, under its biggest claim, and then not using it.

**Default.** The sentence stands and moves to where the data is used. Until the
lookup resolves a real city, the intake asks for date and time only, and says
what each field is for beside it.

## Group E. What may be claimed, and what must be refused

### 21. Is the anatomy a finding or a coordinate

**Why it matters.** "Anxiety sits at your adrenal medulla", stated as a finding,
is the claim that loses the most discerning reader in the roster. The same
anatomy stated as the coordinate system the instrument uses survives him.
`BOOK-ERRATA.md` records the same split inside the book: one line says no
published research does this, another grades the address as measurable.

**Confirm or overrule.** Three documents already recommend coordinate.

**Unblocks.** Every address string, which is 112 labels, and the whole knowledge
base.

**If unanswered.** The product keeps asserting measurements it cannot defend.

**Default.** Coordinate. The product says where it addresses a charge, never
that a nerve was measured. Named plexuses stay, as addresses.

### 22. May psychiatric language appear on a person's own surface

**Why it matters.** Clinical subtitles can be drawn next to a bead on a
person's own wheel. It is the fastest refusal in the clinician segment and it
takes the practitioner segment with it, which is the highest value group in the
panel.

**Unblocks.** The hyper complex entries, and the scope rule for the whole label
model.

**If unanswered.** The product prints diagnoses it is not making.

**Default.** Never on a person's own surface. Clinical composition is kept as
data and scoped to practitioner and developer views, as the label model already
rules for that field.

### 23. What words does the product refuse to say, permanently

**Why it matters.** Seven phrases are priced by how many people each one costs:
the four band names, help, journey, heal and healing, a clinical subtitle,
feminine as a pole name, and "just five minutes a day". A refusal list is
cheaper to enforce than a style guide.

**Unblocks.** A gate. `terms.py` can check a banned list on every build, which
makes the ruling permanent instead of remembered.

**If unanswered.** The words come back the first time somebody writes a headline
in a hurry.

**Default.** Refused everywhere: help, journey, heal, healing, cure, treat,
diagnose, fix, broken, transform, wellness, mindfulness, abundance, alignment,
hold space, unlock, and "just five minutes a day". Refused as a pole name:
feminine and masculine. The pole is left channel and right channel, or
parasympathetic and sympathetic, which is what the printed cards already say.

### 24. What does the product promise about a person's data, in the person's words

**Why it matters.** The strongest ruling in `DECISIONS.md` is that the data is
never sold. Mechanically it is a key, a record not joined to the story, and a
funnel record that exists only to hand back. None of that is written anywhere a
person can read it, and the practitioner will not recommend the product until
she can repeat the consent story out loud.

**Unblocks.** The consent screen, the practitioner page, the sign in seam, and
the footer.

**If unanswered.** The best thing about the product is invisible to the people
who care about it most.

**Default.** Four sentences, plain, in the person's language: what leaves the
device, what never leaves, who can see it, how to remove them. First person
plural, because a promise needs somebody making it. No legal register.

### 25. How does the instrument state what it does not know

**Why it matters.** The interval is the product's honesty and its best answer to
disbelief. It currently appears as a percentage and a band, in places a phone
cannot reach, and "unmeasured" is written three ways.

**Unblocks.** The result page, the analytics copy, the zero state, and the
record.

**If unanswered.** The most trust bearing number in the product is written
differently on each surface.

**Default.** One sentence pattern, used verbatim everywhere: what was measured,
out of what, and how wide the reading is. A move smaller than the interval is
named as noise, not as progress. Never softened to look confident.

## Group F. The result, the ask, and the sentence that travels

### 26. How does the quiz result speak, and in what order

**Why it matters.** For most arrivals the result page is the product. The order
already ruled is the score, then what the three quotients mean, then how it
lands mentally, physically, spiritually and on the soul, then what the app can
do. The panel says that order ships the mechanism and withholds the cost, which
is the one thing ten of seventeen segments came for.

**Unblocks.** The result page, the ad it has to keep faith with, and the share.

**If unanswered.** The result page carries a band name and a percentage, which
is the finding all five review passes flag.

**Default.** Four moves, in this order. What was measured and how wide the
reading is. The number, and the word for it. What the holding costs, in the unit
ruled in question 27. One first move, one address, named. The band word appears
once and carries its entry with it.

### 27. What does the cost line measure, and in what unit

**Why it matters.** Four segments across three panels have asked for a cost line
and the slot is built and empty. The line cannot be written without a unit.
Hours, output, recovery time, money, or a count of addresses carrying.

**Unblocks.** The single largest asked for line in the product, and the highest
willingness to pay in the panel.

**If unanswered.** The result page ships without the thing the paying segments
came for.

**Default.** What the field is spending, in the instrument's own terms: how many
addresses are carrying, how much of the field that is, and what it is dividing
coherence down by. Mechanical and defensible. No hours, no money, no
productivity claim, because the engine cannot defend one.

### 28. Is this the product's sentence

    You are still paying for every charge you never released.

**Confirm or overrule.** Two studies reached it independently. It survives
fourteen of seventeen segments and the three it fails are the three that should
reject it.

**Why it matters.** One sentence sets the ad, the landing page and the share. It
is second person, present tense, mechanical, and contains no adjective about the
person.

**Unblocks.** All acquisition copy.

**If unanswered.** Every headline gets argued from scratch.

**Default.** It ships as the line. Second position, if a question is needed:
"Find out what you are paying to hold it." Under the score: "This is what the
holding costs."

### 29. Rule the six drifting concepts, one word each

`terms.py` reports six concepts written up to five ways each, today: the empty
state, a held address, clearing, the charge value, the person, and measured
against unmeasured.

**Why it matters.** One word per concept is the sharpest rule in the UX file and
the one this codebase keeps breaking. The label model adds roughly 4,900 words,
seven times the current visible prose. Ruling the vocabulary before that tranche
is written is the difference between a gate and a cleanup.

**Unblocks.** Tranche one, and a `terms.py` check that fails the build.

**If unanswered.** The drift multiplies by seven.

**Default.** Empty state: **nothing held**. A held address: **held**, with
running reserved for a saboteur and firing for the moment it fires. Clearing:
**cleared**. The quantity: **charge** on an axis, **weight** only for the summed
load. The person: question 9. Unmeasured: **not measured**.

### 30. At what grain does copy come to him for a ruling

**Why it matters.** The label model is 250 labels and 274 strings. Ruling string
by string is weeks of his time. Ruling nothing means the voice is set by whoever
writes fastest.

**Unblocks.** The writing schedule, and whether tranche one can start this week.

**If unanswered.** Either the writing stalls on approval or it ships unruled.

**Default.** He rules the template and the names. Tier entries are the template
every other class copies, so he signs those seven in full, plus the nine axis
names and the four band names. Everything else is written against the template
and comes back as one list per class, once, for a yes or a strike through.

## What this file deliberately does not ask

The depth button names, `Root_08_Unnamed`, the compressed mid range of the
coherence arithmetic, domain weighting, schema v2, and whether the kink sits at
the highest or the lowest charge. All six are recorded as his elsewhere and none
of them blocks a sentence.

The pattern arithmetic, the channel count and the marker roster are open in
`DECISIONS.md` and they block pricing copy rather than product copy. They are
his to rule on that page, not this one.
