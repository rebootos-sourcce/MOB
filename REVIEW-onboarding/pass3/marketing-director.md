Levels below are the buyer grid levels in `BUYERS.md`. The panel is simulated, so every reaction is a reasoned read, not measured behaviour.

## 1. The proposal as I understand it

One black room: the standing figure, a 26 second silent film of five slides, one decision (twelve starting points), one written sentence, a read back, a 12 line release with his voice, then the Field. The account is asked for only after the reading. The lead's merge kept almost all of mine: no price word, no clock on a decision, one real proof row, Login A first, no "it is okay".

What the merge lost or misread:
- **The seeded read is dishonest as worded.** "The starting point seeds the sentence" means a person who typed nothing useful still sees a lit seat. If the Mirror says "You wrote" over a place they chose, it is a claim with the wrong object. The line must name its source.
- **"Check any row" has no door.** Slide 4 says the tables are open but nothing lets a person open one. That is a claim with no object, the thing I ban.
- **The shareable card (my S, pass 2) fell out.** Trey and Whitney carry the free referral channel and have nothing to post.
- **The Guest gloss fell out.** "Try it on this device. Clearing the browser clears it." was in my table and is not in the spec.
- **A gift counter that falls from 100 toward 88 can read as stock running out**, which is scarcity in a quiet coat.

## 5. My part of the build spec

**Slide copy and dwell** (dwell = max(3.0, 1.0 + words / 2.5), up to 0.5 s). Lead-in 1.0 s of stillness, then:

| # | Dwell | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3.0 s | Welcome to a neurosomatic experience. |
| 2 | 6.5 s | Awareness and intuition is a tool we use to turn your senses inward. (13 words, the ruled line, whitelisted) |
| 3 | 5.5 s | It reads your words. Each points to a place in your body. |
| 4 | 5.5 s | Every table it reads is open. Check any row. |
| 5 | 4.5 s | Discover. Play. Flow. Embody. Then round again. |

Total 25.0 s plus the 1.0 s lead-in is the 26 s ruled. If the lead's cap of 12 words binds slide 2, split it as Sales did (6.5 s becomes 3.0 s plus 3.5 s). Both fit 26 s within half a second.

**Slide 4 proof row.** One real row from the engine, label "An example" in 11 px ink at 70 percent, number at 28 px at 390 and 44 px at 1600, tabular figures, weight 300. The row reads: address name, seat, one table value, read at run time, never typed. A 44 px ring target sits under it, "Open the table". Tap pauses the clock, opens the read-only table view, and Back returns to slide 4 with the clock still paused. If the table view cannot be reached before login, DROP "Check any row" and say "Every table it reads is open inside." Never keep a claim without a door.

**Mirror line at commit, within 6 s, no digits.** Two forms, and the source must show:
- Matched the person's words: "{their word} sits at your {address}. You wrote: {first 12 words}."
- Seeded only: "You chose {starting point}. That sits at your {address}. Add a sentence to read yours."
Never use "You wrote" over a seeded place.

**Empty read.** The lead's line is "Nothing in that matched a pattern. Name how it felt." I would differ: "Nothing matched" is a verdict on her own words, which Marta hears as "it is nothing". My value: "Not quite. Which part sat heaviest?" with the three feeling chips. Give both to the build agent; I recommend mine, and the lead's line is acceptable if the chips sit beneath it.

**Gift counter.** Engine number only, neutral ink role, no accent, never red or amber at any count, updates no faster than twice a second. Say once, as a 4 s caption on the story screen: "100 patterns are open to you. Everything stays visible while you use them." Rising or falling: no "left", no "remaining", no countdown word. Falling from 100 reads as stock, so show it as "N used" beside "of 100" if Art agrees. No padlock draws while `inGift` is true.

**Keep this and Not now.** Two ring controls, equal weight, 48 px. Line above: "Your story is saved to your account. Your name and birth data are not." Print only after Systems confirms it is true. Camille's short form: "The story leaves the device. The name never does." Guest gloss: "Try it on this device. Clearing the browser clears it."

**Banned in the first run (add to the check):** okay, grades, sick, free, trial, limited, left, hurry, "Two minutes", "Nothing to fill in". The two months free stays out.

**Share card (S7).** After the first reading, one quiet ring "Save this card". A 1:1 image of 1080 by 1080 on the `#06060a` stage: the lit seat, the address, the person's first 12 words, one line to run. No username, no birth data, no score. A tiny "Atuned" at 11 px, ink 40 percent. Local only, made from a canvas, nothing sent.

**Funnel gates, local flags only (no network).** Count seconds to first Mirror, the starting point chosen, Skip against Keep this against Not now, and whether the proof row was tapped. A gate fails if the median time to first Mirror passes 90 s, or if the reading is ever empty on a seeded start.

**Differ from a lead ruling:** only the empty-read line (above). Both values given.
