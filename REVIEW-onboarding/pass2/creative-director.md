### Tokens

- **Ground** `--bg`. No new literal. No wash, no blur, no glass.
- **Type** from the skin scale: hero 44 at 1600 and 28 at 390, weight 400, line 1.15, 18em wide. Support 16 in `--mid`. Eyebrow 12, weight 500, `--dim`. Skip and Leave 14. Tile labels 16. Sentence case. Weight 300 is withdrawn: thin white on black shimmers.
- **Colour roles.** Accent only on the current arc and the single live action. Seat hue only on the seat named, one at a time, as a radial gradient at 8 percent, no filter. Ahead arcs at ink 40 percent, which clears 3 to 1.
- **Radii** 10 and 999. **Spacing** 4 px grid. Icons are rings.
- **Motion.** Text in 0.42 s on `cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)` with an 8 px rise. Text out 0.22 s on `cubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1)`, opacity only. Both are on the allowed durations of design gate 12. Stations are spaced by delay, not by long durations. Verbs: still while waiting, travel while charge moves, land (a single settling move, 0.32 s) when something changes. Breath, 4.2 s, starts only when a reading exists. Stillness before the first reading means unread.
- **Flicker rule** (animation's): nothing modulates between 3 and 30 Hz at over 10 percent. Loops no faster than 2.1 s.
- **Controls.** Hold 180 ms pauses. Release resumes after 0.3 s. Left 30 percent goes back, the rest goes on. Arrows step, Space toggles, Esc is Skip. Visible: Skip upper right, a 44 px Pause ring lower left that latches. No Sound ring on the film. One "Hear it" ring on the release opening. The clock holds while any control has focus and while the tab is hidden.
- **Reduced motion.** Same dwell, because timing is reading and not movement. 0.12 s fade, no rise, no draw-on. Pause visible.
- **Distress frame.** The plainest frame in the product. Ink on `--bg`, no ring, no hue, no sound, no counter, nothing moving. Narrative owns the words.
