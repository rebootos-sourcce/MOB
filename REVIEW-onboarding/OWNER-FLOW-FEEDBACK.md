# Owner input, 2 October, round PP: feel and flow in the first minutes

Sent by the owner, verbatim. He pasted it as a review of the auto slider build (the index.html mockup). Its author said they could only read part of the file. Read as his direction; the file extraction caveat means any claim about what the build does should be checked against the build.

Yes. This version is closer.
The important difference is that I would not describe the gap the same way I described the previous build. Something has moved forward. The problem is no longer simply "the intelligence isn't there."
The current problem is that the experience has begun to have the right pieces, but they are not yet flowing into one another.

THE STRAIGHTEST CUT
The previous version felt like: SCRIPT > SCREEN > SCREEN > RELEASE.
This one is beginning to feel like: PERSON > EXPERIENCE > RECOGNITION > RESPONSE.
But it still doesn't quite become: PERSON > FEEL > EXPRESS > ATUNED RECEIVES > ATUNED RESPONDS > PERSON FEELS SEEN > CURIOSITY > RELEASE.
That middle section is the missing thing. It isn't just Feel. It is Flow.

1. The biggest improvement: there is now the beginning of a relationship. The onboarding is starting to behave less like an information sequence and more like an encounter. The earlier build was trying to tell the person "This is what ATUNED does." The new direction is beginning to say "Something is happening here. Let's see what it is." But the experience still feels like a series of designed moments rather than one continuous experience. That is why you are feeling: it's missing feel and flow.

2. FEEL is missing, but the deeper problem is what comes before FEEL. Do not simply insert a screen called "What are you feeling?" The user is still being asked to perform for the system too early. The experience needs to establish: "I don't have to figure this out." That is the emotional contract. ATUNED is going to meet them where they actually are. Not QUESTION > ANSWER > QUESTION > ANSWER. Instead ARRIVE > NOTICE > FEEL > SAY > WATCH > RECOGNIZE. The user shouldn't feel like they're filling out onboarding. They should feel like they're entering an instrument.

3. The missing element is a transition from outer awareness to inner awareness. Right now the experience moves too quickly between conceptual states. We need a moment where the system says, without overexplaining: "Don't solve it yet." Then: "Just notice what's here." Then the Field responds. Then: "What are you feeling?" And only after that: "Tell me what happened." OUTER WORLD > WHAT BROUGHT YOU HERE > INNER EXPERIENCE > FEELING > STORY. That is the missing descent. Without it, Story is still cognitive.

4. The Story shouldn't be the first real act. The first contribution is experience. The story is the person's explanation of the experience. Something feels wrong > I feel pressure > I notice I'm bracing > I remember what happened > I tell the story. Not immediately "Tell me your story." The person doesn't necessarily know the story yet. That's part of what they're here to discover.

5. The Field needs to start responding earlier. This is the biggest visual and experiential opportunity. I want it to begin responding to very small amounts of input. User chooses "Overwhelmed": the Field changes subtly. Not "Here is your Overwhelm visualization." Something starts moving. Then "Take a second." The user feels. Maybe they choose "Heavy": the Field changes again. Then "Where do you notice it?" They indicate "Chest": the Field responds. Now something has happened. The user has not yet told a story. But ATUNED has already begun meeting them. That is flow.

6. The current problem is too much semantic jumping. The experience jumps between CONCERN, FEELING, STORY, BODY, PATTERN, RELEASE, FIELD, which are different levels of abstraction. The experience must bridge those levels. Overwhelmed > "This feels heavy." > "Where?" > "Chest." > "What was happening?" > "I was trying to handle everyone." > ATUNED: "You may be carrying responsibility that feels difficult to put down." Now the transition from feeling to story to pattern makes sense. The system has walked there with the user. It didn't jump there.

7. This is where the Mirror becomes much more powerful. The Mirror is not "ATUNED analyzes your story." The Mirror is: ATUNED shows you what it thinks it heard, and lets you recognize yourself in it. YOU FEEL > YOU SAY > ATUNED REFLECTS > YOU RECOGNIZE > YOU CORRECT > ATUNED ADJUSTS. The emotional payoff is the recognition. The user should have a moment where they think: "Yes. That's what I've been doing." That moment is more important than the sophistication of the underlying algorithm.

8. Don't expose the intelligence too early. We don't want Story Tag, Quality, Intensity, Cause, Body Location, Relationship, Pattern, Uncertainty to appear as a diagnostic report. Those are system objects. The user should experience something much simpler: "I heard this. You seem to be experiencing this. I noticed this. Is that what you mean?" The complexity lives underneath. The Field visualizes it. The user experiences the result.

9. The flow should breathe. The current onboarding still feels like it wants to get somewhere. That creates pressure. The experience needs a rhythm: PROMPT > SPACE > USER > RESPONSE > SPACE > REFLECTION > SPACE > NEXT. Not PROMPT > CLICK > NEXT > CLICK > NEXT. Flow is not more animation. Flow is the absence of friction between one human state and the next.

10. There should be fewer "pages". Stop thinking Screen 1, Screen 2, Screen 3 and start thinking STATE, STATE, STATE. One continuous Field. The Field changes. The typography changes. The question changes. The body changes. The signal changes. But the person doesn't feel like they've navigated to another webpage.

11. The opening needs less explanation and more invitation. Strip the front end down significantly. The user doesn't need to understand 112 addresses yet. They don't need Discover / Play / Flow / Embody yet. They don't need the architecture. They need one thing: "Something brought you here. Let's start there." Then: "What brought you here?" That is enough. Then the Field begins responding. The product can explain itself later because by then the user has experienced it.

12. The correct emotional progression is: ARRIVE > SETTLE > NOTICE > FEEL > EXPRESS > BE SEEN > RECOGNIZE > EXPLORE > RELEASE > NOTICE CHANGE.

13. The first 10 minutes should feel like one experience:
ARRIVE: "Something brought you here. What is it?"
FEEL: "Don't explain it yet. Just notice what's here."
FIELD: Something responds.
FEEL: "What are you feeling?"
BODY: "Where do you notice it?"
STORY: "What was happening?"
MIRROR: "Here's what I heard."
RECOGNITION: "Does that feel like you?"
CORRECTION: "Not quite? Tell me what's off."
REFLECTION: ATUNED adjusts.
PATTERN: "Here's what may be running underneath it."
RELEASE: "I am releasing believing, thinking, feeling, behaving, acting."
NOTICE: "What changed?"
FIELD: The Field remembers.

14. There is another missing ingredient: anticipation. The user becomes curious about what ATUNED is going to notice. I FEEL SOMETHING > I GIVE ATUNED A LITTLE > WHAT WILL IT NOTICE? > ATUNED SHOWS ME > IS IT RIGHT? > YES > WHAT ELSE DOES IT SEE? That is the beginning of the product habit. The user isn't being dragged through onboarding. They're discovering what the instrument can see.

15. The gap now. The current build is VISUAL SYSTEM + INTERACTION SYSTEM + BEGINNING INTELLIGENCE. We are trying to create HUMAN EXPERIENCE > FEELING > EXPRESSION > ATUNED RESPONSE > RECOGNITION > CURIOSITY > DISCOVERY > CHANGE. The missing layer is relational flow: the relationship between the person's inner experience and the system's response. That's what makes ATUNED feel alive.

THE STRAIGHTEST CUT. Do not add more functionality to this build yet. Redesign the first 3 minutes around one question: Can ATUNED make the user feel genuinely met before it asks them to understand anything? If yes, the rest of the architecture starts to work. If no, adding Sniffer sophistication, 100 patterns, 90 day logic, Avatar, or more Field effects will not solve it. The next version should focus almost entirely on ARRIVE > FEEL > RESPOND > EXPRESS > REFLECT > RECOGNIZE. Then let the existing release experience grow naturally out of that.
