# Pass 3 for the onboarding (round PJ)
Read `REVIEW-FRAMEWORK/PASS3.template.md`, then `REVIEW-onboarding/PROPOSAL.md` (the lead's merge of
all twelve pass 2 reports, with rulings), then the pass 2 reports in `REVIEW-onboarding/pass2/`.
Write `REVIEW-onboarding/pass3/<your-seat>.md`, under 1400 words, plain short words, no em
dashes, each term of art explained in the same sentence.

Do these:
1. THE PROPOSAL AS YOU UNDERSTAND IT, in three sentences. Say if the lead's merge lost or
   misread anything your discipline cares about.
2. THE ICP ROOM. Simulate EIGHT people reacting to THIS proposal, one short paragraph each, in
   their own voice: Marcus (founder, level 7), Whitney (phone only, esoteric native, level 5),
   Nils (design skeptic, level 4), Camille (somatic practitioner, level 6), Marta (acute
   distress at 02:00), Renata (operator, level 7, the main target), Trey (quiz tourist),
   Sofia (loves the open tables). For each: what they see in 10 seconds, what they do, where
   they leave or stay, and one line they would say. Judge from your discipline's view.
3. UNIFIED QUALITY out of 100 for the proposal, and the three biggest gaps left.
4. FINAL GRADE `GRADE: NN/100` (pass 1 was NN, pass 2 was NN) and what moved it.
5. YOUR PART OF THE BUILD SPEC: exact values the build agent can implement for your discipline
   (copy lines, timings, sizes, colours as roles, curves, state, gates), consistent with
   PROPOSAL.md. If you must differ from a lead ruling, say so with the evidence and give BOTH values.
6. RANKED RECOMMENDATIONS, each S, M or L, naming the ICPs it moves; mark reskin vs redesign.
7. ONE QUESTION for the owner only if truly blocked, otherwise "none".
Write only your own file.
