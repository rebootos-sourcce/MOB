# The five quotients

Ruled 21 September, twenty second pass, at `TASKS.md` QT1 to QT7 and PT1 to PT2.
His words are kept verbatim wherever they are his.

This is a model of the product and not a feature. One of the five is measured
today. This document says, for each of the other four, how much is already in
the arithmetic under a different name, how much is derivable from what is
stored, and how much needs a field nobody collects. A quotient that needs a
field nobody collects is a design and not a measurement, and it is labelled as
one everywhere it appears.

Everything below was measured against the real roster through the real engine.
The numbers are read off the run at `proto/quotients/index.html` and
`node proto/quotients/measure.js`, never typed into this file by hand except
where a figure is quoted with its date beside it.

---

## 1. What the engine already computes, by name

### CQ, coherence. Measured today, and nothing here changes it.

    compute().CQ            0 to 100, (Intention x Integrity) / Resistance
    compute().tier          the name, off tierOf(CQ) in canon.js
    compute().unread        true when nothing has been entered
    cqCeiling()             what CQ reads once every charge is gone
    cqHeadroom(cqNow)       the gap a release still has in it

CQ is the laws. `Ig` is the mean of the twenty one, `It` is the mean of the
seven band integrities, and resistance is the shadow weight and the gate
multiplier. It has a ceiling a release cannot pass and the ceiling is already
computed.

### IQ, aperture. Not computed, and almost entirely present.

His argument is the right one and the engine proves it. `compute()` already
writes an aperture at every address and calls it something else:

    n.open = clamp(1 - n.sq/10 + n.pole/26, 0, 1.18)

That is one minus the held residue, plus a credit for the coherent opposite
standing at the same address, clamped. It is an opening, per address, and it
has been in the arithmetic since the rebuild. Nothing reads it as a reading.

Beside it, the recognition half is also already built:

    verpScan(text)          counts the six gate cues in a story
    VERPMULT                aware 0.70, ignorance 1.30, as a cost multiplier
    sniffGates(text)        aware against ignorant, and it refuses to compute
                            the sump without both upstream readings
    VERPMIX / gatesLoad     the gate counts, persisted on the record

So IQ needs no new evidence and no new question. It needs an aggregation and a
scale. Section 2 builds it.

### EQ, transmuting. Not named, fully computed.

His words: EQ is transmuting, and it is also WQ, wisdom, because we are
providing the opposite. Providing the opposite is not a thing to build. It is
in the data.

    CHILD[].opp             the coherent opposite of all nine axes, by name.
                            fear to trust, anger to equanimity, shame to worth
    n.rep                   the opposite installed at an address
    n.held                  what is held at the same address
    n.pole                  the opposite minus the held state, floored at nought
    n.jq                    the opposite done past the point where it serves
    compute().poleMean      the installed side, meaned across the ring
    compute().JQ            overshoot, meaned
    compute().excess        the addresses past the point

Every part of EQ exists. What does not exist is a reading that says how much of
what a person carries has turned over, as against how much opposite is standing
in absolute terms. Section 3 builds the ratio and says why it is a ratio.

### AQ, adversity. The fields exist, the readings do not.

His words: AQ is adversity, and it is the release mechanism.

    snapshot(p)             writes cq, dq, sq, pole, jq, loaded, tier on a reading
    p.history               the readings, in order
    seriesRead(p,span)      none, one, or a line, and it already names the three
    p.meter.lines           release lines delivered
    p.meter.unique          the ground opened, which is what a tier buys
    cqHeadroom(cqNow)       what is left to release

Every field AQ needs is on the record and written by the engine. What is
missing is a reading over them, and one structural fact: AQ is the only one of
the five that cannot exist on a first session. That is a definition and not a
gap. Adversity met is a thing you can only have after you have met it.

### PQ, the physical. One quarter present, and the rest has no field.

His words: our nutrition, mindset, the patterns that we hold on to, growth,
everything in the physical that needs to be navigated by the ego. Think about a
boundary.

What the engine holds:

    PUR_EGO                 'With a body attached. Health, fitness, financial
                            stability, wealth, family.' That is his physical
                            list, in the engine, already
    purposeRead(p)          the two centres and what the line between them says
    boundaryCount(p)        commitments drawn, of thirty, and which sides are thin
    boundaryCross(text)     which of the six sides an entry names
    PUR_SIDES               partner, family, friends, community, coworkers, alone

What the engine does not hold, anywhere, in any field:

    nutrition               no field
    sleep                   no field
    movement or training    no field
    growth over time        nothing beyond p.history, which is coherence
    mindset                 the closest is the twenty one laws, and a law is
                            conduct rather than mindset

Four of his five have no field. Section 5 says what the smallest honest version
is and refuses the rest by name.

## 2. The aperture, and it is the one he argued for

> "Fetters narrow awareness. We probably have a more realistic measure of
> awareness, of IQ, based off the fetters a person adds to their system."

### 2.1 What was tested, and what each one did

Five candidate shapes, measured across the fifteen fields the roster loads.

**A, the count of held addresses.** One minus held over reachable, where held is
an address at or above the line at sq 4.

Broken, and broken in the way this repository already has a name for. Marcus
carries ninety nine addresses with his heaviest at 2.51 and reads a perfectly
open aperture. So do Sofia at fifty two and Angela at seventy four. Three of the
six ICPs read fully aware while carrying real load, because an absolute cut at
four on a quantity that is often spread thin says nothing is there. It is the
same defect `compute()` already records at `carrying`.

**B, the spread across seats.** One minus the seats carrying, over seven.

Worse. Eleven of the fifteen carry something in all seven seats, so eleven
profiles read the same number and it separates nobody. The spread is not the
scalar. Where it is narrow is a different question from how open it is, and the
answer to that one is a seat and not a number, so the narrowest seat is drawn
beside the figure instead.

**C, the weight.** One minus the mean sq over ten, across the addresses that can
hold.

Continuous, no cliff, and Marcus gets a real reading at 0.892. But it cannot
tell an empty field from a field with the opposite installed: a blank profile,
Rosa and Lance all read 1.000. Three different fields on one number.

**D, the engine's own opening.** The mean of `n.open` over the ceiling the clamp
allows.

Separates all of them, because `n.open` credits the installed pole. A blank
profile reads 0.847 of the ceiling, Rosa and Lance reach it. Nothing is invented:
this is the quantity `compute()` already writes.

**E, the area of D.** An opening admits in proportion to its area, and area goes
as the square of the radius.

Taken. It spreads the fifteen profiles over a mean rank gap of 0.061 against
0.044 for D, and it does it without clipping anybody. The alternative of
clamping D at one instead of dividing by the ceiling flattened six of the
fifteen against the top.

### 2.2 The shape that shipped

    radius    = mean(n.open across the addresses that can hold) / the clamp ceiling
    area      = radius squared
    factor    = 1 / (aware share x VERPMULT.aware + ignorant share x VERPMULT.ignore)
    aperture  = clamp(area x factor - overshoot/100, 0, 1) x 100

Four things about it, and each is a decision.

**The denominator is measured, not typed.** One address in the ring carries no
child fetter, so no charge can ever land on it and its opening reads a flat one
on every profile alive. Counting it lifts everybody by a fraction of the scale
and puts the ceiling out of reach, which is a silent bias in favour of whoever
is worst off. The address is `Root_08_Unnamed` and it is already on the owner's
open list. When it is named it joins the denominator on its own, because the
module reads the field rather than a number typed in it.

**The clamp ceiling is measured, not typed.** `1.18` is a literal inside
`compute()`. The module runs one maximal field through `compute()` at load and
reads the highest opening back. A change to the clamp moves the reading with it
instead of leaving it stale. Read the count off the run.

**The recognition weights are not chosen.** `VERPMULT` already prices awareness
at 0.70 and ignorance at 1.30 as a cost on everything held. An aperture is the
inverse of a cost, so the factor is one over the gate mix: 1.43 fully aware,
0.77 fully ignorant, 1.00 on an even mix. With no gate cues it returns one and
costs nothing, which is `verpFactor`'s own convention for the same problem and
not a new one. The number does not change meaning when a story arrives. It
gains a term that was inert.

**Overshoot is subtracted, and that is the one term `n.open` does not carry.**
Reason in 2.4.

### 2.4 The failure mode, named rather than avoided

> A measure that says the heaviest person is the least aware is either a true
> finding or a broken scale.

It is both, on two different people, and load alone cannot tell which.

Gordon reads 14.7. He says there is nothing wrong with him and four people left
in a year and each had their reasons. Maximum load, nothing installed, nothing
recognised. Bottom of the scale is a **true finding**.

Tomas reads 15.3. He says he used to drive nine hundred miles and feel nothing
and now cannot get to the end of the street. He sees exactly what changed. He
names it precisely. He cannot move. Six tenths of a point from Gordon is a
**broken scale**.

Load is necessary and it is not sufficient. Nothing else the engine stores
separates them either: both have nothing installed, so the pole is nought for
both, and neither has answered a law differently. The only thing that can tell
them apart is what they write.

Measured, with three gate cues in each of their own voices:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gordon | 14.7 | 14.7 | 3 | 0.77 | 11.3 |
| Tomas | 15.3 | 15.3 | 3 | 1.43 | 21.9 |

Gordon. That is the reason the recognition term is in the arithmetic from the
first commit and not left for later, and it is the reason the page says
**unread** and not **nought** until a story exists.

It is also an argument for the journal being the centre of the product rather
than a feature in it. The aperture's second term is only reachable through what
a person writes.

signatures. Question 1 in section 7.

## 3. EQ, and why it is a ratio

    turn at an address = n.rep / (n.rep + n.held)
    EQ = mean of that across every address where either is present
         minus JQ x 4.2, clamped

**It is a ratio and not a sum, and that is the whole decision.** The installed
side alone is already drawn on the Summary as the pole ring. Reporting it again
under a second name is one quantity in two figures, which this product has a
ruling against and has been bitten by once already, when malignancy turned out
to be coherence doubled and `compute()` had to say so in a comment. The turn is
how much of what sits at an address has turned over.

**It uses `rep` and not `pole`.** `pole` is the opposite minus the held state,
floored at nought, so an opposite that is in but still under water reads as no
transmutation at all. Ana has trust and joy installed at four and three under a
field that swamps both. Reading her turn as nought says she has done nothing,
which is false. The ratio says she has turned part of it and the residue is
still on top, which is what her own sentence says.

**An address with neither is not a nought, it is not part of the question.** A
blank profile has no address with anything on either side, so EQ is unread
rather than nought. Tomas, Nkem and Gordon hold plenty and have installed
nothing, so their EQ is a true nought. The two states are printed differently,
because they say opposite things.

Overshoot is folded at the engine's own weight and drawn as a mark on the
figure as well, because the opposite done past the point where it serves is a
threshold and not a tolerance.

## 4. AQ, and the ruling decides its denominator

> "Every condition is a pattern. It matters whether you believe it or not. It
> can be true, but the belief still creates a condition. Release the condition,
> you can still operate it as if you believe it, because it is true to you. You
> do not need the belief once you know it is true."

A release does not take anything away from a person. It takes the condition off
the thing, and the thing survives.

**So AQ cannot be a count of addresses cleared.** A release does not remove an
address. The address is still in the ring, still named, still where it was, and
after a full release the person can still operate the thing. What comes off is
the condition, and the engine's word for the condition's weight is shadow
weight. If the arithmetic counted addresses it would be saying the thing had
gone, which contradicts the ruling, and the arithmetic would be wrong.

    met  = the highest shadow weight ever recorded on this record, and now
    AQ   = clamp((met - now) / met, 0, 1) x 100

Three states, and they are the ones `seriesRead` already names rather than a
new set:

    none    no reading on the record. work is a distance and there is no
            second point to measure it from. unread.
    one     one reading. still unread, and the page says which.
    line    two or more. AQ reads.

And a fourth case that is not a state of the record but a fact about the
person: **nothing carried is not nought.** Rosa has carried nothing inside the
instrument, so there is nothing that could have been released, and AQ reads not
applicable. Whatever she released she released before she arrived and this
instrument cannot see it. That is a real limit and it is printed rather than
papered over.

**And AQ is the quotient that makes release worth running.** `CLAUDE.md`
already records the ceiling problem: three of the six ICPs can run every
release the product will ever offer them and not move the number they were
shown. Measured on Ana, with charge coming off her axes and `snapshot()`
writing each reading:

| reading | coherence | tier | ceiling | headroom | aperture | turn | release | shadow weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| first | 7.6 | Collapsed | 17.9 | 10.3 | 28.3 | 10.8 | unread | 22.8 |
| after run 1 | 11.8 | Severe | 17.9 | 6.1 | 40.0 | 12.4 | 66.0 | 7.8 |
| after run 2 | 16.5 | Severe | 17.9 | 1.4 | 60.3 | 17.8 | 98.1 | 0.4 |

Coherence moves nine points and she is still near the bottom of the ladder. The
aperture moves thirty two. The release reading says she has spent ninety eight
of the hundred that was ever available to her. That is the credit the
instrument was not giving her, and it is exactly the thing a person who has
done the work needs to see.

`p.meter.lines` is reported beside AQ as the count of release lines delivered.
It is the mechanism and not the reading, and it is never folded in.

## 5. PQ, and what this product will not invent

His five: nutrition, mindset, the patterns that we hold on to, growth,
everything in the physical that needs to be navigated by the ego, and think
about a boundary.

**Four of the five have no field, and they should not be given one by this
document.** Nutrition, sleep, movement and growth are self reported daily
quantities. A product with no backend, no accounts today and a ruling that
storage is the person's own browser cannot collect them honestly, and a number
built out of three tick boxes about food is worse than no number. The product
already carries the rule: a clamped 9999 reads as a ten a person never entered.

**The fifth is already in the engine, twice over.** `PUR_EGO` in `avatar.js`
defines the earthly purpose as health, fitness, financial stability, wealth and
family, which is his physical list almost word for word, and the hexagon under
it is the boundary: six sides, five commitments each, thirty in all, inside
yours to protect and outside choice.

**The smallest honest version of PQ is the boundary, and it is stated.**

    PQ = boundaryCount(p.purpose).filled / 30 x 100

That is coverage of what a person typed. It is not a measurement and it is
labelled **stated** everywhere it appears, for the same reason the cadence is
labelled stated on the ladder prototype. `boundaryCross` is the measured half
and it is thin: it reads which of the six sides an entry names, so a story can
say which side the charge came through. It is drawn as a notch on that side of
the rim and never folded into the figure, because two entries about a boss is
not a reading of a boundary.

**What would make PQ a measurement.** Three fields, and all three are a schema
change and therefore his:

    p.physical.sleep        hours, nightly, one number
    p.physical.move         minutes, daily, one number
    p.physical.fuel         a three state day mark, not a food log

Even with those, PQ would be a coverage and adherence reading rather than a
somatic one, and it would need its own ruling on what a missed day means. Until
then PQ is a design, and the page says so in the word beside the figure.

## 6. Where they are drawn

> "That is good for the marketing team and the rest of the development team,
> because that sounds like it needs to be in the summary and measuring."

### 6.1 Not five gauges, and the reason is a count

The Summary already carries a glance row of six rings: coherence, shadow
weight, carried depth, pole, energy and identification, at
`ui/summary.js:sumGlance`. Adding five quotient gauges makes eleven figures on
one column, and two of the five are already in that row: **coherence** is the
first ring and **pole** is EQ's raw material. Coherence is also already drawn
as the core of the Field wheel. So five gauges would put one quantity in three
drawings on two surfaces.

A radar chart over five axes is the other obvious answer and it is worse. A
radar's area is a function of the axis order, so reordering the five changes
the shape of the reading. This product has a rule about exactly that: identity
is one thing and display order is another, and a figure whose value moves when
the display order moves has confused them.

### 6.2 One drawing: the aperture

One iris. Five quantities, and each is a different physical property of the
same object, so nothing is drawn twice.

    the rim         PQ, boundary. six arcs, one per side. the drawn part is
                    solid and the undrawn part is faint, so a boundary nobody
                    typed reads as an outline and not as a wall. a crossing is
                    a notch pointing in, on the side it came through. thinner
                    stroke than anything measured, because it is stated.

    the opening     IQ, aperture. the clear area in the middle, and it is AREA
                    true: the clear annulus area is the reading. a figure whose
                    area does not equal its own number is a figure that lies.

    the blades      EQ, turn. nine, one per axis, coloured by seat out of PAL.
                    they sit in the load ring and span it exactly. radial when
                    nothing has turned, swung to tangential when the opposite
                    is fully in. a blade past the point where the opposite
                    serves carries a ring at its tip.

    the sweep       AQ, release. a distance travelled around the track between
                    the load ring and the boundary, from the top, clockwise.
                    a distance, drawn as a distance.

    the centre      CQ, coherence. the number and the tier word. NOT a ring:
                    coherence already owns a drawing on the Field and drawing
                    it again here is the thing the rule forbids.

Seen side by side the figure does the work without the numbers. Gordon's iris
is shut with nine radial blades. Rosa's is fully open with nine tangential
stubs at the rim. Lance's is wide open with a ring on every blade tip.

### 6.3 What it replaces

It replaces the glance row rather than joining it. The six rings are what the
five quotients are built from, so nothing is lost:

    coherence       becomes the centre of the iris
    pole            becomes the blades, as a ratio rather than a bare mean
    carried depth   becomes the opening
    shadow weight   becomes the sweep, as work done against it
    energy          stays, as a line under the figure, or comes off
    identification  stays. it is how much of the person has been measured and
                    it is not a quotient

That also answers a defect already filed against that row. `ui/summary.js:82`
records that the shadow weight ring clamps at ten so every profile past it
draws an identical full ring, and says replacing the figure with the two facts
it sums is the right answer and is the owner's call. This is that replacement.

### 6.4 What each one reads when nothing has been entered

The product already rules that both surfaces printing a reading silence
themselves on `r.unread` and show the four doors. The figure follows it.

    the rim         faint all the way round. nothing drawn.
    the opening     not drawn at all. not open, not shut. the load ring is a
                    dashed outline and the space inside it says nothing yet.
    the blades      nine, flat, dim.
    the sweep       absent.
    the centre      an en dash and the word unread, which is what sumUnread
                    already prints.

And each of the five reads its own state rather than a shared one:

    CQ    unread. the arithmetic works and is not shown, because a number off
          a default is a number about the default.
    IQ    unread. and note that a blank field computes to 71.8, which is ABOVE
          most real readings, so a person's first entry moves the aperture
          DOWN. That is honest under his own model, fetters narrow awareness
          and an empty field has none, and it is the one place on this figure
          where a person may feel penalised for being honest. Question 5.
    EQ    unread, and not nought. no address has either side, so there is
          nothing to turn and nothing turned.
    AQ    unread, with the state named: none, one, or nothing carried.
    PQ    nought commitments drawn, and the word stated beside it.

## 7. What is his

Each carries the four part snapshot, in the shape `TASKS.md` already uses.

### Q1. Should the aperture close when the cure is overdone?

> **What it is.** `n.open` credits the installed opposite and clamps, with no
> term for overdoing it. The aperture reading subtracts overshoot at the weight
> `compute()` already charges integrity, because without it the opposite at
> nine and the opposite at ten both read 100.0, and Rosa and Lance both read
> 100.0 on real fields.
> **At stake.** Whether the term belongs in `compute()` or only in the reading
> on top of it. If it belongs in `compute()`, `n.open` changes and every
> surface reading it changes with it, which is an arithmetic core change.
> **Either way.** Left where it is, the aperture and `n.open` disagree by the
> overshoot on any profile carrying it, and the disagreement is documented.
> Moved into `compute()`, the pileup closes everywhere at once and the port
> rule is broken for one line.
> **Look at.** `atuned_src/engine/compute.js` at `n.open`, and the rep sweep in
> section 2.5.

### Q2. Is WQ a sixth name or EQ's second face?

> **What it is.** His words are that EQ is transmuting, and it is also WQ,
> wisdom, because we are providing the opposite. That reads as one quantity
> with two names, and this product's first rule is that a concept has one name.
> **At stake.** Whether the model is five or six, and whether the word a person
> sees on the figure is turn, or wisdom, or both.
> **Either way.** One name, and WQ is a line of copy about what the turn is
> for. Two names, and WQ needs its own arithmetic that is not the turn
> restated, and there is nothing in the engine today that would be it.
> **Look at.** `TASKS.md` QT4, and section 3 here.

### Q3. Does IQ take the `iq` prefix in the engine?

> **What it is.** `iqScore`, `iqList`, `iqApply` and `IQ_STEM` already exist in
> `engine/intake.js` and they mean intake question, not intelligence quotient.

### Q4. Does the quotient figure replace the glance row, or sit beside it?

### Q5. Is a first entry allowed to move the aperture down?

## 8. Schema

Nothing here needs a schema change. Every quotient reads fields that already
exist: `p.axes`, `p.laws`, `p.gates.verp`, `p.history`, `p.meter` and
`p.purpose`. A profile written by an older build reads exactly as it does
today, because the four new readings are functions over the record rather than
fields on it.

The one change that would need a version is `p.physical` for PQ, and it is
additive: a record without it reads PQ as stated boundary coverage, which is
what every record reads today. Schema v2 is the owner's call and this does not
force it.

**What is derived and must never be stored.** All five. Every one of them is a
function of the record, and a stored quotient is a value that drifts when the
model moves and then disagrees with the app about what a person is. The record
keeps `p.history` snapshots, which already store `cq`, `dq`, `pole` and `jq`,
and those four are enough to recompute every quotient at any past reading.
