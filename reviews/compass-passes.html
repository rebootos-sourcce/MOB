A reversal, said out loud. Earlier this evening the ruling was that the compass is a feature and should not take the dominant space, and the build was made smaller to match. Tonight the ruling is that the compass is the centrepiece and should be the entire centre area. These passes work to the later ruling. What it costs is stated in section 5a of reviews/AD-field-compass.md, and there are five costs, one of which is new architecture.
Lighting

Each pass carries everything before it and changes one thing. The change is named, the person who found it is named, and the measurement under each figure is taken by the page itself while it draws, not typed in. Overlapping labels are counted by comparing every drawn text box against every other one. Figure fill is the area the drawn figure covers against the area of the box it is given.

What these counters do and do not measure. They measure the figure on this page, at this card's size. The shipped compass was measured separately, in a real Chromium against source.html: its canvas is 528 by 430 pixels inside a 920 by 903 pixel centre, it draws 23 labels, and 89 pairs of them overlap at 1600 by 1000 and 98 pairs at 390 by 844. That is the number pass 3 is aimed at. A counter that reported on itself and called it the product would be the third probe this week to lie, so it says which figure it read.
