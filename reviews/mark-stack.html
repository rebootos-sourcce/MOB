The stack, drawn

Three directions for the Atuned mark, built out of the structure the engine already computes. Every path is inline, every number is checkable against a file and a line, and every direction names what it gives up. Mika Ueda-Salas, with Sol Amadi on colour and light, Bjorn Haraldsson on type and grid, Petra Nikau on composition and symbol.

The stack is really interesting because the fetters are, all of it is divine geometry. The fetters are the most detailed, and then the very top is binary, right? Benign, malignant. And they all stack and form a shape. So then we also have seven chakra colours. So we have two elements that are very unique to us. And then we also have our soul shape, that kind of squiggly cue.

The previous proposal is withdrawn. It was a gold nimbus argued from phi, and it carried neither of the two things he has now named as ours. Nothing from it is reused.

2. Where His Description And The Engine Disagree

2.1 The Fetters Are Not The Most Detailed Layer. They Are The Only Layer A Person Writes To.

There are 112 addresses under the nine fetters, atuned_src/engine/data/nodes.js:5, each with a seat, a named plexus or nerve, an axis, a distortion and a charge label. Nine is not more detailed than 112.

What is true, and is I think what he means: the fetters are the only rung a person moves. Nine held values and nine opposites, eighteen continuous inputs at 0 to 10 in half steps. Everything above the fetters is derived and read only. Everything below the fetters is derived too: n.sq, n.held, n.rep and n.pole are all computed at compute.js:51 to 57. So the detail gradient he is describing is a gradient of degrees of freedom: eighteen at the fetters, zero everywhere else, one bit at the top. That is a sharper statement than his and it is the one the mark should draw. Found by Mika.

2.2 The Binary At The Top Is Not An Axis. It Is CQ Restated.

compute.js:180, exactly:

const benign=CQ>=50,malig=benign?0:Math.round((50-CQ)/50*100);

Orientation is a threshold on coherence, so it carries no information that CQ does not already carry. The comment above it says so in the file, and records that it used to pretend to be a second axis and was caught. The two real independent axes the engine now computes are Shape, outwardShare, whether the conditioning points at other people or at the person carrying it, and Control, organisedShare, whether it is organised or chaotic. They cross into four governance quadrants at compute.js:204.

So the top of the stack is either one bit that restates the number below it, or two bits that do not. All three directions draw the one bit he named, because that is what he named and it is what the rail prints under the heading Orientation at shell/body.html:256. Each direction can take a second state instead if he rules it, and I have marked where, because a mark whose top rung says nothing new is the weakest rung in it. Found by Mika. This needs his ruling before anything ships.

2.3 Malignant Is Not An Alarm

Malignant is a reading of a field, not a fault in the instrument. #FF2E1F is reserved for something being wrong, and this has been a real defect twice. In all three directions the orientation rung is drawn in currentColor and its two states are a presence and an absence: a closed ring against a broken one, a lit cell against a dim one, a filled disc against a hollow one. No red, at any size, on any ground. Held by Sol.

4. Divine Geometry, Meant Properly

Four constructions were candidates. Two are in and two are out, and the reason is a count in the engine each time.

seed of life
in, direction 1. Seven circles, which is seven seats. Twelve vesica lenses, which is twelve hyper families. Constants sqrt 3 and 2, stated as such.
golden spiral
in, direction 3. Whirling squares in a golden rectangle. Seven quarter arcs on Fibonacci radii, each ratio phi, terminating on a point. The only construction that is a sequence and a terminus at once.
Fibonacci ladder
in, direction 2. The same golden rectangle read as a stack of registers rather than as a curve. Six widths, six consecutive Fibonacci terms.
Metatron's cube
out. Thirteen circles and seventy eight connecting lines. Thirteen matches nothing in the engine. Twelve does, which is the hyper families, and forcing a thirteen circle figure to say twelve is exactly the gesture the brief warns against. Seventy eight hairlines at 18px is a grey disc.
Platonic solids
out. There are five and the rendered rung strip has five tabs, which is a tempting coincidence and nothing more. Nothing in the engine makes a saboteur an octahedron rather than a cube. A mapping with no rule behind it is decoration wearing a mathematician's name.
flower of life
out, and it is the near miss. Nineteen circles, and the engine has nineteen blueprint domains at canon.js with DARC = 360 / 19 already computed against them. That is a real bijection and it belongs to the field surface, not to the mark: nineteen circles will not survive 18px and the blueprint is not part of the stack he described. I am naming it because it is the one I would build next if he wants a second mark for the Field page.

Constructions chosen by Petra, argued against the counts by Mika.

5. The Three Directions

Not variations. A rosette, a ladder and a spiral: one figure built on circles, one on rectangles, one on a curve. Each carries the seven seat colours and the stack, each says how, and each says what it costs.

Direction 1. The Seed

Seed of life, vesica piscis, root three

Seven circles of equal radius, one at the centre and six around it at one radius out. That is the seed of life, and it is the only construction in the canon that produces seven of a thing without choosing to. The seven circles are the seven seats. The centre is the Heart.

320px, 64px, then the 18px text cut twice: benign above, malignant below.

Why This Construction

Why this one and not another. The seed is a root three figure, not a phi figure: its constants are sqrt 3 for the vesica and 2 for the extent. Saying phi over it would be the decoration this product does not ship. What it gives instead is a count. A seed of life has exactly twelve vesica lenses, six pointing at the seat circles and six on the bisectors between them, and the engine has exactly twelve hyper families, six architectures and their six overshoot poles. That is not a resemblance, it is a bijection.

What Direction 1 Forfeits

  • It draws the bottom and the top of the stack and skips the middle. Fetters, hyper held, hyper overshoot, orientation, soul: five readings. Saboteurs and complexes have no construction feature to sit on, because 33 and n are not seed of life numbers. Two of the seven rungs are absent and I will not invent a place for them.
  • The text cut loses orientation. At 18px the boundary ring at radius 68 in a 136 crop is 9.0px across with a 0.24px stroke, so it goes, and with it the only mark that carries benign against malignant. At 18px this direction is seats and soul, not the stack.
  • Six colours at 18px, not seven, and you cannot name them. Each petal is 58.89 by 17 units, which at 18px is 7.8 by 2.2px. That is a coloured leaf, above the one pixel floor, and it reads as a chromatic rosette rather than as six countable seats. The Heart is the centre circle and survives as a ring.
  • It is the most ordinary of the three. A rosette of overlapping circles is the most reproduced sacred geometry figure there is, and half the wellness market has one. The twelve lens bijection is what makes it ours, and nobody reads a bijection at a glance.

Direction 2. The Cairn

Fibonacci ladder, 144 89 55 34 21 13

Six registers, stacked, each narrower than the one below it, each divided into as many cells as that rung has kinds. The bottom register is the nine fetters in their seat colours, the top register is the binary split in two. The silhouette is one stepped shape and the shape is the stack.

320px, 64px, then the 18px text cut twice: benign above, malignant below.

Why This Construction

Why phi here. Six registers on a phi ladder from 144 come out at 144, 88.997, 55.003, 33.994, 21.009, 12.984. Those are six consecutive Fibonacci numbers to within three hundredths of a unit, so the ladder is 144 89 55 34 21 13 with no rounding argument to make. The register heights are 13 and the gaps are 8, both Fibonacci, so the vertical rhythm is the same series as the horizontal taper. This is the golden rectangle read as a ladder rather than as a spiral, which is the other thing the whirling squares construction is for.

Direction 3. The Collapse

Golden spiral in whirling squares

A golden spiral built from whirling squares, seven quarter arcs, radii on the Fibonacci series, running from the widest arc on the outside to a point on the inside. The seven arcs take the seven seat colours in root to crown order and the spiral terminates on the soul squiggle. The collapse sequence as one mark, outside in, which is what the codex already calls the Verity Symbol.

320px, 64px, then the 18px text cut twice: benign above, malignant below.

Why This Construction

Why the spiral and not a ring. The book states the mechanism in one line: the soul is a collapsed wave, a point, the squiggle at the centre of the Verity Symbol, index.html:7229. A spiral is the only construction that is a sequence and a terminus at the same time. Seven arcs, seven radii, each ratio phi, and the innermost arc hands off to the squiggle. The seat ladder and the rung ladder are co plotted because both have seven terms, and I am saying plainly that the co plotting is my decision and not an engine fact: the engine does not seat the rungs.

What Direction 3 Forfeits

  • It is not square. The box is 160 by 100, because a golden spiral lives in a golden rectangle and cropping it to a square would break the construction. At 18px tall it is 29px wide. That is fine beside a wordmark in a top bar and it is unusable as an app icon, an avatar or a favicon without a second lockup, which is a second mark to maintain.
  • The small cut keeps all seven pigments and loses all seven names. Seven discrete arcs at 18px would put the Crown arc at 0.87px of arc length, so the text cut runs one stroke with a seven stop gradient. You see that every seat is present. You cannot tell which is which, and nobody can count them.
  • No symmetry, on either axis. It will never read as a seal, a badge or an institutional mark, and if he ever wants one stamped on a certificate or a practitioner credential this is the direction that cannot do it.
  • The rung to seat co plotting is an assertion. Seven seats and seven spiral stops is a true coincidence of counts and not a claim the engine makes. If he does not accept the pairing, the mark still works as seven seats and the stack reading comes out of it.

10. My Recommendation

Direction 3, The Collapse. It is the only one of the three that carries all seven rungs, all seven seat colours and his own soul mark in one figure, and it is the only one whose construction is also the product's own stated mechanism: the collapse sequence, outside in, ending on a point. The two things he says are unique to us are both load bearing in it rather than applied to it.

The price is that it is 160 by 100 and will never be an app icon. That is a real cost and it is the cheapest of the three to pay, because the fix is a companion lockup rather than a compromise in the mark, and direction 1 is that lockup. The seed is square, it is built from the same seven seats and the same squiggle, and at 64px and above the two read as one family. Ship 3 in the bar and 1 on the icon, and the two are not two marks, they are one mark and its seal.

Direction 2 is the one I would not ship and would not throw away. The Fibonacci ladder on 144 89 55 34 21 13 is the truest drawing of the stack in this document, and it belongs on the Summary surface as the reading itself, live, with the real counts in it. As a mark it collides with a signal meter and it drops the soul. As a data figure it is the best thing here. Bjorn dissents on the recommendation and prefers 2, on the grounds that a mark whose proportions are the data is worth more than a mark that needs a second lockup. His argument is recorded rather than resolved, because the icon problem is his to lose and the ladder problem is mine.

11. What Needs A Ruling

  1. Squiggle or squiggly Q. If the mark he means is the circle with the line cutting through it and the two pins, none of the three is drawn from the right shape and all three change. This is the first question and everything else waits on it.
  2. The top rung. One bit that restates CQ, or two bits that do not. Shape and Control are computed and independent and the rail draws neither.
  3. Direction, and whether there is a second lockup. 3 in the bar with 1 as the seal is my recommendation and it is two files to maintain instead of one.
