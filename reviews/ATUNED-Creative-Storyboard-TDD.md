# 3. THE BRAND IDEA

## ATUNED

ATUNED is an instrument for seeing how you operate.

It listens to the way a person describes their experience.

It looks for patterns.

It shows the user what it sees.

> ATUNED diagnoses neurological conditions.

> ATUNED predicts disease.

> Our hypothesis is...

## BEAT 20 — RELEASE

### Canonical copy

> Release the charge.

Then:

> I am releasing believing, thinking, feeling, behaving, acting.

### Visual

The pattern structure loosens.

Connections separate.

The node becomes less dense.

The Field settles.

### Tonal model

Within the ATUNED model, release is tonal.

The words used in the release are themselves tonal. They are not only semantic instructions. They are intended to alter the quality of the signal being attended to at the narrative site.

The release therefore functions as a disruption of the existing flow.

```text
PATTERN
↓
TONAL IMPRINT
↓
RELEASE
↓
FLOW DISRUPTED
↓
SPACE FOR REORGANIZATION
```

The release does not erase the story.

It disrupts the existing relationship to the story.

### Reframe follows release

Release creates the opening.

Reframe recharges the narrative at the site with a different organizing statement.

```text
RELEASE
↓
DISRUPT THE EXISTING FLOW
↓
REFRAME
↓
RECHARGE THE NARRATIVE
↓
NEW RELATIONSHIP
↓
PRACTICE / EMBODY
```

The reframe is therefore not simply a positive affirmation.

Within the ATUNED model, it is a tonal and narrative replacement for the pattern that was just disrupted.

Canonical reframe language:

> I know that I am...

or:

> I know...

Example:

> I know that I can say what I mean without abandoning myself.

The exact reframe must come from the confirmed pattern, the user's language, and the intended new relationship. It must not be generic positivity.

### Funnel implication

The public experience should eventually make this distinction visible:

> **Release disrupts the old flow.**
>
> **Reframe gives the narrative a new direction.**

This should be demonstrated visually rather than taught as a technical lecture.

### Visual sequence

### Requirement

No claim of guaranteed immediate relief.

The tonal mechanism is an ATUNED model and should be presented as such unless independently validated.

# 19. CREATIVE FAILURE CONDITIONS

The creative fails if:

1. The visitor thinks ATUNED is primarily a chatbot.
2. The visitor thinks ATUNED is primarily a quiz.
3. The visitor thinks ATUNED diagnoses them.
4. The visitor cannot explain what happens after entering a story.
5. The visitor does not understand that they can correct ATUNED.
6. The visitor believes the system's interpretation is automatically true.
7. The visitor has to understand the entire system before trying it.
8. The visitor has to create an account before meaningful value.
9. The visitor must manually export or import a record.
10. The visitor encounters a paywall before experiencing meaningful value.
11. The visitor sees technical architecture before understanding the human benefit.
12. The visitor encounters excessive jargon.
13. The visitor believes a lack of immediate sensation means failure.
14. The visitor interprets body mapping as medical diagnosis.
15. The site feels like a brochure rather than an instrument.
16. The site explains more than it demonstrates.
17. The user is labeled rather than invited to investigate.

# 28. TONAL RELEASE AND REFRAME MODEL

## Purpose

This model defines the relationship between language, tonal imprint, release, reframe, and subsequent practice.

It is important because the ATUNED experience should not stop at:

```text
PATTERN
↓
RELEASE
↓
VERIFY
```

When the user chooses to continue, the intended sequence is:

```text
PATTERN
↓
RELEASE
↓
DISRUPTION
↓
REFRAME
↓
RECHARGE
↓
PRACTICE
↓
EMBODIMENT
```

## Core distinction

### RELEASE

Release disrupts the existing flow.

The language of release is intended to interrupt the existing relationship between the user and the narrative pattern.

Within the ATUNED model:

> Release is tonal.

The release words themselves are part of the intervention.

They are intended to change the tonal quality of the narrative being attended to.

### REFRAME

Reframe gives the narrative a new organizing direction.

Within the ATUNED model:

> Reframe recharges the energy of the narrative at that site.

The reframe should not merely contradict the old statement with generic positivity.

It should establish a new relationship that is relevant to the confirmed pattern.

## Four-carrier model

The current ATUNED model can treat the following as different carriers of the same underlying intervention:

```text
WORD
TONE
ICON
COMMAND
```

They may differ in presentation while serving the same functional sequence:

```text
ATTENTION
↓
ADDRESS
↓
REGISTER
↓
INTERVENTION
```

This is a model-level construct and should not be presented as an established neurological mechanism without supporting evidence.

## Release and reframe are not interchangeable

Release:

> I am releasing believing, thinking, feeling, behaving, acting...

Reframe:

> I know that I am...

or:

> I know...

Release removes or disrupts the old organizing relationship.

Reframe establishes the new organizing relationship.

## Narrative energy sequence

```text
OLD NARRATIVE
↓
TONAL IMPRINT
↓
RESISTANCE / REPETITION
↓
RELEASE
↓
DISRUPTION
↓
OPENING
↓
REFRAME
↓
NEW TONAL IMPRINT
↓
NEW PRACTICE
↓
NEW EVIDENCE
```

The system should not claim that a reframe automatically creates behavioral change.

The reframe becomes meaningful when it is carried into practice and produces observable evidence.

## Funnel communication

The first-time visitor does not need the full theory.

The funnel can express the distinction through animation:

1. A repeated pattern forms.
2. Its signal becomes dense.
3. Release interrupts the flow.
4. The field opens.
5. A new statement enters.
6. The new statement establishes a different path.
7. The user is invited to practice it.
8. The next observation determines whether anything actually changed.

Suggested public language:

> **Release disrupts the old pattern.**
>
> **Reframe gives you something new to practice.**

## Creative requirement

Do not present the reframe as:

> Think positively.

Do not present it as:

> Replace a negative thought with a positive thought.

Present it as:

> A new statement that reflects the relationship you are choosing to practice.

↓

> Within the ATUNED model, the reframe recharges the narrative at that site.

The tonal mechanism remains a product model or hypothesis unless independently validated.

# 48. FAQ: ANSWER THE QUESTIONS BEFORE THEY ARE ASKED

The FAQ is now part of the funnel creative system.

Its purpose is not to explain everything.

Its purpose is to remove the specific questions that prevent trust, first use, second use, and sharing.

The FAQ should appear after the visitor has enough context to understand the questions, and it should remain accessible from the main funnel navigation.

## FAQ 01 — What is ATUNED actually doing?

> ATUNED listens to the way you describe what is happening in your life. It looks for patterns in your words and experience, shows you what it notices, and lets you decide whether it is right.
>
> If you confirm it, ATUNED helps you work with the pattern and notice what changes.
>
> It is an instrument for self-observation, not an authority on who you are.

## FAQ 02 — How does ATUNED know where to look?

> ATUNED starts with what you actually say and report.
>
> It looks at the words, feelings, behaviors, context, relationships, and other signals in your story. It may connect those signals to a body or Field address within the ATUNED model.
>
> That address is a way of organizing the experience. It is not a medical diagnosis.

## FAQ 03 — How do I know the reading is accurate?

> You don't have to take it on faith.
>
> ATUNED shows you what it noticed and asks whether it got you right.
>
> You can say that's it, not quite, or correct it.
>
> Your correction changes the record. You remain the authority on your own experience.

## FAQ 04 — What happens if ATUNED gets me wrong?

> Tell it.
>
> That's part of the design.
>
> ATUNED treats your correction as new evidence instead of forcing the original interpretation to fit.
>
> A wrong reading is not a failure of the relationship. It is information about what the system still needs to understand.

## FAQ 05 — What is the difference between release and reframe?

> Release works with the old pattern.
>
> Reframe gives you a new direction.
>
> In simple terms: release disrupts the old flow. Reframe helps establish a different relationship with what you found.
>
> ATUNED does not ask you to replace a difficult experience with a positive thought. The reframe should be connected to what you actually discovered and what you want to choose next.

## FAQ 06 — What should I expect to feel?

> There is no required feeling.
>
> You might notice relief, a different perspective, movement, more space, less pressure, or nothing obvious at first.
>
> If your mind is very active, subtle changes can be difficult to notice. You do not have to force a sensation.
>
> No sensation is also information. ATUNED records what you actually notice rather than telling you what you should have felt.

## FAQ 07 — Why would I use ATUNED again after the first session?

> Because the first session is only one piece of the picture.
>
> You can work with another pattern, return to something you've already explored, test what changed, or see whether separate patterns are connected.
>
> Over time, ATUNED can use your confirmed history to make the next experience more specific to you.

## FAQ 08 — Does ATUNED remember what I've already worked on?

> That is one of the important parts of the system.
>
> ATUNED can retain supported evidence about your stories, confirmed patterns, releases, practices, observations, and outcomes so future sessions do not have to begin from zero.
>
> The system should remember what has evidence behind it, not turn every interpretation into a permanent fact.

## FAQ 09 — Is ATUNED therapy, coaching, meditation, or a personality test?

> It is not one of those things exactly.
>
> ATUNED is an instrument for self-observation and practice.
>
> It can include reflection, body awareness, release exercises, behavioral practice, and questions. It does not replace a clinician, therapist, or medical professional.
>
> It also does not decide who you are from a test score.

## FAQ 10 — What happens to my information?

> ATUNED is designed around user control and evidence-based memory.
>
> Your experience should remain yours. The system should be able to show what information it used, where an interpretation came from, and what was confirmed versus inferred.
>
> Privacy, persistence, deletion, export, and account behavior are product requirements and must be implemented and verified rather than implied by marketing language.

## FAQ editorial rule

If a FAQ answer cannot be demonstrated by the product or supported by the current architecture, mark the statement for validation instead of turning it into a stronger claim.

# 49. REAL-WORLD VALIDATION PLAN

The previous focus-group exercise was synthetic.

The first objective is to publish the FAQ, observe where people still hesitate, and collect actual language from users.

After the user experiences ATUNED, ask:

1. Did the reading identify something you actually recognize?
2. Did you feel able to correct ATUNED when it was wrong?

8. Would you want ATUNED to remember your previous work? Why?

# 71. FAQ HANDSHAKE

The FAQ is not filler copy.

It is a research instrument.

The FAQ must answer the questions that the simulated audience is most likely to have before trust, repeat use, sharing, and payment.

## Question 1
### What is ATUNED actually doing?

Required answer:

> ATUNED listens to the way you describe what is happening in your life. It looks for patterns in your words and experience, shows you what it notices, and lets you decide whether it got it right. From there, you can work with the pattern and observe what changes.

## Question 2
### How does ATUNED know where a pattern sits in my body?

Required answer:

> ATUNED uses a model of body addresses and somatic associations. Your words and reported experience help select where the system looks. These addresses are part of the ATUNED model. They are not a medical diagnosis or a claim that ATUNED has measured a neurological condition at that location.

## Question 3
### How do I know the reading is accurate?

Required answer:

> You do not have to take it on faith. ATUNED shows you what it heard and what it thinks may be happening. You can confirm it, reject it, or correct it. Your correction becomes part of the record.

## Question 4
### What happens if ATUNED gets me wrong?

Required answer:

> Say so. “Not me” stops that interpretation from becoming the confirmed pattern. You can tell ATUNED what feels closer, and it can work from the correction.

## Question 5
### What is the difference between release and reframe?

Required answer:

> Release is about interrupting the old pattern. Reframe gives the story a new direction. Release creates space. Reframe gives you something new to work with. Then practice tests it in real life.

## Question 6
### What should I expect to feel or notice?

Required answer:

> Anything from a clear shift to almost nothing. You do not have to force a sensation. If your mind is very active, subtle changes can be hard to notice at first. No sensation is also information. ATUNED asks what you actually notice rather than assuming a result.

## Question 7
### Why would I use ATUNED again after the first session?

Required answer:

> Because one pattern is rarely the whole story. You can return to the same ground, explore another pattern, see what changed, and continue building a record of what you are working with.

## Question 8
### Does ATUNED remember what I have already worked on?

Required answer:

> The product is designed to remember confirmed patterns, corrections, releases, practices, evidence, and outcomes. It should remember durable learned state rather than treating every session as a blank conversation.

## Question 9
### Is this therapy, coaching, meditation, or a personality test?

Required answer:

> ATUNED is an instrument for self-observation and practice. It is not presented as a replacement for clinical care, diagnosis, or therapy. It combines conversational reflection, pattern exploration, release, reframe, practice, and longitudinal memory.

## Question 10
### Is my information private, and who can see my reading?

Required answer:

> ATUNED is designed around privacy-conscious storage and explicit account and API boundaries. Exact privacy claims must match the implemented product and published privacy policy. Your reading should not be treated as public by default.

The privacy answer must be updated whenever implementation changes.

The receiving AI must never invent privacy guarantees.
