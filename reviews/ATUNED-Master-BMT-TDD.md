# 0. EXECUTIVE DECISION

ATUNED is not primarily an AI product.

AI may be used inside the system where useful, but the product's core value does not depend on an LLM producing answers. Algorithms, structured mappings, deterministic engines, evidence handling, state transitions, release systems, practice systems, memory, trace relationships, and decision logic do much of the actual work.

Therefore:

> **AI is infrastructure, not the category.**

The customer should not need to care whether a particular operation is performed by AI, a deterministic algorithm, a rules engine, a pattern engine, a database query, or another computational method.

The customer needs to experience:

> **ATUNED sees me.**

> **I can correct it.**

> **Something changed.**

> **ATUNED remembers.**

The product hierarchy remains:

```text
THE MECHANICS OF BEING
content + framework + mechanics + methodology
        ↓
SOURCE OS
operational intelligence
        ↓
ATUNED
customer-facing experience
        ↓
SERVICE
human-supported application, research, learning, iteration
```

The current architecture identifies system congruency, rather than an engine rewrite, as the major technical problem.

# 1. COMPANY AND BUSINESS THESIS

## 1.1 Purpose

ATUNED exists to democratize access to practical mind-body self-observation and change.

The long-term ambition is:

> **Give ordinary people simple tools and techniques for seeing what is impacting them, understanding how it operates through their lives, and doing something about it.**

The product is intended to increase a person's ability to observe, discern, practice, verify, and choose.

## 1.2 Capital experiment

The immediate business objective is:

> **Raise $1,000,000 to run a structured product and service experiment.**

The raise should not be framed as:

> We need $1M to build an app.

It should be framed as:

> **We are raising $1M to test whether a new instrument for mind-body self-observation can create measurable, repeatable, longitudinal value and become a high-impact service business.**

The experiment must answer:

1. Does the instrument create meaningful first-session value?
2. Can users recognize themselves in the mirror?
3. Does correction increase trust?
4. Does release create a useful experiential shift?
5. Does verification create confidence in the process?
6. Does practice produce observable behavioral evidence?
7. Does ATUNED become more useful as it accumulates evidence?
8. Do users return because there is new ground?
9. Can the service layer accelerate learning and outcomes?
10. Can the resulting economics support a scalable business?

## 1.3 Why service first

The software is the instrument.

The service is the environment in which the company learns what actually works.

> **You do not have to force a sensation.**
