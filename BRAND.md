# Brand

What Atüned is before it is anything it does. The name, the mark, the promise,
and whether the product keeps it.

This file is the brand director's, and it is written to be argued with. Every
claim in it names the surface or the function it rests on, because a brand
sentence with nothing under it is a debt the product has to pay later.

---

## 1. The one sentence

    You are already the most powerful version of yourself, and this reads what
    is holding it down.

It is his, compressed. Three tests, and it passes all three.

**Is it true.** `cqCeiling()` in `atuned_src/engine/compute.js` computes what
coherence reads once every charge is gone. That is the most powerful version,
as a figure out of a hundred, and it is arithmetic rather than a sentiment.
`cqHeadroom()` is the gap to it. Both exist on every profile today.

**Can a competitor say it.** No, and the reason is structural rather than
rhetorical. Every other product on this shelf takes the character sheet as
input, from what somebody types in about themselves, and counts upward from
zero. A product with no ceiling in its arithmetic cannot state one, and a
product that counts upward cannot run the gauge backwards.

**Does the product deliver it.** Not yet, and this is the finding of this
document.

### The debt, named, and it is smaller and worse than it first looked

The first version of this paragraph said the ceiling and the drag are shown on
no surface. That was checked and half of it was wrong, so here is the measured
version.

**The ceiling is never printed.** `cqCeiling()` is read into `RUN.ceil0` at the
top of a release in `ui/release.js` and nothing renders it. No surface anywhere
states what your ceiling is.

**The drag is printed, and the frame is inverted.** `cqHeadroom()` reaches a
person three times, at `ui/summary.js:634` and `:643` and in the note after a
run at `ui/release.js:198`, and every one of them says a version of *release
has about 8.3 left in it*. That is the same number the brand needs, wearing the
opposite frame: it reads as a limit on the tool rather than as the distance to
the most powerful version of you.

**So the debt is a framing debt more than a build debt**, which is worse,
because a framing debt looks finished. The position is a ceiling and a drag
against it, and the product currently prints a quota remaining on a lever. Two
changes close it: print the ceiling somewhere a person lands, and say what the
headroom is the headroom to. `TASKS.md` BL2 asks for the first and nothing asks
for the second.

**And those three strings carry a naked number.** *About 8.3 in it* and *about
8.3 left in it for you* say 8.3 of what, which V7 refuses and which the voice
gate reports. The honest form names the ceiling it is counting down to, so the
brand fix and the voice fix are the same edit.

## 2. The position

**The category sells addition. This one sells subtraction, and it is the only
one that can**, because the engine measures the drag rather than the score.

That is not a tone of voice, it is a gauge decision, and it decides every
surface. A stat that goes up as you earn it is every other gamified product. A
stat that is already high and is being held down is this one. `TASKS.md` BL1
states it as a spec and it should be read that way.

**The shelf is instruments, not wellness.** Leica, Nomos, Braun under Rams,
Teenage Engineering, Leatherman. Not the shelf it will be displayed beside.
Everything in that category is soft and this is not, and that is the whole
position. The category clichés are out by name: soft light, lotus flowers,
gradients, and the word that means a long trip toward yourself, which is also
refused in code by `marketing/refuse.js` under the voice rule.

## 3. His narrative, and it is the spine of both pages

His words, and they are the best statement of the mechanism anybody has made
here:

> Our bodies are naturally expressive. Stress forces the body to contract. When
> we identify with that contraction it creates a bias in the mind and a kink of
> resistance in the form of that stress response. We condition that stress
> response and eventually that node turns into a cluster. We begin to
> procrastinate more, feel anxious more, feel more shy, more depressed, more
> overwhelmed. Eventually those clusters form networks, where the body begins
> to tense up in different locations. The problem begins. Now our nervous
> system has these kinks all over it, blocking the natural energy flow of the
> body.
>
> Atuned helps you identify the point of origin of the stress response, and
> integrates the kink, the cluster and the network. And in doing so, brings
> inner peace mentally and physically. It accelerates the mind body connection
> and it naturally releases stress from the body.
>
> There is nothing like this. It holds up a mirror to you and peers directly
> into your soul. This is you. We help you become the best version of yourself.
> In mind, body, spirit and soul.

**It is a storyboard and it is used in his order**, on `funnel/about.html`
under the Why section: the natural state, the contraction, the identification,
the conditioning, the cluster, the network, the symptom, then the instrument.
It is not reordered to build to a crescendo.

### It is also the engine's own ladder in plain words, and that was checked

Read against `compute()` in `atuned_src/engine/compute.js`, cited by name
rather than by line because line numbers drift:

| His word | The engine | The gate |
|---|---|---|
| the kink | one address carrying charge, `n.sq` | loaded at 4 of 10 |
| the cluster | a saboteur, three or four addresses in one band | mean 3.7 of 10, in `sab33Detect` and over `ALL_SAB` |
| the network | `cxs`, two saboteurs in one of six architectures, then `hys` over a family of those | two complexes, or one running at 6.5 of 10 |
| above it | `sups`, the superego | two hyper complexes at 5.6 of 10 |

Saboteurs inside one architecture are seated at different bands, so the complex
is the first rung that appears in more than one place in the body. That is what
his sentence says about the network, and it is not a coincidence: he is
describing the arithmetic from the outside.

**This is the strongest thing a positioning line can be.** It is checkable
against the code that produces the reading, by somebody who does not trust us.
The about page says so on the page rather than leaving it in a file.

### The two phrases that are claims rather than mechanism

Named so nobody adds a third.

1. **brings inner peace mentally and physically**
2. **naturally releases stress from the body**

Both are ordinary wellness language rather than the regulated kind, so neither
carries the exposure the earlier line did. Both pass `marketing/refuse.js`.
Everything around them in his paragraph is mechanism and is checkable against
the engine. These two are not, and they do not need removing. They need
knowing.

## 4. The word heals, and how it was settled

**What was on the table.** His earlier positioning line: *"Atuned is a world's
first neurosomatic tool that heals the mind body connection. Attunes moral
integrity to improve vital energy. And heals disease in the mind and body from
the effects of stress, and the damage it causes across our human complex."*

**What the claim would have added, priced honestly.** It buys one thing: the
word disease, which is the strongest word available for the stakes. Against
that it costs four.

- It is a therapeutic claim and a regulated one in every market this sells in.
- It is refused in code. `marketing/refuse.js` rule `medical` catches heals
  against a named condition, and `GUARD.md` states that no measurement can move
  that rule.
- It contradicts what the product says about itself from the inside. The
  compass says this is a reading of what is running and not of who is running
  it, and the referral says a configuration needs a licensed clinician
  alongside and not instead. Outward copy claiming to heal disease would be
  saying the thing the instrument itself refuses to say.
- It is not needed. The mechanism is more specific than the claim, and specific
  beats strong on this shelf.

**How he settled it, and it is the better move.** He did not remove the word,
he replaced a claim with a mechanism. The chain, the point of origin, and
integrating the kink, the cluster and the network say more than heals ever did,
and every rung of it is checkable against the arithmetic. Neither seat proposed
that. It is recorded here because the method is worth reusing: **when a claim
is too strong to keep, the replacement is not a softer claim, it is the
mechanism underneath it.**

**Still standing, and outward only.** The word diagnostic. `CLAUDE.md` opens by
calling this a somatic diagnostic instrument and that is the right word inside
the repository. On a page, beside a sentence about stress and the body,
`refuse.js` refuses it: the medical rule catches `/diagnos\w+/` with no
exception, and it is right to, because the engine holds the clinical
correspondences internally and refuses to print them at the person they are
about. So the outward noun on `funnel/about.html` is **a somatic reading
instrument** and the inward one stays diagnostic. His call whether the gate
grows a named exemption instead, the way `NOBODY` does for H18.

## 5. World's first, and the sentence it rests on

**The claim, stated narrowly enough to check:**

> Gamified self development is a crowded shelf, and the nearest thing on it is
> a skill tree a person fills in by hand. Every product there takes the sheet
> as input, from what somebody types in about themselves, and counts upward
> from zero. This one derives the sheet from a reading of the person's own
> written story, and it runs the gauge the other way round: the ceiling is
> stated first, computed from that person's own reading, and the figure that
> moves is the drag standing between.

**How to check it.** Two questions, both answerable by opening a competitor.

1. Does the product read the sheet out of a story, or take the sheet as input.
2. Does it show a ceiling before it shows a score.

**Why it is narrow on purpose.** First at gamified self development is
indefensible and a hostile reader takes it apart in an afternoon. First at
deriving the sheet from a reading and inverting the gauge is two mechanical
properties, and nothing found does either. Claim what can be checked and the
claim survives being checked.

**One condition on using it.** The second half of that sentence is only true
once the ceiling is on a surface. Today the product computes it and hides it,
so the claim is true of the engine and not yet of the experience. See the debt
in section 1.

## 7. The name, the mark and Source OS

**Atüned**, with the umlaut. The mark on a page is the drawn dot pair, sky blue
wordmark, letters given room, two white dots sitting on the bar rather than on
the letter, answering to the background through `--um`. Ruled in
`DECISIONS.md`. **The mark in a string is the character**, because a title, a
bookmark and a search result have no CSS.

**Source OS**, in that casing, never the caps form. Standing voice ruling:
sentence case, no all caps copy, so the app's own casing is the correct one and
the brief's is the informal one. It sits under the wordmark in `--au`, which is
the real gold. Not `--gold`, which is an alias for the blue accent and has
rendered Source OS blue twice already because the token lied about its own name.

### The two gaps in the shipped funnel, and how these two pages handle both

`marketing/AESTHETIC.md` reported both and could not fix them because
`funnel/` is another seat's. They are still open in `index.html` and
`quiz.html`, and the fix is specified here.

1. **The landing carries the umlaut mechanism and not the wordmark.** The dot
   rule is in its stylesheet and its `<title>` is the plain spelling, so the
   page holds the machinery for the mark and never sets it in the one place
   that cannot use CSS. `about.html` and `buy.html` write `<title>Atüned,
   about</title>` and `<title>Atüned, tiers</title>` with the character, and
   carry the drawn dots on the page. **The fix for the landing is one
   character**: `<title>Atüned</title>`. The quiz's title is `Atuned test` and
   takes the same change.
2. **Source OS appears nowhere in `index.html` or `quiz.html`**, in any casing,
   although FN4 asks for it by name. Both new pages carry it directly under the
   wordmark in `--au`, at 12.5px with wide tracking, as `.os`. **The fix for
   the landing is one element**, the same `.os` paragraph under its `h1`, and
   the boot form beside it is `Powered by Source OS`.

Neither file was touched. Both changes are one line each.

## 8. Voice at the brand level

The range the copy inside it works in, and it is set by `.claude/skills/
atuned-voice`, which owns the sentence. This owns the register.

- Mechanical and precise. Short sentences. Physical metaphors only.
- Sentence case in body, title case in headers, and a value is body.
- Every number says what it is out of in plain words.
- A figure's label is one word, and the unit rides on the figure.
- No em dashes anywhere. The count stated to a person is 112.
- Never a reassurance against a fear nobody has raised.
- The product never addresses itself and never praises.

**One terminology item for him, and it is new this round.** His narrative uses
**integrate** for what the tool does to a kink, a cluster and a network. The
product uses **release** for the act and **released** for the result, and
`tools/terms.py` exists to stop exactly this kind of drift. Two readings are
available: they are one concept under two words, in which case one of them
goes, or integrate names the whole chain and release names one line at one
address, in which case they are two concepts and both stay. The second is what
the pages assume, because it is what the arithmetic supports. His ruling.

## 9. Where the product is off brand, with the surface named

Brand reads the first four seconds and then stops, which is this seat's own
blind spot, so these are named rather than fixed.

1. **The ceiling is not on any surface, and the drag is framed as a quota.**
   Section 1. The position is subtraction against a stated ceiling, no screen
   states one, and the three places the gap does reach a person read as how
   much the release lever has left rather than how far you are from your own
   ceiling.
2. **`funnel/index.html` draws the loop as four numbered steps and calls the
   fourth one Body.** Ruled otherwise twice: `TASKS.md` FL1 says the word is
   **embody**, and FL2 says it is drawn as a circle and never a list, because a
   numbered column of four says the fourth one is the end, which is the
   opposite of a loop. The landing is signed off and was not touched. Both
   changes are small and neither is this seat's to make.
3. **The landing's own `<title>` and the missing Source OS.** Section 7.
4. **Checkout does not exist**, so the first surface that asks for money cannot
   take it. `buy.html` says so in one line, once, and does not apologise for it.
   The alternative is holding the page until checkout ships, which is his call
   and is in section 10.
