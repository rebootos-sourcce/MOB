## The five mechanisms that run under every row

These are the general findings the per charge rows lean on, so they are stated
once rather than repeated nine times.

**1. A message matched to the state a person is already in is more persuasive
than the same message unmatched.** This is the whole basis for a lookup rather
than a single line. Cesario, Grant and Higgins 2004 found regulatory fit raised
perceived persuasiveness across four studies, and the effect disappeared when
the real source of the feeling was made salient first, which is the
misattribution account rather than a content effect.
[Cesario, Grant and Higgins 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15008644/).
Motyka and colleagues' meta analysis reports **medium sized effects on
evaluation, intention and behaviour**, with promotion fit larger on evaluation
and prevention fit larger on behaviour. **Abstract only**: the pooled d was not
reachable, so no number is quoted here.
[Motyka et al 2014, Journal of Consumer Psychology 24(3) 394 to 410](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcps.2013.11.004).

**2. The second person makes a reader process the argument harder, and that
cuts both ways.** Burnkrant and Unnava found recall higher under self
referencing, and that argument strength mattered *more* under high self
referencing than low. So addressing somebody directly does not paper over a
weak claim, it exposes it.
[Burnkrant and Unnava 1989](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/0146157289154015).
That is the mechanical reason the proof field on every hook has to be real.

**3. Curiosity is a gap, and the gap has a size.** Loewenstein's information
gap account has curiosity arising from a perceived gap between what is known
and what is wanted, peaking when a person holds a little knowledge and falling
when they hold almost none or almost all.
[Loewenstein 1994, Psychological Bulletin](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/232440476_The_Psychology_of_Curiosity_A_Review_and_Reinterpretation).
This is why the what if form works and also why it stops working: a person at
grid level 1 holds almost no reading of themselves and the gap is not
tractable, and a person at level 10 already holds the answer. The curve of
curiosity and the curve in `BUYERS.md` fail at the same two ends.

**4. Controlling phrasing provokes reactance and non pressuring phrasing does
not.** Altendorf and colleagues tested autonomy supportive against controlling
wording in a tailored health intervention and measured reactance as the
outcome; "you may" and "feel free" read as autonomy supportive where neutral
phrasing did not.
[Altendorf et al 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6393822/).
Every hook in this system offers and none instructs.

**5. And the inconvenient one, stated because leaving it out would be the
dishonest version.** Fear appeals work. Tannenbaum and colleagues pooled 127
papers and 248 samples at 27,372 participants and found a positive composite
effect at **d 0.27**, with no identified circumstance under which fear appeals
backfired, and larger effects where the message carried high severity and high
susceptibility.
[Tannenbaum et al 2015, Psychological Bulletin 141(6) 1178 to 1204](https://socialactionlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Tannenbaum_Appealing-to-Fear-A-Meta-Analysis-of-Fear-Appeal-Effectiveness-and-Theories_2015.pdf).
The refusal in `refuse.js` therefore does not rest on fear failing. It rests on
two measurements in this repository and one ruling, and they are set out in that
file's header and in `GUARD.md`.

## What the seats do to a line

The seat is not decoration on the charge. It is where the sentence's physical
noun comes from, and it is the reason two hooks at the same charge are two
different lines.

| Seat | The body word the line reaches for | Hooks |
|---|---|---|
| Root | lower back, gut, hips, sciatic | H05, H10, H17 |
| Sacral | pelvic floor, pelvis, gut, taste | H01, H03, H04, H11, H15 |
| Solar | upper abdomen, the clamp | H08, H13 |
| Heart | chest, ribs, the vagus down past the chest | H02, H07, H12, H14 |
| Throat | voice, neck, the words arriving | none. 6 of 1000 sit here and it is a named hole |
| 3rd Eye | behind the eyes, where the nerves cross | H09 |
| Crown | the room, the ring, the nerve at the top of the head | H06, H16 |

**Shame at the sacral and Shame at the crown are two hooks, not one hook with
two colours.** H01 reaches for the pelvic floor and reliability. H06 reaches
for a room with a gap in it. Serving either for the other is precisely the mis
addressing this system is built to avoid, which is why `match.js` does not fall
back from a charge and seat key to a charge only one, even though a fallback
would raise coverage from 857 to 976 of 1000 before the band gate.

## What the engine's own table did that no persona would have

`NODES` carries a charge label `c` on each address, drawn from eight labels
including Resentment and Joy. Neither is one of the nine axes. `canon.js`
`REROUTE` maps the eight onto the nine and three keyword patterns override it
by address name. So:

- **Envy** carries Resentment on its row and routes to **Anger**.
- **Hypervigilance** and **Expectation** both carry Fear and route to
  **Anticipation**.
- **Guilt Of Pleasure**, **Addiction** and **Spiritual Escapism** all carry Joy
  and route to **Apathy**.

That last one is worth stopping on. The engine says the address whose aspect is
*joy* routes to the *apathy* axis, because what is held at an address named for
joy is the denial of it. A persona library would never produce that. It is in
the data, and it is the reason H11 says "nothing tastes like much" rather than
anything about pleasure.

The first cut of assertion 15 in `tests.js` compared a hook's charge against
the raw `c` and failed on six hooks of seventeen. The assertion was wrong and
the hooks were right. It now reads the routing off the engine rather than
restating it.
