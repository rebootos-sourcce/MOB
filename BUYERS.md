# Buyer resonance

The owner's grid, recorded whole. Ten levels, the state at each one, what the
body is doing there, and how likely a person in that state is to buy.

**The commercial column is deliberately not in the engine.** The state and the
somatic condition are, on `TIERDEF` in `engine/data/canon.js`, because they
are a reading of a person and the product exists to give a person their
reading. A buying probability is a reading of a market. Somebody opening this
to look at themselves must never find a percentage next to their own band
saying how likely they are to pay, and the two must not live in one table
where a renderer could reach the wrong field.

Two names were changed where they collided with a word the band table already
owns, and both changes are named here so the grid and the engine can be
compared line by line.

Level 4 in the grid is called Oscillating. Oscillating is already the band at
41 to 60 in this product, named for crossing the median in both directions,
and the grid's level 4 sits at 31 to 40. It carries **Frustrated** in the
engine, which is the grid's own description of it. Level 5 keeps Searching.

## What this says about the product

The curve is not monotonic and that is the finding. It peaks at 10, 9 and 8,
collapses through 5 and 4, and floors at 1. The product sells hardest to
people who are already working, and it is close to unsellable to the people
whose readings are lowest.

That is worth stating plainly rather than designing around quietly. The
onboarding cannot be written for level 8. Anyone at 4 or 5 is the largest
population and the hardest sell, and the grid says exactly why: at 4 the work
hurts, and at 5 it is not mystical enough.
