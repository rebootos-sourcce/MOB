# Research: chakras, somatics, mindset, transformation, energy

Commissioned by the owner for the CQ seat. Four bodies of literature, read for
one purpose: to find out which of the claims this product already makes are
load bearing and which are decoration. Every source is cited by URL. Every
finding carries its evidence tier. Where the code and the evidence disagree,
this file names the file, the line, and which of the two should move.

Atüned is a diagnostic instrument. It reads self report and renders where
charge sits. It does not treat anything, and this file flags every place the
research tempts a claim the product cannot support.

## HOW THIS WAS BUILT

Ten searches across four literatures, source documents fetched where the egress
proxy allowed it and read through search summaries where it did not. Fifteen
primary or review papers identified by author, year and journal. Then the code:
`atuned_src/engine/data/canon.js`, `atuned_src/engine/compute.js`,
`atuned_src/engine/data/nodes.js`, `atuned_src/engine/data/practice.js` and

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE INSTRUMENT were produced by running the engine rather
than by reading it. The script is in the scratchpad and the outputs are quoted
with the numbers they came out at.

Nothing outside this file and `TEAM.md` was touched.

# PART ONE. THE CHAKRA SYSTEM IN ITS SOURCE TRADITIONS

## WHAT THE SANSKRIT SOURCES ACTUALLY CARRY

The chakra is a device inside a specific practice. It is a visualisation
scaffold used in tantric ritual to install a deity, a mantra seed and an
element at a location on the body's vertical axis. It is not a personality
map and it is not a diagnostic grid. The number of seats is set by the ritual,
which is why the number moves from text to text.

**The count is not seven in the sources.** The Netra Tantra gives six. The
Kaulajñāna-nirṇaya gives eight. The Kubjikāmata Tantra, dated to the ninth or
tenth century, is the subject of a full monograph by Dory Heilijgers-Seelen
titled *The System of Five Chakras in Kubjikāmata Tantra*, and the count in its
title is five. Some texts run past twelve seats and some stop short of five.
The variation is not sloppiness. Each system is fitted to the rite it serves.

Sources for the count and the variation:
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subtle_body
- https://archive.org/details/systemoffivechakrasinkubjikamatatantradoryheilijgersseelen_202003_785_w
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chakra

**The seven seat text is late and is one text among many.** The
*Ṣaṭ-cakra-nirūpaṇa*, "description of the six centres", was written by
Pūrṇānanda in Bengal in the sixteenth century. It carries six seats plus the
crown, which is where the familiar seven comes from. It reached English in 1919
through Sir John Woodroffe, writing as Arthur Avalon, in *The Serpent Power*.
Woodroffe's translation made interpretive choices that pushed the text toward
readings it does not always carry, and the modern Western system inherited the
choices rather than the text.

- https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/shat-cakra-nirupana-english
- https://www.bhagavadgitausa.com/sat_chakra_Nirupana.htm

**The petals are a phonetic index, not an intensity dial.** Root carries four
petals bearing the letters va, śa, ṣa, sa. Sacral carries six. Solar carries
ten. The counts across the set sum to the fifty letters of the Sanskrit
alphabet. A petal is a syllable address, so the structure is closer to a keyed
index than to a gauge.

- https://www.researchgate.net/publication/347524225_Study_on_the_symbolic_of_Sat-Chakra-Nirupana

**The colours in the sources are not a spectrum.** The
*Ṣaṭ-cakra-nirūpaṇa* gives colours tied to deities and elements: crimson
petals at the root, golden regions, smoky grey at the throat, white at the
brow, the radiance of a thousand suns at the crown. Grey and white are not
rainbow entries and the sequence does not run in wavelength order.

**The seed mantras are not in one text either.** Christopher Wallis, a Sanskrit
scholar publishing as Hareesh, states that no single Sanskrit source assigns
the bīja mantras now taught as the chakra mantras.

## THE DEPARTURE, NAMED

The modern Western seven seat model is a construction, and it can be dated. It
was assembled between roughly 1879 and 1977 by five hands. Five specific things
were added that the sources do not carry.

**One. The count was frozen at seven.** Blavatsky and the Theosophical Society
from 1879, then Leadbeater, Besant, Steiner and Bailey, settled on seven out of
a range that ran from five to more than twelve. Seven is a defensible choice.
It is a choice.

**Two. The seats were welded to nerve plexuses.** Leadbeater's *The Chakras*,
1927, put each seat at an anatomical plexus. This is the single most consequential
addition, because it is the move that makes the model look like anatomy. It is
a twentieth century identification with no source text behind it.

**Three. The names were replaced.** Root, Sacral, Solar, Heart, Throat, Third
Eye, Crown are Theosophical, not Sanskrit. Mūlādhāra means "root support".
Svādhiṣṭhāna means "her own dwelling". The English names are locations. The
Sanskrit names are functions.

**Four. The rainbow was bolted on in 1977.** Christopher Hills, *Nuclear
Evolution*, mapped the seven seats onto the seven bands of the visible
spectrum. There is no Indian or Tibetan precedent for it. The most familiar
feature of the modern system is the newest one.

**Five. The psychological attributes were added last.** Self worth at the
sacral, communication at the throat, will at the solar: these are twentieth
century Western additions layered on by the same chain, culminating in the
therapeutic chakra literature of the late twentieth century. The sources give
each seat a deity, an element, a syllable set and a ritual function. They do
not give it a psychological competency.

- https://books.google.com/books/about/Rainbow_Body.html?id=zq1zDQAAQBAJ
- https://www.theosophical.org/publications/quest-magazine/the-rainbow-body-how-the-western-chakra-system-came-to-be
- https://www.alanjonesuk.com/post/the-rainbow-delusion-how-the-west-reinvented-the-chakras
- https://lithub.com/on-the-most-misunderstood-aspect-of-yoga/

## THE HONEST FRAMING OF A SEVEN SEAT MODEL

A seven seat vertical model is a coordinate system. It has three properties
that survive the history above.

It is a **stable partition of the trunk and head**, which means two readings of
the same person can be compared.

It is **memorable**, which means a person can hold the whole of it, unlike a
list of a hundred and twelve locations.

It **roughly tracks real autonomic geography**. The pelvic, hypogastric,
celiac, cardiac and pharyngeal plexuses are real structures at roughly the
stated heights. The seat is a bin, and the bin has a physical floor and ceiling.

What it is not is a claim about a field. That is Part Four.

# PART TWO. SOMATIC PSYCHOLOGY, WITH THE EVIDENCE ATTACHED

## POLYVAGAL THEORY. THE FRAME IS CONTESTED. THE PARTS ARE NOT

Porges introduced polyvagal theory in 1995. It asserts a three stage
phylogenetic ladder of autonomic response, a uniquely mammalian myelinated
ventral vagal circuit that supports social engagement, and respiratory sinus
arrhythmia as the readable index of that circuit.

**What is contested, and by how many people.** Paul Grossman published
"Fundamental challenges and likely refutations of the five basic premises of the
polyvagal theory" in *Biological Psychology* in 2023, having circulated the
draft to evolutionary biologists, a vagal neuroanatomy authority and
cardiovascular autonomic physiologists. The three load bearing objections are:
the phylogenetic story is wrong, because a myelinated cardiac vagus and
neurogenic bradycardia are not mammal exclusive and appear in non mammalian
species; the dorsal and ventral anatomical split is not as clean as the theory
requires; and treating respiratory sinus arrhythmia as equivalent to cardiac
vagal tone is a category error, because respiration rate and depth drive it
independently of vagal outflow.

In February 2026 Grossman and thirty nine psychophysiology and neuroanatomy
experts published "Why the polyvagal theory is untenable". Porges replied in
the same issue, and has consistently framed the theory as a working model open
to revision.

- https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0301051123001060
- https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Paul-Grossman/publication/400718181_THE_POLYVAGAL_THEORY_IS_UNTENABLE_An_international_expert_evaluation_of_the_polyvagal_theory_and_commentary_upon_Porges/links/698def9112f837212a1a47f2/THE-POLYVAGAL-THEORY-IS-UNTENABLE-An-international-expert-evaluation-of-the-polyvagal-theory-and-commentary-upon-Porges.pdf
- https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/behavioral-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnbeh.2025.1659083/full
- https://www.polyvagalinstitute.org/criticaldiscussionofpolyvagaltheory

**What survives regardless of who wins.** Three things, and they are the three
this product touches.

The vagus nerve is real and carries roughly four fifths of its fibres afferent,
body to brain. Heart rate variability is a real measurable quantity. And slow
breathing raises it: the baroreflex mechanism is understood, arterial pressure
falls on inhalation and rises on exhalation, and breathing at roughly four and
a half to seven breaths per minute produces the largest low frequency HRV
increase. Two time domain measures, SDNN and RMSSD, both rise at that rate.

One caveat worth carrying. The individual resonance frequency moved between
test and retest in two thirds of participants in one study, so a person's
optimal rate is not a fixed constant.

- https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.00756/full
- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-021-87867-8

**The practical reading.** Use the vagal and breathing mechanism. Do not use
the phylogenetic ladder, and do not use the words ventral and dorsal as though
they named two states of a person. A product that says "your nervous system is
in dorsal shutdown" is asserting the most contested premise in the theory as
though it were settled.

## PETER LEVINE AND SOMATIC EXPERIENCING. PROMISING, THIN

Somatic Experiencing works on interoceptive tracking and incomplete defensive
responses rather than on narrative.

**The strongest single result.** Brom and colleagues, *Journal of Traumatic
Stress*, 2017: the first randomised controlled trial of SE for PTSD. Effects on
post traumatic symptom severity ran at Cohen's d between 0.94 and 1.26, and on
depression between 0.7 and 1.08, both pre to post and pre to follow up. Those
are large effect sizes on the Cohen scale, where 0.8 is conventionally large.

**The state of the whole base.** Kuhfuß and colleagues, *European Journal of
Psychotraumatology*, 2021, scoping review through August 2020: eighty three
articles screened, sixteen met inclusion. Their own assessment is that quality
is mixed. Only a minority were randomised controlled trials. Most had small
convenience samples, non standardised outcome measures and high heterogeneity.
Few carried long term follow up or monitored therapist fidelity. Their
conclusion, verbatim in substance: the results are promising yet require more
support from unbiased randomised controlled trial research.

- https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdf/10.1002/jts.22189
- https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/20008198.2021.1929023
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34290845/

**The practical reading.** One good trial plus a thin base is enough to support
a mechanism sentence and not enough to support an outcome sentence. It is also
somebody else's intervention, delivered by a trained human. Nothing about it
transfers to a browser.

## BESSEL VAN DER KOLK. THE BOOK IS NOT THE EVIDENCE

*The Body Keeps the Score*, 2014, is the most read text in this field and the
most carefully audited.

**The audit.** A 2025 analysis in *BJPsych Bulletin* extracted one hundred and
twenty two discrete claims from the book: forty two on neurobiology, fifty one
on treatment efficacy, twenty nine on other topics. It found multiple instances
where the cited evidence was incomplete, selectively presented, erroneously
presented, or inconsistent with the wider literature.

**Two specific failures worth naming, because they are the ones this product is
nearest to.** Cortisol in PTSD is frequently low rather than high, which is the
reverse of the book's account. And there is no consistent neurological evidence
that trauma is stored in muscles, organs or disconnected brain regions.

Richard McNally at Harvard raised conceptual and empirical problems with van
der Kolk's theory in the *Canadian Journal of Psychiatry* in 2005. A 2023
editorial in *Research on Social Work Practice* raised concerns about
misrepresented conclusions and reliance on outmoded research.

- https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/bjpsych-bulletin/article/evaluating-evidence-behind-popular-trauma-narratives-neurobiological-and-treatment-claims-in-the-body-keeps-the-score/5DE000F0254747495B1CAFF4051C3B75
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13386322/

**The practical reading, and it is the sharpest finding in this file.** "The
body keeps the score" is a metaphor that reads as a mechanism. The sentence
"stored in the tissue" has no neurological referent. This product uses a
vocabulary built on exactly that sentence. See WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE
INSTRUMENT.

## INTEROCEPTION AND THE INSULA. STRONG ANATOMY, BROKEN RULER

**What is strong.** The insula is the interoceptive cortex and the anatomy is
settled. Mid and posterior insula receive direct interoceptive input from the
thalamus and act as primary interoceptive cortex. Anterior insula is
preferentially involved in conscious awareness of those signals and in
integrating them with goals and emotional state. Critchley's 2004 work
established that right anterior insula supports representations of cardiac
response that are accessible to awareness. Patients with focal anterior insula
lesions show reduced performance on interoceptive tasks, which is lesion
evidence for necessity rather than correlation.

- https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn2555
- https://elifesciences.org/articles/42265
- https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-psych-020924-125202?crawler=true
- https://med.stanford.edu/content/dam/sm/scsnl/documents/insular_cortex_2024_menon.pdf

**What is broken.** The field's standard measuring stick. Zamariola and
colleagues, *Biological Psychology*, 2018, showed that heartbeat counting task
accuracy scores largely reflect systematic under reporting, and that the
correlation between actual and reported beats is low. The scores track a
person's prior belief about their own heart rate rather than their perception of
it. Performance is contaminated by time estimation and by prior knowledge. A
2025 *Psychophysiology* paper compares three ability measures and finds they do
not agree with each other.

- https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0301051118303739
- https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/psyp.70078
- https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/17470218231172725

**The practical reading.** Interoception is real, localised and measurable in
principle. The objective bedside test for it is unreliable, which means that
a self report questionnaire is not obviously worse than the lab task. That is
good news for a product that reads self report, and it is a narrow piece of
good news, not a licence. Garfinkel's three way split is the useful frame:
accuracy, sensibility and awareness are different quantities, and a self report
instrument measures sensibility only.

## BODILY MAPS OF EMOTION. THE STRONGEST FINDING FOR THIS PRODUCT

Nummenmaa, Glerean, Hari and Hietanen, *PNAS*, 2014, "Bodily maps of emotions".
Seven hundred and seventy three participants coloured body outlines for where
sensation rose and fell across six basic and seven non basic emotional states.
The maps are topographically consistent. Anger and fear load the upper torso,
arms and head. Sadness shows diminished sensation in the limbs. Disgust
localises to the abdomen and throat.

The findings replicated in Swedish speakers and held across Finnish and
Taiwanese Hokkien samples, which is a Western European against East Asian
comparison. A 2024 *PNAS* paper extends the method to music induced sensation
across Western and East Asian subjects and replicates again.

- https://www.pnas.org/doi/abs/10.1073/pnas.1321664111
- https://www.cs.helsinki.fi/u/ahyvarin/teaching/niseminar5/PNAS-2014-Nummenmaa-646-51.pdf
- https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2308859121

**The practical reading.** This is the evidence base for the Body page. Naming
where an emotion is felt, asking a person to mark it on a figure, and reading a
topography off it is a validated method with cross cultural replication. It is
a map of **reported sensation**, not of stored charge, and that distinction is
the whole of the honest version.

## FEAR AVOIDANCE AND PAIN. A REAL CAUSAL PATH, AND ITS LIMIT

Vlaeyen and Linton, fear avoidance model of musculoskeletal pain, reviewed in
*Journal of Behavioral Medicine* 2007 and updated as "twelve years on" in
*Pain* 2012. The model is a cognitive behavioural account of why a minority of
acute low back pain episodes become chronic. Components: pain severity,
catastrophising, attention to pain, escape and avoidance behaviour, disability,
disuse. Confronting pain reduces fear over time. Avoiding it maintains or
escalates fear toward a phobic state. Since 2000 numerous studies have linked
pain related fear to increased disability, with support for its role in
inception, in the transition from acute to chronic, and in maintenance.

The limit is stated by the authors: oversight of potentially important factors
and inconsistent findings on the postulated pathways have led to proposed
revisions.

- https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s10865-006-9085-0.pdf
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22321917/

**The practical reading.** There is a real, well evidenced psychological
pathway into physical pain. It runs through fear, attention and avoidance
behaviour. It does not run through charge resident at an anatomical address.
The direction of the arrow matters: fear predicts disability and chronicity, it
does not cause a specific tissue lesion at a specific plexus.

# PART THREE. MINDSET AND TRANSFORMATION, WITH REPLICATION STATUS

This is the literature with the worst replication record of the four, and the
failures are large and specific.

## GROWTH MINDSET. THE EFFECT IS REAL AND ABOUT A TENTH OF THE ADVERTISED SIZE

Sisk, Burgoyne, Sun, Butler and Macnamara, *Psychological Science*, 2018, two
meta analyses. The correlational arm aggregated two hundred and seventy three
effect sizes from roughly three hundred and sixty five thousand students. The
correlation between mindset and achievement came out at r of about 0.10, which
is about one percent of the variance in achievement.

Macnamara and Burgoyne, *Psychological Bulletin*, 2023, one hundred and twenty
two studies: apparent effects on academic achievement are likely attributable
to inadequate study design, reporting flaws and bias. Among the studies most
closely following best practice the overall effect was not significant, at
p of 0.666. Aggregate effects sit at Cohen's d of roughly 0.05 to 0.10, which
is at the floor of what is conventionally called small, with substantial
publication bias in the literature.

- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29505339/
- https://www.researchgate.net/publication/365261074_Do_Growth_Mindset_Interventions_Impact_Students'_Academic_Achievement_A_Systematic_Review_and_Meta-Analysis_with_Recommendations_for_Best_Practices
- https://msutoday.msu.edu/news/2018/05/study-finds-popular-growth-mindset-educational-interventions-arent-very-effective

## POWER POSING. FAILED, AND THE FIRST AUTHOR SAID SO

Carney, Cuddy and Yap, 2010, reported that expansive posture raised
testosterone, lowered cortisol, increased risk tolerance and increased felt
power. Ranehill and colleagues, *Psychological Science*, 2015, ran a larger
conceptual replication. Felt power replicated. Every physiological and
behavioural effect did not.

In September 2016 the original first author, Dana Carney, published a statement
saying "I do not believe that power pose effects are real", and detailed the
data handling choices that produced the original significant findings.

- https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/0956797614553946
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_posing
- https://www.bps.org.uk/psychologist/decade-power-posing-where-do-we-stand

**Why this one matters most here.** Power posing is the purest form of the claim
that a body position changes a hormonal state which changes an outcome. That is
structurally the same claim as "hold this posture to release this address". The
subjective half survived replication and the physiological half did not. Any
posture based feature in this product should therefore promise a felt change and
nothing below the skin.

## EXPRESSIVE WRITING. REAL, SMALL, AND RELEVANT BECAUSE THIS PRODUCT TAKES A STORY

Frattaroli, *Psychological Bulletin*, 2006, meta analysis of one hundred and
forty six studies. Overall effect r of 0.075, which converts to Cohen's d of
about 0.15. Statistically significant. Practically small. Replication is mixed,
with both successful and failed independent replications and considerable drift
from the original protocol in later work.

- https://bpb-us-e2.wpmucdn.com/faculty.sites.uci.edu/dist/c/602/files/2019/08/Frattaroli-psych-bulletin-2006.pdf
- https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/1089268019831645

**The practical reading.** The core act of this product, writing a charged
story down, has a small real effect in its own right. That is a floor worth
having and it is a floor of about 0.15 on Cohen's d, not a transformation.

## SCARCITY AND COGNITIVE LOAD. CONTESTED

Mani, Mullainathan, Shafir and Zhao, *Science*, 2013, "Poverty impedes
cognitive function". Wicherts and Scholten's comment in *Science* argued the
findings were an artefact of dichotomising income and of ceiling effects on
short tests, and a reanalysis without dichotomisation failed to corroborate
them. Replication is mixed: effects found in Zambia and Uganda at sizes from
small to very large, failures in Brazil, and Carvalho and colleagues in 2016
found no difference in cognitive performance before against after payday in a
United States sample.

- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23990553/
- https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1246680
- https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11238-021-09836-x

## THE BARNUM PROBLEM, AND IT APPLIES DIRECTLY TO THIS PRODUCT

Forer, late 1940s: participants completed a personality assessment, then all
received the same ten generic statements as their personal result. Mean rated
accuracy was 4.3 on a five point scale. Acceptance rises when the statements
are flattering and when the source is perceived as an authority.

The finding that matters for an instrument: people do rate genuinely accurate
feedback as more accurate than bogus feedback, but the gap is small. Which means
a person telling you your reading is accurate is weak evidence that it is.

- https://www.britannica.com/science/Barnum-Effect
- https://psychexamreview.com/the-forer-effect-or-barnum-effect/
- https://atticusli.com/replication-crisis/forer-barnum-effect/

**The practical reading.** "This is so accurate" from a user is not validation.
Validation is test retest reliability, convergence with an established
instrument, and discriminant power: a reading that could not have been another
person's. This product currently has none of the three, and `accuracy()` in
`atuned_src/engine/compute.js:297` reports a fitted confidence figure rather
than a validity figure. That distinction is in PART FIVE.

# PART FOUR. ENERGY WORK. WHAT IS MEASURABLE AND WHAT IS NOT

## MEASURABLE, WITH INSTRUMENTS, TODAY

- **Heart rate variability.** Beat to beat interval variation. SDNN and RMSSD in
  the time domain. Rises under slow breathing at roughly four and a half to
  seven breaths per minute.
- **Electrodermal activity.** Skin conductance, sympathetic sudomotor output.
- **Surface electromyography.** Resting muscle tone at a named muscle.
- **Cortisol.** Salivary or serum, with the caveat from PART TWO that it is low
  rather than high in much of PTSD.
- **Skin temperature, respiration rate, pupil diameter.** All routine.
- **Ultraweak photon emission.** This one is real and is routinely
  misrepresented. Every living system emits photons spontaneously, in the
  visible band, at an intensity detectable only with cooled sensitive cameras
  and invisible to the eye. The mechanism is understood: relaxation of
  electronically excited reactive oxygen species produced during lipid
  peroxidation and protein and nucleic acid oxidation. It is used to assess
  oxidation status of skin, and it rises under ultraviolet exposure and heat
  shock. One 2021 proof of concept paper in *Microchemical Journal* reports
  increased emission in anger states.

- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31349151/
- https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1011134421000191
- https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0026265X21006445
- https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0105700

## NOT MEASURABLE, AND TESTED

- **A detectable field around a person that a practitioner can sense.** Rosa,
  Rosa, Sarner and Barrett, *JAMA*, 1 April 1998. Twenty one experienced
  therapeutic touch practitioners, hands through a screen, asked to say which
  of their own hands the investigator's hand was held above. They could not do
  it at better than chance. The study has been attacked on design and defended
  in print, and the result has not been overturned by a better designed trial.
- **Biofield therapies as treatment.** NCCIH states that biofield therapies
  including Reiki, therapeutic touch, qigong and distant healing have not been
  clearly shown to be effective for any health condition, and that there is
  little scientific evidence supporting the existence of therapeutic biofields
  or the ability of practitioners to manipulate them. A 2025 scoping review and
  evidence map in *Journal of Integrative and Complementary Medicine* maps the
  landscape without resolving it. One 2024 randomised controlled trial of
  biofield energy treatment on psychological symptoms was retracted.
- **Chakra spin, chakra colour, chakra opening.** No instrument reads any of
  these. There is no published measurement protocol to fail.
- **Solfeggio frequencies.** 396, 417, 528, 639, 741 and 852 Hz were derived by
  Joseph Puleo, from about 1974, by Pythagorean reduction applied to the Book of
  Numbers, and published with Leonard Horowitz in 1999 in *Healing Codes for the
  Biological Apocalypse*. There is no record of anyone using these specific
  frequencies before the 1990s. They share a name with Guido d'Arezzo's
  eleventh century solfège, which is a singing pedagogy and not a frequency set.
  The word "ancient" attached to these numbers is false, and checkably so.

- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9533499/
- https://library.stlawu.edu/system/files/2020-12/rosa_close_look_at_therapeutic_touch.pdf
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Therapeutic_touch
- https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1089/jicm.2024.0773
- https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11890045/
- https://muzencosmos.com/en/solfeggio-en/solfeggio-history-origins-en/
- https://www.soundmedicineacademy.com/pages/sound-healing-blog/solfeggio-frequencies

## THE HONEST FRAMING FOR A PRODUCT THAT READS SELF REPORT

Three sentences, and they are the whole of it.

**One. The instrument reads what a person reports, and says so.** The input is
a typed story and a set of answers. The output is a structured description of
that input. Nothing in the pipeline touches tissue. That is not a weakness to
be hidden. It is the specification, and the specification is defensible because
self report is a real measurement class with real psychometrics.

**Two. "Energy" is a vocabulary, and the product says which kind.** In physics
energy has units. In this product the word names felt intensity at a reported
location. Those are two different words that share a spelling. A product that
lets a reader think it means the first one is trading on a confusion.

**Three. The map is a map of the report, not of the body.** Nummenmaa licenses
"people consistently report anger here". It does not license "your anger is
stored here". One is a topography of reports with cross cultural replication.
The other is a claim about tissue with no neurological referent.

Twelve findings against the code. Verdict is one of three: **agrees**, the code

should move.

## THE TWELVE, IN FULL

**1. The seat count is a choice and the code does not say so.**
`atuned_src/engine/data/canon.js:66` declares `const BANDS` with seven entries.
Every downstream divisor follows it: `compute.js:125` divides the band
integrity sum by seven. The evidence says the sources give five, six, eight or
more. **Silent.** The code is not wrong, it is unattributed. Nothing needs to
move in the arithmetic. One comment above `canon.js:66` should record that
seven is the Theosophical settlement of a range, dated, so the next person to
propose an eighth seat argues against a recorded decision instead of a habit.

**2. The palette is a 1977 artefact carrying a 2026 justification.**
`canon.js:78` sets `PAL` red through violet in wavelength order. The comment
above it argues the palette from autonomic response and from chroma spread,
which is good design reasoning and is not the palette's provenance. The
provenance is Christopher Hills, *Nuclear Evolution*, 1977. **Contradicts, but
only in the comment.** The colours should not move: they are a working
identity system, contrast checked, with Root at 4.8 to 1 on the dark ground
recorded at `canon.js:77`. The comment should move. Two sentences of
attribution, because a house that argues its palette from the autonomic system
and inherits it from a 1977 New Age paperback has a hole in its own audit trail.

**3. "The chakra is the field generated by a nerve plexus" is a 1927
identification stated as anatomy.** `atuned_src/engine/data/kb.js:19`, glossary
entry Chakra: "The nonphysical field generated by a nerve plexus. It's not a
metaphor, a field with measurable properties. Seven primary chakras, each
generated by its corresponding nerve plexus." Glossary entry Plexus: "The
plexus is the hardware; the chakra is the field it generates."
`practice.js:113` carries `FLOWSEAT`, which pairs each seat with a plexus and a
vertebral level. **Contradicts.** The vertebral levels are good anatomy. The
plexus names are real structures at roughly the right heights. The word
"measurable" is false, and "it's not a metaphor" is the exact sentence the
evidence refuses. **The code should move.** Cut "measurable" and cut "it's not
a metaphor". The defensible form: the seat is a region of the trunk and head,
named for the autonomic plexus that serves it, used as a coordinate.

**4. Twenty eight in-body addresses name something that is not a plexus, and
twenty of them sit at Crown.** Measured, not read: a script over
`atuned_src/engine/data/nodes.js:6` counts twenty five addresses naming central
nervous system structures and three naming something else. By seat: twenty at
Crown, seven at Third Eye, one at Root. Two of the three have no defensible
referent at all. The Self-Exclusion address is placed at the "Superior Sagittal
Sinus", which is a dural venous sinus. It drains blood and carries no nerve
fibres. The Endless Seeking address is placed at the "Ascension Column", which
is not a structure in any anatomy.

And the distribution is lopsided. In-body addresses per seat, measured off the
engine: Root 16, Sacral 16, Solar 16, Heart 15, Throat 12, Third Eye 12, Crown
21. The seat with the thinnest evidence carries the most addresses. The two
seats with the best evidenced somatic correlates, Throat at the pharyngeal and
vagal territory and Third Eye at prefrontal and autonomic control, carry the
fewest.

**Contradicts, and it is the structural finding in this file.** The glossary
asserts plexus generation as the mechanism of every seat. A quarter of the
address table does not have a plexus in it. **Both should move, in this order.**
First the two non referents: Self-Exclusion and Endless Seeking need real
addresses or need to stop naming anatomy. They are named here by key and not by
index, which is the rule this repository already carries.
`Root_08_Unnamed` is already on the owner's open list at
`CLAUDE.md`, so there is precedent for an address held open rather than
faked. Second the mechanism sentence in `kb.js:19`, per finding 3. A model that
says "plexus" and then names the corpus callosum is not wrong about the corpus
callosum. It is wrong about the word "plexus".

**5. The seven frequencies in `FLOWSEAT` are the Puleo set.**
`practice.js:113` carries `hz:963, 852, 741, 639, 528, 417, 396`. That is
Puleo's six plus 963, derived from Pythagorean reduction of the Book of Numbers
around 1974 and published in 1999. **Contradicts** if the product ever presents
them as traditional, and it is a checkable falsehood, which is the worst kind.
**The code should move.** Two options. Cut the `hz` field, which costs nothing
because nothing computes on it. Or keep it and label it by provenance, as the
modern sound work set, dated to the 1990s. Do not carry it unlabelled. The
first person who checks will find Puleo in one search, and the product's whole
credibility rests on being the thing in this category that does not do that.

**6. "Aura: measurable" and "Chakra: measurable properties" are the two
sentences that would fail a regulatory read.** `kb.js:19`, entry Aura: "The
biophoton field generated by the state of the nervous system. Measurable.
Determines social magnetism or repulsion. Reflects the charge load in the
underlying nodes." Entry Biophoton field: "The measurable light field generated
by the state of the nervous system." **Contradicts, in three separate places in
one entry.** Ultraweak photon emission is real and measurable. It is generated
by oxidative chemistry, not by nervous system state. One proof of concept paper
links it to anger. Nothing links it to social magnetism, and nothing links it
to charge at an address. **The code should move.** The word "Measurable." as a
free standing sentence has to go. If the concept is kept, the honest entry is:
felt presence, reported by others, with no instrument behind it.

**7. Coherence is defined as "measurable" and what is measured is a
questionnaire.** `kb.js:19`, entry Coherence: "Measurable reduction of internal
contradiction." Entry CQ: "(Intention × Integrity) ÷ Resistance. Baseline: 100."
The arithmetic is at `compute.js:124` to `compute.js:140`. **Contradicts, in
one word.** CQ is computed, deterministically and reproducibly, from
self reported answers. Computed is not measured. A thermometer measures. A
formula over a questionnaire computes. **The code should move**, and it is a
one word fix: "Computed reduction of internal contradiction", plus one clause
saying from what.

**8. "Charge held in tissue" and "release at the address" have no neurological
referent.** `kb.js:19`, entry Charge: "Stored survival energy held at a node.
Installed by a stress response that never finished." Entry Release: "The
discharge of stored charge through the nervous system. Physical and observable:
heat, shaking, tears, breath shifts." Entry Reading-as-release: "reading a
properly constructed Letting Go sequence produces somatic release at the
anatomical address the sequence specifies." The arithmetic that implements it
is `compute.js:48` to `compute.js:57`, where `n.held` is charge times
susceptibility times an integrity relief term.

**Contradicts.** The *BJPsych Bulletin* audit is explicit: there is no
consistent neurological evidence that trauma is stored in muscles, organs or
disconnected brain regions. The observable part of the Release entry is fine:
heat, shaking, tears and breath shifts are real and reportable. The word
"stored" is doing work the evidence does not support, and "produces somatic
release at the anatomical address" is a mechanism claim with a named location
in it, which is the most falsifiable form the claim could take.

**Which should move.** Not the arithmetic. `compute.js` is a bookkeeping system
over a self report vector and it is internally consistent, reproducible, and
already carries `unread` at `compute.js:153` so it refuses to read a person who
has entered nothing. That is good instrument discipline. What should move is the
mechanism language around it. The defensible frame, and it costs nothing:

> An address is a label for a reported pattern at a reported location. Charge is
> the reported intensity. A release is a procedure a person runs, after which
> the reported intensity is lower. The instrument tracks the report.

Every sentence there is true, every one is checkable, and the product loses
nothing it can actually deliver.

**9. The Body page is the best evidenced surface in the product, and the code
already knows it.** `atuned_src/ui/map.js:344` carries the comment "THE
NUMMENMAA TREATMENT", and the implementation does exactly what the reference
does: fills the silhouette, makes the field continuous with one blur over the
whole heat group rather than a gradient per mark, and clips to the skin so the
field stops at the body. `canon.js:108`, `CHILD`, gives each of the nine axes a
`loc` in plain physical language: lower back and gut for Fear, upper abdomen for
Anger, centre chest for Sad. Against the Nummenmaa maps: anger and fear in the
upper torso and head, sadness with diminished limb sensation, disgust at the
abdomen and throat.

**Agrees.** Fear at lower back and gut is slightly lower than the reference
puts it. Anger at upper abdomen against upper torso, arms and head is a partial
match. Disgust at "lower abdomen, skin" against abdomen and throat is a partial
match. Nothing needs to move. What should be added is one line of attribution
above `canon.js:108` naming the 2014 *PNAS* paper, because this is the one place
in the product where a design decision has a replicated experiment behind it and
the file does not say so.

**10. The pain patterns assert aetiology the evidence does not carry, and the
evidence offers a better sentence for free.** `practice.js:140`, `PAINREG`.
Each region carries a `common` list, which is a list of complaints, and a
`pattern` line, which is a causal claim. Examples, verbatim: feet, "Contact with
the ground refused. The root address will not discharge." Legs, "Ground not
trusted. Fear at the root refusing to let weight down." Torso, "Anger held at
the celiac and grief at the cardiac."

**Contradicts.** These are aetiological claims about named complaints, some of
them medical: plantar fasciitis, sciatica, thyroid strain, frozen shoulder,
reflux, IBS. Saying that plantar fasciitis is contact with the ground refused
is a diagnosis of cause, and it is offered to a person who may have a tendon
problem, a thyroid nodule or an ulcer.

**And the fix is an upgrade, not a retreat.** The fear avoidance literature
gives the real mechanism and it is better copy than the invented one. Fear of
movement, attention to pain, and avoidance behaviour predict disability and
predict the transition from an acute episode to a chronic one. So the legs line
becomes something like: "Pain here often comes with avoiding weight on it.
Avoidance predicts how long it lasts." That is mechanical, physical, short, and
has Vlaeyen and Linton behind it. **The code should move**, and it moves toward
a stronger sentence.

**11. The one measurable lever in the product is not instrumented.** Slow
breathing at roughly four and a half to seven breaths per minute raises HRV
through the baroreflex, and both SDNN and RMSSD rise. `atuned_src/engine/data/practice.js`
carries breath practices with durations and tiers. Nothing in the file carries a
breath rate. **Silent.** This is the only place where the product could hand a
person a number with a mechanism behind it and does not. The move is small:
carry the rate on the practice, state the range, and state that the individual
optimum moves between sessions, because it did in two thirds of participants in
one test retest study. Cost is a field on a data row. It puts one genuinely
evidenced quantity in a product currently built entirely of computed ones.

**12. `accuracy()` reports confidence, and a reader will take it for validity.**
`compute.js:297`. The function is honest about what it is: the header at
`compute.js:281` states the fit, the layer ablation, the additive against
multiplicative comparison, and the mean absolute error of 5.68 against 7.28.
The output is a percentage plus a band. **Silent, and the silence is the
defect.** What it computes is how much evidence the instrument has: held
addresses, law coverage, expression fill. What a person reads off a percentage
labelled accuracy is how likely the reading is to be true about them. Those are
different quantities and the second one has never been tested. Given the Barnum
finding, a person's own agreement cannot supply it either.

The move is a word and a clause. Not "accuracy" but "coverage", or
"confidence", with the clause: how much you have given the instrument to read,
not how right it is. Validity needs test retest, convergence with an
established measure, and discriminant power, and none of the three exists yet.
That is a research programme, not a copy fix, and it should be named as one on
the backlog rather than implied by a percentage.

# PART SIX. CLAIMS THE RESEARCH TEMPTS AND THE PRODUCT CANNOT SUPPORT

Each of these is reachable from a real paper by one step of overreach. That is
what makes them dangerous. The step is named each time.

**"Regulates your nervous system."** Tempted by the polyvagal literature and by
HRV biofeedback. The overreach: HRV rises during slow breathing. Regulation is
a clinical claim about a persistent state. The product does not measure HRV and
does not follow anyone past the session. Safe form: names what a person reports,
before and after.

**"Your nervous system is in shutdown."** Tempted by polyvagal's dorsal state.
The overreach: asserting the single most contested premise in a theory that
thirty nine physiologists have published against. Safe form: describe the
reported state in the product's own vocabulary and do not borrow the neurology.

**"Releases stored trauma."** Tempted by van der Kolk and by Somatic
Experiencing. The overreach: two words. "Stored" has no neurological referent,
and "trauma" is a clinical category this product must not enter. Safe form:
reported intensity at a reported location, and whether it moved.

**"Equivalent to therapy."** Already ruled and already protected.
`DECISIONS.md` carries the rule: throughput, never outcome, and the copy says
"as many patterns as", never "the same as" and never "instead of".
`atuned_src/engine/plan.js:270` implements it. This research does not reopen it.
It strengthens it, because the Somatic Experiencing base is one good trial and
a mixed quality review, so the comparison target is thinner than assumed.

**"Measurable coherence" and "measurable field."** Tempted by the fact that
several adjacent things genuinely are measurable. The overreach is borrowing the
word across the boundary. Findings 6 and 7 above.

**"Clears the cause of your back pain."** Tempted by the fear avoidance
literature, which is real. The overreach: fear avoidance predicts disability
and chronicity. It does not establish that a named pain has a psychological
cause in a given person, and the named complaints in `PAINREG` include
conditions with structural and endocrine causes. Finding 10 above.

**"Ancient frequencies."** Tempted by nothing at all, and false. The numbers
date to the 1990s. Finding 5 above.

**"Raises your IQ" or "restores focus."** Tempted by the scarcity literature.
The overreach: that literature is contested, with a reanalysis in *Science* and
failed replications. Not available at any strength.

**"Your reading is ninety percent accurate."** Tempted by `accuracy()`. The
overreach: confusing evidence coverage with validity. Finding 12 above.

**And the one that is not a claim about the body at all.** The product must not
imply that a person who does the work and does not improve has failed at it.
The growth mindset literature is the cautionary case: a real but very small
effect, at Cohen's d of roughly 0.05 to 0.10, was sold as a general mechanism
for achievement, and the cost landed on the people for whom it did not work.
This product carries the same shape of risk and has already built one guard
against it, `cqCeiling()` at `compute.js:263`, which computes what coherence
would read with every charge gone. That function is the honest answer to
"will this work for me", because for three of the six ICPs the answer measured
off the engine is that release moves the number by roughly one point or less.
Keep it visible.
