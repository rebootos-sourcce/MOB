The cause

Mindset programming.

A set of instructions laid down early, still running, and running you. It was written by things that happened to you before you could read, and it has never been reviewed. It is not a mood and it is not a personality.

What is actually happening

Your story fires as a threat, and the mind leaves the body.

Something happened. You made a meaning out of it. The meaning hardened into a rule. The rule now fires as a stress response, and under a stress response the mind goes up and the body goes quiet. That split is the injury.

The mind runs a story the body is paying for. The body holds a charge the mind will not look at. They stop speaking to each other. Everything on the list above is that gap, reported from one side or the other.

The measure of the gap is coherence. Coherence is the alignment between what arrives from the world, how you read it, what you intend, and what you then do. Four stations, one circuit. When coherence is low the circuit leaks, and the leak shows up as mental, physical and spiritual disease. Every practice you have tried was reaching for coherence without a way to read it.

What it is for

This is a purpose based product.

It exists to close the gap between your mind and your body, and to hand you your own instructions so you can change them. That is the whole purpose and there is not a second one underneath it.

It is not built to hold your attention. It is a mirror and a set of instruments. It says: this is you, this is what is running you, from the top to the bottom, and here is the order to take it apart in.
