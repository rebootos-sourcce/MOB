The unit

One pattern is one thought line.

A pattern is one thought line, at one address, by way of one channel. Reading it in thought spends it. Nothing here asks you to say anything out loud.

What a pattern takes apart is a kink: one address holding a stress response. Clusters are built out of kinks and networks are built out of clusters, so a rung on this ladder is a rate at which you can integrate the kink, the cluster and the network. The chain is on the about page, with the arithmetic under each rung of it.

  • Lines21,400 in total The field holds 112 addresses and 107 of them can be released, at four channels each and fifty lines a channel.
  • Run25 patterns at most One release run. The cap is a ceiling and a run built under it costs what it says before you begin it.
  • Reruns0 patterns Ground you have already opened is free to run again, as often as you like, on every tier.

So a tier is a rate of new ground and nothing else. At 400 patterns a month, the 21,400 lines in the product take fifty four months to walk.

The gift

A hundred patterns, before you decide anything.

A new person gets 100 patterns, free, with everything visible. Four full runs at the 25 cap, spent on your own material, and then you know what this is rather than having been told.

Nothing expires. Unspent patterns bank until there are enough for a run, so a weekly grant smaller than a run adds up instead of draining.

Tier four

For somebody leading other people.

Tier four carries the same 1,200 patterns a month as tier three, so the volume is not what separates them. What it adds is the cohort lead suite: manage profiles, build rituals and hold accountability for the people you lead.

A lead sees the outputs and not the tools. Fetters, saboteurs, complexes, hyper complexes and the analytics behind them. Not the spiritual material, and not the story cloud: the story is the person's own words, and the record and the story are never held joined.

How sight is granted

The person grants it by name. The list of who has sight sits on their record where they can read it, and one move takes it back. There is no silent default and no grant that arrives with a subscription.

What it is worth

Measured as throughput, against our own lowest figure.

The codex behind the engine puts a therapy session at one to six patterns released, and half an hour of meditation or breathwork at nine to eighteen. A month of daily half hours is 270 to 540 patterns.

Tier one is 400 a month, which sits inside that month. That is the sentence the whole table exists to support, and it is a rate of release rather than a claim about a life.

Two rules on saying it

Throughput, never outcome. How many patterns come off is checkable against the table the engine reads. What a life does afterwards is not, and nothing here will put a figure on it.

The low end gets said. A range of one to six quoted at six is the most flattering reading of our own number, and it is the first thing a hostile reader checks.

Stopping

Leaving costs nothing and takes nothing with it.

A cancellation destroys nothing. The plan is one field on your record, the record is yours, and your reading, your stories and your imprints stay where they are.

Nothing here expires, nothing counts down, and no figure in the product is taken away for a day off. A person has to be able to stop and be glad they used it, which is the line this whole thing is built behind, and a ladder that made leaving expensive would be a different product.

A card or a plan is changed from one button, on a page held by the processor. No card detail is ever typed into the instrument, because the instrument has no network in it to carry one.
