Mission

Find the limiters. Release the limiters.

The capacity is already in you. Most of it is spent holding a set of old instructions down, and what is left over after that is what you have been calling your ability.

About

A somatic reading instrument.

Atüned, a neurosomatic instrument that identifies the point of origin of a stress response and integrates the kink, the cluster and the network built on top of it. In doing so it brings inner peace mentally and physically. It accelerates the mind body connection and it naturally releases stress from the body.

It tunes moral integrity against the 21 laws of integrity, and the result is vital energy: the capacity that holding a pattern was spending, back in the hands of the person who was spending it.

You write what happened, in your own words. The engine reads the charge out of the words and lands it at a named plexus or a named nerve. Then it hands you the order to take it apart in.

It runs as one file in your browser. No account, no server, and no request of any kind while you use it.

Vision

A person reads their own instructions the way they read a blood panel.

A person can already get a figure for their sleep, their pulse and their blood, and can act on it without asking anybody's permission. Nothing hands them the instructions running underneath, so the mind's half of the bill is still guessed at, and guessing is why the same month keeps arriving.

The reading of that half belongs on the same footing as a lab result. One figure for coherence. An address for every load. A place in the body for each address. And every table the arithmetic runs on open for inspection, because a mirror you cannot take apart is not a mirror.

A practitioner sees it when the person grants it, by name, with the grant visible on the record and revocable in one move.

Why

A kink becomes a cluster. A cluster becomes a network.

  1. Your body is expressive by default. Movement, breath, voice, appetite. Nothing held.
  2. Stress makes it contract. That part is ordinary and the contraction is supposed to pass.
  3. You identify with the contraction. A bias forms in the mind, and a kink of resistance forms in the shape of that stress response.
  4. The response gets conditioned. Practised often enough that it fires without the event that taught it.
  5. The node turns into a cluster. Three or four addresses now contract together as one structure.
  6. Clusters form networks. The body tenses in different locations at once, because the structures are wired to each other.
  7. Now the problem begins. You procrastinate more, feel anxious more, more shy, more depressed, more overwhelmed. The nervous system has kinks all over it, blocking the natural energy flow of the body.
  8. The instrument finds the point of origin. It integrates the kink, the cluster and the network, in that order, starting where the sequence started.

That sequence is computed, not asserted

The engine builds the reading in exactly those rungs. A kink is one address carrying charge, and 4 of 10 counts as loaded. A cluster is three or four addresses contracting together at a mean of 3.7 of 10. A network pairs two clusters inside one of six architectures, and then stacks those pairs, and because clusters in one architecture are seated at different places the network is the first rung that shows up in more than one part of the body.

Every threshold in that paragraph is readable inside the product, on the same screen as the reading it produced. Check the chain against your own life and against the arithmetic that drew it.
