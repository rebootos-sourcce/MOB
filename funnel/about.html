About

A somatic diagnostic instrument.

Atüned, a neurosomatic instrument that reads where stress has written itself into a body. It tunes moral integrity against the 21 laws of integrity, and returns the vital energy that holding a pattern was spending.

You write what happened, in your own words. The engine reads the charge out of the words and lands it at a named plexus or a named nerve. Then it hands you the order to take it apart in.

It runs as one file in your browser. No account, no server, and no request of any kind while you use it.

  • Not a coach. It has no opinion about your life.
  • Not a guide. It reads what is running, not who is running it.
  • Not a friend. An instrument that flatters you is a broken one.
  • Not a practice app. The practice comes out of your own material.
  • Not a meditation timer. Nothing here is timed.
  • Not a personality test. A type is a label. This is a reading.

What is first, stated so it can be checked

Gamified self development is a crowded shelf. Every product on it fills its character sheet from what a person types in about themselves, and counts upward from zero.

This one derives the sheet from a reading of the person's own written story, and it runs the gauge the other way round. The ceiling is stated first, computed from that person's own reading, and the figure that moves is the drag standing between. Both halves are checkable, and checking them is two questions: does the product read the sheet out of a story rather than taking it as input, and does it show a ceiling before it shows a score.

Vision

A person reads their own instructions the way they read a blood panel.

A person can already get a figure for their sleep, their pulse and their blood, and can act on it without asking anybody's permission. Nothing hands them the instructions running underneath, so the mind's half of the bill is still guessed at, and guessing is why the same month keeps arriving.

The reading of that half belongs on the same footing as a lab result. One figure for coherence. An address for every load. A place in the body for each address. And every table the arithmetic runs on open for inspection, because a mirror you cannot take apart is not a mirror.

A practitioner sees it when the person grants it, by name, with the grant visible on the record and revocable in one move.

Why

The mind runs a story the body is paying for.

Something happened. You made a meaning out of it. The meaning hardened into a rule, and the rule now fires as a stress response. Under a stress response the mind goes up and the body goes quiet. That split is the injury, and everything filed under stress is a report from one side of it.

The measure of the split is coherence: the alignment between what arrives from the world, how you read it, what you intend, and what you then do. Four stations, one circuit. A low reading means the circuit leaks, and the leak is paid for in the gut, the jaw, the lower back and the sleep.

Which is the argument against addition. A practice laid on top of a leak raises the load on both sides of it, and a month later the reading has not moved. The leak is the thing with a number on it, and that number moves when something comes off.

Why now

The measuring habit arrived and left the mind out.

Three things are true now that were not true when this shelf was built.

ReadingPeople already read their own bodies off a figure. A decade of wearables made that ordinary, and none of that stack reads the instructions.

The last one is the change nobody advertises. For most of the time this shelf has existed, a product that read somebody's nervous system had to send it somewhere to do the reading.
