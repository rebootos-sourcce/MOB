What is Atüned actually doing?

It reads what you write about your life, in your own words. In the Atüned model, words point to places in the body, and it shows you each place it found.

On your first story you answer each place, yes or no. A yes sends it into a release: a short run of lines, read or heard, aimed at that place. Afterwards it asks what changed, and keeps your answer.

It is an instrument for watching yourself, not an authority on who you are.

How does Atüned know where to look?

It starts with what you actually wrote. The engine reads your words against a table. Each word it knows points to an address. The Atüned model has 112 of them, and every address has a place in the body.

An address organises what you wrote. It is not a medical diagnosis, and nothing in your body is measured to find it.

How do I know the reading is accurate?

You do not have to take it on faith.

On your first story it shows you what it heard, one place at a time, and marks the ones that are its guess. You answer each one, Yes or Not me. If it missed something, press Correct it and say what is off.

You are the authority on your own experience.

What happens if Atüned gets me wrong?

Tell it. Not me keeps that place out of your release, and your no is kept on your record.

Correct it reads what you write the same way it read your story. Each place it finds there gets its own Yes and Not me.

A wrong read tells you about the instrument, not about you.

What is the difference between release and reframe?

A release carries two kinds of line, both aimed at the same place.

Release lines work on the old pattern. They take the limit off.

Reframe lines give it a new direction. They put the opposite quality in, where the limit was.

What should I expect to feel?

There is no right feeling. You might notice less pressure, more room, movement, a new view of it, or nothing at first.

A busy mind can miss a small change, so do not force one. Feeling nothing is information too.

After a release it asks what changed. Nothing changed and Not sure are kept exactly like Something moved.

Why would I use Atüned again after the first session?

A first release is short. A story often lights more places than it works, and those are still there.

Release those, write a new story, or run one again and see whether your answer to what changed is different.

Does Atüned remember what I have already worked on?

Yes, in this browser. It keeps your stories exactly as you typed them, what it read in each one, every yes and no you gave, and every release with what you said changed.

Each thing it read stays marked as your words or its guess, so a guess is never stored as something you said.

Clear this browser's data and the record goes with it, unless you exported a copy first.

Is Atüned therapy, coaching, meditation or a personality test?

None of those. You use it to watch yourself and to practise.

It reads what is running, not who is running it.

It reads self report. A clinician reads a person, and those are different jobs. If you are under care, stay under it.

What happens to my information?

It stays in this browser, on your device: your stories, your answers, what the instrument read and every release. It does not sync, and nobody at Atüned can read it.

The free test makes no request at all, and a story typed into it is never saved.

Some things leave, and only when you choose them:

  • An account. Your sign in details, if you make one.
  • Help. A message you send us, posted to a channel on our Discord server.
  • Dictation. Your speech, which your browser's maker turns into text.
  • A voice. The lines of a release, if you pick a voice that runs online.
  • Payment. Your card, typed on Stripe's page and never into Atüned.
