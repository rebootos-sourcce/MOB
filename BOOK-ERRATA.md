## Contradictions inside the book

**1. One address, both confirmed and unvalidated.**
Line 1895 prints `Alderman's Nerve · Working Address · Unvalidated`. Line 1899
prints `Alderman's Nerve. Located in the feminine left side, posterior lower
pelvic region. Confirmed address.` Four lines apart. One of the two words is
wrong.

**2. The evidence tier contradicts the front matter.**
Line 481 states, of the plexus addressing, `No published research does that`,
and cites the meta-analysis of two hundred and two studies finding no reliable
autonomic signature. Line 9500 grades the same claim `High confidence, observed
through direct somatic mapping across thousands of releases. The address is
measurable.`

Both are true under the book's own definition at line 9459, where high
confidence means documented across thousands of sessions. But `the address is
measurable` is not that, and a reader who reaches Appendix A without the front
matter reads an empirical finding where a practice record was meant.

Suggested wording: practitioner-observed, unreplicated.

**3. The Positive Intelligence roster has the wrong member.**
Line 9480 lists Controller, Victim, Perfectionist, Pleaser, Hyper-Achiever,
Hyper-Rational, Hyper-Vigilant, Restless, Stickler and Judge. That is ten,
which is the right count, but Perfectionist is not one of them and Avoider is.
The engine has it right. This sits in the one appendix whose whole value is
that it is scrupulous.

**4. The archetypes are called Jungian in the text and are not.**
The running text says the twelve Jungian archetypes. The Attribution appendix
states plainly that Jung never enumerated twelve and that the roster is Carol
Pearson's. The appendix is correct. The label in the text is the first thing a
hostile reviewer takes.

**5. Two appendices are cited that do not exist.**
Appendix G2 at line 2129 and Appendix G9 at line 2354. The book has Appendices
A to E. G is a key prefix used inside Appendix D, not an appendix.

**6. Two numbers wearing one name.**
Line 3807: `There are 108 chakras in the body. 54 on the left side, 54 on the
right side.` That is a different claim from the 108 somatic nodes, using the
same number, inside a book whose architecture is 112. The number appears 33
times and 31 of those pair it correctly. Line 1251 is the other loose one, in
dictation.

**7. Hyper-complexes counted two ways.**
The glossary says eight. Every other passage says six, and the engine has six.

**8. Two koshas rosters.**
Chapter 10 names eight. The glossary names six, and they are not the same six.
A ninth term appears in neither list.

**9. Chapter 13 states three archetype counts in one paragraph.**
Thirteen, twelve and fifteen. The glossary later settles on twelve without
retracting the other two.

**10. Chapter 23 counts nine and prints ten.**
It says nine laws, restates that Peace is the ninth, and its own table runs to
ten numbered elements. One element's heading reads Wonder while its body is
entirely about Beauty, and it duplicates another element's symbol.

**11. The CQ scale is conflated in its own defining sentence.**
Lines 361 and 2341 both say the reading is 100 when every law is ten of ten,
mixing the instrument's 0 to 10 scale with the display's 0 to 100 in one
sentence.

**12. Three formulas presented as one.**
The front-matter glossary prints the ratio as the formula. Chapter 9 says
plainly that the ratio `is not the operational form`, and names the estimator
instead. The book is right in chapter 9; the glossary needs to match it.

## Where the engine and the book disagree, and the engine moved

`The Still Mind (2,500)`, `The Open Heart (3,500)`, `Clear Perception (4,500)`,

The owner then ruled the figure at **fifteen thousand**, against his own anchor
of fifteen thousand cleared by the age of fifty, with the beginning of nirvana
around ten thousand and breaking duality around five hundred. His ruling governs
and the engine now carries fifteen thousand.

So the book and the product now disagree, and it is the book that should move.
Against a total of fifteen thousand, every threshold the book prints is an exact
thirtieth: 2,500 is five, 3,500 is seven, 4,500 is nine, 10,000 is twenty. Only
11,664 is not, and it is thirty thirtieths of 11,664 rather than of the total
the same chapter implies. The square of the node count is a satisfying figure
and it breaks the only pattern the other four hold to. **The book's to settle.**

**The threshold names, unresolved and the owner's.** The book names them The
Still Mind, The Open Heart, Clear Perception, The Ground and Ascension. The
engine names the same five distances Entry, Buddha nature, Integration, Field

**The twelve masters are never listed as twelve.** The book states that "the 12

only as poles and never on the coordinate system, which is the other half of

**The mirror axis is filled, under a stated assumption.** Lao Tzu carries

**Which of the two should move: the book, or the engine.** The engine, once he

## The Mark Measured Against Its Own Claims

Not a disagreement between the codex and the engine. A disagreement between a
file's comments and the file's own arithmetic, recorded here because it is the
same failure mode and the same remedy: measure the thing rather than describe
it.

`tools/awareness.js`, first pass, claimed in its header that every proportion
is a power of the golden ratio. Measured against the built curves, five of its
statements were false.

**The stroke did not cross itself.** The whole description of the mark is one
stroke that crosses itself. The ascent and the exit centrelines never
intersected: nearest approach one unit, at a height of 29 out of 100. The ink
is wide enough that the two legs overlapped, so the rendered picture showed a
closed eye and the geometry did not have one. Three passes of looking at it did
not find this, because looking cannot. 342 degrees of turn is the first value
that actually crosses. 315 does not.

**`CROSS` named a place the drawing does not have.** Exported as 38.1966 with
a comment calling it where the stroke meets itself. Nine and a half units from
anything that happens anywhere on the mark. It is solved off the built curves
now and returns null when there is no crossing, which is an answer.

**`EYE` was a phi shaped costume on a number that was not the eye.** Written
as `R*2-R*PHI/PHI`, which multiplies and divides by the same number and cancels
to exactly `R`. It read 9.017 where the eye was 9.918, and it was exported, in
a file whose header says every proportion is a power of the ratio. This is the
worst of the five, because it is the failure the whole golden ratio literature
warns about, committed in the source rather than in a marketing document.

**The mark was reversible.** `width(0)` and `width(1)` were the same number to
the last digit and both terminals were the same flat chord. A stranger could
not tell which end the brush started at, because the geometry did not say. A
single stroke that carries no direction is a shape. It lands at a chord now and
sweeps to nothing.

**The brush was told the wrong story about where it was.** The width ran across
six Beziers by their index rather than by their length, so the four short arcs
of the turn took two thirds of the profile and each long leg took a sixth. That
is why the exit kept rendering as a wire whatever was adjusted at that end. It
runs on arc length now.

**And one thing the art director got wrong, recorded for the same reason.** The
prescribed constants were a brush of twice the height over phi to the fifth on
a loop at the height over phi to the fourth. Every ratio in them was exact, the
eye landed on one over phi cubed and then on one over root five, and the thing
they drew was a fat letter P. The defect list was right and the prescription
was not. The constants are the height over phi to the fifth for both the
display radius and the brush, which is his drawing measured.

**Whose call: mine, and done.** Nothing here is the owner's material. It is a
file that described itself incorrectly.
