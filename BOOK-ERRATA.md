# The Mechanics of Being, errata

Every item here was located in the manuscript before it was written down.
Line numbers are against the text extraction of the current edition, so they
move if the book moves; the quotation is the anchor.

Findings that could not be located in the text were discarded rather than
listed, including two quotations that other readers reported and that do not
appear in the book.

Sorted by what it costs the book if a reader catches it.

---

## Contradictions inside the book

**1. One address, both confirmed and unvalidated.**
Line 1895 prints `Alderman's Nerve · Working Address · Unvalidated`. Line 1899
prints `Alderman's Nerve. Located in the feminine left side, posterior lower
pelvic region. Confirmed address.` Four lines apart. One of the two words is
wrong.

**2. The evidence tier contradicts the front matter.**
Line 481 states, of the plexus addressing, `No published research does that`,
and cites the meta-analysis of two hundred and two studies finding no reliable
autonomic signature. Line 9500 grades the same claim `High confidence, observed
through direct somatic mapping across thousands of releases. The address is
measurable.`

Both are true under the book's own definition at line 9459, where high
confidence means documented across thousands of sessions. But `the address is
measurable` is not that, and a reader who reaches Appendix A without the front
matter reads an empirical finding where a practice record was meant.

Suggested wording: practitioner-observed, unreplicated.

**3. The Positive Intelligence roster has the wrong member.**
Line 9480 lists Controller, Victim, Perfectionist, Pleaser, Hyper-Achiever,
Hyper-Rational, Hyper-Vigilant, Restless, Stickler and Judge. That is ten,
which is the right count, but Perfectionist is not one of them and Avoider is.
The engine has it right. This sits in the one appendix whose whole value is
that it is scrupulous.

**4. The archetypes are called Jungian in the text and are not.**
The running text says the twelve Jungian archetypes. The Attribution appendix
states plainly that Jung never enumerated twelve and that the roster is Carol
Pearson's. The appendix is correct. The label in the text is the first thing a
hostile reviewer takes.

**5. Two appendices are cited that do not exist.**
Appendix G2 at line 2129 and Appendix G9 at line 2354. The book has Appendices
A to E. G is a key prefix used inside Appendix D, not an appendix.

**6. Two numbers wearing one name.**
Line 3807: `There are 108 chakras in the body. 54 on the left side, 54 on the
right side.` That is a different claim from the 108 somatic nodes, using the
same number, inside a book whose architecture is 112. The number appears 33
times and 31 of those pair it correctly. Line 1251 is the other loose one, in
dictation.

**7. Hyper-complexes counted two ways.**
The glossary says eight. Every other passage says six, and the engine has six.

**8. Two koshas rosters.**
Chapter 10 names eight. The glossary names six, and they are not the same six.
A ninth term appears in neither list.

**9. Chapter 13 states three archetype counts in one paragraph.**
Thirteen, twelve and fifteen. The glossary later settles on twelve without
retracting the other two.

**10. Chapter 23 counts nine and prints ten.**
It says nine laws, restates that Peace is the ninth, and its own table runs to
ten numbered elements. One element's heading reads Wonder while its body is
entirely about Beauty, and it duplicates another element's symbol.

**11. The CQ scale is conflated in its own defining sentence.**
Lines 361 and 2341 both say the reading is 100 when every law is ten of ten,
mixing the instrument's 0 to 10 scale with the display's 0 to 100 in one
sentence.

**12. Three formulas presented as one.**
The front-matter glossary prints the ratio as the formula. Chapter 9 says
plainly that the ratio `is not the operational form`, and names the estimator
instead. The book is right in chapter 9; the glossary needs to match it.

## Arithmetic

**13. A vision claim runs backwards.**
A 20/10 to 20/20 improvement is stated as a gain. 20/10 is sharper than 20/20.

**14. A client total sits far outside the stated rate.**
A 3,200-pattern total for one twelve-week client is repeated four times,
against a stated average of about 400 a month.

## Safety, and these are the ones to fix first

**15. The word suicidal with no stop line.**
Line 5649 gives every axis an action-threshold word and sadness gets
`suicidal`. The chapter runs the ladder as a working tool. No stop line appears
anywhere near it.

Line 10969 records that the screen exists, and where: the Practice Manual. A
reader who owns only this book meets that rung with nothing.

**16. The crown link is unscreened here.**
The same Colophon entry says the manual bars it for psychosis, mania, bipolar
history, acute distress, dissociators, and a chain not held from the root up.
None of that is printed in this book, where the practice is described.

**17. Five clinical mappings carry no referral line.**
The psychopathy passage says refer to licensed clinical professionals. The
mappings for borderline, obsessive-compulsive, depression, anxiety, bipolar and
dissociation do not. The standard exists in the book and is applied once.

**18. Two cure accounts with no case-level caution.**
Carmen's stroke, resolved by a thirty-minute phone call, with no emergency
line. Keeley's psoriasis and Noelle's tinnitus, presented as gone, with the
underlying conditions reframed as nerve issues and no referral.

**19. One passage that can land as blame.**
Line 1310 says defending your innocence loads the same as guilt. To a reader
who was actually harmed, that reads as a verdict on them. It needs the sentence
that distinguishes self-protective denial from the mechanic being described.

## Still open, because guessing would be worse

**25. The operational CQ form.** The book names the estimator as
`CQ = √(Intention × Integrity) × 10 / (1 + 0.6·SQ/10)`. The engine computes the
ratio. Before this can be implemented, SQ has to be pinned to a field: the book
calls it `node load plus integrity gaps`, and the engine has DQ as a sum and
SQm as a mean. They are not the same number and the formula divides by one of
them.

**26. DQ as derived.** The book says DQ equals 100 minus CQ and is not an
instrument. The engine returns DQ as the raw sum of held charge, and the whole
interface reads it that way. Changing it changes what the word means on every
screen.

**27. The release channels, and the book disagrees with itself three ways.**

The verb chain at line 1023 gives six: believing, perceiving, thinking,
behaving, acting, feeling. The printed card at line 8123 gives nine: those six
plus speaking, saying and doing. Chapter 33 gives a third six.

The printed card runs right channel before left, at lines 8122, 8174, 8227 and
8279. The ruling on file says left then right.

A full card is two hundred statements over one address, at line 1483. The
engine runs four passes and names no channel at all.

The engine also uses arithmetic that appears nowhere in the book: twenty one
percent off the held value on release, and the coherent opposite installed at
sixty two percent of what was removed. Those numbers are mine from the rebuild.

**27b. The rebound is copy, not behaviour.** The text about day four and a half
and day twenty seven is correct against the book's own maths, and nothing in
the engine implements a time based decay, so a person who comes back later sees
no rebound.

**28. Four kinds have no coherent opposite.** Laws, domains, masks and seats.
Every child fetter has one. Writing them is forty-six new assertions.
