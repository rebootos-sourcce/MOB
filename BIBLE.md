# The Bible

What has been ruled and is no longer open.

A thing in here is not re-argued without new evidence, and the evidence has to
be named. `DECISIONS.md` is the owner's rulings as he made them, with the open
items marked open. This is the settled subset, stated as law, so the team has
one place to check before spending a round on something already decided.

Anything the team proposes and the owner accepts is added here, in his words
where he gave them.

## Voice

No em dashes. Anywhere. Including commit messages and documentation.

Never say 108. The count stated to users is 112.

Sentence case. No all caps UI copy.

Mechanical and precise. No soft wellness language. Short sentences. Physical
metaphors only.

Grounded and direct. Humble. Insightful. Meaningful context. Nothing abstract.

Menus: one word, and the word describes exactly what the thing does.

One word per concept. A real distinction keeps its own word; held, installed
and firing are three states and stay three words.

A reading is not a score. Never print a count against a total.

A label this product puts on a person carries a definition, the behaviour it
produces, and the direction out of it. A word like Severe with nothing
attached is a judgement. The same word with those three is a reading.

## Form

Icons are ring, not fill.

If it has a name it has an icon. The icon has a family. The family has a
colour. The colour is associated with something meaningful.

Muted palette, argued from autonomic response.

The seven seats are the colour system:

    Root #C4635E  Sacral #D19255  Solar #D4BC70  Heart #6FC5A3
    Throat #65B8D4  3rd Eye #8296DB  Crown #A98BCE
    accent #7EB8D4  alarm #FF2E1F

The alarm colour is reserved for something being wrong. A high reading on a
scale where high is good is never the alarm colour.

Four lightings: Dark, Snow, Punch, Glass. Each genuinely its own, and the
design gate proves it.

A named thing renders as a badge: the icon carries the percent as a ring, and
the pill at its lower right carries the number.

Top level navigation is tabs, not buttons. No boxes.

## Architecture

`source.html` and `engine.js` are build products. Never edited.

MANIFEST decides load order and nothing else may. Data before engine, engine
before renderers, renderers before ui.

The engine may not touch the host. No `document`, `window`, `navigator`,
`localStorage`, `fetch`, `new Image`.

TAB integers are identity. They are persisted and compared and they never
renumber. Display order is `TABDEF` and moves freely. Compass is 8 and
Settings is 9 because they were appended for this reason.

Port, do not rebuild. The arithmetic core keeps its bodies and signatures.

Validate at the boundary and never lie about a failure. A missing field is an
older profile and is filled from the blank. A wrong type or out of range field
is refused by name and never silently clamped.

Every write that can fail reports through `status()`. A control never claims
success before it has it.

Reproduce a failure before fixing it, and re-measure after. Check the tool
against a known good case first: a tool that lies is worse than no tool.

Reduced motion gets the end state, not a faster animation.

## The product

The app opens on Field.

The name is Atüned. The subtitle is Source OS, under the wordmark, in the
accent.

It is a somatic diagnostic instrument and a mirror. It says: this is you, this
is everything that is running you, from the top to the bottom.

Coherence is the alignment between the world around you, what arrives from it,
the way you read what arrives, the intention behind it, and the action that
follows. Four stations, one circuit, and the instrument reads every register
in it.

The field is the geometry of awareness's radiance. It is harmonic, so patterns
sit at registers rather than anywhere, and what radiates outward carries the
colour of the loudest seat.

Everything is exposed. Transparency is held as a natural law, not a policy: a
mirror you cannot inspect is not a mirror.

## Commercial

Sight is not for sale. Every tier including free sees the whole reading. What
a tier buys is new ground.

Two months free is out. Whether there is any annual discount at all is open
and is the owner's.

The record never carries a customer id, subscription id, email, key, secret or
token. The boundary refuses them by name.

We never sell anybody's data. Ever.

The name never leaves the device. A key replaces it. The record is never held
joined to the story. The story without the record is what refines the models.

A practitioner seeing somatic and psychological self report is a consequential
grant. Explicit consent, a visible list of who has sight, and revocation.
Never a silent default.

A cohort lead never sees the story cloud.
