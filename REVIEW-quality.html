The team grading itself

The work, scored on eight computed dimensions, one iteration per commit, ninety one page versions of the last day’s output.

Read this first. This is the team grading its own work, which is the weakest kind of evidence there is. Nobody outside the team scored anything on this page. The rubric was written by the same seat that is being scored by it, the dimensions were chosen after the work was already done, and a seat that wanted a higher number could have chosen eight different ones. What the instrument does buy is repeatability: every dimension is computed off the bytes of the artefact by sim/combination/quality.js, so two readers running it get the same number, and the trajectory is not a matter of anybody’s mood.

Three things would make it stronger, in this order. One, the owner scores the same nine families on the same eight dimensions and the two columns are printed side by side, which turns this into a calibration and not a self report. Two, a dimension is added that this file cannot compute: whether the change reached atuned_src at all, read off the commits rather than off the artefact’s own claims. Three, the rubric is frozen before the next round rather than written after it.

The finding

The work is improving and it is not improving where it is weakest. The mean family score went from 15.6 to 16.7 out of 32, so the direction is up. But of forty three transitions between one iteration and the next, four raised the dimension that had just been the weakest one, which is 9%. The seats are polishing what is already strong.

The weakest dimension across every page version is Names where it lands at 0.95 of four. Name the file under atuned_src the change lands in. An artefact that names no landing place is a picture.

Two of the forty three transitions dropped a dimension that had been higher in the iteration before, so the movement is not one way.

The instrument found two defects in itself

Both were caught by checking the tool against a case whose answer was known, which is the rule this repository already carries, and both had made the team look better than it is.

  • The voice probe swallowed every failure. The gate exits non zero when it finds one, the first cut called it through a wrapper that throws on a non zero exit, and the catch scored nothing. Every artefact read four of four on voice. A direct run on one of them printed a failure the same minute. The gate is now read off the failing exit as well, and the file refuses to score anything until the gate has reported a failure on a line known to fail: it found one on that line just now.
  • A family average computed off a join cannot be pulled down by a bad page. The first cut joined a family’s pages and scored the text, so a funnel of four pages scored full marks for naming where it lands because one page named a path. Five of the eight dimensions are scored per page now and the family is the mean.

The voice gate reported 388 hard failures in total across the page versions it could read. 59% of them are the capitals rule, and on these artefacts that rule is mostly catching file names and identifiers rather than shouting copy. The rule is a ruling and the count stands, but a gate aimed at what a customer reads is being run against engineering pages, so the honest fix is a mode for internal pages and not a lower score.

What this cannot see

Four things, and they are the four that decide whether the work was any good.

  • Is it good. whether the artefact is well designed. No regex reads composition, and the team scoring its own taste is the weakest evidence in this document.
  • Was it the right thing to build. a perfect artefact nobody needed scores full marks here. Only the owner’s queue answers this.
  • Is the claim true. the voice gate says so itself: no gate reads a sentence against the reading it names.
  • Did it land. whether the change reached atuned_src at all. This file reads what the artefact says, not what shipped.

So a family at 20.0 of 32 is not better work than a family at 11.0. It is work that carries more of the apparatus this repository asks for. The highest score on the page is the dial prototype and the lowest is the feather prototype, first, and the second one is a prototype from the first hour of the day that nothing has been asked of since.
