# Coherence Quotient Self‑Assessment

This is not about perfection. This is about alignment. Alignment to intention and action, self and fulfillment, being fully embodied without conditions inhibiting your shine.

How to use: For each trait below, rate yourself 1–10 (1 = no expression; 10 = full embodiment). In the “Notes” column, jot a quick sentence on why you chose that number—what’s in the way, or what’s flourishing.

1–3: Inverted trait. System leaks at the root. Entitled.

10: Embodied as a living field. Unshakeable. Unifying.
