The Mechanics of Being -- Lance Powell
The Letting Go Cards
Nine axes. Nine release statements. Nine installation targets. The release protocol at the address level.
How to Use
Each card names the emotional axis, its anatomical address, the track statement (what the pattern says), the release statement (what you direct to the node), and the installation target (what you are building toward). The Letting Go Statement is a release command sent through nine logic gates simultaneously. Each gate corresponds to a chakra region. Run the full nine-gate statement below, with the axis-specific track word substituted in the blank.
STEP 01
Find the axis that is active. Feel the address. Make contact with the charge before speaking.
STEP 02
Run the full nine-gate statement below. Speak it or sub-vocalize it. The body does not require volume -- it requires direction.
STEP 03
After the release, state the installation. Not as an affirmation -- as a somatic claim. Hold it at the address until the body confirms it.
STEP 04
Run the snowball technique: bring to mind every memory, every person, every situation that carries this charge. Release each in sequence. The chain builds momentum.
STEP 05
The release is complete when the body settles. Breath deepens. Vision may clear. The Saran wrap sensation dissolves. That is the signal.
BILATERAL NOTE
All releases run bilaterally -- 50 left / 50 right simultaneously. The right side is the outward expression channel. The left side is the inner world channel. Both must clear.
The Full Nine-Gate Letting Go Statement
I am letting go of believing ___.
I am letting go of perceiving ___.
I am letting go of thinking ___.
I am letting go of feeling ___.
I am letting go of speaking ___.
I am letting go of acting from ___.
I am letting go of relating through ___.
I am letting go of creating from ___.
I am letting go of being ___.

Substitute the track word from each axis card into the blank. Each gate corresponds to a chakra region from crown to root. All nine gates fire simultaneously. The pattern cannot reboot through an unaddressed gate.
