## 2 · THE SUPPLEMENT
**Read this section before using a single line of it.**

141,519 words. Structured as Let Go Cards. Each block carries roughly 50
limiting beliefs, roughly 50 embodied truths, and a summary mapping with a
region, nerves and a polarity.

On the surface this is exactly what `volumes/*.json` needs. **It is not,
and here is the evidence.**

### It is AI-assisted, not dictated

Conversational scaffolding is still in the file.

```
45 x  "Would you like me to"
13 x  "Here's the"
 5 x  "Excellent —"
 3 x  "Perfect —"
 1 x  "drawn from your"
```

> *Excellent, you're now honing Card 3B (Regret) into its Masculine Pelvic
> expression... Would you like me to render this in the TULA Card 3B template
> format?*

That is a transcript of a working session, not a corpus. Some of the content
inside it is certainly Lance's. **Nothing in the file marks which.**

### It breaks the voice rule 138 times

**138 em dashes.** The one absolute rule. Every line needs a pass before it
goes anywhere near a book.

### Its addresses are not the canon addresses

34 distinct nerve names. **16 of them are not in the 112-node map.**

Not in the map: Radial, Ulnar, Median, Femoral, Tibial, Peroneal,
Intercostal, Pelvic Splanchnic.

Those are real anatomy. They are limb and peripheral nerves, and the map is
built on plexuses and central structures. A mapping like *Region: right
elbow and forearm, primary nerves radial, ulnar, median* is an arm, not a
node.

**Working rule: never interpolate a somatic address.** Importing these would
put sixteen addresses into the books that canon does not carry.

### Its stem is a fourth variant

```
canon        ...feeling, speaking, saying, voicing that I am
supplement   ...feeling, speaking, saying, and doing that I am
originals    ...acting, feeling that I am              (six channels)
generator    ...acting, feeling that I am              (now corrected to nine)
```

**doing** where canon says **voicing**. One word, and it changes the ninth
channel.

## WHAT THIS CORPUS CAN AND CANNOT DO

**It cannot fill the eleven volumes as-is.** Not because it is bad, but
because the project's own rules forbid it. Unmarked provenance, 138 rule
breaks, sixteen addresses outside the map, and a stem that disagrees with
canon.

**It can do three real things.**

**1. Supply shame objects.** The grammar document says the shame object
cannot be generated and must come from the corpus or from Lance. The
dictation files are corpus. That is the single hardest missing piece.

**2. Supply capacities.** The concrete plural present-tense capacities after
`because`. There are thousands in the embodied truths.

**3. Supply vocabulary.** What Lance actually reaches for, measured rather
than assumed.

---

## RECOMMENDED ORDER

1. **Clean A and B.** Script the mechanical repairs, then find entry
   boundaries. This is the real corpus and it is untouched.
2. **Strip the supplement.** Remove scaffolding, remove em dashes, then have
   Lance mark what is his. Until he does, treat every line as Claude's.
3. **Rule the ninth channel.** `voicing` or `doing`.
4. **Do not import the supplement's addresses.** Any pair built from it takes
   its address from the 112-node map or gets flagged.
