The DQ marks: what "doubled" means

DQ is the total of the shadow a person is carrying: all the held charge on all 112 addresses of the body. Across the DQ bar in the left column there are seven upright marks, one for each seat of the body, root on the left to crown on the right, and they now run the whole width of the bar. This sheet shows how tall those marks are drawn, two ways, for the same two people.

Example people: Marcus (a creative director, low charge) and Tomas (a long haul driver, high charge). Figures read out of the built page on 1 October. Mockup only.
