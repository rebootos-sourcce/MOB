CQ and DQ on one bar: where does the line sit?

You asked for one bar, CQ at one end and DQ at the other with a gradient between, and a line whose position is measured by how much the person oscillates. The engine has one oscillation measure, and it is a width and not a position, so the line needs a rule. Three rules, same two people, same bar.

Example people: Marcus (CQ 62, DQ 11) and Tomas (CQ 31, DQ 53). Mockup only. In every version the line also moves slowly, the way the Compass marker does.
