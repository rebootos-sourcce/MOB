Left rail, lines: round PC

Mockups only, nothing in atuned_src is touched. Every picture is the built app (source.html) with the readings block of the left rail swapped for the new one, figures read from the engine. This goes back to the rail's own grammar and changes only what you asked to change: one two-colour bar for CQ and DQ with a moving edge, and every other reading on its own horizontal line with the mark inside the line.

The change, today on the left, hard edge in the middle, soft edge on the right. Diane, 1600 by 1000, rail open

What changed, and what did not.

  • CQ and DQ are one solid bar. CQ's colour on the left, DQ's on the right, no gradient. The seam is the termination point: its x is CQ's share of the bar and it swings left and right by the oscillation range, 2.5 + (1 - CQ/100)^2 x 26 points, cqRange in ui/personas.js, on the drift renderPol2 writes. The bar is a hundred points wide, so the range in points is the range in per cent of the bar. Nothing is converted to pixels.
  • CQ and DQ are the only words on screen. The two numerals sit inside the ends, painted twice so a letter changes ink where the colour does, the way the rail's own rows do. The seven shadow marks stand in the DQ colour, root to crown, and are clipped to it.
  • Every other reading is one horizontal line in the same bar the rail has now: tinted track, fill, the mark inside the line at the left, the figure at the right in small type. No names. Names live in the tooltip.
  • Orientation and Balance use the same two-colour bar with a static edge, because they are the same kind of thing, two systems and a share. Halo and pitchfork sit inside the ends. Masculine and feminine sit inside the ends.
  • Removed from the block: the vertical wire down the left, the Radiance caption that had no bar, and the Style Bar and Arc toggle. The Arc is the dial form.

Colours, from the tokens

The large fields are mixed toward a mid grey, 84 per cent seat on the three readings and 58 to 60 on the two split bars, because saturation, not hue, is what makes a surface read calm. High on any of them is never red. The alarm token is not used anywhere in the block.

How the pictures were made

  • make-data.js reads the four people's readings out of the built engine into data.json.
  • make.js opens source.html, empties the readings from the left rail, mounts rail-lines.js in the same place and photographs it. make-frames.js and make-crops.py do the strips and the side by sides.
  • The clock is frozen at 5.0 seconds for the stills, a moment when the edge is moving, so the trail shows.
