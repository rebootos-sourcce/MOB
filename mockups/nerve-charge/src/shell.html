Nerve charge, three ways

The address deck on the Knowledge page lists every address the engine reads: 108 somatic, each seated at a named plexus or nerve, plus 4 field anchors, two above the body and two below. One row each. This page draws the same rows three ways, read off a scratch build of this branch for six ICPs, one heavy profile and a profile with nothing entered. Review SI7, 30 October.

Held is the charge sitting at the address. Installed is how much of its opposite is in. Left after is what is still held once the opposite is counted. All three are the engine's own figures, out of all of it.

The drill head, the one bar a person opened

Why C

  • An address carries two amounts, not one split. Held and installed are each out of all of it and do not add to a hundred. In r1's own grammar that is the amount family twice, so C is r1's amount rule mirrored about one zero.
  • B needs a figure the engine does not compute. Its needle sits at a midpoint made up for the end icons. Derek's Possession reads 63% toward Groundedness there, a number the drill never states and the engine never computes.
  • A prints figures it cannot fit. Sofia's Nihilism is held at 11% and reads "1." at phone width, a different number.
  • Every figure, icon and word in C is the engine's own. The full review is reviews/SI7-nerve-charge-grammar.md.
