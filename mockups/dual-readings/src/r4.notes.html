How to read it

  • One line runs down the middle of everything, the body's midline. Masculine is on the left and Feminine on the right because this is a mirror, the strip's own ruling.
  • A pair sits off the line by its lean: a bead on a beam, the beam coloured from the line to the bead, the figure over the line.
  • An amount is drawn the same on both sides of the line. Its shape says it has no direction, only how wide it is. The wider, the more.
  • Flow is the line itself, root at the foot and crown at the top. The channel is as wide as what still gets through, hatched where it has collapsed, and pinched at the seat that holds back the most, which says how much.
