How to read it

  • A pair is a level dial. Straight up is 50 and 50. The needle tilts to the side you lean to, the two halves of the arc are the two poles, and the figure under it is how far.
  • Vitality is a charge ring. Awareness is an aperture: it opens as the figure rises and is shut at nought. Will is willpower: a shaft pushing up toward a gap, and under about 45% it does not get through. DQ is a weight ring in red. Radiance is a light that brightens with the figure.
  • Flow is the wave, root to crown, copied whole from the shipped rbWavePts. Smooth is open. Rough and shrunken is where a seat holds back, and the ringed seat is where you are blocked.

Three rounds

  • Round 1. Dani: the second row had two cells wider than the three above, so the grid was not evenly spaced. Mika: the Radiance glow was a filled disc, the one thing a ring set may not be. Anders: the wave was rewritten seven paths a frame for every panel, forever.
  • Round 2. The second row's cells are now the same size as the first's and centred, so the cluster is symmetrical. The light is a soft falloff. The wave is written only when the passes change. New: the dial printed cq in lowercase, against round OV.
  • Round 3. Acronyms keep their case. Blocked uses the engine's held rule. Ines: a dial's angle is harder to read to the nearest few percent than a position on a line, and three dials in a 300px rail are 90px each.

Grade

B The best symbols of the four, and the only one where awareness is an aperture and will is willpower at full size. It is the shortest of the four, 624 pixels at phone width against 431 for today's rail, measured 27 October. But a dial is the least precise way to read a pair, and it reverses a standing ruling he gave in his own words.

What all four share, and why

  • Two families, two drawings. A pair is one reading measured from a centre, so it gets a line at 50 and one mark. An amount is one reading measured from none, so it fills or travels from one end.
  • CQ is drawn against its own shadow, not against DQ. DQ is not 100 less CQ (ruled in engine/compute.js), and no sampled person got past 60 on it. A line at 50 between CQ and DQ would draw a figure the engine does not compute. The self-read shadow (lawSQ, 27 October) is 100 less CQ by construction, and the engine already tips the field negative under CQ 50, so that line is real. DQ moves under How much, where the arithmetic puts it.
  • Every figure is read off the build at HEAD 87dd2cdf, for all 16 of PEOPLE, through the real compute(), leanRead(), leanSwing(), cqRange(), balance and flSeats(). Five people are shown, plus a profile with nothing entered.
  • Colours are the house's own: CQ the accent blue, every cost side Root red, Positive the Heart green, Masculine Solar, Feminine Throat, Vitality Solar, Awareness 3rd Eye, Will Throat. Radiance is drawn in light, because it is the brightness of the field and not a seat.
  • Blocked uses the engine's own held rule (flSeats: at least two addresses, and 28 percent of the seat, carrying), so a person with nothing held is never called blocked.
  • Motion is ported, not invented: rbDrift for the coherence band, leanSwing for Orientation, Balance still because nothing in the engine moves it, ENTER_SPAN 380 and ENTER_STAGGER 62, a 700ms throw that never passes either end. Reduced motion lands on the figure in the first frame.
