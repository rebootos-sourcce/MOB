A circuit you keep walking, with your avatar in the middle

Your note: too many words, we are visual only, and show discover, play, flow and embody as the circuit it is. So the loop is drawn as a closed path with a light travelling round it, and every screen below is cut to what a real screen would show.

What the circuit says, without saying it

A path of becoming, drawn

It never ends.The light keeps going round. There is no last station and no number, which is your ruling: the four close, never a list.
Each lap carries one old habit round.A dark knot is picked up at discover, takes its seat's colour at play, comes apart at flow, and at embody goes into the avatar as light. That is your content chain: the journal, the imprint, the release, the ritual.
The avatar is what changes.One seat lights per lap, root first, crown last, and the core behind it turns from the Field's dark red to its sky blue. Integrating the conditioning, as a picture.
Each lap leaves a ring.The Field is drawn as rings round a core. Here every lap walked adds one, so the Field reads as the path a person has already walked.
Free will is the next press.Nothing pushes the light but the person. In the product it moves only when they do the station, which is why the ring is also the tutorial's only progress mark.

Outside, what was looked at and what was taken

Borrowed moves, not borrowed looks

Apple's Activity rings. A ring closing is read at a glance and the closing is the reward. Taken: the loop closes with light, and nothing else is needed to say a turn is done. Not taken: three rings to close every day, which turns a practice into a quota. Apple HIG, Activity rings.
Journey, thatgamecompany. The whole game is one walk toward a light, and at the end you are back at the start. Taken: the circuit has no end screen; the light keeps going. Journey, GDC, Designing Journey.
Headspace and Calm, the breathing shape. Motion that repeats teaches a rhythm without a word. Taken: one lap is one steady beat, so the loop feels like a rhythm and not a checklist. GoodUX on Calm.
How We Feel. Colour carries meaning before any label does. Taken: every knot takes its seat's colour at play, the same seven the Field uses. How We Feel, UI motion.
Gentler Streak, Apple Design Award 2024. Progress shown as a path that respects rest, not a streak that shames a missed day. Taken: a missed day does not break the circuit; the light waits where it was. Behind the Design, Gentler Streak.

What to measure

So the next round can tell whether the circuit read as a circuit

1. Second lap. Share of people who start discover again within a day of finishing embody. A circuit that reads as a list ends at embody.
2. Tapped a station. Whether anyone taps the ring to move, which says whether it reads as a control.
3. Leave, by station. The one to watch is U2, James's stop.
4. The back arrow pressed after U4. It is the only way to undo, and nobody is shown it anywhere else.
5. A real release within 24 hours of the tutorial, against people who skipped it.
Then five people, already onboarded, watched at phone width. Ask them afterwards to draw what they just did. A circle means it worked.
