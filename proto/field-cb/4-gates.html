The six gates, near the centre and in opposition

"Do not stack the six above the centre, they're in opposition... I don't want them that far out from the centre... we don't need to see it reading three or four times on the screen." The six are three oppositions: awareness against ignorance, detachment against attachment, intention against aversion. Each layout below keeps every pair visibly opposed and prints each share once. A and C keep the six within a gate's width of the core; B reaches twice as far and trades that distance for a straight read. All three sit around centre B from item 1. The shares are example values, since no reference profile has lit a gate yet; they sum to 100, as the engine's do.
