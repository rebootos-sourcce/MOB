Seat names, centred on their own arcs

"With the circle design, I don't want the names like Third Eye, Root, etc, sticking out like a handle for a wheel... It has to be centred, like the one on the lower right after the dial." In the sheet he was looking at, the lower right picture is the Dial, and the names sticking out are on the Wheel above it: seven seat names run outward on the Wheel's rim. The Dial and Frames already set each seat as its mark, centred on its own run, with no word on the drawing. So this sheet brings the Wheel in line with the Dial. The Wheel is the product's own canvas, captured; only the seat marks and words are drawn over it.
