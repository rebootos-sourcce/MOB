The story page, four designs

Four arguments about what this surface is, not four layouts. Each one states what it believes first and draws from there. Every figure on every page is read off the engine at run time, and each page runs on its own with no network.

One
The instrument

The story page is an instrument, and an instrument shows its trace. A seven column staff down the inside edge of the journal plots every word the sniffer places at the height of the line that produced it, and three findings sit under it: the route through the body, the seat it dwells at, and the heaviest thing in it.
Two
The mirror

The story page is a mirror. You put a sentence in and the instrument gives it back in its own words, beside the line it came from. Nothing is done to the writing. A leader runs out of each word the engine read to a line of the instrument's own writing in the margin.
Three
The body

A story is a route through a body, and the page draws the route where it happens. The figure is the shipping outline and the 72 traced nerve branches. The seats light as the words land, the route is stroked between them in order, and pressing a seat is how you reach what is held there.
Four
The bench

A story is stock, and this is the bench where it is cut into parts and the parts are worked. Three zones and two hairlines. The rack lights the parts your words are feeding while you write, and clamping one turns the story down to the words it came from.
