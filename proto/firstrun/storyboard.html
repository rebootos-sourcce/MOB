The three questions that need the drawing

Both answers side by side. Five more are in DESIGN-firstrun.md section 9 and are listed at the foot of this page.

Q1

What does the box turn off?

"Do you want to check this box so you don't have to see this again?"

AThe whole opening, one switch

As storyboarded. Ticked, the person goes straight in now and on every later launch, until the switch in Account says otherwise. Costs the greeting by name on later launches.

BOnly the greeting, shown every launch

"Hello, Sofia." after every boot until the box is ticked; the walk through is one time on its own. Costs one press on every launch for everyone who never finds the box.

Q2

Is "the starting square" Frames?

"If we can get that lined up with the start, the starting square, that'd be cool. It also needs to have a loading screen in between." FA confirmed "the square" is Frames.

ALand on Frames

The first run lands on the Field drawn as nested frames. Later launches keep whatever view the person last chose. Costs porting the Frames arrival, which is not built yet, and its own checks.

BLand on the Wheel, as shipped

"Starting square" read as square one. The boot hands off to the check in with no cut and the Field opens as it does today. Costs nothing to build, and misses him if he meant the square.

Q4 to Q8

The rest, in one line each

Q4. Is the name required? Optional loses the greeting and three numbers for whoever skips it. Required loses Diane and James at an ask placed before a payoff.

Q5. Does the opening end at the door of the first story, or walk through it? At the door keeps "no real charge". Through it makes the first session end in a change, at the cost of a forced first entry.

Q6. Which four surfaces are the stations? Journal, imprints, release, ritual, as he said on 20 September, or Story, Field, Ritual, Body, as the prototype drew it for the tour ruling.

Q7. May the opening keep the two marks? Kept, a replay can show whether the shape moved. Kept nothing, the signal test has no memory.

Q8. Does Root energetics open on the first landing? Open once, the person lands on three numbers that are theirs. Closed, as ruled, the only thing about them sits behind a fold.
