Fifteen addresses name a structure anatomy does not have, and one more, Endless Seeking at the Crown, claims something that is not anatomy in any sense. Each is drawn where the Body page places it today, labelled with its own name and what it claims to be.

How the measured side was placed

The head. The second pass measured each structure on three published brain atlases, Harvard-Oxford, Juelich and Neuromorphometrics, in millimetres. It then matched the drawn head to a reference head: the top of the drawn skull is its crown, and the middle of the drawn ear is its ear points. That match is reused here unchanged.

The torso. The first and third passes sourced each structure’s level on the spine. The product’s own seat markers are the ruler: Heart at the 4th and 5th chest vertebrae, Solar at the last chest and first lower back vertebrae, Sacral at the last lower back vertebra, and Root at the base of the spine.

The figure has no ribs or spine drawn, so these are close placements, which is the bar you set.

Today’s positions come from the shipped build, source.html at commit d034324, checksum adcf3956b4840126343b4c784661e03c. probe.js in this folder runs the product’s own placement function, figure outline and clip in Chromium.

It was checked three ways before anything here was drawn. The marks the Body page renders on three loaded profiles, 32, 41, 97 marks, sit where the probe says to within 0.005 of a figure unit. Every address the probe calls never shown was hidden one at a time on Rosa and Gordon’s profiles, and the screen did not change. Addresses on the body, used as controls, did change it.

The findings are the three anatomy reports logged in TASKS.md as BI3a (Heart and Throat), BI3b (3rd Eye and Crown) and BI3c (Root, Sacral and Solar), carried into research.js with the report each line comes from. Nothing under atuned_src was changed.
