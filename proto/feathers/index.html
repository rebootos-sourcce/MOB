The three layers, told apart without a magnitude

Three cues, redundant on purpose. Barb rake is a hatch direction, which is categorical and not ordinal: ninety degrees is not more than thirty four, it is different from it. Rachis dash is the same kind of channel and it survives at a small core radius where the rake stops resolving. The tip mark is a ring, not a fill, and all three tips are drawn at one constant stroke so the terminal carries no size.

Colour is not available and was not taken. Colour is already the seat, and the seat colour is what lets a feather be traced outward to the band it belongs to. Radius is not available either: all three layers now share one scale, hub to rim, so a value of five sits at the same radius whichever layer it came from. That repairs the older defect where a triad at its full value reached 8.60 on its own gridlines and a seat reached 9.25.
