What a second reading unlocks

Ana, across three readings

Release is the only one of the five that cannot exist on a first session, and that is a definition rather than a gap. Here is what happens to Ana when charge comes off her axes and snapshot() writes a second and a third reading. Coherence moves nine points and she stays at the bottom of the ladder. The aperture moves thirty two. The release reading says she has spent almost all the headroom she ever had. That is the credit the instrument was not giving her. The release control's own arithmetic lives in ui/release.js and is not reproduced here, because a second copy of it is a defect and this repository already carries one.

What load alone cannot see

Gordon and Tomas

Read from load alone these two are a fraction of a point apart. Gordon says there is nothing wrong with him and four people left in a year. Tomas says he used to drive nine hundred miles and feel nothing and now cannot get to the end of the street. One cannot see it. The other sees it exactly and cannot move. A measure that cannot tell them apart is measuring load and calling it awareness. Type into the box and watch the second term separate them.
