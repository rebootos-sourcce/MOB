    Designed, not built

    The turn: one closed circle of the loop

    One turn closes when a person has touched discover, play, flow and embody, in any order, over any number of days. It cannot be farmed: ten stories close nothing. It is the counter his words ask for: "the gamification exists to keep that turning."

    Known defect in the design: committing a story also takes a reading, so embody is stamped the same moment as discover. Not ported until that is fixed.

    Designed, not built

    Awards: what moved, dated, never removed

    Four families. Cleared, one per seat. Held, one per axis, named for the opposite pole. Moved, a band crossed upward. Closed, a share of the distance to the person's own ceiling. Two of the four need new fields on every reading before they can be computed, which is a schema change and his call.

    Designed, not built

    Karma, the points

    Paid after the fact for the record, never promised in advance for the act. Drafted rates: one pattern for a ritual marked done, five for a mark. The rate per mark is unsettled across three documents, and whether karma is a second unit or a name for earned patterns is open.
