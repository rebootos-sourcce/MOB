“purpose based product”, “results driven product” Two compound labels that sound like every product page. What they mean is carried into the guarantee below as It has one purpose, and it answers to results.

Why it exists
Your sentence was taken down two ways. The brief has If you don't get the results you don't want. The backlog has If you don't get the results, you don't want, get your fucking money back, which reads as two conditions: no results, or you do not want it. D is the second reading. It guarantees your satisfaction, not a result, which gets it out of the problem in point 1 below.
The mechanics are not written here, and they need your ruling before any of this ships.
  1. What results means. The funnel says in two places that it promises throughput, the number of patterns released, and not outcomes in a person's life. A guarantee on results means one of two things. It could mean the reading itself: the about page already has a section called Results listing ceiling, drag, addresses, seat and order. Everyone gets that, so the guarantee is true but almost empty. Or it could mean a change in their life, and then the page contradicts itself. Option D avoids the question.
  2. Nothing can be refunded yet. The tiers page says Checkout is not open yet, so nothing on this table can be charged today. The guarantee cannot go live before checkout does.
  3. The guarantee is the one thing on the page that expires. The tiers page also says Nothing here expires, nothing counts down. So the recommended placement is under the price ladder, away from the Stopping section where that sentence sits.
  4. How it works, none of which is copy. How someone claims it. When the week starts: first charge, or every renewal. Whether it is the same on every tier, including tier four with its cohort. Whether the hundred free patterns count as part of the week. What a refund does to the person's record, given the tiers page promises A cancellation destroys nothing. All of it is logged as open in TASKS.md CN, and nothing here decides them.
