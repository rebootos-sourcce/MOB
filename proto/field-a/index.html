The nameplates are spikes, and the spike is the word

Third eye, crown, root there are doing a couple of things. They are pointing out, and it is a word. So it is a single line acting like a spike. But it is taking up a huge amount of real estate. And it takes up a degree of space.

Two costs in that sentence and both are measured below, on the shipping Field, at 1600 and at 390. The leader eats radius. The plate reserves arc. Four answers follow, and each one is held to those numbers.

Measured by Bjorn and Sol

The seven seat names are set radially outward, starting 10 px past the ring at 1600 and 5 px past it at 390, at 12 px, weight 600. A radial run occupies a radial interval, so the word is the leader: it starts at the ring and points away from the reading for as long as the word is, and there is no separate line doing it. Each one also registers as an occupied angular slot before any saboteur plate is placed, so every other name on the surface routes around it.

At 1600, on a stage of 664 by 726

The free radius is 332 px. The reading reaches 171 px of it, 51.5 per cent. The names run out to 225 px, so 54 px of radius past the ring belongs to seven words. The seven plates close 19 degrees of 360 at their own bearings. The annulus they sit in is 66,884 px of canvas carrying 2,530 px of ink. It is 3.8 per cent lit.

At 390, on a stage of 374 by 335

The free radius is 168 px. The reading reaches 78 px of it, 46.5 per cent. The names run out to 126 px, so 48 px of radius past a 78 px ring belongs to seven words: 62 per cent of the reading’s own radius, added outside it. The seven plates close 42 degrees of 360, one degree in every nine. The annulus is 31,075 px of canvas carrying 1,608 px of ink. It is 5.2 per cent lit.

Why the phone is worse

The word is 12 px whatever the wheel is. The wheel halves and the word does not, so the same seven names take 16 per cent of the free radius at 1600 and 29 per cent at 390, and the arc they close goes from 19 degrees to 42. The reservation is a fixed cost charged against a shrinking budget.

One caveat, and it is about type
