Strain marks

Tension drawn the way an animator draws strain. Short marks come off every address past five. Rising load, they run outward and point away. Falling load, they run inward and point at it. They step at twelve frames a second while the rest runs at sixty, so the hot ones stand out. Zoom in and names appear around the pointer, dissolving at the edge.
