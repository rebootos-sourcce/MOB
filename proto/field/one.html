Plumb

Seventeen marks, and the ring is a pressure vessel. Outward from the ring is the pressure at that seat, inward is the wall it is held against, and the one notch is the address to work. Nothing else is drawn.

Outward is pressure· The heaviest single address at that seat, on a 0 to 10 scale.
Inward is the wall· Integrity at that seat, on a 0 to 10 scale. It is what the pressure is held against.
The notch· The single heaviest address of 112. One thing to work, not a list.
