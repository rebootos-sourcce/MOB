The same ring, and this is the third state of it, not a third drawing

A seven band ring already exists twice in the product and both are one object. The funnel's tab mark is that drawing with every band full. The quiz ring is the same call with loads, thickening seat by seat as a person answers. This page takes its geometry from funnel/ring.js by reference: the sweep, the start angle, the band fraction, the hairline and the function that turns a load into a width. Root sits at six o'clock and climbs the right here for the same reason it does there. Change that file and this changes with it. Nothing is retyped.

One correction to the brief, because prior art is only useful if it is accurate. The app's boot mark is not a seven band ring. It is a spine, seven seat dots, one hairline ring and twenty four address ticks, and funnel/ring.js says so itself: the boot ring is a hairline at 1.29 per cent of the diameter and has nothing to colour. The app carries no icon at all, so the mark state ships on the funnel and not in the product. Two instances to agree with, then, and the shell is the third state of one object. Mark at full band. Meter filling as answers land. Shell at the Field's own radius, where a band can be pressed.

What the space is worth, off the shipped constants

In ui/wheel.js the address ring ends at R.shell, which is U*0.74 at depth C. The seven seat names are radial text at R.shell*1.058 and their hit boxes run out to R.shell*1.058+U*0.10, which is U*0.883. So the seven words reserve an annulus a seventh of the radius thick, outside the drawing, where the room is scarcest. Hover carries the name here, so that annulus goes back to the shell: radius from 0.74U to 0.883U is nineteen per cent more radius and forty two per cent more disc. That is the space, and it is arithmetic off their own numbers rather than an impression.

The frame cost, measured on this machine

Each row is the atomise transition driven from shut to open in forty steps at device pixel ratio two, timing the paint only. The budget for sixty frames is 16.7 milliseconds and the paint is what a direction spends of it. Idle costs nothing, because nothing is drawn when nothing is moving: the loop stops at the end of the transition and a hover repaints one frame. The measurement is this container and not a handset, so the honest number is the share of the budget and the count of what is drawn, both below.

His to rule, and the drawings cannot settle either one

Does a band span its seventh, or its share of the hundred and eight? The mark says a seventh, and the argument is in funnel/ring.js: seven equal arcs, because an alternating rhythm cannot close on an odd count. A reading says the share, because Crown holds twenty one addresses and Throat holds twelve, so at equal sevenths one address at Crown is drawn smaller than one address at Throat and the same amount of colour means two things in two places. Kerf takes the second answer and the other three take the first. One object cannot hold both, and whichever loses, the favicon and the shell stop being the same drawing.

Do the four anchors get a mark on the shell? The count stated to a person is 112. The seven seats hold all but four of them, and those four are the field anchors above and below the body, seated nowhere. A shell of seven bands draws the seated ones and says nothing about the anchors, on a surface whose whole job is to be the field.

What is here and what is not

Live off ../../engine.js and its own roster, loaded the way tools/loopsim.js loads it, so every number on this page is the engine's. Nothing is fetched: the page, the engine, the ring and the tokens are the only four files and all four are local siblings. The request log is beside this file at requests.log.

Two things a reviewer should know before reading the drawings. The seats are not equal. Crown holds twenty one addresses and Throat holds twelve, read off W at run time and printed on every band below. Seven equal sevenths therefore draw an address at Crown smaller than an address at Throat, which is why one of these four divides the ring by address instead. Held and filled in cannot both be true at one address. In compute(), sq is the charge left after the pole and pole is the pole past the charge, both clamped at zero, so an address is carrying, or it is filled in, or it is clear. The three way split one of these directions draws is exact and not a bucket somebody chose.
