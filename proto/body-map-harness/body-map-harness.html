Nerve to super-complex, on one map

The approved man, front and back, with the real vector nerves (CNS in bone white, autonomic in blue, somatic in gold) and the full chain running on top of them: an address co-firing with others into a saboteur (a tension line between them), two saboteurs of one family compounding into a complex, a family's complexes compounding into a hyper-complex, and two hyper-complexes pairing by weight into a super-complex. Click any rung in the panel at right and the chain climbs or descends with it. Pattern shows this running. Pain is where you paint where it hurts, to see which lines cross it.

      Hyper-complexes ·

      A family compounds once it is running two complexes, or one complex at 6.5 or over.

        Hyper-complexes pair by weight, heaviest first, at 5.6 or over.
