Borrowed moves, not borrowed looks

Apple Watch Breathe, and the Headspace breathing circle. A shape that grows and shrinks is followed without a word. Taken: the core breathes, four seconds in and six out. CSS-Tricks on the Breathe animation, a design read of Headspace.
