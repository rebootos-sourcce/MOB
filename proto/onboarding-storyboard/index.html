Borrowed moves, not borrowed looks

Apple Watch Breathe, and the Headspace breathing circle. A shape that grows and shrinks is followed without a word. Taken: the core breathes, four seconds in and six out. CSS-Tricks on the Breathe animation, a design read of Headspace.
Calm's first screen. It opens on one breath before it asks for anything, and the ask lands on a calmer person. Taken: the signal test comes before discover asks for a story. GoodUX on Calm's new user flow.

Where they stop

Four people, the signal test in your words

Angela, 36, seeker.The signal test is the thing she came for, and she feels yes sit differently at the heart and at the throat. Stops only if she feels nothing and reads that as failing, which is why same is an answer, never a fail.
Derek, 39, high performer.Ten breaths with his eyes closed is where he checks whether this is a meditation app, and "sit back, relax" is the line that tells him it is. Stops at the breaths unless the ring and the ten bars show him it ends. Question O.
James, 57, C suite.Will not say a word aloud, and does not have to: yes is thought, never said, and every step works with a tap and silence. Stops at the bad day thoughts if they read as his. They drift onto the figure, not onto him.
Marcus, 44, creative director.Reads the drawing before anything else. One big word on the whole test is the proof it is not a brochure. No forced stop.

Four on the signal test, then the ones still open from this morning

M

Your words are the script now. Do they reach the person as a voice, or as text on the screen?

"We could also use a voice script or just use my text."Round JY. It reads as either being fine, so both are built and neither is chosen for you. Press Text and Voice on the player at the top to see and hear each.

1A voice

Eyes can close through the ten breaths, which the exercise wants. Cost: the voice must live inside the one file. The device's own voice, on the player now, is free and sounds like a machine. Your recorded voice, at this morning's rate of about 1 MB per two minutes, is near 2 MB for this script.

2Your text on the screen

Works with the sound off, which is most phones in public. Cost: your longest line runs four lines at phone width, and it sits there through ten breaths a person should take with eyes closed.

3Both, as built

Text on by default, the voice a switch. Cost: two things to keep word for word in step, and a recorded voice still to make.

O

"Sit back, relax": your words, or the rule you set against them?

"If you're ready, sit back, relax, Take 10 deep breaths and exhale with each subsequent exhale longer than the previous."Round JY, as dictated and as built. The house voice gate stops this line: "No soft wellness language." Its own example of a failing line is "Sit back and relax, and turn your senses inward," and its fix is "Sit down. Put both feet on the floor," which was the first line of this test this morning.

1Keep it, as built

Your words, unedited. Cost: the voice gate fails on it, and Derek hears a meditation app in the first four words.

2"If you're ready, sit down. Take 10 deep breaths..."

Two words swapped, the rest yours. Cost: it is an edit to your dictation, which the team does not make without your word.

P

At the throat, does the person think yes again, or only move their awareness there?

"Now move your awareness in your body right around the heart. And when you're ready, think yes 10 times. Actually, have them think around the throat. That move their awareness to their throat. And see if they can feel the quality difference."Round JY. "Think around the throat" reads as the same yes, said again at the throat. That is how it is built, and it is a reading of your words, not a quotation of them.

1Yes ten times at each, as built

The same thought in two places, so the difference felt is the place. Cost: 25 seconds more, and the throat line "Think yes 10 times" is the team's words, not yours.

2Yes at the heart only; at the throat, awareness alone

Closer to the words you dictated for the throat. Cost: the two halves then differ in two ways, the place and the thought, so the person cannot tell which one they felt.

Q

"Not now": when does the signal test come back?

"You can skip this for later."Round JY. The skip is built, as "Not now", the word the shipped opening already uses for skip. Where "later" is, is not said.

1. The next time the app opens. Cost: it asks again on every open until done, which is a nag.
2. After the first full turn of the loop. Cost: the first story is written before the person has felt the body answer a thought.
3. Only from Profile, where the opening switches live. Cost: most people who skip it never see it.

1. Start or Not now. Share who start the test, and share who come back to it after Not now.
2. Through the ten breaths. Leave by breath. The breath they leave on says whether the length is the problem.
3. Same or different. Share who tap different. That is the moment the test exists for.
4. Leave, by step. Especially at the breaths, Derek's step, and at the bad day thoughts, James's.
5. Voice on or off, text on or off, against finishing the test, while question M is open.
6. Came back on day two within an hour of the chosen when. The number the whole turn exists to move.
