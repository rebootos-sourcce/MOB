Where precision and plain words collided

"The line" is gone from onboarding. The product uses it for one exact thing: a place in the body carrying enough to show in full. "They show faint until more of your writing makes them heavier" says the same event without the word. Cost: the person meets "the line" later with nobody having said what it is. The tutorial storyboard says it, once, with the wheel on screen.

"Field" is not said on any frame. It is the tab name and the product's word for everything it has read about you. "This wheel is you" is what a ten year old can hold. Cost: the bar says Field and the frame said wheel, so the person has to join the two themselves.

The pattern names are kept exactly as the engine prints them. Self-Silencing, People Pleasing, Need For Approval. They are data, not copy, and one word per concept means this storyboard does not rename them. Some are the hardest words on these frames. Manipulation Through Emotion is one of the twelve this story finds, and it is not shown on a chip for that reason.

"Commit" is kept. It is the shipped control word on the story box. A ten year old says Save. Changing it is a product wide rename, not an onboarding line.

Your loop words are kept. discover, play, flow, embody are ruled, and embody is not a ten year old's word. Not changed. Asked below.

Your dictated opening is kept word for word. "Bias", "conditioning" and "highest experience of life" are abstract by your own new rule. The team does not edit dictated words (ruled in DECISIONS.md), so it is question B.

Defects already found, not new asks

From the 26 September walk (round FN, eight defects logged). These are the ones this flow walks a stranger straight into. A person greeted by name who is then told what is "most shut" in them, by a record holding nothing, will not trust the next thing it says.

1. Readings on a blank record. The right rail's archetype and domain percentages, and the lit Warrior and Sage seats, still print before anything is entered (backlog item AV24).

2. Ritual says "the root is carrying the most" on a blank record. The embody frame lands on this surface.

3. CQ prints "0%" as its largest number after a first story. That reads as a score of zero, and a reading is never a score (a standing ruling).

4. The When line misprints. Anyone who types "after" at the start reads "When after I put the kettle on, ...". The embody frame's first chip starts with "after".

5. The faint drawing on discover and play is new. The engine does not change. The wheel has to draw a first story faint where it now draws nothing.
