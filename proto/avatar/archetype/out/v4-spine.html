Design notes. Spine, variation 4

The job
In Sofia's words, a somatic practitioner: "show me where in the body this lives, and what it pulls on." The owner asked for the saboteur to be read as the impact of taking a role on. Here the impact is drawn on the body as well as said.
The count
Eighteen named buttons and one: nineteen, the same as the round GU wheel, and over the target of twelve. What pays for it is order: seven rows of two or three, read top to bottom in the body's own order, where the wheel asked the eye to go round. Measured live above.
Where they stop
Angela meets eighteen names at once, with only the glow to tell her where to start. James sees a pulsing line land on his solar plexus and may read it as a diagnosis; the panel's Impact line says it is what the role costs, which has to be in view when the line is. Derek gets the most model per glance of the five, and the arithmetic behind it is on the page.
The move
Lay the wheel out as the body, name every mark, and turn Impact into a line between two seats.
Instrument
Rows scrolled past before the first open. How many archetypes are opened before a press, compared with the other four. Whether the line changes which archetype is kept, the pick before and after the line appears.
Grade
B. The most distinctive of the five and the one that carries the product's own idea, that a way of acting has a place in the body. It does not lower the count.
