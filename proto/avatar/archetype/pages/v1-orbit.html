Your avatar

Each part of your life holds the archetypes that act from it. Tap a part to open them.

Design notes. Orbit, variation 1

The job
In Angela's words: "show me which of these is me, on me." The wheel put the archetypes on a second chart beside the avatar. Here they are on the avatar, so a pick is visibly a change to the thing she is building.
The count
Seven satellites, the open satellite's two or three moons, one button: eleven at most, against the round GU wheel's nineteen. Measured live above.
Where they stop
Derek wants the whole system at once and has to open seven satellites to see all eighteen. James may not know which part of his life a way of acting sits in; the glow on a satellite, which is his own field's reading, is what gets him to the right one. A first run has no glow, so the page opens with nothing chosen and one instruction.
The move
Fold by seat. The wheel's seven bands become the avatar's seven satellites, which the page already has, so no new structure is learned.
Instrument
Satellites opened before the first press. Whether the first satellite opened is the glowing one. Time from page open to first press.
Grade
C to B. It removes the second chart and halves the choices. What keeps it from an A: nothing on screen compares all eighteen at once.
