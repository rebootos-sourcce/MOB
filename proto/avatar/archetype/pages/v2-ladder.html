Design notes. Ladder, variation 2

The job
In Derek's words: "what does the instrument say I am, and do I agree." The wheel answered with a glow whose size had to be judged against seventeen other glows. Here the answer is the order of the list.
The count
On a loaded profile, five rows, one open row's button, and one control for the rest: seven. On a first run nothing can be ranked, so all eighteen show in body order, grouped by area, and the count is eighteen before one is opened. That is the cost of this layout, and it lands on the person with the least to go on. Measured live above.
Where they stop
Angela is told, in order, which is what she wants, and may press the top row because it is the top row. James reads "reads first" as a verdict on him; the compare line under a pick ("both are kept") is what keeps it a reading. A first run meets a list of eighteen with nothing to sort it.
The move
The halo becomes the sort order, and the detail opens in place. A bar is drawn and never numbered, because a reading is not a score.
Instrument
Share of first presses on the top row. Share who open "the rest". How often the pick differs from the top row, which is the gap the compare line names.
Grade
B minus for a loaded profile, the lowest load and the most legible reading of the five. C for a first run. It also drops the system view: nothing here says an archetype sits at a seat until a row is opened.
