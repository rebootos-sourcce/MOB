Design notes. Three, variation 3

The job
In Angela's words: "just tell me which ones to look at." She is level 5 on the buyer grid, the largest group and the hardest sell, and she is the one the wheel cost most.
The count
Three buttons and one control: four, the lowest of the five. Hick's law is the reason, and it is also the whole of the cost below. Measured live above.
Where they stop
Derek wants to see the system and has to press five times to see it all, and never sees it as one picture. James sees Sage, Ruler and Warrior first, which are his own field's top three, and may feel typecast before he has chosen anything. Angela reads three whole descriptions side by side, which is the comparison the wheel never let her make.
The move
Show less, and show each one whole. The description and its impact are on every card, so nothing has to be tapped to be read.
Instrument
Which set the first press lands in. How many sets are seen before it. Share of first presses on the first card.
Grade
B for Angela and a first run, C plus for Derek. The strongest answer to "too much at once", and the weakest to "show me the model".
