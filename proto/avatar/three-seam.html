The Seam

His own word, drawn literally. Five rails in his order, avatar to ritual to psyche to body to story, and a thread between two of them only where the person has actually made that joint. The record is the weave, the dotted rings are the reach, and today is the sag in it.

What This Refuses To Draw

It refuses to draw a person at all. There is no figure, no light and no body, so there is nothing that can be read as a picture of how a person is doing. It also refuses to draw a layer as earned: the five rails are always there, because a person has a body and a story before this product touches them.

The Symbol Argument

A seam is a joint that shows. It is the honest opposite of a finish, and what makes a seam trustworthy is that you can see what it holds together. This is the one approach that answers the sentence he actually said, which was about sewing layers and not about a figure. Sag is the right weather channel because a load on a line is the physical fact the voice already reaches for, and because a sagging thread is plainly still attached.

Not Bulky, And Nothing To Be Taught

It is the bulkiest of the three and the measurement says so: at twelve runs on a loaded profile the weave carries over a hundred and forty strokes, and the reading a person takes off it is a density, which is one reading for a great deal of ink. It also fails nothing to be taught outright. A body needs no caption and a lantern needs four sentences; a five rail weave needs a paragraph, and the owner rejected two Field concepts for exactly this. It is kept in the set because it is the literal answer to his own sentence and because the joints it names are the ones the product has to build whichever drawing wins.

The Honest Risk

A diagram is not an avatar. A person at grid level two, numb, is not going to look at a wiring harness and feel represented, and 800 of the 1000 arrive at level four or below. Its blank state is the worst of the three: five dotted lines and one ring, which is close to an empty screen on the surface the product is naming as its centre. It is the strongest systems answer and the weakest first minute.
