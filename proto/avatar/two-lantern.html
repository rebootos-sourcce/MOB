The Lantern

No body. A housing that closes as the record accumulates, a light sized by the one quantity in the engine that only ever rises, and a dotted boundary the light cannot pass. Clearing walks the bead outward. Turning a peg moves the boundary the bead is walking toward. This is the Field’s own language, so nothing here is a second vocabulary to learn.

What This Refuses To Draw

It refuses anatomy entirely, so it cannot be read as a claim about a body part. It refuses to size the light by coherence, which is the obvious move and the wrong one, because coherence barely moves under release: Marcus goes from 39.2 to 40.5 over twelve runs. It refuses any count on the graphic.

The Symbol Argument

A lantern is a housing and a light and the two are independent: a good housing with no fuel is dark, and a bright flame in a broken housing does not carry. That is exactly the relation between a record and a reading. It inherits ring not fill without an argument, because a lantern is drawn as edges. The light is the installed coherent opposite, which relCoolDown puts in at 62 percent of what it removed, and which is the only quantity in the whole engine that cannot fall when a person does the work.

Not Bulky, And Nothing To Be Taught

Six kinds of mark and six readings, which is one more than the armature carries and is the honest cost of having no anatomy to hang them on. It is the least bulky of the three by ink: the whole figure is concentric strokes and one low alpha wash, and it holds at 100 pixels square in the contact sheet, which the other two barely do. It is the worst of the three on nothing to be taught. Housing, light, boundary and bead all need one sentence each, which is four sentences before a person can read their own picture, and the owner has already named that failure mode by name.

The Honest Risk

It may not read as you. The Proteus effect literature is consistent that identification with a self representation is what changes behaviour, and a ring diagram gives a person nothing to identify with and nothing to make their own without adding cosmetics, which is the treadmill this whole document is trying to avoid. It is also the approach most likely to be mistaken for the Field, which is a real cost: two surfaces that look the same are one surface with a bug.
