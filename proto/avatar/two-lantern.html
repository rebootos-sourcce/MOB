The Lantern

No body. A housing that closes as the record accumulates, and a light sized by the one quantity in the engine that only ever rises. The weather is a veil of grey shutters that block the light and never touch a ring. This is the Field’s own language, so nothing here is a second vocabulary to learn.

What This Refuses To Draw

It refuses anatomy entirely, which means it cannot be read as a claim about a body part. It refuses to size the light by coherence, which is the obvious move and the wrong one, because coherence barely moves under release: Marcus goes from 39.2 to 40.5 over twelve runs. It refuses any count on the graphic.

The Symbol Argument

A lantern is a housing and a light, and the two are independent: a good housing with no fuel is dark, and a bright flame in a broken housing does not carry. That is exactly the relation between a record and a reading. It also inherits the ring not fill rule without an argument, because a lantern is drawn as edges. The light is the installed coherent opposite, which relCoolDown puts in at 62 percent of what it removed, and which is the only quantity in the whole engine that cannot fall when a person does the work.

The Honest Risk

It may not read as you. The Proteus effect literature is consistent that identification with a self representation is what changes behaviour, and a ring diagram gives a person nothing to identify with and nothing to make their own without adding cosmetics, which is the treadmill this whole document is trying to avoid. It is also the approach most likely to be mistaken for the Field, which is a real cost: two surfaces that look the same are one surface with a bug.
