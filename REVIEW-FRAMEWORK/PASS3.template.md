# Pass 3: simulate, pitch, final grade (standing framework)

Read every pass 1 and pass 2 report for this subject, and the unified proposal the
creative director and systems director wrote in pass 2. Write `pass3/<your-seat>.md`,
under 1400 words, plain short words, no em dashes.

1. THE PROPOSAL IN ONE PARAGRAPH, as you now understand it.
2. THE ICP ROOM. Simulate each ICP in turn (at least six from `PANEL-10k.md` and
   `BUYERS.md`: always a phone only arrival, a skeptic, a practitioner, someone in acute
   distress). For each: what they see in ten seconds, what they do next, what makes them
   leave, one line in their own voice. Judge only from your discipline's view.
3. UNIFIED QUALITY. Score the proposal against the highest unified quality (one voice,
   one grammar of colour, shape, motion, word, symbol, from first screen to habit),
   out of 100, with the three biggest gaps left.
4. FINAL GRADE: `GRADE: NN/100` (pass 1 was NN, pass 2 was NN) and what moved it.
5. RANKED RECOMMENDATIONS for your discipline, each S, M or L, each naming the ICPs it
   moves, and which are a reskin and which a redesign.
6. ONE QUESTION for the owner only if truly blocked. Otherwise "none".
