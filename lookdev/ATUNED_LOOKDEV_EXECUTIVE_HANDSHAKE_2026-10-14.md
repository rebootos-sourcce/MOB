## Brand / creative north star
- Public promise: **SEE WHAT’S RUNNING YOU.**
- ATÜNED is an instrument for seeing how you operate.
- The Field is the interface, not a decorative background.
- Use precise geometry, nodes, connections, paths, bands, waves, body regions, and meaningful state changes.
- The product loop is: observe → show what was observed → test/confirm/correct → release → verify → preserve evidence.
- Visual tone: distinctive, precise, alive, restrained, high-contrast, large typography, purposeful motion.
- Avoid wellness-category clichés, generic SaaS dashboard styling, generic gradients, decorative particles, stock-photo dependence, mystical portals, religious imagery, and decoration that does not communicate product mechanics.
- Do not let the interface imply that model inference is fact. Distinguish observation, hypothesis, correction, confirmation, and verified change.
- Keep copy concise and contextualized. Less text, more visual demonstration; no important ambiguity.
