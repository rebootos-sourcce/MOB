# Your data, on one page

> **Warning.** This page summarises a generic privacy policy draft written by an AI. It is not legal advice. A lawyer must review the policy before anyone relies on it. If this page and the full [Privacy policy](privacy.html) disagree, the full policy governs.

## What stays on your device

Everything you enter. Your name, birth details, answers, stories, imprints, snapshots, avatar and plan word. It sits in your browser's storage, on your device, and nowhere else.

We set no cookies. The app and the pages load no ads, no analytics tags and no outside scripts. How you use the app is not reported to us.

Clear your browser's data and it is gone. Export a file if you want a copy.

## What leaves it, and when

Nothing leaves until you do one of these.

- **Create an account.** Your email and password go to us. So does your record, so it can be recovered if you lose the device.
- **Sign in.** Email and password, and we send back a token that lasts 90 days.
- **Forgot your password.** Your email, to send a one hour link.
- **Pay.** Stripe takes the card on its own page. We never see the number.
- **Turn on the morning note.** Your browser's push address and the hour you picked.
- **Dictate.** Your browser sends your voice to its maker, Google for Chrome and Microsoft for Edge. We never get the audio. Type and nothing is sent.
- **Ask for a name meaning.** Your name goes to us, then to Anthropic, only after you say yes.
- **Turn on research sharing.** A copy of your imprints, releases and readings. Off until you turn it on.

## Who sees it

- **You.** All of it.
- **Us.** Your account, plan and a log of account events. Not your stories. Stored records are scrambled, and our support tools do not open them.
- **Our providers.** Cloudflare hosts it. Stripe bills it. A mail service sends your reset link.
- **A practitioner.** Only if you say yes, one person at a time, with a list of what they see. One press takes it back.
- **Nobody else.** We do not sell your data. We do not share it for advertising.

## How to delete it

- **This device.** Settings, Privacy, Delete this record. Gone at once.
- **Your account.** Settings, Privacy, Delete account. Your account and our copy of your record are deleted at once. Stripe keeps billing records the law requires. Backups clear in [PLACEHOLDER: days].
- **A copy of everything we hold.** Export account data.
- **Cancel a paid plan.** Manage billing in Settings. Cancelling deletes nothing.
