# Your data, on one page

> **Warning.** This page summarises a generic privacy policy draft written by an AI. It is not legal advice. A lawyer must review the policy before anyone relies on it. If this page and the full [Privacy policy](privacy.html) disagree, the full policy governs.

## What stays on your device

Everything you enter. Your name, birth details, answers, stories, imprints, snapshots, avatar and plan word. It sits in your browser's storage, on your device, and nowhere else.

We set no cookies. The app and the pages load no ads, no analytics tags and no outside scripts. How you use the app is not reported to us.

Clear your browser's data and it is gone. Export a file if you want a copy.

## What leaves it, and when

Nothing leaves until you do one of these.

- **Create an account.** This comes after your first release. Your username and passphrase go to us. A recovery email goes too, only if you choose to give one. So does a copy of your answers, stories, imprints, releases, readings and summaries, so you can get them back if you lose the device. Your name and birth details do not go. They stay on your device.
- **Sign in.** Username and passphrase, and we send back a token that lasts 90 days.
- **Forgot your passphrase.** Your username, so we can send a one hour link to your recovery email. With no recovery email, we cannot reset it.
- **Pay.** Stripe takes the card on its own page. We never see the number.
- **Turn on the morning note.** Your browser's push address and the hour you picked.
- **Dictate.** Your browser sends your voice to its maker, Google for Chrome and Microsoft for Edge. We never get the audio. Type and nothing is sent.
- **Ask for a name meaning.** Your name goes to us, then to Anthropic, only after you say yes.

## Who sees it

- **You.** All of it.
- **Us.** Your account, plan and a log of account events. Not your stories. Stored records are scrambled, and our support tools do not open them.
- **Our providers.** Cloudflare hosts it. Stripe bills it. A mail service sends your reset link.
- **A practitioner.** Only if you say yes, one person at a time, with a list of what they see. One press takes it back.
- **Nobody else.** We do not sell your data. We do not share it for advertising. We do not use it to train any model.

## How to delete it

- **This device.** Settings, Privacy, Delete this record. Gone at once.
- **Your account.** Settings, Privacy, Delete account. Your account and our copy of your record leave our live system at once. A locked backup stays for 90 days, and then it is gone. Stripe keeps billing records the law requires.
- **Change your mind.** Within those 90 days, write to hello@atuned.world and we restore the account. After 90 days we cannot. Your birth details come back only from your device.
- **A copy of everything we hold.** Export account data.
- **Cancel a paid plan.** Manage billing in Settings. Cancelling deletes nothing.

## Money

Refund any charge within seven days of it. After seven days, no refund. Write to hello@atuned.world.

## Where you live

- **Europe and the United Kingdom.** You may ask for access, correction, deletion, a file of your data, to object, and to take back a consent. You may complain to your data protection authority.
- **California.** You may ask what we hold, correct it, delete it, and limit its use. We sell nothing and share nothing for ads.
- **Washington and Nevada.** There is a separate page for consumer health data.

## What this is not

Atüned is not medical care. It does not diagnose. If you are not safe, call or text 988 in the United States, or your local emergency number.
