# The compass, the descent, and the groups around them

Three asks in one pass. One is built, one is specified, and one has a
straight answer that is not the one hoped for.

---

## 1. Built. The cone has two ends and both are doors

The compass was a picture of a direction with nothing at either end of it.
Now the top and the bottom each carry their own hit box and open their own
roster.

Every name and every description came out of the codex. Nothing was invented,
because the material was already written and the product's job is to carry it.

**The eight mirror pairs**, quoted intact, with the seat this product already
measures the quality at:

| | Quality | Coherent | Inverted | Seat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IL | Illumination | Jesus | Lucifer | Heart |
| DE | Desire and will | Ramakrishna | Asmodeus | Sacral |
| OR | Order | Moses | Set | Throat |
| PO | Power | Musashi | Moloch | Solar |
| PE | Perception | Buddha | Geryon | 3rd Eye |
| TR | Trust | Rumi | Charon | Heart |
| CH | Charge | Elijah | Phlegyas | Root |
| RE | Revelation | Meister Eckhart | The Furies | Crown |

Musashi against Moloch was asked for by name in an earlier pass. The book had
already answered, and the answer came with a diagnostic question attached:
**who pays the cost of this person's discipline?** Each of the eight carries
one, and that is the line that does the practitioner's work rather than the
name.

**Both ends are behaviours and the copy says so.** Nobody meets Lucifer. A
person runs pride as false light, and Lucifer is what four thousand years of
people called that behaviour when they saw it. The names earn their place
because they carry the historical footprint, and the echo is in the room
today. A gate asserts the sentence is on the surface.

**The numbers, which was the open question.** Ruled: give them all numbers and
let a person move against them. They are not a rank, because the codex is
explicit that the masters are not a summit:

> The Ascended Masters are not at the top of the upward spiral. They are
> coordinates on the CQ cone.

So the number is a **position on an axis**, nought at the inverted pole and a
hundred at the coherent one, and it is read rather than asked: the seat's held
charge and the integrity measured there. Eight numbers, one per axis, and a
person is somewhere on every one of them whether or not they look.

**The twelve.** The codex says twelve masters anchor the cone but never lists
twelve in one place. The coordinate list holds nine and the mirror pairs hold
eight, they overlap on five, and the union is exactly twelve. That arithmetic
is now the roster and is recorded in `BOOK-ERRATA.md`, because a reader who
counts either list alone will find nine or eight and conclude the book is
wrong.

**The descent.** Nine circles, read as the codex reads them:

> Read as a diagnostic tool, not allegory, each circle is a behavioral
> configuration at a specific depth of compression.

Depth follows coherence, so nothing descends at or above the median range and
the ninth is the floor. Satan frozen is stasis rather than power, which is the
detail that keeps the bottom of this product from reading as a villain.

**The dark read, and the rule that comes with it.** Malignant shape plus
decoherent field, both at once. Neither alone is it: a malignant shape in a
coherent field is a hard week, and a decoherent field with a benign shape is
somebody suffering. The pair is what the descent describes, and the product
says so once, as a reading of what is running rather than a label on a person.

Below the floor it stops working the problem and says so, which is the book's
own instruction and now the product's:

> Practitioners encountering this configuration: work at the fetter level. Do
> not attempt to address the structural layer. Refer to licensed clinical
> professionals for parallel support.

Three steps, narcissism to Machiavellianism to psychopathy, with the
malignancy threshold named where the book names it: **harm to others becoming
instrumental**. The third step is not ours to work and the surface says so.

Gated: 33 engine checks and 14 browser checks, including that a cleared field
is never shown the referral and that the nine circles stay readable to anybody.

## 2. Specified. The compass with volume

The ask: a detailed rendered compass you can spin, so what is at stake is
legible from a single image.

It is two cones meeting at the median, which is the shape the codex already
describes. The CQ cone opens upward, the DQ cone opens downward, and the waist
is the median range that the ten band scale already names. That geometry is
the argument for volume: a flat line cannot show that the two cones widen as
they go, and the widening is the point. Distance from the axis is how far a
behaviour has travelled from the quality it started as.

    apex          Source. Not a destination, the condition underneath.
    upper cone    eight axes, widening. the twelve sit on the surface.
    waist         the median range, forty to sixty. the swing lives here.
    lower cone    the same eight, inverted. nine circles by depth.
    terminus      the Decoherent Blueprint. stasis, not power.

**How it should be built.** The existing wheel canvas already carries an
isometric projection, a drag to reframe and a zoom, so this is a second
projection of an existing renderer rather than a new dependency. No library.
Eight meridians for the eight axes, the person's position plotted on each one,
and the surface of the cone drawn from the twelve above and the nine below.
Drag spins it about the vertical. The vertical never tilts past the point
where up stops reading as up.

**What it must not become.** A score with a 3D chart on it. The rule holds:
show more than say, and never a count against a total. The volume earns its
place only if it makes the eight axes readable at a glance, which a list of
eight numbers does not.

Not built in this pass. It is a day of work on the renderer and it should
follow the laws of integrity being wired onto the same surface, since those
are the other thing with a coherent pole and an inverted mirror.

## 3. Groups, cohorts and the community

**The word.** A cohort is now a group of people a practitioner teaches
together. That word was taken this morning by `PAT_COHORT`, the generational
band in the horizon model, and has been given back: the birth band is
`PAT_GEN` and the classroom keeps the word cohort. One word per concept.

**The shape.**

    practitioner   one person with sight of others, by explicit grant
    group          people a practitioner works with together
    cohort         a set of groups, moving through the same material

A group is a container for grants, not a new kind of record. Everything a
practitioner can see is still what a person has explicitly granted, which is
the existing ruling and does not bend because there are now twelve people in a
room instead of one. A group makes the grant easier to issue and easier to
revoke, and it makes revocation visible to the group rather than silent.

What a group adds that a list of individuals does not:

- **A shared horizon.** Everybody in a cohort is working the same material, so
  the markers can be read as a distribution rather than one at a time. That is
  the practitioner's actual question: who is stuck, and on what.
- **Aggregate without exposure.** A practitioner should be able to see that
  four people in a cohort are loaded at the throat without being able to read
  four journals. The aggregate needs its own grant, separate from the
  individual one, and the individual grant must never be implied by group
  membership.
- **A leaving path.** Somebody leaves a group and the sight ends. That has to
  be one action and it has to be theirs, not the practitioner's.

**The warning that came out of the progression work and still stands.** A
practitioner panel with a sortable coherence column is a leaderboard with a
licence. If the group view sorts by CQ it has built the thing the research
already rejected. Sort by what needs attention, never by who is ahead.

**The community.** No, I cannot manage Discord from here. There is no Discord
tool in this session and nothing that would let me create a server, a channel
or a role. Setting one up is yours, and I can write the channel structure,
the rules and the onboarding copy for it.

What *is* connected is beehiiv, and its community side is more than a
newsletter: channels with premium tier gating, members and roles, invites,
posts and comments, moderation and reports, and direct messages. Every one of
those is reachable from here. Given that the tier ladder and the referral are
already designed against beehiiv, a community there would sit inside the
paywall that already exists rather than beside a second identity system on
another platform. Discord is the better room. beehiiv is the better plumbing.
Worth deciding before either is built, because the invite mechanic and the
twenty five patterns attach to whichever one holds the accounts.
