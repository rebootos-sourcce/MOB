# The feelings wheel, as data for the sniffer

Source: the wheel the owner sent on 1 October (round PA). Three rings: seven
primary families, their secondary words, and their tertiary words. The sniffer
looks for every word here, groups each word to its family, and maps the family
to the assemblage points (the addresses and charge families in the engine) it
relates to. The mapping to addresses is for the build to propose and the owner
to confirm.
