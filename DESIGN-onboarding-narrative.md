## His storyboard, in full

### 1. The Funnel

The funnel begins with curiosity.

The user does not need to understand ATUNED before entering it. They need
to recognize something about themselves.

The opening message is:

> **There is more running you than you can see.**

The premise is simple.

Your stories leave patterns in the way you think, feel, behave, and
respond.

ATUNED helps you see those patterns.

The funnel introduces the areas where people may already recognize
friction:

Stress and reactivity.

Disconnection from yourself.

Repeating patterns.

Difficulty focusing or feeling clear.

Emotional experiences that continue to affect behavior.

A sense that something is out of alignment.

The funnel should also establish the larger purpose of the company.

The navigation provides access to:

**Mission.** Why ATUNED exists.

**Vision.** What a more coherent human experience could look like.

**About.** The person who developed the system, the experience behind
it, and why the work exists.

The funnel should not feel like a sales page hiding the product. It
should feel like the first piece of the product.

### 2. The Coherence Assessment

The user gets an initial glimpse of themselves. The assessment is not
presented as a diagnosis. It is a starting point.

> **How coherent are you right now?**

The user gets a first picture of their current state. The important part
is that the result is more than a number. The field begins to show where
things appear clear, where there is tension, and where there may be
something worth exploring.

The user gets the feeling: **Something is happening underneath what I
already know about myself.** That creates the next question: **What is
it?**

### 3. The First Pattern Glimpse

Before creating an account, the user gets a small experience of the Story
Engine. They give ATUNED something real. For example: "I keep putting off
the conversation."

ATUNED begins to work with the user's own language. It can move from
**Story** ("I keep putting off the conversation.") to **Imprint**
(avoiding discomfort) to **Pattern** (avoidance) to **Impact** (the
situation continues, stress remains unresolved, energy is consumed by
anticipation, the behavior repeats).

The user sees that ATUNED isn't simply asking them questions. It is
listening to their stories and looking for what is underneath them. That
is the point where account creation becomes meaningful.

> **This is only the surface.** Create an account and keep going.

The user's work should follow them into ATUNED. They should never have to
start over.

### 4. Onboarding

Once inside, ATUNED answers the question: **What does this thing actually
do?**

The experience begins with the field. Not a dashboard. Not a tutorial
full of instructions. The user encounters ATUNED as something that is
paying attention.

> **Welcome to ATUNED. This is your field. Let's see what's running.**

The user can identify why they are here, but they are not forced to
define themselves before experiencing the system: something keeps
happening, I want to understand myself, I'm feeling stuck, I'm curious,
I'll tell you. Curiosity remains a legitimate starting point.

### 5. The Signal Test

The signal test is the first moment where ATUNED demonstrates its premise
rather than explaining it. The user is guided into their own sensory
experience.

> **Let's show you what's at stake.** Turn your senses inward. Think the
> word yes ten times. Feel its quality in your body. Does it have a
> direction? Does it have a temperature? Does anything move? Now think no
> ten times. Same steps. Do you feel the quality difference between the
> two?

The user then reports what they experienced. The point is not to tell
them what they should have felt. The point is to establish a direct
experience. The explanation comes afterward.

> **This is the mind body connection.**

The stories we relate to do not exist only as ideas. They can become
associated with emotional and bodily responses. ATUNED is designed to
help people find those stories, understand the patterns associated with
them, and work with them.

> **This is what we help you find and release.**

### 6. What ATUNED Does

The four fundamental outcomes become clear:

**SEE.** Find the stories and patterns that are running you.

**UNDERSTAND.** See how those patterns affect your thoughts, emotions,
body, behavior, relationships, and performance.

**RELEASE.** Use personalized protocols to empty the body of stories that
no longer serve you.

**ATTUNE.** Build the behaviors, rituals, and practices that keep you
aligned with who you are choosing to be.

This is the transformation. The software's operating loop is: Discover to
Play to Flow to Embody.

### 7. The Day One Tutorial

The tutorial is not a feature tour. It is the user's first experience of
the actual ATUNED method. The tutorial begins with something the user has
already expressed.

> **Let's look at something from your journal.**

The user sees their own words. This matters. ATUNED is not giving them a
generic example of avoidance. It is working with their life.

### 8. Discover

Discover is where ATUNED finds the pattern. The system separates the
story into its components: **Situation** (a conversation is coming up),
**Internal response** (tension, resistance, anxiety), **Pattern**
(avoidance), **Impact** (stress, unfinished business, drained energy).

The user begins to see that what looked like one isolated event may
actually be part of a larger pattern. Discover is not about labeling the
person. It is about revealing what is happening.

### 9. Play

Play is where the user explores. This is where ATUNED becomes
experimental rather than instructional. Instead of asking a contextless
question like "What are you avoiding?", ATUNED asks within the context of
what it has already learned:

> **What situations do you tend to avoid?**

Difficult conversations. Conflict with others. Being seen or judged.
Important decisions. Commitments or obligations. Other situations.

The user begins to discriminate between the different circumstances in
which the pattern appears. Play is not entertainment. It is
experimentation. The user gets to discover: **What actually changes when
I work with this?**

### 10. Understand

The system then shows the larger pattern. A situation can trigger a
story. The story can produce an emotional response. The emotional
response can affect the body. The body state can influence behavior. The
behavior can reinforce the original story. ATUNED makes that relationship
visible.

**Situation** (a person or event) to **Story** (what the person tells
themselves about it) to **Body response** (emotional and nervous-system
response) to **Behavior** (avoidance, suppression, overreaction,
withdrawal, control, or another learned response) to **Outcome** (the
situation remains unresolved or repeats).

The user can now see: **This isn't just this one moment.** Something is
running through it.

### 11. Release

This is where the deeper purpose of ATUNED becomes explicit. The system
has identified something that may no longer serve the user. ATUNED can
provide a personalized release protocol. The user isn't simply told "let
it go." The system has context. It knows the story. It knows the pattern.
It knows how the pattern has been appearing. It can select an appropriate
protocol for the user to work with it.

> **Empty the body of stories that no longer serve you.**

This becomes one of the defining ideas of the entire product. The journal
fills the bank. The Story Engine sees what is in the bank. The pattern
system organizes it. The release system helps empty it. The user
continues expressing. The system continues seeing. The work becomes
longitudinal.

### 12. Flow

Flow is what happens after insight and release. The question becomes:
**What do you do now?**

ATUNED turns the work into rituals and daily practices. The Ritual
Builder identifies behaviors appropriate to the user's current patterns
and goals: expressing the story, noticing the moment a pattern activates,
returning attention to the body, using a particular release protocol,
practicing nervous-system regulation, changing a specific behavioral
response, creating a daily ritual.

Flow is where change becomes repeatable. It moves the user from "I
understand this" to "I know what I do with this."

### 13. Embody

Embody takes the work out of the application and into life. The system
gives the user specific behaviors to practice: notice the moment you want
to avoid the conversation, pause, notice what happens in your body, use
your assigned practice, make the next choice consciously.

The goal isn't to become someone who knows more about themselves. It is
to become someone who operates differently. That is Embody. The
application becomes less important as the person's capacity increases.

### 14. The Continuing Journey

After the first release, ATUNED becomes a long-term system. The user
continues feeding the Story Engine: **Journal** (express what is
happening), **Story Bank** (build a body of lived experience), **Pattern
Bank** (see what repeats), **Discover** (find what is running), **Play**
(experiment with ways of working with it), **Flow** (create rituals and
practices), **Embody** (take the work into life), **Progress** (see what
is changing over time).

The system remembers. A pattern that was active can become released. A
released pattern can become integrated. If it returns, ATUNED can
recognize that history and investigate what changed. The user's history
becomes part of the instrument.

### 15. The Larger Promise

The product is not ultimately about journaling. It is not about having a
better dashboard. It is not even primarily about self knowledge. The
deeper promise is emotional mastery. ATUNED is designed to help people
empty the body of stories that no longer serve them, improve mental
clarity, improve focus, develop greater emotional regulation, optimize
human performance, recognize and change recurring behavioral patterns,
realign with their moral compass, develop greater coherence between what
they know, feel, say, and do, support mental, physical, and spiritual
well-being, and build a more intentional way of living.

And the core loop remains simple: discover what is running you, play with
ways to change it, flow through practices that keep you aligned, embody
the change in your actual life.

That gives ATUNED a much clearer identity. It is not asking "how can I
help you understand yourself?" It is asking "what are you carrying, how
is it running you, and what happens when you no longer have to carry it?"
