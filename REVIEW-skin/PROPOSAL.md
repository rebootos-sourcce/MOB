# The unified skin proposal (lead's merge of pass 2, round PH)

Eleven pass 2 reports converged (QA's measured report is still being written). Pass 3
simulates, grades and turns this into a build spec. The concept, in the creative seat's
words, is "Tare": every surface shows the load and the person under it, and release is the
load coming off. Three moves make the product read as one thing.

## Move 1: one figure
A free, pre-drawn light figure (the picked Aura prism, outline) is the hub of the Field, the
head of Summary and the centre of the Avatar page. Light is coherence, bend is load, NO number
on it; a warm dim floor so it never goes dark and reads as punishment. Unread: a pencilled,
still outline. Detail stays on the tiers (registers, masks, layers). Pre-drawn once per reading
change with the breath done in CSS opacity (a live figure fails the frame budget). Embody opens
on the Avatar (move `sec:` on `TAB.INTAKE`), Knowledge moves out. The Character tab becomes
"Masks" (one noun: Avatar is the figure, Masks the paid layer).

## Move 2: one ring
The loop drawn as a closed four-arc ring in neutral ink in the top bar, with a return arrow;
the four section buttons stay as the tap targets (skin, not redesign). At 390 one 44 px button
opens a sheet. The ring never changes with time; a quarter is lit by the newest dated act.

## Move 3: one grammar
- COLOUR: hue means PLACE only (the seven seat hues, which do not move). State separates by
  lightness and form. Tiers, practice tracks, root types, layers, loop sections and paid tiers
  move to one slate ramp in four steps. ONE CQ colour (`--cq-ink` per lighting) with an unlit
  slate floor, never red and never invisible. Alarm red only for measured wrong and the
  record dot. Accent loses chroma so it sits at least 0.08 from Throat. Unlit seat = ink at 40
  percent. Seat colours have ONE source: canvases read CSS tokens once per lighting change
  (no per-frame `rgba` or `mixc` allocation), the engine palettes stay as defaults.
- TYPE: `--fs` steps 11, 13, 16, 20, 28, 44 (the onboarding round already adopted it),
  weights 400, 500, 600; `button{font:inherit}`; delete `text-transform:capitalize` (sentence
  case wins, proper nouns exempt, one named edit to design gate 17, one token to revert);
  first sentence on open at 16 px or more.
- SHAPE: radii 4, 10, 16, 999; spacing on a 4 px grid; icons rings (stroke 1.6, round caps);
  solid stroke = measured, dashed = stated or not read, a lock = a sealed double ring.
- MOTION: four verbs (still = unread, breathe = read and live at 4.2 s, travel = charge moving
  with direction, land = you changed something, 320 ms one overshoot); one clock, one `MOTION`
  object, periods of 2.1, 4.2 or 8.4 s only; Compass on elapsed time (it runs at twice speed
  at 120 Hz today); durations stay the four gate 12 allows (120, 220, 320, 420 ms).
- LOCKS: one sealed mark per surface, never grey, named by the next rung ("Opens on tier
  three"), about nine padlocks on the first Field screen go to three or fewer; nothing sells
  beside a reading; "Unlock all sight" behind `?dev=1`.
- UNREAD STATE is its own screen: the hero sentence "There is more running you than you can
  see.", four doors (stay while unread), a quiet fifth to the tables, the privacy line; no
  verdicts, no zeros, no padlocks.
- COPY: one name per concept (charge not weight; band for the coherence ladder, tier for paid
  only; held, released, not read yet); a dash for empty; no count against a total; a person's
  quote labelled "In your words"; retire "Source AI" and the pronoun "we"; the funnel told true
  (the buy page, the quiz scores, the landing loop words).
- DEFECT FIXES that ride along: the 390 wide "not read yet" pill over the zoom buttons; Source
  OS wordmark (the owner's `#343434` ruling stands; show him the 1.36 to 1 number beside it);
  the glasswhite seat contrast; Snow's black stage; Arial buttons in Summary.

## What a skin cannot do (redesign candidates, out of this skin)
The loop ring as the ONLY navigation with the avatar at its centre; a live shared figure
object turning into the Compass cones; the avatar as home (reverses the Field-first ruling);
transcluded reading and any chain join keys (schema).

## Engine and plan dependencies
Many of these overlap plan blocks J4 (Field summary first), J5 (masks by tier), J9, and the
architecture slices P11 (lock) and P18b (Next). The skin build orders itself after them.
