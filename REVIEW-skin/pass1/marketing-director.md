## THE SOUL
This is an instrument that reads a person back to themselves in plain, exact sentences. The Summary paragraph is the product and the best ad it has. Everything around it, the padlocks, the dashes, the profile picker, is the app talking about itself instead of the person.

## WHAT BREAKS COHERENCE (ranked)
1. The first screen has no promise. Cost: every segment spends its four seconds on a dashboard. Where: Field, unread.
2. Padlocks and "See tiers" appear before value. Nine on the first screen. Cost: S1, S3 and S6 read it as sold to, and `ATUNED-experience-icp-model.md` section 2 lists "paywall before value" as forbidden.
3. The centrepiece is behind the lock. Character, the avatar figure, is tier three. Cost: S6 and S7 never see the thing "What is Atuned? Your avatar" promises.
4. The unread panel prints a verdict ("Most shut: Truth"). Cost: S3 and S8 lose trust in one line.
5. The proof is buried. Codex under Embody, measurement note at footnote size. Cost: S3, S4, S11.
6. Phone first screen puts the doors below the fold and overlaps the pill with the zoom buttons. Cost: S7, the largest segment.
7. Title case and truncation against the voice rules. Cost: small, but S3 counts them.

## REDESIGN CANDIDATES
- One: the unread state should be its own screen, not an empty instrument. Hero sentence, four doors, the codex door. A skin cannot do it, because it changes what is on the stage and not how it looks. Gain: the first four seconds go from 3 to about 7 for every segment at once.
- Two: Avatar free preview. This is a product ruling, not mine. I flag it because the centrepiece cannot also be the upsell.

## ARITHMETIC
- Panel, simulated: 315 of 10,000 reach a score as built, 1,948 with the fixes already recommended. That is the number a first screen can move. Willingness to pay does not move with a skin.
- S6 plus S7 is 2,300 people at accepted $19 and $11 a month. Their value is retention floor and referral. Chase them with the free tier visible, not with locks.
- What would have to be true: the first screen carries at least half of arrivals to a committed story. I cannot measure that. A two-headline test on the landing and a count of who reaches Story commit would replace this guess.

## RISKS
- Everything here is a simulated focus group plus screenshots, not observed users.
- The demo profile, Marcus at 62, shows a best case. A real S1 at 34 sees a lower band. Capture that screenshot before anyone pitches this.
- The login I admire is a mockup. The build opens on the Field. Login A first is ruled but not in the shots.
- Removing locks may cost tier conversion. The panel says pay follows demonstrated demand, so I accept that trade.
